## CHAPTER 38: THE SPECTRAL HAIR TIE & THE LOST MEMORY

Pierre lunged across the flooded floorboards, driving the bronze museum javelin toward the beast's flank.

"I do not like the brain eating," Pierre announced with a shudder of profound disgust. "That is exceedingly repulsive. Here is a gift for you!" <!-- L1042:pierre -->

He yanked hard to wrench the weapon free, but the brittle ancient bronze gave way with a sharp snap, lodging the spearhead firmly in the beast's coarse hide.

"What a piece of junk!" Pierre muttered in disbelief, tossing the splintered shaft aside. His fingers fumbled at his belt pouches and caught the stopper of an amber glass flask. "Let us see what this will do." <!-- L1071:pierre -->

Pierre unstopped the amber vial as he dropped from the podium, splashing the museum etching acid across the thrashing hair tendrils. The corrosive solvent bit into several dark coils with a sharp hiss of gray smoke, but the deluge pouring from the emergency ceiling sprinklers immediately diluted the liquid before it could burn deeper. Pierre stumbled back through the spray, flicking burning droplets from his woolen coat.

"I genuinely believed a proper conditioning rinse would be of service!" Pierre grumbled, wiping sulfuric residue from his spectacles. "Instead, it has merely produced unruly smoke!" <!-- L1082-L1084:pierre -->

The transformed woman hissed through clenched teeth, her focus never wavering as she pulled her whipping coils tighter around Eusacles's throat to accelerate the extraction.

Across the stage, Alfie observed Pierre's desperate attempt at a hair treatment. Her painted seashell eyes flashed with inspiration as she snapped her carved fingers.

"Right! Mage Hand!" Alfie rasped, thrusting both arms upward. "Except not a hand, mate—a Mage Band! A proper elastic scrunchie to tie these filthy tendrils away into a tidy bun! Corral the coils, Pierre!" <!-- L1087-L1101:alfie -->

A ring of glowing violet energy materialized above the platform, stretching wide like an elastic hair tie.

Pierre plunged forward into the storm of thrashing tendrils, batting the electric whips inward toward the shimmering ring.

"Your hair is an absolute disaster!" Pierre shouted through the roar of cascading water. "Alfie has provided a magnificent accessory! Show some gratitude!" <!-- L1104-L1109:pierre -->

Pierre threw his arms around the writhing mass, sweeping the coils clean into the aperture. Alfie yanked the psychic cord shut, snapping the spectral band tight around the woman's crown. But as Pierre gripped the final tendril, a searing jolt of psychic backlash surged up his arms and tore into his nervous system.

Pierre dropped hard to both knees, clutching his temples as his woolen beret tumbled into the flooded aisle. A cherished, golden memory—strolling the sunlit gardens of a French chateau with Janette, the laughter and warmth of youth—crumbled into mist. In its place settled only a hollow, synthetic chill: the distant recollection of exchanging sterile messages with a virtual acquaintance across a computer terminal, never having walked beside her at all.

"Janette," Pierre whispered, his voice trembling as he stared at his wet hands. "The chateau... where has it gone?" <!-- L1111-L1118:pierre -->

Yet the band held fast. Constrained into an immaculate, tight topknot, the severed tendrils were yanked away from Eusacles's neck, breaking the psychic siphon.

"Thanks, buddy," Eusacles gasped, coughing as he rubbed the dark bruises on his throat. <!-- L1119:eusacles -->

"Disgusting," Alfie muttered in deep satisfaction, ducking into the dry recess beneath the mahogany teacher's desk. <!-- L1120-L1135:alfie -->

On the opposite side of the stage, Professor Dravin stepped forward, his eyes narrowed with authoritative academic resolve. He raised his tarnished silver bell and focused his concentration until the air vibrated with violet threshold energy. A massive spectral force took shape in mid-air above the flooded orchestra pit: a dense, leather-bound scholarly encyclopedia, its heavy buckram covers and vellum pages humming with ancient gravity.

"A scholarly instrument," Dravin announced with stern academic gravity, directing the hovering volume toward the advancing satyrs. <!-- L1159-L1173:dravin -->

With a swift flick of his wrist, the heavy lexicon hurtled through the mist, slamming into the lead beast's horned brow with the concussive force of an anvil.
