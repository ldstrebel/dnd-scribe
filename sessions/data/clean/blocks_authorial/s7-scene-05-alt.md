## CHAPTER 46: RUM PANCAKES & THE THREAD OF FOUR CENTURIES

The aroma of sizzling batter and spiced berry compote hung thick over the sunny cedar porch. Eusacles sat on a wooden bench, propping his boots on the railing as Alfie perched on the table beside a towering stack of golden flapjacks dripping with dark blackberry glaze.

Eusacles tilted his head, watching the tiny driftwood duelist spear a bite of pancake with his miniature dagger. "You know, this might be a little insensitive of me to ask, but I do want to know. Can you taste things?" <!-- L0706:eusacles -->

Alfie chewed thoughtfully, painted wooden brow furrowing. "Yeah, I can. I mean, I don't have much to compare it to, but yeah, I like pancakes. And let me tell you, this is some really good jam." <!-- L0711:alfie -->

"I've really only ever eaten pancakes, to be honest, up to this very particular moment," Alfie added, licking a speck of purple syrup from his thumb. "Now I've had pancakes and jam." <!-- L0732:alfie -->

Mike stepped out from the kitchen doorway with a broad grin, carrying a heavy amber glass bottle. "I mean, what else do you really need? You got pancakes, and maybe a glass of rum on the side." He set a glass down in front of Alfie—a standard heavy-bottomed shot glass that, to a two-foot figurine, resembled a massive ceremonial chalice. <!-- L0733:mike -->

Eusacles reached out, catching the rim of the glass. "Hey, hey, hey! Alfie's not even twenty-one years old. Not even one yet!" <!-- L0741:eusacles -->

Alfie puffed out his carved chest, resting a hand on his needle hilt. "Yeah, I'd say I'm probably like four hundred and fifty-eight or something, collectively, if you're thinking about all the threads." <!-- L0742:alfie -->

"Well, you did say you were born four days ago," Eusacles pointed out. <!-- L0745:eusacles -->

"Well, that's also simultaneously true," Alfie shot back with a jaunty shrug. "It's a bit of a conundrum, isn't it?" <!-- L0748:alfie -->

Before Eusacles could object, Alfie's right arm jerked upward with sudden, uncanny coordination—the same lingering thread consciousness that had drawn him to Dr. Thorne's anomaly binder seizing the shot glass. Alfie tipped the dark liquor back in one colossal gulp. <!-- L0758:alfie -->

Alfie's button eyes widened in shock as the burning alcohol hit his carved throat. "Whoa. That takes me back. I feel that every memory I have has rum in it." <!-- L0760:alfie -->

Eusacles stared in awe. "Can action figures get drunk?" <!-- L0767:eusacles -->

Pierre, strolling up the porch steps after leaving Susanna with a fresh plate, adjusted his spectacles. "Nope. They can get hammered." <!-- L0768:pierre -->

Alfie hopped down from the table. The moment his tiny boots struck the cedar decking, his balance swayed into an exaggerated, rolling gait, arms swinging loosely at his sides like an old salt navigating a pitching frigate. He weaved toward the lantern post, thoroughly tipsy. "I'm going to go over to the lanterns over there." <!-- L0780:alfie -->

Pierre blinked, watching the miniature sailor swagger across the porch. "Mike, did you put more than just rum in the pancakes?" <!-- L0783:pierre -->

"Well, we put the rum in the pancakes, and then we also put the rum in the jam," Mike answered cheerfully, leaning against the doorframe. "And then, of course, we put the rum in the rum." <!-- L0784:mike -->

Alfie turned around, clinging to the porch railing with one hand and holding up his silver thimble with the other. "You just... you're too funny, mate! You talk crazy. You got any more of that rum?" <!-- L0789:alfie -->

Teddy stepped out onto the porch, raising a firm hand. "No more for Alfie. But we do have a bit of a problem, because usually we would offer you food, but there's rum in the pancakes and rum in the jam." <!-- L0797:teddy -->

"I murdered someone earlier," Alfie proclaimed loudly to the clearing, swaying on his heels. <!-- L0800:alfie -->

"No, no, you killed someone earlier," Eusacles corrected patiently from the bench. "They weren't innocent." <!-- L0801:eusacles -->

"Yeah, exactly," Alfie slurred happily. "Called me a doll. You got any more?" <!-- L0804:alfie -->

A soft, melodic voice drifted from the bottom of the porch steps. Rosa, the resident free-love artist from the 1970s in her embroidered peasant blouse and flowing denim skirt, smiled gently up at them. "Wow. I heard there was some violence that happened, and I think Alfie and I should have a little chat about what occurred. Pierre, I heard you might want to as well." <!-- L0806:rosa -->

Pierre let out a long, relieved breath. "Actually, that would be very nice." <!-- L0807:pierre -->

"Well, you want to talk about my new scarf?" Alfie asked, proudly pointing to the black tendril tied around his collar. "It's really cool." <!-- L0808:alfie -->

"Wow, that is a fantastic scarf," Rosa said warmly, scooping the little driftwood duelist up onto her shoulder. "Say more about that." <!-- L0811:rosa -->

As Rosa carried Alfie toward the garden studio with Pierre following behind, Pierre paused, glancing back at Teddy by the railing. "Teddy, one thing is not clear to me. Why have they not found you here?" <!-- L0821:pierre -->

Teddy leaned on his cedar cane, gazing thoughtfully out toward the misty tree line. "It is a good question," he answered. "I've heard reports they have been in the Lost Roads before, sending standard creatures like the ones you've faced. But I was always careful never to lead the Redactor here. That's what I always called that shadowy inky creature—the Redactor. It seemed thematic." <!-- L0822-L0824:teddy -->

"I was always able to make sure I never led it to the Margin or into our sector of the Lost Roads," Teddy explained. "I would sneak away while hidden." <!-- L0827:teddy -->

"There were times I left the Margin to chase fragments," Teddy continued, his expression hardening. "And once, when I was called by the gods to meet with them, the Lost Roads spit me out at an inconvenient place. When I went to find another route, I saw an opportunity to take the Hermes Express. But the Redactor was already there, waiting. It seemed to have teleported ahead." <!-- L0830:teddy -->

"Rather than lead it back into the Lost Roads, I jumped on with Hermes," Teddy said. "His protection allowed me to escape. But unfortunately, I didn't give sufficient payment, so he just deposited me right back at the Margin instead." <!-- L0831:teddy -->

Pierre scoffed, shaking his head. "Seems kind of strange if the gods were calling you! They should have paid your taxi fare!" <!-- L0832:pierre -->

"Why are the gods always acting like they're broke?" Eusacles chimed in. <!-- L0833:eusacles -->

Teddy gave a dry chuckle. "Hermes told me, 'Leave the messaging to me,' and sent me back. He seemed peeved that they would even need me to be there in the first place." <!-- L0834:teddy -->

"That sounds like a monopoly to me," Pierre declared. "No wonder he is rich!" <!-- L0835:pierre -->

"He wants to control the messaging," Eusacles agreed. "Classic dictator behavior." <!-- L0836:eusacles -->
