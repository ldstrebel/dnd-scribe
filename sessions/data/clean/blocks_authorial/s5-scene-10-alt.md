Inside rested a thick buckram binder, its edges worn to raw gray board, humming with ozone chill—an active temporal Fragment. <!-- L1215-L1218 -->

Without hesitation, Dravin reached down and bodily seized Alfie around his waist.

Mid-air, Alfie kicked his carved legs, groaning in despair. "Not again! Not me again!" <!-- L1219:alfie -->

"Sorry, Alfie," Dravin whispered, slamming the protesting doll face-first into the cold buckram binder. <!-- L1256:dravin -->

* * *

The instant Alfie struck the cracked leather, the universe tore open.

The auditorium shattered into a strobe of violet lightning, plunging them into a subterranean darkness reeking of damp masonry, bleach, and bitter sulfur. In an unrecorded basement ward beneath the 1948 university foundations, rows of iron cots stretched into the gloom, bearing unresponsive patients in milky sedative trances. A frantic physician in a stained smock plunged needles into their veins, scribbling notes on a clipboard. <!-- L1229-L1230 -->

Before their eyes, reality thinned to wet ink on parchment. An unseen hand dragged a fountain pen across the binder's ledger:

STABLE.

With a violent stroke, the ink twisted, rewriting the record:

STALE. <!-- L1232 -->

The vision convulsed, snapping back to an earlier hour. The younger physician stared at an amber beaker with mounting horror, hurling it against the stone wall with a guttural cry of revulsion, smashing burners and research notes to obliterate what he had discovered before it could be buried. <!-- L1233 -->

*CRACK.*

* * *

Reality snapped back like a physical blow. Panicked screams tore through the auditorium as blood-red emergency strobes flashed across the ceiling. Two hundred scholars were overturning desks, stampeding toward the fire exits in sheer bedlam.

Slamming inward through the rear double doors, three towering figures in trench coats strode into the hall. Beneath their dark brims, thick ram horns curved back from their brows, and cloven hooves shattered the floor tiles as sulfurous yellow eyes locked onto the stage. <!-- L1242 -->

The Reductors had arrived for the binder. Satyrs—or satans.

Pierre leveled his bronze javelin, and Eusacles rolled his morningstar in his fist, grinning grimly behind his dark glasses as they braced for the onslaught.
