Late into the night, the newcomers settled into the cedar guest cabin, weary from their planar journey across the Lost Roads. Pierre lay propped against the pillows, balancing chilled cucumber slices over his eyelids with delicate precision. When Naomi barged through the doorway clutching an armful of parchment, Pierre bolted upright, clutching his cream jar in indignant protest. "It can wait until morning! I am in the middle of my face care, and I only have so many cucumbers!" Before she could stammer out her research notes, Pierre gently and firmly swung the heavy timber door shut, latching the bolt with a polite reminder that eight in the morning would be quite early enough. <!-- L0881-L0891 -->

Morning arrived in crisp mountain sunlight, heralded by a brisk knock on the porch planks. Mike stood framed in the doorway, balancing an iron griddle, a smoking pot of coffee, and a platter of golden flapjacks. "Saturday pancakes are up!" he announced with an easy grin, leaning against the wooden railing. "Eat first. Naomi's been planning non-stop since she landed here over a decade ago, but we've been in this valley a hundred years—the mystery isn't going anywhere." Setting his spatula aside, Mike looked down at his apron, his voice softening with quiet conviction. "People wonder how an ordinary guy like me stays grounded here without demigod blood. Truth is, I keep a card in my pocket from my daughter. Just three words—To Dad. That concrete tether is what keeps me human." <!-- L0900-L0922 -->
