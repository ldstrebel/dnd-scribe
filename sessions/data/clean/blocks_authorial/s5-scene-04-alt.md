## CHAPTER 30: THE CAMPUS AT UNIVERSITY UNIVERSITY & THE TIN-FOIL PROTEST

Pierre balanced the flat bronze tip of his museum javelin over the iron grate, deftly flipping another crepe as dark rum sizzled violently, filling the room with sweet, caramelized vapor. <!-- L0407-L0409 -->

Eusacles took a massive, unceremonious bite, eyebrows shooting up above his aviator frames. "Damn. That is a straight-up pirate crepe." <!-- L0420:eusacles -->

At the far end of the bench, an electric tremor seized the grain of Alfie's driftwood arm. The invisible Fate thread bound to her core pulsed violently. Drawing her miniature whittling knife, her needle blade flickered in the hearthlight as she shaved away ribbons of gray timber. Amber light welled from the gouges, hardening into the sharp runic buckle of a cincher. <!-- L0422-L0426 -->

"What do you reckon would be most useful, mates?" Alfie asked, holding up her glowing timber arm. <!-- L0429:alfie -->

"What are you doing to your arm, little mate?" Eusacles asked around a mouthful of crepe. <!-- L0435:eusacles -->

"A bit of Wordcraft birthright," Alfie rasped proudly, blowing away wood dust. "Changed *Chaos Bolt* to *Chaos Belt*. Carved it right into my timber so I don't burn an honest reserve of spark. I picture reaching down in a brawl, pulling out whatever strange device the loom feels like tossing me." <!-- L0446-L0487:alfie -->

"An admirable adaptation," Dravin agreed, tapping his notebook. <!-- L0500-L0514:dravin -->

Theodore approached the table, spreading a rolled canvas terrain map. "Before you set off, you have a logistical choice. Hermes can fold space through celestial postal corridors and drop you on the campus steps—for a price. The alternative is the Forgotten Trail through the untamed foothills. Overgrown and wild, but keeps you entirely off the celestial registry." <!-- L0528-L0537:theodore -->

Pierre waved his fork dismissively, his lip curling with aristocratic outrage. "I would rather walk barefoot across broken tiles than pay an athletic courier who thinks France is an imaginary prank! Continental shipping only! We take the Forgotten Trail!" <!-- L0538:pierre -->
