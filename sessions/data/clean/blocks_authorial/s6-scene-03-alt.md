## CHAPTER 34: THE ISLAND-TIME SMITE & FELINE SIGHT

"I do not think this beast cares for your curriculum, Professor!" Pierre shouted, lunging forward with his museum spear as the satyr wrestled Dravin across the oak boards. From the shadowed stage wing, heavy footsteps thudded against the hardwood as Eusacles strode onto the platform, his denim jacket open and dark aviator sunglasses gleaming beneath the harsh halogens. Pierre pointed his javelin tip toward the cloven-hoofed infiltrator. "Right there! Beat this beast!" <!-- L0481-L0495:pierre -->

Alfie bobbed her driftwood head from the edge of the podium blotter. "It's just generally good vibes, you know? Just keep the good vibes going." <!-- L0506:alfie -->

Eusacles muttered in agreement as he reached for the heavy brass chain hooked to his denim belt loop. "No bad vibes allowed on this stage. Hey, is this thing considered a fiend?" <!-- L0516-L0520:eusacles -->

The gambler unclipped his brass pocket watch. The timepiece chimed, wrapped around his broad wrist, and unfurled with blinding golden friction into a massive spiked iron morningstar. Across the flanged head, engraved letters read *Good Vibes*, backed by *Island Time*. Eusacles brought the weapon down with concussive demigod fury directly onto the satyr's horned brow. Golden radiant flame burst from the spikes—a brutal Divine Smite that cracked through planar bone, leaving the beast reeling and drenched in black ichor. Eusacles nodded with grim satisfaction as the fiend collapsed. <!-- L0525-L0556:eusacles -->

Across the hall, the transformed woman hissed in raw fury as her vanguard dropped. She lashed her crackling brain tendrils toward the stage, but Alfie was already dancing to her cassette tape on the desktop, and the electrical coils snapped harmlessly through empty air. Overhead, the shrill alarm finally triggered the building's emergency sprinkler valves. A freezing torrent cascaded from the ceiling, flooding the aisles and charging the rising floor water with arcs of blue static. <!-- L0559-L0568 -->

Pierre marched to the front rim of Alfie's static ward, glaring down at the crackling woman in the flooded aisle. "You are producing an excessive quantity of electricity!" he announced with haughty Parisian indignation. "It is entirely unnecessary, and you are doing far too much!" He locked his gaze onto her brow and exerted his mental will. An intense tremor shuddered through Pierre's temples, accompanied by a low, guttural purr vibrating deep behind his skull. Behind his spectacles, his pupils flattened and sharpened into luminous, vertical feline slits. The hall remapped into brilliant chromatic heat signatures, revealing the pulsing anatomy of the hostiles before his mental vice froze the woman rigid in mid-stride. <!-- L0579-L0584:pierre -->

Pierre blinked rapidly, his hand rising to steady his beret as the strange chromatic vision persisted. "Alfie, is there something wrong with my eyes? I feel extraordinarily bizarre right now." Alfie held the miniature cassette toward him with a wide grin: "Mate, you're a proper rock star and you should get paid!" Pierre lifted the polished bronze blade of his museum spear, using the flat metal surface as a hand mirror. He caught his reflection and recoiled in horror from his own slitted feline pupils. "Mon dieu! Alfie, I should not have looked at you! I could have turned you to stone! This is terribly irresponsible of me!" He peeked through his woolen sleeve at her painted torso. "Thank heavens you have no mortal blood in your body, or you would be a carved garden ornament by now!" <!-- L0595-L0609:pierre -->

Alfie spun her sewing-needle rapier with an easy grin. "Maybe I'm actually such a good friend for you to have! You can't turn me to stone, so it's a pretty good deal, mate!" Pierre extended his leather-gloved fist, and the little doll tapped her carved wooden knuckles against his fingers as the last students fled through the lobby doors, leaving only the dazed Dr. Thorne huddled beside Dravin. <!-- L0610-L0620:alfie -->
