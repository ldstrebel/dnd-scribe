## CHAPTER 34: THE ISLAND-TIME SMITE & FELINE SIGHT

"I do not think this beast cares for your curriculum, Professor!" Pierre shouted, lunging forward with his museum spear as the satyr wrestled Dravin across the oak boards. From the stage wing, heavy footsteps thudded against the hardwood as Eusacles strode onto the platform, his denim jacket open and dark aviator sunglasses gleaming. Pierre pointed his javelin tip toward the cloven-hoofed infiltrator. "Right there! Beat this beast!" <!-- L0481-L0495:pierre -->

"No bad vibes allowed on this stage," Eusacles muttered, unclipping the heavy brass chain from his denim belt loop. "Did someone call for good vibes? Hey, is this thing considered a fiend?" With a sharp flick of his wrist, the brass pocket watch expanded into a massive spiked iron morningstar engraved with the words *Good Vibes*. <!-- L0516-L0547:eusacles -->

Across the reverse face, engraved letters read *Island Time*. Without waiting for an answer, Eusacles brought the flanged head down with concussive demigod fury directly onto the satyr's horned brow. Golden radiant flame burst from the spikes—a brutal Divine Smite that cracked through planar bone, leaving the beast reeling and drenched in black ichor. <!-- L0556 -->

Across the hall, the transformed woman hissed in fury. Seeing her vanguard crushed, she lashed her crackling brain tendrils toward Alfie, but the driftwood figurine was already dancing to her cassette tape, and the electrical coils snapped harmlessly through empty air. Overhead, the deluge from the emergency fire sprinklers began cascading into the aisles, charging the pooling water with blue static. <!-- L0559-L0568 -->

Pierre marched to the front rim of the ward, locking eyes with the transformed woman below. "You are producing an excessive quantity of electricity!" he announced with aristocratic indignation. "It is entirely unnecessary, and you are doing far too much! *Hold Person!*" The somatic enchantment flared, and deep in Pierre's temples, a guttural purr vibrated as his pupils sharpened into vertical, luminous feline slits. The hall remapped into brilliant chromatic heat signatures, revealing the pulsing anatomy of the intruders before his mental vice froze the woman rigid in mid-stride. <!-- L0579-L0584:pierre -->

Pierre blinked rapidly, his hand flying to his black woolen beret. "Alfie, is there something wrong with my eyes? I feel extraordinarily bizarre right now." He held up the polished bronze blade of his spear, recoiling at his own slitted feline gaze. "Mon dieu! Alfie, I could have turned you to stone! Thank heavens you have no mortal blood, or you would be a carved garden ornament by now!" <!-- L0595-L0607:pierre -->

"Alfie, yes," Pierre sighed with theatrical relief, lowering his weapon. "You and your incomprehensible musical tastes are remarkably accommodating." <!-- L0609:pierre -->

Alfie spun her sewing-needle rapier with a grin. "Maybe I'm actually such a good friend for you to have! You can't turn me to stone, so it's a pretty good deal!" Pierre offered his leather-gloved fist, and Alfie bumped her tiny wooden knuckles against his fingers as the last students fled through the lobby doors, leaving only the dazed Dr. Thorne huddled beside Dravin. <!-- L0610-L0620:alfie -->
