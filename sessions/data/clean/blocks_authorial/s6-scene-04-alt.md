## CHAPTER 35: THUNDERWAVE & THE PETRIFYING CARTRIDGE

Across the glowing barrier, the transformed woman remained frozen fast in Pierre's unseen psychic vice. Tendrils of her serpentine hair twitched against the restraint, crackling with errant bursts of blue-white voltage that scorched the upholstery of the nearest auditorium seats. Pierre adjusted his black woolen beret with elegant fingers, peering over the rostrum as ozone stung his nostrils. "You stay right there," Pierre called out haughtily, forcing steady Parisian composure into his voice. "You listen to me, and you remain civilized." <!-- L0623-L0627:pierre -->

Alfie perched on the edge of the battered mahogany desk, her painted seashell button eyes narrowing at the frozen adversary. The tiny driftwood figurine planted her carved feet firmly against the wood. "Right then!" she declared with fierce determination, drawing back her needle rapier before thrusting it forward. A concussive shockwave of sonic thunder erupted from the slender blade, tearing down the aisle with the force of a detonating cannon. The atmospheric blast shattered rows of student desks into kindling and staggered the paralyzed adversary backward, shearing showers of black sparks from her coiling hair. Alfie immediately hopped backward, ducking beneath the heavy oak rungs of a lecture chair for cover. <!-- L0635-L0687:alfie -->

Up on the stage apron, the thunderous concussion gave Professor Edward Dravin the exact opening he needed. The scholar braced his heels against the wet oak floorboards and threw his shoulder upward, dislodging the satyr pinned atop him and sending the horned brute sprawling across the platform. Dravin rose to his feet, pushed his wire-rimmed spectacles up his nose, and found a clear, unobstructed firing lane down into the central aisle. <!-- L0688-L0707 -->

"A scholar does not discard an effective tool," Dravin remarked mildly, withdrawing the blued-steel pneumatic pistol from his inner tweed pocket. The museum weapon hummed with pressurized gas, loaded with petrifying darts designed to neutralize anomalous biological specimens. "She cannot move. A stationary target is quite sufficient to test the mechanism." <!-- L0712-L0731:dravin -->

"Close the distance, Professor!" Pierre urged from the orchestra railing, leaning out over the drop as hooves clattered nearby. "Point-blank! Put the muzzle right to her brow so the shot cannot fail!" <!-- L0738-L0743:pierre -->

"It is a ranged dart, Pierre," Dravin replied calmly, raising the iron sights to center the front post directly on the paralyzed woman. <!-- L0745-L0749:dravin -->
