As the dining hall thinned, a crisp woman with severe, squared shoulders approached Pierre's table clutching a dark leather clipboard. <!-- L0757 -->

"Hello, my name is Anna Smith," she announced with clipped military cadence. "I am conducting your intake for import into the Margin. You mentioned several curious things earlier... specifically regarding snakes. I assume gorgon heritage, perhaps?" <!-- L0758 -->

Pierre blinked, instinctively smoothing his lapels before adjusting his spectacles. "Well, Anna, you have correctly identified my heritage, and that does mean something to me. But honestly, I was concerned when I heard there were half-bloods here. I would honestly just like to know where the gorgons are." <!-- L0759-L0760 -->

Anna made a sharp pencil stroke on her pad. "We rarely get half-monsters here on purpose. Why were you sent away from your settlement?" <!-- L0761 -->

"That is a very personal question for a first day, do you not think?" Pierre replied, raising a brow. "What I can tell you is that my mother told me to come to America to find perspective. As far as I can tell, crashing into Greek libraries is a pretty normal day in America." <!-- L0762 -->

Anna held his gaze for a second, then softened her posture by a fraction of an inch. "I apologize for my terseness. I have been told it is from my background, and I apologize for the aggressiveness." <!-- L0768 -->

"No, no, I find you quite lovely," Pierre reassured her, offering an appreciative wave of his hand. "You are direct and to the point. There is nothing wrong with you—it is merely the situation you have been put in." <!-- L0769-L0771 -->

"I prefer to work in the shadows on patrol," Anna conceded quietly, "but I served as an army nurse in the United States Army. You sound as though you are on a pilgrimage. What is your goal here?" <!-- L0772 -->

"I know who my mother is," Pierre said gently. "I am doing this as a favor to her. I figured I would stay two or three weeks, fly back to Paris, tell her there was nothing for me to learn, and work on my own skills." He narrowed his eyes in playful suspicion. "One quick question... is your name really Anna Smith?" <!-- L0774-L0778 -->

"Of course," Anna countered smoothly without flinching. "US Army nurse. Go America. Burgers and whiskey." <!-- L0781 -->

Pierre laughed, tapping his saucer. "Okay, Anna. Your secret is safe with me. And I will take an extra piece of bread—the bread here is quite good." <!-- L0784 -->

Across the hall, Mike leaned over a rustic trestle table across from Professor Edward Dravin, spinning a pencil between his fingers. "All right, Edward. Looks like you got stuck with me. Curious—why was a distinguished professor like you riding a rickety bus outside Vegas?" <!-- L0787-L0789 -->

"There is actually a tremendous amount of Greek mythology in Las Vegas," Dravin replied with dry academic relish, straightening his tie. "The origins of gambling lie directly within Greek mythology: Hermes and Pan casting lots; the Fates determining mortal fortune through chance." <!-- L0790-L0794 -->

"Well, learn something new every day," Mike grinned, shaking his head. "Egg on my face!" <!-- L0795 -->

After the meal, Teddy escorted them down the lantern-lit pine path to the guest cabin, observing, "Out here, you learn that every myth was just history before someone decided to edit the manuscript." Inside the cedar room, an elderly resident named Rosa was smoothing heavy woolen blankets over the cots. Seeing Alfie balanced upon Pierre's shoulder, she asked if the little traveler preferred soil and timber to water. <!-- L0809-L0833 -->

Alfie adjusted his sewing needle rapier with a sharp click of his wooden heel. "Dolls don't usually drive ships, mate. I don't recommend it." <!-- L0849 -->

Before Rosa could fold the next quilt, the timber door banged open. Naomi stood framed in the entrance, gasping for breath with an armful of rolled parchment. "Sorry I'm late! I was knee-deep in research, but it's super important!" <!-- L0873 -->
