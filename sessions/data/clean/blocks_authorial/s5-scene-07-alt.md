## CHAPTER 31: THE 1948 TRIAL NOTES & THE TEMPORAL SEAM

Ascending the limestone steps, the companions pushed through a circle of demonstrators brandishing placards reading *THINK ABOUT IT*. "There was no refrigeration in 1948!" a demonstrator shouted at Eusacles, tapping his tin-foil fedora. "The trial notes were completely unsigned! Think about it!" <!-- L0809-L0824:protester -->

Pierre paused on the granite riser, tilting his head with reluctant aesthetic admiration. "In America, they wear baked potato wrapping shaped into rather nice hats, I must say," he murmured, pointing with approval at the sharp creases in the foil fedora brim. <!-- L0813-L0815:pierre -->

"Fascinating thesis," Dravin murmured, steering the party through the bronze doors. "I only wish I possessed a spell to heat that foil." <!-- L0827-L0830:dravin -->

Inside, the amphitheater rose in steep tiers of curved mahogany beneath a vaulted plaster ceiling, packed with hundreds of scholars. Brushing past the flustered university faculty host who was supposed to introduce the guest speaker, Dravin stepped decisively up to the mahogany lectern, tapping the brass receiver to silence the room. <!-- L0854-L0884 -->

"Attention, colleagues and students," Dravin announced with the effortless resonance of a tenured chair. "This afternoon we have the privilege of hearing from Dr. Aris Thorne on the 1948 clinical trials breakthrough. Please grant her your undivided scholarly focus. Doctor, the lectern is yours." <!-- L0887-L0889:dravin -->

Alfie pumped her miniature wooden fist from his shoulder. "Give him a shout, mates! He knows his onions, this one!" <!-- L0894-L0898:alfie -->

Dr. Thorne stepped forward in her tailored olive coat, offering a grateful smile. "Thank you, Professor. I was beginning to dread the introduction." <!-- L0903:thorne -->

"A small courtesy, Doctor," Dravin bowed patricianly. "What room have the organizers assigned you for debriefing? I shall ensure hot tea is prepared." <!-- L0904:dravin -->

"The private green room behind the organ loft," she replied gratefully, gesturing backstage. <!-- L0905:thorne -->

As the house lights dimmed, Dravin drifted off the side stairs toward the backstage lock with the stolen keys. <!-- L0907-L0913 -->

Pierre wedged himself into the front row, poking the flustered faculty host with his javelin ferrule. "Pardon, monsieur," Pierre hissed. "Where is the sculpting laboratory?" <!-- L0931-L0935:pierre -->
