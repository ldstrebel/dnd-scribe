## CHAPTER 31: THE 1948 TRIAL NOTES & THE TEMPORAL SEAM

"If you asked where the janitorial college was, I could tell you right now," Rick Ready retorted, snapping his cleaning rag on its retractable cord. "The art building? It's not my zone!" <!-- L0797:attendant -->

Pierre tipped his woolen beret with icy Parisian condescension, stepping backward from the custodian. "It was thoroughly displeasurable to make your acquaintance, Monsieur Ready. I hope I never see you again!" <!-- L0798-L0800:pierre -->

"Likewise!" Rick Ready barked, turning his cleaning trolley back toward the quad. <!-- L0801:attendant -->

Leaving the custodian behind, Pierre strolled off in a haughty huff, completely unaware that his artistic outrage had served as a masterful distraction. Dravin fell into step beside him, quietly patting the heavy brass ring of master keys safely tucked inside his tweed breast pocket. <!-- L0809 -->

Ascending the limestone steps, the companions passed a circle of demonstrators brandishing placards reading *THINK ABOUT IT*. Each demonstrator wore a pointed tin-foil fedora folded entirely out of heavy-gauge tin foil. Pierre paused on the granite riser, tilting his head with reluctant aesthetic admiration. "In America, they wear baked potato wrapping shaped into rather nice hats, I must say," he murmured, pointing at the sharp creases in the foil fedora brim. <!-- L0813-L0815:pierre -->

"It’s a universal constant, Pierre," Eusacles said with a wry smirk, adjusting his aviator sunglasses. "Back in Vegas, we have guys wearing colanders on the Strip swearing the roulette wheels emit lunar frequencies." <!-- L0818:eusacles -->

A demonstrator thrust a placard toward them. "There was no refrigeration in 1948! The trial notes were completely unsigned! Think about it!" <!-- L0820:protester -->

Eusacles stared past his dark lenses. "Right. And what's your take on modern aerial anomalies?" <!-- L0823:eusacles -->

"Fascinating thesis," Dravin murmured, steering the party through the bronze doors. "I only wish I possessed a spell to heat that foil." <!-- L0827-L0830:dravin -->

Inside, the amphitheater rose in steep tiers of curved mahogany beneath a vaulted plaster ceiling, packed with hundreds of scholars. <!-- L0854 -->

Eusacles cast a critical eye across the hall. "Lot of concrete for an Ivy League pretender. This was definitely a government job." <!-- L0855-L0857:eusacles -->

Near the front stage riser, an earnest faculty host was about to reach for the podium to clear his throat and open the assembly. Brushing past the startled host, Dravin stepped decisively up to the mahogany lectern, tapping the brass receiver to silence the room. <!-- L0884-L0885 -->

"Attention, colleagues and students," Dravin announced with the effortless resonance of a tenured chair. "This afternoon we have the privilege of hearing from Dr. Aris Thorne on the 1948 clinical trials breakthrough. Please grant her your undivided scholarly focus. Doctor, the lectern is yours." <!-- L0887-L0889:dravin -->

Alfie pumped her miniature wooden fist from his shoulder, needle rapier tapping against her driftwood leg. "Give him a shout, mates! He knows his onions, this one!" <!-- L0894-L0898:alfie -->

Dr. Thorne stepped forward in her tailored olive coat, offering a grateful smile. "Thank you, Professor. I was beginning to dread the introduction." <!-- L0903:thorne -->

"A small courtesy, Doctor," Dravin bowed patricianly. "What room have the organizers assigned you for debriefing? I shall ensure hot tea is prepared." <!-- L0904:dravin -->

"The private green room behind the organ loft," she replied gratefully, gesturing backstage. <!-- L0905:thorne -->

As the house lights dimmed, Dravin drifted off the side stairs toward the backstage lock with the stolen keys. <!-- L0907-L0913 -->

Pierre squeezed into the front row beside the displaced faculty host, tapping the man's corduroy shoulder with his javelin ferrule. "Pardon, monsieur," Pierre hissed. "Where is the sculpting laboratory?" <!-- L0924-L0934:pierre -->

Dravin slipped the stolen brass master key into the backstage lock, turning it with a silent click. <!-- L0935 -->
