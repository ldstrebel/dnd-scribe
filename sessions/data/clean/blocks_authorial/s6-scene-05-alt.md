## CHAPTER 36: ELECTRIFIED SHALLOWS & THE SHATTERED JAVELIN

Professor Edward Dravin stood braced beside the podium, taking deliberate care not to meet the adversary's gaze directly. With the transformed woman held motionless in Pierre's unseen mental grip, her writhing coils bore an ominous resemblance to classical depictions of the Gorgon. Dravin turned his back to her, slipping a small silver compact mirror from his tweed waistcoat pocket and raising it beside his cheek. He angled the glass over his shoulder and aligned the iron sights of the blued-steel pneumatic pistol in the mirror's reflection, centering the front post on her shoulder. <!-- L0766-L0773 -->

"I have no interest in dealing mortal harm," Dravin announced evenly above the hiss of cascading water. "The objective is empirical restraint." <!-- L0774-L0797:dravin -->

He squeezed the pneumatic trigger. A sharp hiss echoed through the lecture hall as the blued dart flew across the space and struck the woman's shoulder. Yet instead of petrifying into inert stone, the dark solution melted directly into her flesh. The neural tendrils around her temples pulsed with sudden, blinding brilliance, absorbing the chemical charge. Dravin let out a low groan of frustration. As his concentration sharpened, the column of pale moonlight overhead shifted, sliding across the orchestra pit and locking back onto the woman. The searing lunar brilliance burned her thrashing coils with cold light, while leaving Pierre safely beyond its perimeter.

Across the platform, the horned satyr recovered from its stumble, wheeling on cloven hooves to charge Eusacles. The beast lowered its curled horns in feral fury, slamming hard into the demigod's chest with concussive force that drove the breath from his lungs. <!-- L0798-L0804 -->

Eusacles grunted beneath his dark sunglasses, absorbing the blow and setting his heavy boots into the wet floorboards. "Well, if it ain't broke," Eusacles muttered, gripping his flanged morningstar with both hands. "Let's see how you like a second round." He whipped the spiked head in a vicious counter-arc, but the creature ducked beneath the whistling steel, hissing with bared fangs. <!-- L0805-L0812:eusacles -->

With a violent snap of strained tendons, the paralyzed woman tore free of Pierre's mental hold. She sprang forward onto the stage apron, her aura flaring with crackling blue current as an oppressive, suffocating pressure radiated across the platform. The persistent glare of the lunar beam blinded her, and she lashed her whip-like hair coils blindly toward Dravin. The electrified tendrils whistled through empty air, snapping harmlessly against the wet mahogany rostrum. All across the auditorium, the deluge from the emergency sprinklers pooled inches deep over the floorboards, carrying blue arcs of static through the shallow water while two fresh blobs of ink dropped from the shuddering planar tear. <!-- L0827-L0836 -->

Pierre tightened his grip on his bronze museum javelin, spotting a second horned assailant circling the stage perimeter toward Alfie's position beneath the desk. He splashed through the shallow puddles, brandishing the spear with aristocratic indignation.

"Excuse me! I was not finished speaking with you!" Pierre called out, stepping into the creature's path. "Why are you walking away from me? Listen, this is very rude. You have left me no choice now but to pierce you! Taste the wrath of an authentic antiquities piece!" <!-- L0863-L0878:pierre -->

Pierre drove the bronze spearhead forward with theatrical fencing leverage, striking true into the beast's flank.

A brittle snap echoed above the roar of the sprinklers. The ancient bronze head sheared clean off beneath the ferrule, shattering against the creature's hide and leaving Pierre clutching a splintered wooden rod. The severed hair tendrils of the nearby woman flailed in chaotic motion, spitting arcs of current that set the puddle foam sizzling. <!-- L0884-L0894 -->

Pierre blinked down at the broken haft in stunned disbelief, then thrust the jagged stump toward the hissing woman.

"You know, this is precisely what occurs when one fails to apply conditioner every evening!" Pierre declared with lofty disdain. "They become entirely unruly! You should seriously consider adding some protein to your grooming regimen!" <!-- L0895-L0900:pierre -->

The woman shrieked in mingled pain and frustration as the lunar radiance blistered her skin and the spear strike broke her momentum. Beside the podium, Dr. Aris Thorne pressed both hands to her face, trembling in utter shock.
