"University," Alfie repeated from Dravin's shoulder, reading the second half of the wrought-iron archway. "University." <!-- L0641-L0643:alfie -->

The collegiate quadrangle unfolded in grand limestone splendor, Gothic spires piercing the overcast autumn sky. Beneath a granite stair, demonstrators waved pamphlets, their crisp fedoras folded entirely out of heavy-gauge tin foil. "If the compound was chemically viable in 1948, why did the archives seal the formula?" a demonstrator argued loudly. "Big Pox was eradicated, but history records the medicine turned to sludge and the doctor vanished into infamy!" <!-- L0657-L0703:protester -->

"Her lecture starts in ten minutes at the main auditorium!" another student warned, pointing toward heavy oak portals. "Standing room only!" <!-- L0732-L0734 -->

Dravin glanced around the bustling courtyard. "If the hall is packed, we will have no access to her primary documents. We require entry backstage." <!-- L0735-L0739:dravin -->

Pierre was not listening. His eyes had locked onto a pristine neoclassical marble bust shoved ignominiously against an olive-drab municipal trash bin. Shock and disgust contorted his Parisian features. 

"Sacré bleu!" Pierre gasped, marching straight toward a custodian pushing a brass cleaning trolley. "A sculpted masterpiece of Hellenic beauty shoved behind municipal garbage! Where is the barbarian responsible for this desecration?" <!-- L0753-L0761:pierre -->

The custodian halted his cart, snapping his cleaning rag in defensive outrage. "Excuse me, pal? I’m Rick Ready! I’m always ready! And custodial doesn't dictate aesthetics, buddy!" <!-- L0762:attendant -->

"You polished it with sandpaper, Monsieur Ready!" Pierre yelled, brandishing his javelin tip at the statue's nose. "You have ruined the patrician bridge! In France, sculptors would weep blood at such domestic vandalism!" <!-- L0768:pierre -->

"Look, pal, I don't run the art department!" Rick Ready snapped back, waving his arms.

Recognizing that Pierre had launched into an unscripted, authentic Parisian fit, Dravin did not hesitate. Gliding quietly behind the sputtering custodian's back, Dravin unclipped the brass ring of university master keys from Rick Ready's belt loop and vanished into the colonnade shadows. <!-- L0771-L0778 -->

"Prove to me you have an art department, Monsieur Ready!" Pierre challenged, waving grandly toward the quad. "Show me this so-called sculpture hall! Lead the way!" <!-- L0786-L0790:pierre -->
