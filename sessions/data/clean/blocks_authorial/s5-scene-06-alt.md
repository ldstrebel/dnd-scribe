"University," Alfie repeated from Dravin's shoulder, reading the wrought-iron archway. "University." <!-- L0641-L0643:alfie -->

The collegiate quadrangle unfolded in limestone splendor, Gothic spires piercing the overcast autumn sky. Pierre adjusted his woolen beret with critical European appraisal. "I must concede, this is the finest stonework I have encountered in America. Look at the balance of those arches! Quite magnificent. Almost sinister." <!-- L0644-L0648 -->

Beneath a granite stairway, two undergraduate demonstrators waved pamphlets, each wearing a pointed tin-foil fedora folded entirely out of heavy-gauge tin foil. "If the compound was chemically viable in 1948, why did the archives seal the formula?" a demonstrator argued loudly. Beside him, an earnest student pushed up his horn-rimmed glasses, insisting the terminal subjects never consented to the agony. <!-- L0657-L0686 -->

Eusacles tilted his dark aviator glasses down his nose. "Big Pox? What fatal disease are you nerds shouting about?" <!-- L0687-L0694:eusacles -->

"Big Pox was a devastating epidemic!" the fedora demonstrator snapped back. "The mad doctor’s clinical trial in 1948 promised an absolute cure, but history records the medicine turned to sludge and the physician vanished into infamy!" <!-- L0698-L0711:protester -->

"It was eradicated before our era, Eusacles," Dravin intervened smoothly, stepping forward in his rumpled tweed coat. "My colleagues and I are visiting researchers. We understood Dr. Aris Thorne was delivering a retrospective on these very trials?" <!-- L0726-L0730:dravin -->

"Her lecture starts in ten minutes at the main auditorium!" the second student warned, pointing toward the heavy oak double doors. "Standing room only!" <!-- L0732-L0734 -->

Dravin stroked his chin, calculating their entry into the hall. If the auditorium was packed, reaching Dr. Thorne's primary research binder would be impossible from the audience. They needed master keys to the backstage hospitality wing. <!-- L0735-L0739:dravin -->

"I'm helping! Hold on, I want to help," Pierre announced abruptly. <!-- L0753:pierre -->

Pierre, however, had not listened to a single word about Dr. Thorne or Big Pox. His eyes had locked onto an exquisite classical marble bust shoved into an alcove directly behind a greasy, bulging olive-drab plastic trash bin. Fresh bird droppings coated the sculpted curls of the marble brow. <!-- L0757-L0759 -->

"Sacré bleu! What ungodly barbarism is this?" Pierre shrieked, storming toward the alcove. "A sculpted masterpiece of Hellenic beauty, hidden behind a municipal garbage receptacle! I bet there is bird poop all over the shoulders of the statue! Where is the barbarian responsible for this desecration?" <!-- L0761:pierre -->

A burly university custodian pushing a brass cleaning trolley froze in his tracks. A red rag attached to a spring-loaded retractor dangled from his belt beside a massive ring of brass keys. The custodian unclipped his cloth with a sharp snap. "Excuse me, pal? I’m Rick Ready! I’m always ready! I wipe this down regularly! There’s no way there’s bird droppings on that marble!" <!-- L0762:attendant -->

Pierre stepped in close, squinting at the statue. To his momentary surprise, the marble was indeed spotless—yet as his gaze traced the softened contours of the cheek and nose, his eyes narrowed with renewed outrage.

"You are rubbing too vigorously, Monsieur Ready!" Pierre shouted, waving his bronze javelin tip dramatically toward the softened features. "That is why the fine detail is lost here and here! You have scrubbed away the classical chisel marks! In France, sculptors would weep blood to witness such domestic vandalism!" <!-- L0768:pierre -->

"What are you, some sculpture student?" Rick Ready sputtered, reddening with defensive rage. <!-- L0771:attendant -->

While Rick Ready argued red-faced at Pierre, Professor Dravin calmly stepped behind the custodian's trolley. With the deft precision of a tenured historian borrowing restricted manuscripts, Dravin slipped beneath Rick's belt flap, unclipped the heavy brass ring of master keys, and stepped back into the shadows of the archway. Dravin held up the jangling keys and offered a discreet wink. Pierre, completely consumed by his own aesthetic vendetta, didn't even notice. <!-- L0776-L0778 -->

"No, I make my own sculptures, thank you very much!" Pierre scoffed. "Why would I come to America to learn art? France has the finest sculptors in the world! You imported this piece because your program makes nothing of note!" <!-- L0786:pierre -->

"We got a top-tier fine arts department here at the university!" Rick Ready roared, puffing out his chest. <!-- L0787:attendant -->

"Prove it to me, Monsieur Ready!" Pierre challenged, pointing imperiously toward the campus. "Where is your sculpture hall? Lead the way!" <!-- L0788-L0790:pierre -->
