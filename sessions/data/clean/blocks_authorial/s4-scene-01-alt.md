## CHAPTER 26: THE MEDUSA PROTOCOL

In the high-ceilinged atrium of the North Carolina Museum of History, the climate-controlled stillness shattered the instant the display vitrine began its hydraulic rise. Pierre and Alfie wheeled out the metal archaeology cart bearing their artificial limestone fragment—crafted in the back rooms with Alfie's razor-sharp wordcraft to mimic the missing ancient script. But before the overhead crane could clear two feet, the perimeter security detail struck. A guard in a sharp dark blazer hurled a heavy metallic cylinder beneath the glass, its digital display flashing an eighteen-second countdown as black calcifying sludge hissed across the ancient limestone tablet. <!-- L0105-L0118 -->

As defensive wards flared, the mundane illusion concealing the guards melted like hot wax. Beneath the fluorescent track lights, neat crew-cuts erupted into nests of coiling, hissing vipers. On their lapels, four identical silver nameplates glinted in the strobe: *Gordon. Gordon. Gordon. Gordon.* Pierre adjusted his round spectacles, leaning over the cart in polite Parisian bemusement. "Hello. You all have the exact same name. Is this your actual name?" <!-- L0142-L0158 -->

"This is our actual name, Gordon," the lead operative hissed. Drawing a squat pneumatic pistol, Gordon fired a black sludge pellet that struck a museum attendant, turning his arm into coarse gray stone. <!-- L0159-L0164 -->

"Could I perhaps borrow your pistol, Mr. Gordon?" Pierre asked with genuine scientific eagerness. "What do I have to do to become a Redactor?" <!-- L0165-L0169 -->

"You are the subject," Gordon hissed coldly. "You will never be a Redactor." <!-- L0170 -->

From behind the metal column of the lifting rig, Professor Edward Dravin shouted a frantic warning as the klaxons began to wail. <!-- L0172 -->

Ducking low behind the display housing, Alfie gripped the hilt of his needle rapier. "He just pulled a gun out, mate! We better do something before we're both carved into marble statues!" <!-- L0175-L0181 -->

Pierre grinned recklessly, rummaging through the cart's lower tray until his hands seized a heavy-duty orange extension cord. In swift loops, Pierre knotted the thick rubber cable around the driftwood doll's waist. "I am not very athletic, Alfie, so plus or minus two feet is all I can guarantee. You must adjust in the air!" <!-- L0188-L0206 -->

"Right," Alfie gave a resolute nod of his carved wooden chin. "Let's do it!" <!-- L0208 -->

Across the polished marble floor, the third Gordon leveled his pneumatic sidearm directly at the reception desk, taking aim at Nincy as she recorded the entire breach on her camera ring light. <!-- L0210-L0215 -->
