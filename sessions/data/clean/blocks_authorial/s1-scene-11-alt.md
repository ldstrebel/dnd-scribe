Down near Eusacles's boots, a tiny wooden hand brandished a needle rapier with an impatient swish. <!-- L1957 -->

"I'm four feet below you, mate!" a sharp, raspy Cockney voice piped up from the wet grass. "Somebody's got to pick me up if you're trying to talk to me!" <!-- L1992 -->

Eusacles frowned, crouching down to inspect the tiny speaker. The moment his face drew near, the cold point of the sewing needle pressed against the stubble of his cheek. <!-- L1998 -->

"Yeah, me," the doll muttered, its shell buttons clicking as it stood tall. "I know things. I do. I know the dark." <!-- L2007 -->

Before Eusacles could recoil, the doll suddenly went limp, toppling sideways into the clover. <!-- L2015 -->

Dravin leaned down, tapping the carved chest with gentle sternum rubs. "Come on back now, buddy..." <!-- L2030 -->

With a sharp click, the doll sprang back to life, immediately swinging its tiny needle wildly at tall crabgrass stalks like a miniature scythe. <!-- L2040 -->

"You're half-bloods, right?" Eusacles asked, staring between Dravin and Pierre in the moonlight. "Like, you're half-bloods too?" <!-- L2053 -->

"On my mother's side," Dravin replied thoughtfully, pushing his glasses up his nose. "Her great-grandmother was Greek. That is why I study Greek mythology." <!-- L2069 -->

"Have you ever had your class come to life before?" Pierre asked curiously. <!-- L2071 -->

"I like to think every time I teach, the class comes to life," Dravin smiled warmly. <!-- L2072 -->

A distant sound cut through the cicadas—a faint chuckle carried on the night breeze down the fence line. Pierre and Eusacles both turned toward the treeline. <!-- L2100 -->

"Did you hear that?" Eusacles whispered, narrowing his eyes into the shadows. <!-- L2102 -->

Pierre knelt down on the shoulder of the road and gently offered his open palm. "Would you like a ride?" <!-- L2110 -->

The doll stopped swiping at the grass and looked up with its polished coral eyes. "A ride would be grand. Thanks, mate." <!-- L2118 -->
