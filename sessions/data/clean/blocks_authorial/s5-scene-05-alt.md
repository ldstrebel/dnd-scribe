The trail plunged into an ancient corridor where towering hemlocks blotted out the sky, turning daylight to a perpetual twilight smelling of wet slate and displaced temporal ozone. Dravin led the file, bronze shield strapped to his forearm. <!-- L0539-L0548 -->

"All in favor of the Lost Roads," Dravin murmured as he checked their heading. "I will do my best to navigate us through." <!-- L0550-L0558:dravin -->

"I'm telling you, these roads are a disaster," Eusacles muttered from behind his dark aviators. "We're going to wander in circles until next Tuesday." <!-- L0566-L0567:eusacles -->

The brush parted suddenly into a silent clearing. Kneeling on a mossy boulder was a small translucent boy in patched dungarees, shimmering like heat off asphalt. He held out two empty, cupped palms. "I made this," the boy insisted earnestly. "It's a fun little toy, isn't it?" <!-- L0580-L0583:child -->

"You didn't make a thing, mate," Alfie snorted from Dravin's shoulder. "There's literally nothing in your hands." <!-- L0581-L0585:alfie -->

"Some would say imagination is your greatest tool, kid," Eusacles countered dryly. "You're not wrong." <!-- L0584-L0586:eusacles -->

Pierre backpedaled, leveling his bronze javelin two-handed, spectacles reflecting the child's pale glow. "Alfie, one does not converse with spectral woodland children! It is an obvious curse! It will transform into an unspeakable gremlin and haunt our boots forever!" <!-- L0600:pierre -->

The boy’s smile faded. "Oh. Okay," he whispered, dissolving backward into the ferns like woodsmoke. <!-- L0603-L0604:child -->

"You dropped your nothing!" Alfie shouted after him, brandishing her needle rapier. <!-- L0613:alfie -->

Before they took ten paces, the canopy groaned. *CRACK.* A splintering boom shook the earth beneath their soles, followed by the thunderous, invisible footsteps of a colossal phantom charging through the timber. Dravin drew his tarnished bell, forming a four-point defensive ward with Pierre and Eusacles until the phantom shockwaves abruptly died into the moss. Stepping through a rusted maintenance gate, the hemlocks fell away, revealing the sprawling limestone quadrangle of University University. <!-- L0614-L0638 -->
