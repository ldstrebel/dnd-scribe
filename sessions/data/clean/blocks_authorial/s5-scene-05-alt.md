With Pierre’s vehement refusal of divine postal services settling their route, the companions shouldered their gear and departed the Margin. Stepping past the outer ward-markers, the narrow path plunged into an ancient corridor where towering hemlocks blotted out the sky, turning daylight to a perpetual twilight smelling of wet slate and displaced temporal ozone. <!-- L0539-L0541 -->

They had weighed the alternative back at camp. "How much does the Hermes Express cost?" Dravin had asked Teddy, who only shrugged. There was no set fare—items, a story, a favor. <!-- L0543-L0544:dravin -->

"I have no money. I have no stories," Dravin had admitted dryly. "I feel I have nothing to lose." <!-- L0545:dravin -->

"I am not interested in paying his odds. What a waste," Pierre had declared, tossing his beret back. "And besides—consider our experience so far upon the Lost Roads. We met a beautiful woman, and we abandoned you, Eusacles. Both of these seem very good to me." <!-- L0547:pierre -->

Professor Dravin led the file, bronze hoplite shield strapped across his left forearm. "All in favor of the Lost Roads," Dravin murmured as he checked their heading. "I will do my best to navigate us through." <!-- L0548-L0558:dravin -->

"I'm telling you, these roads are a disaster," Eusacles muttered from behind his dark aviators, ducking beneath a mossy bough. "We're going to wander in circles until next Tuesday." <!-- L0566-L0567:eusacles -->

Dravin urged patience, using the rim of his bronze shield to part the tangled briars as the path climbed deeper into the switchbacks. <!-- L0574 -->

The brush parted suddenly into a silent clearing. Kneeling on a mossy boulder was a small translucent boy in patched dungarees, shimmering like heat off asphalt. He held out two empty, cupped palms, looking up with wide, hopeful eyes. "I made this," the boy insisted earnestly. "It's a fun little toy, isn't it?" <!-- L0580-L0583:child -->

"You didn't make a thing, mate," Alfie snorted from Dravin's shoulder. "There is literally nothing in your hands." <!-- L0581-L0585:alfie -->

"Some would say imagination is your greatest tool, kid," Eusacles countered dryly. "You're not wrong." <!-- L0584-L0586:eusacles -->

Pierre backpedaled, clutching his beret and leveling his bronze javelin two-handed, spectacles reflecting the child's pale glow. "Alfie, I do not know if you have watched American horror films, but one does not converse with spectral woodland children! It is an obvious curse! It will transform into an unspeakable gremlin and haunt our boots forever!" <!-- L0600:pierre -->

The boy’s smile faded. "Oh. Okay," he whispered, dissolving backward into the dense ferns like woodsmoke. <!-- L0603-L0604:child -->

"Hurry! Now is our moment to sprint while he's off the path!" Pierre urged, waving his javelin toward the ascending trail. <!-- L0610-L0612:pierre -->

"Hey!" Alfie shouted after the trees, brandishing her needle rapier. "You dropped your nothing!" <!-- L0613:alfie -->

Before they took ten paces, the canopy groaned. *CRACK.* A splintering boom shook the earth beneath their soles, followed by the thunderous, invisible footsteps of a colossal phantom charging through the timber. *THUD.* Another deafening shockwave slammed into the earth twenty yards to their flank. <!-- L0614-L0615 -->

Dravin drew his tarnished silver bell, planting his boots firmly on the shaking moss. "Right, let's all stand in a circle so that we can all see three-hundred-and-sixty degrees around us!" <!-- L0621:dravin -->

Alfie scrambled to her feet on Dravin’s shoulder, leveling her needle rapier into the gloom. "Back to back to back to back!" <!-- L0622:alfie -->

"Well, you're backing like to shin, but yeah," Eusacles muttered, planting his work boots and readying his watch-chain. <!-- L0623:eusacles -->

"Back to back to angled to back!" Alfie corrected, planting her tiny feet against Dravin's tweed collar. <!-- L0626:alfie -->

The phantom footsteps shook the soil one final time—and abruptly ceased. The wind died, and complete, dead silence fell over the ridge. <!-- L0627 -->

Dravin lowered his bell, glancing dryly around the empty clearing. "Well, that was easy, guys. Let's go." <!-- L0628:dravin -->

With the terrifying shockwaves vanished into the quiet woods as quickly as they had arrived, the party resumed single file. They hiked along the winding switchbacks for another quarter-mile until the hemlocks gave way to an unkempt laurel hedge and crunchy gravel. Ahead stood a rusted iron chain-link gate flanked by a weathered wooden signboard: `TRAIL CLOSED — UNIVERSITY OF UNIVERSITY POLICE & MAINTENANCE DEPT.` <!-- L0629-L0634 -->

Beyond the perimeter fence rose the soaring limestone towers, slate roofs, and sprawling green lawn of the collegiate quadrangle. <!-- L0638 -->
