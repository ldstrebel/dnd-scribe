Cool, damp night air rushed into their lungs. <!-- L1794 -->

The dazzling light collapsed into velvety darkness. Pierre tumbled softly into a grassy ditch, his canvas rucksack clutched in his arms. Overhead, thousands of stars glittered through dense pine branches while cicadas sang in the humid mountain evening. Beside him lay Professor Edward Dravin, the unconscious form of Eusacles, and the limp driftwood doll. <!-- L1800-L1810 -->

Dravin blinked against the gloom, pushing his crooked spectacles up his nose. "I can't see a single thing," he muttered. As his knuckles brushed the glass frames, a sudden amber luminescence flared behind the lenses, piercing the pitch-black night to illuminate the grassy shoulder. Beside them, an old two-lane asphalt highway stretched into mountain mist, bordered by a sagging wooden fence. <!-- L1820-L1830 -->

Dravin scrambled over to Eusacles, pressing two fingers against the gambler's cold neck. "Breathe, boy, just breathe," Dravin urged frantically, applying desperate first-aid compressions to his collar. A faint, involuntary silver glow pulsed from Dravin's fingertips, seeping into the bruised tissue. The soothing warmth settled over Eusacles's chest, steadying his ragged pulse until his eyelids fluttered open. <!-- L1850-L1860 -->

"Oh, god damn it..." Eusacles groaned, rubbing his bruised jaw. "I am so sick of the teleportation. Why do I feel hungover?" He blinked up at the professor and the French boy. "Wait... how do you all know my name?" <!-- L1870-L1877 -->

"You were muttering it in your sleep," Pierre answered earnestly. <!-- L1880 -->

Eusacles struggled upright, dusting clover from his tank top. A few yards away along the ditch, Dravin examined a rusty green road sign half-buried in the weeds. <!-- L1900 -->

"The sign says Charleston, West Virginia — 60 Miles," Dravin announced, pushing his glasses up his nose. <!-- L1919 -->

Pierre tilted his head with an anxious squint. "Can you remind me... is a mile one kilometer or two? Because that sounds very far." <!-- L1925 -->

"About two hundred kilometers," Dravin answered with academic precision. <!-- L1930 -->

"Oh," Pierre muttered, blinking. "Like Versailles to Brittany." <!-- L1940 -->

"Two hundred kilometers..." Eusacles repeated, his voice climbing in disbelief. "That is so far from Vegas! Not even a little bit close! All I wanted to do was play roulette!" <!-- L1956 -->
