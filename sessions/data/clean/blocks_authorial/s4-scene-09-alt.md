## CHAPTER 28: ECHOES ACROSS THE LOST ROADS

"I believe that is a North Carolina Velociraptor," Professor Dravin replied solemnly, eyeing the peculiar exhibit. Seizing the quiet lull beneath the wailing alarms, Pierre operated the auxiliary claw controls to hoist an ancient glass vitrine, salvaging an authentic Spartan hoplite bronze cuirass and war javelin, while Dravin strapped on the heavy bronze hoplite shield. <!-- L0991-L1016 -->

"Went to the North Carolina World History Museum, and all I got was this hat made for a stuffed animal," Alfie grumbled, adjusting his green-and-gold souvenir brim. <!-- L1018-L1023 -->

Sirens began to wail across downtown Raleigh as police cruisers responded to the museum alarm. Ducking through the rear service portal into the storage room, Dravin pulled open the rusted door leading into the swirling gray ether of the Lost Roads. Navigating the non-Euclidean fog with steady instincts, the company traced their returning steps through the mist until the cold ether dissolved into the familiar scent of pine and the warm lantern-light of the Marigold Inn. <!-- L1028-L1086 -->

By the crackling common room hearth, Mike looked up from his steaming mug with a welcoming grin. "Well, what's up? Looks like you guys had a successful run." <!-- L1088-L1090 -->

"We found a particle of ancient stone," Pierre reported proudly, showing off the fragment alongside their salvaged Spartan armor. "And we gave them fresh bread in exchange, so I think we deserved credit!" <!-- L1091-L1093 -->

Mike's brow furrowed in grave concern upon learning of the encounter: "I would not have thought Redactors would be on the scene that fast." <!-- L1098-L1100 -->
