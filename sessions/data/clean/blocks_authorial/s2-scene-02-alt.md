Mike caught the trio's baffled looks and nodded in easy sympathy. "Oh, I see the misunderstanding here. It's called The Margin, and we kind of love that idea. We build our language around it—words like *edited*, *corrected*, or *redacted*. There's definitely space for you guys here, though you'll have to share a guest cabin. A real wood log cabin out in the boonies." <!-- L0515-L0517 -->

Dravin tilted his chin with an academic smirk, pushing his glasses up his nose. "Now when you say that, is that c-a-b space i-n? The cab in?" <!-- L0518 -->

Mike barked a laugh, pointing an appreciative finger at the professor. "I like this one! He's a good dude right here. We could definitely use some humor around here. But seriously, we'll introduce you guys around. There's Theodore—he prefers Theodore from new people, but old folks call him Teddy. He's going to take down your information. We all have different theories about why we're even here, seeing as we've all been through a very traumatic experience." <!-- L0521-L0523 -->

Eusacles stepped closer to the fire, the timber railing creaking beneath his massive palms. "Mike, what was your traumatic experience that brought you here?" <!-- L0524 -->

"Last I saw, I was on a cruise ship with my friends, riding through the seven seas," Mike murmured, his easy demeanor fading as he stared into the orange embers. "All of a sudden, the ship started going down. Everything flashed blinding white. Next thing I know, I woke up standing alone in front of an ATM on a dark street in West Virginia. Nobody else was there. Just me. Teddy was still leaving the Margin back then, hunting for displaced folks. I'm from California, so West Virginia felt totally alien to me. Teddy sensed something off me—or maybe I just looked completely confused—and brought me in. He's been here by far the longest of anyone." <!-- L0528-L0540 -->

Pierre stepped reluctantly from the briars, adjusting his dark woolen beret with prim elegance. "I think this is not the right place. I am supposed to be on a pilgrimage. Maybe a random question, but... does your hair have snakes?" <!-- L0549-L0550 -->

Mike touched his wavy brown hair, bewildered. "My hair is completely natural." <!-- L0551 -->

"Yes, so too is mine," Pierre asserted defensively, straightening his cuffs. "But does it wiggle and sometimes bite perhaps, or no?" <!-- L0552 -->

"I think we might be a little bit lost in translation here," Mike chuckled nervously. "It sounds like your hair is snakes, and that's a bit wild." <!-- L0558-L0560 -->

"No, it is very well tamed," Pierre insisted, adjusting his spectacles. "They are not wild anymore. My mother told me to come to America to find a place to learn about my heritage. Going into a log cabin in the middle of America was not what she had in mind." Glancing sideways at Mike, Pierre added under his breath, "I have learned Mike's name, but I am not going to get too emotionally attached to Mike yet. He has a very strange California accent." <!-- L0563-L0572 -->

Pierre cradled the miniature driftwood figure in his palms, his voice softening with sincere warmth. "I feel bad for calling you a doll. What should we call you? Maybe Alonso... or Alfie? A little Alfie?" <!-- L0577-L0604 -->

The little sailor's coral eyes gleamed in the firelight. "Yeah, Alfie. I think that's a pretty nice name." Turning toward the hearth, Alfie's carved mouth twitched. "Mike, you were talking about a ship... a ship that crashed in the water." Suddenly, the little wooden limbs locked rigid, trembling violently against Pierre's wrist. "I remember... the dark, and the quiet..." <!-- L0605-L0628 -->

Pierre cupped his hands gently around the shivering carved wood, anchoring the doll with steady warmth. "Alfie. Alfie, it is okay. Bring it back." <!-- L0629 -->

Alfie stared into the dark, his voice a frail rasp. "...water." <!-- L0630 -->
