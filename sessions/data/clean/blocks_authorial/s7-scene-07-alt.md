## CHAPTER 48: THE DIVINE FLAVOR DUEL & THE VISITOR IN THE RAIN

The morning sun broke through the tall pine boughs, casting long golden rectangles across the tables of the Margin's mess hall. Eusacles shuffled in, dark circles under his aviators, muttering to himself as he collapsed onto a bench beside Pierre and Alfie.

Pierre set down a tin cup, leaning over with an eager grin. "Alfie, did you enjoy the cinema last night? It was the only disc we could find in the supply cabin." <!-- L0994:pierre -->

"Incredible," Alfie declared, perched on the tabletop with his arms crossed over his soccer scarf. "Alfie loved it." <!-- L0998:alfie -->

"Honestly, Alfie, I don't really know what happens in it either," Pierre admitted with a light shrug. "They never made much sense to me. But I think this one, he turns into a spider, or maybe a spider turned into him. I'm not entirely sure." <!-- L1002:pierre -->

The heavy oak door banged open against the wall, rattling the tin mugs on the sideboard. Naomi marched into the hall, hair pulled into a severe ponytail, brandishing an accordion folder thick with manila case files. "All right! We watched a movie, we relaxed, everybody is fine. Rosa told me to give you all the night to recover, and it's been the night. So now it's time. There is still one more fragment out there." <!-- L1004:naomi -->

"Naomi, you know the deal," Pierre chided, wagging a slender finger. "You show us the bootleg copies of Harry Potter and Marvel, and then we will go to work." <!-- L1008:pierre -->

Mike emerged from the kitchen carrying a smoking iron skillet, setting down three massive plates of golden pancakes topped with dark berry sauce.

Eusacles picked up his fork, poked at the steaming stack, and sighed deeply. "I am getting real tired of these pancakes. They're really good, but they need something else." He raised a calloused hand over the plate, muttering a Vegas tavern cantrip. "I cast Divine Flavor on these pancakes." <!-- L1015:eusacles -->

Pierre's eyes widened in profound culinary horror, his fork clattering against the tin plate. "Do not touch my pancakes!" <!-- L1023:pierre -->

Before Eusacles could bring the fork to his lips, Pierre snatched his newly mended bronze javelin from beneath the bench. Overcome with theatrical outrage, he lunged across the corner of the table, driving the bronze point directly into Eusacles' left flank. The freshly forged metal punched clean through denim and flannel, biting into the flesh beneath the ribs. "How does that taste to you, huh? I can direct-deliver it to your intestines!" <!-- L1025:pierre -->

Eusacles gasped, clutching his side as crimson seeped around the spearhead. "What the fuck! I didn't think you'd take it that seriously!" <!-- L1028:eusacles -->

From behind the counter, Mike waved his wooden spatula in frantic circles. "Whoa, whoa, whoa! What are you guys doing? Take it outside!" <!-- L1029:mike -->

Pierre released the haft, pacing the floorboards with flailing arms. "I have no ingredients to work with!" Pierre cried, gesturing wildly toward the barren pantry. "We have flour, water, and rum! That is all! Where are the eggs? Where is the unsalted butter? If I am to bake proper croissants for Alfie, who has never tasted one in his entire life, we require proper provisions!" <!-- L1037:pierre -->

Mike grabbed a grease pencil, hastily scribbling across a brown paper flour sack. "All right, all right, I got it, bro! Scrambled eggs, butter, yeast—we can do this!" <!-- L1045:mike -->

Pierre stopped pacing, eyeing the crimson stain spreading across Eusacles' shirt with a faint twinge of awkward guilt. "Perhaps... perhaps it was a slight overreaction. Yesterday the weapon was completely useless, and today it strikes with the force of an Olympian titan. That is hardly my fault." <!-- L1047:pierre -->

"I didn't even touch your plate!" Eusacles barked, wrenching the bronze point free and pressing both calloused palms over the puncture. "Bro, I am not trying to see my dad again already! Holy shit!" <!-- L1053:eusacles -->

A pale golden warmth flared beneath Eusacles' fingers as he channeled *Cure Wounds* to knit the torn muscle back together. <!-- L1059 -->

Pierre immediately knelt beside him on the bench, hauling a curved suture needle and silk thread from his rucksack. "You cannot simply use magic for everything! What are you doing? Hold still!" Pierre snapped, aggressively driving the needle through the closing skin to cinch it shut with tight medical knots. <!-- L1069:pierre -->

Eusacles winced, gritting his teeth behind his sunglasses. "Are you digging deep on purpose?!" <!-- L1071:eusacles -->

"Are you guys done?" Naomi cut in, stepping up to the end of the table with folded arms, tapping her boot against the pine boards. "I have been up for four hours waiting to drop this on you. You already know part of it." <!-- L1074:naomi -->

Pierre tied off the final stitch with a decisive flick of his shears, rose smoothly, and wiped his hands on a clean napkin. He grabbed a jug of orange juice and a bottle of rum from the credenza, splashing a generous pour straight across Eusacles' freshly stitched flank as sterilization. "There. Now I have a clean glass." Pierre filled four tumblers to the brim, sliding one to Alfie and setting another before the wounded demigod. <!-- L1078 -->

"Pierre, make it a double for me," Eusacles groaned, leaning his head back against the timber wall. "I've had a hell of a night, and you did just stab me." <!-- L1079:eusacles -->
