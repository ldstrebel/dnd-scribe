## CHAPTER 42: THE UTOPIAN CART & THE PURPLE FLARE

The deafening howl of the campus fire klaxons had finally ceased, but amber strobe lights still carved sharp arcs across the wet lawns. Distant sirens wailed toward the auditorium entrance, pulling emergency crews away from the darkened perimeter. Half a quad away, beneath the shadow of a concrete arcade where the temporal mist of the Lost Roads met West Virginia brick, Pierre, Alfie, and Eusacles shoved the gray rubberized janitor cart against a pillar.

Inside the deep vinyl bin, wedged among discarded rags and architectural trash, bound by electrical cord and zip ties, the transformed captive stirred. The dark ink tendrils sprouting from her scalp twitched, their oily sheen catching the flashing yellow beacons. Her swollen eyelids fluttered open, locking onto the trio with cold venom.

"You have no idea what we're capable of," she hissed, her voice vibrating with an unsettling rasp. <!-- L0214:creature -->

Eusacles crossed his denim-clad arms, aviators reflecting the distant beacons. "Well, yeah. That's why we have you here. Obviously, we're going to ask you some questions because we have no idea what you're capable of. And that's where you come in, because you're gonna tell us." <!-- L0215:eusacles -->

"That's not going to happen," she snarled, straining against the heavy cord. "You think you're just going to boss me around and tell me what to do? You fell to the ground like glass." Her black-veined eyes bore directly into Eusacles. "I was able to rip that from your mind." <!-- L0216-L0218:creature -->

"I mean, that's a good point," Eusacles conceded with an indifferent shrug, tapping his heavy iron morningstar against his combat boot. "But now you're in a trash can. Anyway, all right, guys. She's being real resistant. Let's just kill her. That'll be way easier." <!-- L0219:eusacles -->

Pierre adjusted his wire-rimmed spectacles, lifting an elegant hand in protest. "Patience, Eusacles! We have not even begun to offer her choices. She has potential. We must discern what she was sent here to accomplish." <!-- L0220:pierre -->

Eusacles slid down against the damp brickwork to rest his battered shoulders. "I got to take a nap. I think I should rest." <!-- L0227:eusacles -->

"Take your rest, my friend," Pierre murmured. Leaning closer over the rim, black beret tilted forward, Pierre raised an eyebrow. "I am wondering... do you know who my mother is? It always sounds a little pedantic to ask, but every time I bring her up, people seem to clam up entirely." <!-- L0242:pierre -->

The captive let out a wet, harsh cackle. "I absolutely know who you are, and it's simply embarrassing that you have yet to find out what you truly are. You think you're simply a Gorgon? That's not even close. You're a joke. You're a prank." <!-- L0247-L0248:creature -->

Pierre blinked, a sudden crimson flush rising up his neck beneath his scarf. He turned toward the rim of the cart. "Alfie, would you mind slapping her for me?" <!-- L0249:pierre -->

The two-foot driftwood duelist did not hesitate. Alfie dropped straight down onto her chest with a hollow wooden thud, miniature boots planting firmly against her sternum. In a flash of polished steel, the curved hook at Alfie's wrist hovered barely an inch from the woman's left eyeball. "Now that's my friend you're talking to," Alfie growled, painted button eyes narrowing. "You watch what you say." <!-- L0264:alfie -->

The woman spat a fleck of dark blood against Alfie's sailcloth tunic. "And you expect me to listen to you? You're junk. You're a doll carved from driftwood junk." <!-- L0266-L0270:creature -->

Alfie's small wooden jaw set like iron. "We don't say the D word around here. We don't say it. And because you said it, I think you owe me." With a sudden, surgical hiss, Alfie's needle rapier lashed out. A three-inch severed tendril tumbled free, spurting thick black ichor across the plastic rim. "That one's free. Next time you insult me or one of my friends, it's going to be two." <!-- L0273:alfie -->

The captive shrieked in agony as the stump pulsed violently. Alfie scooped up the dying tendril and wrapped it around the collar of her tunic like a morbid fur stole atop her maroon university soccer scarf. <!-- L0280 -->

Pierre stepped forward, weaving his fingers through the cool air. Magic hummed at his fingertips, sinking into her panicked mind. "Okay, you think you know so much about me, my mother, and the powers of the universe. Prove it. *Brag.*" <!-- L0283:pierre -->

The woman strained against the subtle compulsion, her pupils dilating into dark pools. "I don't even need to prove myself to any of you. You are nothing. You are simply a mistake, an edit left in the book, a typo." <!-- L0286:creature -->

Alfie tilted her wooden head, rapier resting casually against her shoulder. "Yeah, right from my angle, mate. You don't look like much, tied up in a trash can." <!-- L0289:alfie -->

"Yeah, you're talking a lot of trash for someone who's literally in a trash can," Eusacles echoed, leaning forward from his perch. "You keep saying we're trash, but you're the one in the bin. Obviously there's a bigger picture going on and you're just some pawn or peon they use as fodder. But your life is literally in our hands right now, and we're not super motivated to keep you alive because you're not giving us anything useful. So we're gonna try this again: paint us a picture of what's going on, or we send you to Hades." <!-- L0294:eusacles -->

Pierre smoothed his lapels with exaggerated nonchalance. "I do believe our mutual friend knows the lord of the underworld's wife, so perhaps a personal introduction could be arranged." <!-- L0296:pierre -->

The woman's throat rattled as she sneered up at Pierre. "You think mama's boy over there is going to come and save you? Does he think his mommy comes and is going to save him? I don't think so. Well, I don't see anyone coming to save you." <!-- L0298-L0302:creature -->

"The real question right now is whether anyone is coming to save you," Eusacles countered smoothly. <!-- L0303:eusacles -->

"That's a great question, isn't it?" Her voice pitched higher, manic and feverish as the severed stumps on her crown flared with violet sparks. "You wanted me to talk a little bit about myself. Maybe I will! You think you're some heroes? Think about what has happened. Think about it! My master saved these innocent people who were being operated on with a simple change. A simple change! He saved people's lives! The means do not justify the ends. Remember that." <!-- L0304-L0309:creature -->

Pierre frowned, adjusting his spectacles. "Your master has done this before, or only with this experiment?" <!-- L0310:pierre -->

"He has grown in power," she declared, her eyes shining with fanatical devotion. "These changes across time—there have been more of them than you have even been able to touch. I will give you credit, you've experienced more of his work than anyone else has in the past. But I don't think he's going to be paying attention to the artifacts as much anymore, knowing how much of a nuisance you have become." <!-- L0311-L0313:creature -->

Pierre tilted his head. "And what are these artifacts to him? Are they not just a way to see his great creations? Why are they not being shared with everyone?" <!-- L0314:pierre -->

"The artifacts are the incorrect past trying to recreate itself!" she hissed, baring her teeth. "His perfect changes are manifesting in this world, and they need to be erased before they take over and replace what had happened! You don't understand. People's lives have been impacted by these changes. If they grew too strong and changed back, the mist would be broken. People would just disappear—people that were dead in one timeline, but alive in the correct one!" <!-- L0315-L0317:creature -->

Pierre gave a slow, theatrical sigh. "Have you seen the Marvel movies yet?" <!-- L0318:pierre -->

Alfie glanced down from the cart's rim. "Yeah, mate. I was born like four days ago." <!-- L0319:alfie -->

"If you have not listened to Smash Mouth, that is part two of American culture," Pierre lectured gently. "I thought perhaps you would learn that messing with the time continuity is a number-one bad idea." <!-- L0320:pierre -->

"I mean, it sounds like a bad idea, but I don't have a movie to back it up," Alfie replied dryly. <!-- L0322:alfie -->

"Maybe we can make some pancakes and have some movies when we get back," Pierre suggested with a faint hopeful smile. <!-- L0323:pierre -->

"That sounds like a great plan," Alfie agreed. <!-- L0325:alfie -->

Eusacles pushed himself up, resting his knuckles against the janitor cart's rubber handle. "That's a good point. Hey, one last question. This master of yours messing with time and all that—is he splintering the timelines into multiple infinite realities, or is he just messing with one timeline and having to watch out for paradoxes? How does this work?" <!-- L0326:eusacles -->

"He is saving people's lives, creating a more perfect world!" the woman insisted fiercely. "And you all, for whatever reason, are now representations of this imperfect past becoming reality, not going to where it belongs." <!-- L0327:creature -->

Pierre raised a slender forefinger. "A quick clarification on the saving people part. Are you saying that the ends do or do not justify the means?" <!-- L0329:pierre -->

"The doctor, for example, was absolutely torturing innocent people to try and come up with a solution to this illness," she retorted, her voice trembling with righteous fury. "Innocent people would have died for no reason. The means do not justify the end." <!-- L0330-L0332:creature -->

Pierre spread his hands in an exasperated shrug. "Okay. So your master changing history... what has he unleashed upon the world by trying to literally change the ends?" <!-- L0333:pierre -->

"He is fixing it to be a more utopian place, a more fair, just place," she snarled, her breath growing shallow and ragged. "But he's not going to keep chasing these down, because he knows they're inside you now. And he's going to be chasing down *you*." <!-- L0334:creature -->

Eusacles looked over at Pierre, his expression hardened into grim resolve. "Well, that's all I needed to hear. So, do we take out the trash now, or what?" <!-- L0335:eusacles -->

Pierre winced, glancing nervously down at Alfie. "Alfie is new to the world. I feel like we maybe should protect him from this part of the job, you know." <!-- L0336:pierre -->

"He's a natural-born warrior," Eusacles countered. <!-- L0337:eusacles -->

"Yes, but he does not have to be a cold-blooded killer," Pierre insisted. <!-- L0338:pierre -->

"To be clear, we're talking about our action figure here who just sliced a hook into her face," Eusacles pointed out. <!-- L0341:eusacles -->

"Yes, interrogation is fine. It is not killing anyone yet," Pierre argued. <!-- L0342:pierre -->

Alfie stepped forward onto the rim of the cart, drawing the needle rapier back with calm, unblinking determination. "All right. Well, it's fine. I got it. I'm filled with memories of really bad men, so it's fine." <!-- L0343:alfie -->

Before Pierre could shout a warning, Alfie thrust the steel needle with brutal precision straight up the captive's nostril. <!-- L0345 -->

Pierre gagged, spinning on his heel and stumbling into the campus boxwood hedges to retch violently. <!-- L0349 -->

The woman shuddered, her eyes rolling back as blood welled from her mouth. Clinging to the ragged thread of her life, she gargled in shock: "I'm not a problem for you anymore... but he's coming." Her head dropped limp against the plastic rim, and from the deep wound in her skull, a brilliant flare of violet energy burst upward into the night sky, illuminating the trees like an arcane signal beacon. <!-- L0350:creature -->
