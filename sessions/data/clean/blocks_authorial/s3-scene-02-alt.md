Along the shifting path between fractured Greek colonnades, they walked alongside the dark-robed maiden. When Dravin pressed for how they ought to address her, she turned back with a faint, wintery gaze. <!-- L0468 -->

"You may call me Ally," she murmured, her voice carrying a soft, crystalline chill. "The maiden of Persephone." <!-- L0470:ally -->

"How much further to the museum would you say?" Alfie piped up from Pierre's bag, his carved driftwood face tilting up with a cheeky grin. "Because I'm a bit hungry." <!-- L0551 -->

Ally offered a faint, cryptic warning, her dark eyes reflecting the silver mist. "You will not travel for very long. The Lost Roads are a time warp—sometimes you arrive a day later than when you left, sometimes thirty minutes, and sometimes before you even left. It is truly fascinating, these Lost Roads." <!-- L0561-L0562:ally -->

"Should have brought some pancakes for the road, mate," Alfie sighed, resting his chin against the canvas lip of Pierre's rucksack. <!-- L0569 -->
