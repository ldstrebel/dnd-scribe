## CHAPTER 41: THE JANITOR'S CART & THE ARCHITECTURAL MODELS

Dr. Aris Thorne stumbled against the mahogany edge of the podium, her knuckles turning bone-white against the railing as she surveyed the ruins of her lecture hall. Overhead, the emergency sprinklers sputtered their final droplets into the murky puddles below. She stared in unvarnished terror at the shattered rows of seats, the scorched upholstery, and the bound, gray-skinned woman lying motionless upon the carpet. Alfie hopped onto the brass trim of the stage apron, folding his small carved arms across his tiny linen trench coat. His painted seashell button eyes narrowed to sharp slits. "I do not love when people call me a doll. Let's just start there." <!-- L1441-L1446:alfie -->

Beside him, Eusacles adjusted his bronze hoplite shield with solemn demigod gravity. "Action figure," Eusacles repeated. <!-- L1450:eusacles -->

"Action figure. Exactly," Alfie affirmed, tapping the pommel of his needle rapier against his carved thigh. <!-- L1451:alfie -->

Professor Dravin wiped the heavy condensation from his wire-rimmed spectacles, tucking his leather-bound lexicon securely beneath his arm. "I will alternate between *Spare the Dying* and *Spore the Dying* to keep her unconscious and alive," Dravin observed calmly, ensuring the creature's volatile pulse remained subdued. <!-- L1460-L1464:dravin -->

Thorne's breath came in ragged, hysterical gasps as the high-pitched wails of approaching police sirens echoed through the rainy campus quadrangle outside. "They wanted my work!" Thorne gasped in terror, clutching her satchel to her ribs. "What about the lost roads? I have to get out of here!" <!-- L1493-L1499:thorne -->

Pierre adjusted his woolen beret with an imperious, mock-sympathetic flourish, looking down his nose at the trembling academic. "You are very important to your own story, Madame. I listened to your entire lecture, and nothing of merit was spoken outside the vision. Take all of your money, invest everything into this solitary research, sever all ties with your colleagues, and flee into the dark. We have a janitor's cart to commandeer." Overcome with panic, Thorne bolted across the green room and shoved through the emergency exit into the rainy night. <!-- L1500-L1518:pierre -->

Pierre produced his heavy iron ring of stolen custodial keys, rattling them against the brass locks along the dim department corridor until he jimmied the deadbolt of an undergraduate drafting studio. He hoisted Alfie up onto a wide wooden drafting table covered in intricate balsa-wood scale models, while Dravin rolled a squeaking gray utility cart into position beside the unconscious prisoner. <!-- L1529-L1532 -->

"This is incredible," Alfie whispered in genuine awe, perching atop a balsa skyscraper to admire the tiny balconies before hopping down to help push the rolling bin toward the door. "All right, back to the lost roads with our hostage." <!-- L1538-L1542:alfie -->
