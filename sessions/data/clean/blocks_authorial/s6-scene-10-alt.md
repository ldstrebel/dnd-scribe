## CHAPTER 41: THE JANITOR'S CART & THE ARCHITECTURAL MODELS

Dr. Aris Thorne leaned against the splintered rostrum, her knuckles turning bone-white against the mahogany rim. As the oppressive psychic aura lifted, her daze broke into naked horror. She looked from the scorched floorboards to the bound, gray-skinned adversary lying motionless in the pool of water, then pointed a trembling finger at the party.

"What... what is all of this?" Thorne stammered, her voice shrill with terror. "What happened here? What is that creature? And you... you aren't from here! You are a talking doll!" <!-- L1441-L1443:thorne -->

Alfie stepped to the edge of the desk, folding her carved wooden arms across her tiny linen coat as her painted seashell button eyes narrowed to sharp slits.

"I do not love when people call me a doll. Let's just start there." <!-- L1444-L1449:alfie -->

Beside her, Eusacles adjusted his dark sunglasses with solemn demigod gravity.

"Action figure," Eusacles rumbled. <!-- L1450:eusacles -->

"Action figure. Exactly," Alfie affirmed, resting a tiny hand near the pommel of her sewing-needle rapier. <!-- L1451:alfie -->

Dravin looked toward the bound captive, then glanced up at his companions.

"Can we bring her back to the Margin?" Dravin asked thoughtfully. <!-- L1452:dravin -->

"Certainly not," Pierre replied, shuddering at the tactical imprudence. "We cannot expose our sanctuary to an adversary capable of tearing open planar rifts. We shall transport her to the threshold of the Lost Roads, interrogate her in isolation, and if pursuit arrives, lose them in the labyrinth of ways." <!-- L1454-L1459:pierre -->

Professor Dravin knelt beside the bound captive, adjusting his wire-rimmed spectacles as he studied the strange residue clinging to the boards.

"I do not know precisely how these phenomena function," Dravin observed mildly, inspecting his family signet. "However, I get the distinct impression I can keep her alive, or cause dark mushrooms to erupt from her flesh, which appear to be exceptionally lethal." <!-- L1460-L1471:dravin -->

Pierre turned his attention back to the trembling professor, adjusting his woolen beret with an imperious flourish.

"We will take our captive to the Lost Roads," Pierre declared, resting his hand on his cane. "We tie her securely, and when she opens her eyes, the first sight she beholds will be Alfie's needle pointed at her throat. As for the professor—telling the truth of what occurred here is punishment enough. A world she cannot comprehend and no colleague will ever believe." <!-- L1485-L1492:pierre -->

Thorne backed away toward the platform stairs, clutching her satchel to her chest as police sirens echoed across the rainy quadrangle outside.

"What were you talking about? What are the lost roads?" Thorne demanded, panic fraying her words. "Clearly those beasts came for my research! I have uncovered something monumental. I think... I think I am exceedingly important!" <!-- L1493-L1499:thorne -->

Pierre stepped smoothly into her path, his voice dripping with mock-reverence.

"You are very important to your own story, Madame," Pierre declared, looking down his nose with solemn grandeur. "The ancient pestilence is returning, and if not for your tireless scholarship, the world will surely plunge into ruin. You must take all of your money and invest everything into this solitary endeavor. Sever every tie with your colleagues—they are nothing but envious distractions seeking to steal your glory! Flee into the shadows and work only in secret!" <!-- L1500-L1517:pierre -->

Thorne stared with wide, manic eyes, taking in every word as absolute revelation. Without another syllable, she snatched her briefcase, bolted across the green room, and threw her weight against the emergency fire door, vanishing into the rainy night. <!-- L1518 -->

Pierre watched the heavy door click shut, a triumphant smirk curving his lips.

"I believe we have successfully ruined her life," Pierre remarked smoothly. "Never call Alfie a doll." <!-- L1519-L1522:pierre -->

"That is the last time anyone makes that mistake," Alfie agreed with grim satisfaction. "Right then, let's find this janitor's cart and dump our captive into the bin." <!-- L1523-L1528:alfie -->

Pierre produced a heavy brass ring of stolen custodial keys, rattling them along the dark basement corridor until he jimmied the lock of an undergraduate drafting hall. Dravin helped hoist the bound captive into a squeaking gray canvas utility cart, while Pierre guided Alfie onto a massive wooden layout table covered in student projects. <!-- L1529-L1537:pierre -->

Alfie perched atop a delicate balsa-wood skyscraper, looking down over intricately cut cardboard bridges and tiny plastic trees with genuine wonder.

"This is incredible," Alfie whispered, tapping a miniature foam balcony. "Amazing work, really. All right then—back to the lost roads with our hostage." <!-- L1538-L1542:alfie -->
