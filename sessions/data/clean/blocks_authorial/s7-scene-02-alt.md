## CHAPTER 43: THE SIX-FINGERED SHADOW & THE PATRONUS FLIGHT

The blinding purple flare arced high over the university rooftops, bathing the brick arches and trimmed lawns in an unnatural violet glare before vanishing into the night. An oppressive silence crashed down over the breezeway. The janitor cart sat motionless in the gloom, its plastic rim slick with cooling black ichor.

"Time to go," Pierre whispered, backing away from the cart. <!-- L0354:pierre -->

Eusacles adjusted his grip on his morningstar. "We should leave, right? Like one hundred percent." <!-- L0357:eusacles -->

Alfie leapt lightly from the lip of the bin, landing squarely on the shoulder of Eusacles' heavy denim jacket. "Time to go." <!-- L0361:alfie -->

Pierre caught Professor Dravin by the elbow, guiding the academic swiftly toward the shadows along the perimeter. Dravin moved in a daze, clutching his tweed coat around himself as the cold wind whipped through the colonnade. Eusacles fell into stride beside them, head turning constantly. "Leave. We're leaving now." <!-- L0366:eusacles -->

Eusacles' dark aviators caught a flicker of movement across the lawn. Beneath the amber halo of a distant security light, something crawled through the gloom. It possessed no feet, drifting inches above the manicured grass like a pool of living calligraphy. Where its hands emerged from its ragged ink cloak, six elongated fingers flexed in twitching rhythm. Its head was a blank oval void, yet beneath the translucent surface of the skin, dozens of drowned, silent faces shifted and strained against each other in endless torment. The thing was sniffing the air, tracking the ionized trail left by the purple flare.

"We got to get out of here," Eusacles muttered through clenched teeth, his voice dead flat with urgent dread. "I will explain when we're gone, but we're getting out of here now. And we can't be followed." <!-- L0376:eusacles -->

"Alfie, can you ride on my shoulder so you can lead me, so I don't get lost a third time?" he asked as they broke into a disciplined jog toward the tree line. <!-- L0390:eusacles -->

"Yeah, all right, mate," Alfie replied, anchoring miniature driftwood boots into the denim seam. <!-- L0393:alfie -->

As they slipped between the weeping willows toward the threshold seam where reality thinned into mist, Alfie pulled out a scrap of cotton waste, scrubbing furiously at the needle rapier. "This won't come off. She's really gross." <!-- L0408:alfie -->

Alfie gave the hilt a sharp flick, button eyes gleaming in the dark. "You know, she called me a doll after all." <!-- L0410:alfie -->

Pierre wiped his mouth with the back of his sleeve, his face still ghastly pale under his beret. "Alfie, I have to admit I was not expecting such ruthlessness from you, and I am a little surprised by my own reaction to it as well. Normally when I deal with monsters, they turn to stone. There is no blood. That was... it was a lot for me." <!-- L0413:pierre -->

"Yeah," Alfie muttered, sheathing the stained needle. "The ink was a bit much." <!-- L0414:alfie -->

"Yeah, that was a bit much," Eusacles agreed, his gaze fixed on the thickening fog ahead. "Hey, while we're on this road and walking... the thing that I saw back there. You ever look at something and just know it's dangerous and dreadful? Imagine like a no-faced, multiple-faced figure. Doesn't have feet, lots of inkiness, and like six fingers." <!-- L0425:eusacles -->

"No facial features, but lots of faces inside the void," Eusacles continued, his voice tight. "Almost as if something was trapped within a body and didn't know what it was. A horrible conglomeration of ink, probably multiple consciousnesses fighting each other. Or maybe some being capable of shifting its appearance. I don't know. I didn't like it. I was horrified." <!-- L0431:eusacles -->

Alfie shivered on his shoulder. "Yeah, that's not for me." <!-- L0433:alfie -->

"Not my type either," Pierre murmured. <!-- L0435:pierre -->

"I don't know if y'all know what that is," Eusacles said, glancing back over his shoulder into the misty trees. "If you do, I'd love to hear about it, because I don't imagine this is the last time we'll see it. It looked like it was tracking, like a bloodhound. It looked like it was honing in on that flare that just got shot off." <!-- L0436:eusacles -->

They reached the crest of the grassy embankment where the modern world ended and the ancient paving stones of the Lost Roads emerged from the fog. Dravin slipped through first, his silhouette dissolving into the pearlescent vapor. Alfie and Eusacles followed closely behind.

Pierre lingered at the threshold, looking back through the damp weeping willows toward the open quad.

Across the lawn, fifty paces away, the six-fingered shape had reached the janitor cart. It drifted above the blood-soaked rim, its blank oval head tilting as its many submerged faces swirled. Slowly, deliberately, the creature turned toward the tree line, its hollow visage locking onto Pierre.

Pierre's breath hitched in his throat. Moving on pure instinct, he raised his slender hand and snapped his fingers, weaving a desperate thread of illumination into the mist. "Expecto patronum!" he whispered under his breath. <!-- L0440:pierre -->

A sudden burst of radiant white light coalesced at his feet. A tiny, luminous rabbit—sculpted of dazzling silver luminescence—bounded out across the lawn with playful leaps, zigzagging between the weeping willows and circling the towering ink wraith in bright, incandescent loops.

Pierre stared in disbelief, his jaw hanging open. "Oh my god. That is a royal bunny." <!-- L0442:pierre -->

The six-fingered creature paused, its shifting faces momentarily transfixed by the brilliant white hare darting around its vaporous skirts.

"I see this creature... it is like a dementor with more faces," Pierre stammered, his pulse hammering against his ribs as he stepped backward into the fog. "I do not like it either!" <!-- L0448:pierre -->

"There is a patronus, a little white bunny running around it, though," he called out as he threw himself through the seam. "And I do not exactly know where that came from!" <!-- L0451:pierre -->

The temporal mist closed over him with a muffled snap, cutting off the quad, the strobing sirens, and the dreadful six-fingered watcher.
