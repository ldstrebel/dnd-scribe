"I think it is best if you never touch that rock again," Alfie warned, pointing a carved wooden thumb toward the pedestal. <!-- L0441-L0442 -->

"That sounds very wise to me," Pierre agreed, scanning the chaotic atrium. Dravin was cornered between two of the serpent-headed operatives, while the lead Gordon hovered near the console, preparing to drop the vitrine and seal the ticking bomb. "I am going to make a run for the explosive!" <!-- L0443-L0450 -->

Darting low beneath the raised glass, Pierre snatched the warm, beeping cylinder off the limestone slab. "Pierre believes that all Gorgons are essentially French," Pierre declared with absolute philosophical conviction, "and if there is one thing Frenchmen cannot resist, it is fresh bread!" Flipping open his canvas lunch satchel, Pierre produced a crusty French baguette. With surgical speed, he gouged a hollow into the crumb, shoved the ticking bomb deep into the crust, and held the loaf aloft like a holy relic. <!-- L0451-L0460 -->

"Fresh bread!" Pierre bellowed across the gallery. <!-- L0461-L0465 -->

The effect was instantaneous. All four Gorgons froze mid-stride, their viper crowns rearing straight up as their nostrils caught the aroma of golden-baked wheat. Spotting a distant museum sheep display, Pierre adjusted his aim away from the animals and hurled the baguette bomb across the polished marble. "If you are running for bread, then I am playing fetch with the Gorgons!" <!-- L0466-L0487 -->

Sure enough, the nearest Gordon broke into a desperate, inhumanly fast sprint, tearing past Pierre to tackle the flying loaf into an empty alcove. <!-- L0490-L0502 -->

With the first operative lured away, Professor Edward Dravin stepped forward to face the second Gordon. "The old tried and true," Dravin muttered, clasping his hands behind his back. Rather than drawing a weapon, he began to lecture in a dry, relentless, and utterly hypnotic academic drone, dissecting the morphological variations of archaic Athenian pottery. The scholarly incantation settled like lead over the operative's serpentine crown; Gordon's eyes rolled back, his viper locks went limp, and the Redactor collapsed face-first onto the linoleum in deep slumber. <!-- L0504-L0550 -->
