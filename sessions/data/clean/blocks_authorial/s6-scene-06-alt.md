## CHAPTER 37: THE MOTORCYCLE MEMORY & THE ACCELERATING RIFT

Beside the teacher's desk, a bloated mass of living ink lunged toward Dr. Thorne's open briefcase. Alfie sidestepped the dark ooze with casual ease, her tiny wooden frame swaying to the tinny brass tempo of her cassette player. She lowered her hook arm, snagging the quivering globule like a scrap of wet laundry, before driving her sewing-needle rapier straight through its center. The mass hissed sharply against the wet oak floorboards, dissolving into greasy dark foam that washed away in the sprinkler runoff. <!-- L0914-L0927 -->

Across the platform, Professor Dravin began to perceive the empirical nature of the phenomena. The pale column of lunar light was not an independent beacon; it responded directly to his focal point.

"Stay behind the line!" Dravin called out, observing the lunar column track his vision across the stage. <!-- L0932-L0954:dravin -->

Dravin swept his gaze deliberately across the apron, and the beam tracked like a theater spotlight. The searing brilliance caught one horned assailant charging up the center aisle, while the outer rim enveloped a second beast attempting to circumvent Alfie's protective perimeter. The lunar barrier forced both creatures through its burning luminescence. Their coarse hides blistered and smoked as they shoved past the platform railing, battered and howling, but still driving forward.

The advancing satyrs charged with savage desperation. Two smaller ink blots slithered into the column of moonlight, dissolving instantly into dispersed droplets beneath the radiant heat. The transformed woman wheeled around the rostrum and caught sight of Pierre ducking behind the mahogany lectern. Her slitted gaze locked onto his face, but as she caught the vertical, predatory feline gleam in his pupils beneath the woolen beret, she recoiled with a sharp hiss. She diverted her strike away from the Frenchman and lunged toward Eusacles. Her crackling hair tendrils lashed forward like electrified steel cables, striking the demigod squarely across the chest. The concussive discharge blew his aviator sunglasses into the spray, his spiked morningstar clattered across the boards, and Eusacles collapsed motionless onto the flooded platform. <!-- L0966-L0987 -->

At the rear of the stage, the violet planar breach began to flicker. The edges of the seam frayed and shuddered violently, its humming frequency dropping as the portal lost stability. The satyrs wheeled toward their leader in panic, realizing the collapsing rift could no longer sustain dragging a captive demigod into the ether. <!-- L0991-L1003 -->

The woman snarled in mounting frustration, dropping to her knees beside Eusacles's fallen frame.

"We will just have to do it here!" the woman commanded, her double-harmonic voice echoing through the flooded auditorium. <!-- L1004-L1007:woman -->

She buried her talons into Eusacles's brow, and the memory siphon took hold. A temporal vacuum flared outward from his temples, pulling the surrounding water into concentric rings of spray. Shimmering filaments of neural luminescence coiled from his mind into her outstretched fingers. An entire year of Eusacles's mortal existence began to unspool—hundreds of miles of sun-baked Nevada asphalt, the roar of midnight highway throttles, the lonely neon glare of desert truck stops—all condensed and stripped from his consciousness. The vast expanse of memory collapsed under crushing planar pressure, crystallizing into a single heavy relic of polished chrome: the gearshift of his first motorcycle.

Pierre peered over the edge of the rostrum, horrified revulsion gripping his chest. He had seen strange artifacts in the museum, but watching an adversary harvest the living memories of a companion crossed every line of decency. His jaw tightened beneath his spectacles, and aesthetic offense hardened into cold resolve.

"Eating memories is thoroughly repulsive," Pierre muttered, stepping lightly through the electrified shallows. "And far worse than anything my javelin could suffer!" <!-- L1024-L1040:pierre -->

Pierre scrambled atop the polished oak podium, balancing along the narrow ledge before hurling his full weight downward in a desperate leap toward the woman's crown.
