## CHAPTER 37: THE MOTORCYCLE MEMORY & THE ACID FLASK

Beside the teacher's desk, a bloated mass of living ink lunged for Dr. Thorne's abandoned leather briefcase. Alfie sidestepped the dark ooze with jaunty Cockney swagger, humming the swinging brass cadence of her brass cassette tape as she drew back her sewing-needle rapier. With a deft, surgical flick of her carved wrist, she pinned the squirming globule clean to the wet boards, watching it hiss and dissolve into greasy dark foam. <!-- L0903-L0914 -->

Across the stage, Professor Dravin extended his open palm to focus the pale column of moonlight against the advancing Satyr vanguard. "A perimeter shield," Dravin called out, his authoritative voice slicing cleanly through the roar of the overhead sprinklers. "*Moonbeam* anchored." The lunar luminescence seared the lead beast's pelt, forcing the horned vanguard to stagger back with smoke billowing from their shoulders. <!-- L0932-L0954:dravin -->

Yet the transformed Medusa was undeterred by the burning light. Pivoting on the balls of her cloven feet, she locked her predatory, slitted amber eyes onto Eusacles. Her crackling hair tendrils whipped forward like electrified steel cables, striking the bronze warrior squarely across the sternum with concussive psychic force. His gold aviator sunglasses flew into the spray, his heavy morningstar *Good Vibes* clattered loudly across the oak planks, and Eusacles collapsed limp and motionless upon the flooded floorboards. <!-- L0958-L0987 -->

A sickening temporal vacuum flared violently around his brow. Pale, shimmering neural luminescence coiled from his temples into the woman's outstretched talons as an entire year of Eusacles's mortal existence condensed and unraveled before their eyes—endless sun-scorched stretches of Nevada asphalt, roaring midnight rides, neon casino horizons—all compressed with brutal planar force into a single tangible chrome relic: the polished gear shift of his beloved 1956 Indian motorcycle. <!-- L0991-L0999 -->

At the edge of the stage, the dimensional rift shuddered violently under the sheer gravitational mass of the stolen memory. The woman bared her sharp vipers' fangs, snarling in frantic desperation as the breach threatened to collapse under the strain. "We will just have to do it here!" the woman commanded, coiling her whip-like tendrils tighter around Eusacles's skull to complete the extraction. <!-- L1004-L1016:woman -->

Pierre watched from beneath his black woolen beret in rising, visceral horror. He had crossed paths with grotesque creatures before, and beneath his aristocratic posturing, raw panic clawed at his chest—yet he forced his flamboyant composure back into place. "Eating memories is thoroughly repulsive," Pierre muttered, his jaw tightening as pure aesthetic offense turned into cold resolve. "And far worse than anything my javelin could suffer!" Stepping lightly around the flailing tendrils, Pierre scrambled atop the polished oak lectern. Balancing on the narrow molding with theatrical momentum, he leaped into the air directly toward the creature's crown, brandishing an unstopped amber vial of corrosive museum etching acid. <!-- L1024-L1040:pierre -->
