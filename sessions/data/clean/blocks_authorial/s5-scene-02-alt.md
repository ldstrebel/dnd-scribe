Naomi pushed aside a stack of mugs and smoothed a faded architectural photograph across the scrubbed oak. The monochrome print revealed a sprawling collegiate quadrangle flanked by soaring stone pediments and ivy-draped Gothic colonnades. <!-- L0156 -->

"A prestigious northeastern campus," Naomi explained, tapping where Greek letters were carved deep into the limestone lintel. "Founded in 1885. Our subject is scheduled for a public address tomorrow afternoon: Dr. Aris Thorne. On paper, she is an investigative medical historian touring a dense academic volume on buried mid-century clinical trials. But she’s an anomaly. The archives claim the original physician tested experimental compounds on unwilling subjects in 1948 to eradicate Big Pox. Some believe the formula cured the disease; others claim it was lethal poison. The handwritten trial notes themselves are an active temporal anchor." <!-- L0157-L0170:naomi -->

Pierre leaned over the print, woolen beret tilted low over his brow. "And you, Professor? With all your classical connections, perhaps you have given guest lectures in their hallowed halls?" <!-- L0171-L0173:pierre -->

Dravin smoothed the lapels of his rumpled tweed coat with patrician calm. "My pedigree is Stanford and the Pacific coast, Pierre," he replied, lifting his teacup. "Two hours away by air, perhaps, but a world apart in academic temperament." <!-- L0174-L0183:dravin -->

Before Dravin could take another sip, a rapid clatter of rubber soles rattled the exterior cedar decking. A lithe figure bounded up the porch steps, vaulted clean over the railing, and landed in the center of the common room. The young courier wore vintage canvas sneakers with small white wings stitched along the ankles, his messenger bag slung across a bright athletic jersey.

"What is up, Forgotten Friends?" the courier announced with a beaming grin, tossing a lock of golden hair out of his eyes. "Express delivery from the higher registers. Do we have a Professor Edward present?" <!-- L0184:hermes -->

Dravin raised a single aristocratic eyebrow. "Present." <!-- L0185:dravin -->

"Yeah, that tracks," the courier nodded, sliding a heavy, wax-sealed parchment envelope from his satchel. His gaze locked onto Pierre’s woolen beret and delicate wire rims, amusement spreading across his sharp features. "Whoa. You look familiar. Are you a demigod?" <!-- L0186-L0188:hermes -->

Pierre bristled, squaring his narrow shoulders behind his rucksack straps. "If you have seen Michelangelo's *David*, monsieur, you may recognize the classic proportions. It is a common mistake." <!-- L0189:pierre -->

"You’re French!" The courier burst into delighted laughter, slapping his thigh. "Oh, man, I didn't think you guys were real! What an absolute classic prank! A real live Frenchman!" <!-- L0190:hermes -->

Pierre lowered his voice into a frantic whisper toward Alfie. "Alfie, I am deeply confused. Who is this athletic boy? Is this someone we were warned about?" <!-- L0191-L0194:pierre -->

"I'm Hermes," the courier called out cheerfully, flashing two fingers in a casual salute. "Messenger of the gods, courier of the weave, deliverer of divine parcels. Drop-off complete. If you ever need priority transit across the veil, you know where to whistle. But fair warning—celestial dispatch isn't free. Catch you on the flip side!" <!-- L0197-L0213:hermes -->

With a dazzling grin, Hermes pivoted on the balls of his winged sneakers and sprinted back out into the mist toward the camp perimeter. <!-- L0215-L0216 -->

Alfie scampered up onto Dravin's shoulder, her little steel hook clicking against a copper button on his coat. "Does he deliver all the post, or just the stuff with gold leaf?" Alfie rasped, her seashell eyes fixed on the heavy seal. <!-- L0217-L0219:alfie -->

Theodore lumbered down the timber staircase, wiping his weathered hands on a linen towel. "He only runs routes when Olympus or the Underworld demands an urgent tether," the caretaker murmured reverently. "Whatever rests in that envelope was dispatched by a sovereign hand." <!-- L0222-L0224:theodore -->

Pierre stepped forward urgently, pointing a trembling spatula at the wax. "Professor, you must burn that parchment immediately! In America, mysterious registered envelopes always mean jury duty! Do not look at it!" <!-- L0226:pierre -->
