Naomi spread a monochrome architectural photograph across the scrubbed oak table, revealing a sprawling quadrangle flanked by limestone colonnades. "A prestigious northeastern campus," she explained, tapping the carved Greek archway. "Our subject, Dr. Aris Thorne, speaks tomorrow. On paper, she investigates buried 1948 clinical trials that supposedly tested experimental Big Pox cures on unwilling subjects. I believe her trial notes are an active temporal anchor." <!-- L0156-L0170:naomi -->

Pierre leaned in over the print, spectacles catching the light. "And you, Professor? Have you given guest lectures in their hallowed halls?" <!-- L0171-L0173:pierre -->

"My pedigree is Stanford and the Pacific coast, Pierre," Dravin replied calmly, lifting his teacup. <!-- L0174-L0178:dravin -->

"Ah, the West Coast," Pierre mused airily. "That is only one or two hours away, no? A quick jaunt across the provinces." <!-- L0179:pierre -->

Dravin chuckled into his cup, shaking his head. "Yes, my sweet, sweet European boy. You could fly for five hours and still be looking down at corn. The American continent is not an afternoon stroll through the Loire Valley." <!-- L0183:dravin -->

Before Pierre could retort, rubber soles clattered along the cedar decking. A lithe youth in vintage canvas sneakers vaulted clean over the porch railing and landed in the common room, a messenger bag slung across his bright athletic jersey. <!-- L0184 -->

"What is up, Forgotten Friends?" the courier beamed. "Express delivery from the higher registers. Do we have a Professor Edward present?" <!-- L0184-L0185 -->

As the boy produced a heavy, wax-sealed parchment from his satchel, he blinked at Pierre's beret. "Whoa. You look familiar. Are you a demigod?" <!-- L0186-L0188 -->

"If you have seen Michelangelo's *David*, monsieur, you may recognize the classic proportions," Pierre sniffed. <!-- L0189:pierre -->

"You’re French!" The courier burst into laughter. "Oh man, I didn't think you guys were real! What an absolute classic prank! I'm Hermes. You know—messenger of the gods, courier of the weave." <!-- L0190-L0197:hermes -->

Pierre narrowed his eyes with Parisian disdain. "That is extraordinarily on the nose for a name. It is like me walking down the street calling myself *Stoneman*." <!-- L0198-L0209:pierre -->

Hermes chuckled, sliding the parchment before Dravin. "If you ever need priority transit across the veil, you know where to whistle. But celestial dispatch isn't free. Catch you on the flip side!" With a casual salute, the courier vaulted back over the rail and sprinted into the mist. <!-- L0210-L0216 -->

Alfie scampered onto Dravin's shoulder, inspecting the heavy pomegranate wax seal. "Does he deliver all the post, or just the stuff with gold leaf?" <!-- L0217-L0219:alfie -->

Theodore lumbered down the timber stairs. "He only runs routes when Olympus or the Underworld demands an urgent tether. Whatever rests in that envelope was dispatched by a sovereign hand." <!-- L0222-L0224:theodore -->

Pierre stepped forward, pointing an urgent finger at the envelope. "Professor, you must burn that immediately! In America, mysterious registered envelopes always mean jury duty! Twelve strangers arguing about lawnmowers! In France, our historical tribunals did not waste months on deliberation—we had the guillotine! Throw it into the stove!" <!-- L0226-L0230:pierre -->
