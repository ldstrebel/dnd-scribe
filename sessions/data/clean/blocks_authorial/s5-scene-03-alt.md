In the days since the mist had separated the companions on the Lost Roads, Eusacles had made himself useful across the Margin. He had walked the perimeter through the small hours, kept camp watch, and run errands for Naomi. He had also claimed the most decrepit cabin on the grounds, a burnt-out timber shell that looked ready to collapse into kindling, and set about making it his own. The work was still a long way from finished: a few patched boards, a salvaged door that shut against the draft, and zero thought yet for aesthetics. But a hammock hung securely between two charred posts, keeping him dry and warm at night. <!-- L0276-L0283 -->

Eusacles strode out of that cabin now and into the misty clearing, yawning broadly and rolling his broad shoulders beneath his denim jacket. An enchanted watch-chain glinted at his belt loop, humming with faint divine resonance. Then he stopped dead on the timber decking, blinking through his dark aviator sunglasses at the sheer absurdity before him. <!-- L0290 -->

Over by the hearth, Pierre was standing with dramatic flourish, using the bronze point of an authentic Greek javelin to flip sizzling rum crepes in a blackened skillet. Leaning against the timber bench sat Professor Dravin, stoic as an ancient statue, clad in the hammered bronze hoplite cuirass and shield he had carried back from Raleigh. And perched atop a wooden rain barrel sat Alfie, her little driftwood frame utterly swallowed beneath a massive navy blue baseball cap.

"Morning, fellas," Eusacles rumbled, cracking his knuckles as he stepped forward. "Did you manage to steal anything decent from that museum, or did y'all get lost as hell in the fog like I did?" <!-- L0291:eusacles -->

Pierre gestured grandly toward the pine bench with his javelin. "Observe, my friend! The Professor has returned clad as a Spartan warlord, and Alfie has acquired the crown jewel of American culture." <!-- L0292:pierre -->

Perched atop the barrel, Alfie puffed out her sailcloth chest and tugged down the brim of the enormous cap. The embroidered acronym arched across the crown in bright gold thread, swallow-tailing far past her miniature ears. <!-- L0293-L0298 -->

Eusacles squinted at the massive cap swallowing her seashell eyes. "That is... certainly a unique piece of gear." <!-- L0312:eusacles -->

"A souvenir from the gift shop," Alfie announced proudly, her raspy Cockney voice echoing across the courtyard. "I think it's beautiful. Matches my driftwood complexion, don't it?" <!-- L0313:alfie -->

"The hat says NCMONAWMR across the front," Eusacles muttered, squinting at the alphabet soup. "NC what? What does all that stand for?" <!-- L0323-L0325:eusacles -->

"North Carolina Museum of Natural and World History," Dravin replied smoothly, taking a calm sip of tea. "The curator had an admirable collection of classical arms. We engaged four timeline redactors attempting to destroy the Greek stele. Pierre threw a spiced baguette at their explosives, and Alfie touched the relic directly." <!-- L0326-L0338:dravin -->

As Dravin spoke, his fingers brushed the heavy parchment resting against his tweed lapel. The brass pomegranate signet pinned to the wool flared with a sudden, deep crimson heat. The warmth radiated through the fabric into his chest, carrying a faint scent of crushed pomegranate seeds and autumnal frost. Dravin drew the letter out, his eyes scanning the flowing celestial script in quiet contemplation. <!-- L0241 -->

"You look rather pale, Professor," Pierre observed, tilting his woolen beret back with a smirk. "I warned you. It was jury duty, wasn't it? A summons to deliberate upon stolen livestock in some backwater county." <!-- L0258-L0260:pierre -->

Without answering, Dravin slid the parchment across the rough pine table. <!-- L0265-L0268:dravin -->

Pierre scooped it up, the smirk still playing across his lips as he began to read—and then the smirk evaporated. His eyes widened as the flowing script pulsed with faint ember light:

*"My attendant informed me of your arrival and safety at the Margin,"* the letter read in ancient, formal cadence. *"I am descending into the Underworld for the turn of the season. Know that the veil between realms grows thin as the cold deepens. If you require my counsel or threshold aid, call upon the earth. I am watching over you, my son."* <!-- L0242-L0244:persephone -->

Pierre swallowed hard, his theatrical confidence silenced by the ancient signature. Without a word, he passed the parchment across to Eusacles.

Eusacles took the letter, his rough thumb tracing the warm wax of the seal. "Persephone..." he murmured, his jaw tightening as he read. "She's already descending into the Underworld." <!-- L0256:eusacles -->

Eusacles handed the parchment back to Dravin. In solemn silence, the professor carefully folded the heavy sheet and slid it back into his inner breast pocket beside his tarnished silver bell. <!-- L0249:dravin -->

Eusacles leaned against the timber railing, folding his thick arms. "Did you read the stone or just touch the stone?" <!-- L0340:eusacles -->

Alfie's posture slumped slightly on the barrel, her seashell button eyes gazing into the damp mist. "I touched it, mate. Terrifying and enlightening all at once. It was oarsmen—rowing in the pitch dark through freezing brine, water crashing over the gunwales, and men shouting in the black. I've had the whole rotten nightmare on repeat behind my eyes ever since my fingers touched that rock." <!-- L0348-L0353:alfie -->

"It is unsettling, when Alfie goes to bed," Pierre confided to Eusacles in a stage whisper. "Sometimes she simply stares out into the middle distance, and you cannot tell whether she is looking at you or through you." <!-- L0357:pierre -->

"That's why I like the lanterns at night, mate," Alfie said without turning. "Don't spend too much time sleeping. Or thinking." <!-- L0358:alfie -->

"Two distinct timelines branched from that tablet," Pierre explained, stepping over to inspect the edge of Dravin's shield. "One was *beacon* and one was *beckon*. A lighthouse that beckoned raiders safely toward the shore, and another that acted as a beacon of warning that shattered their keel on the shoals. A single letter altered in the inscription, yet whole villages burned or survived because of it." <!-- L0361-L0366:pierre -->

"Alfie—had you seen it before? Did you recognize the boat?" Pierre asked, more gently. <!-- L0367:pierre -->

"See, that's the thing." Alfie's seashell eyes stayed fixed on the mist. "I'm confused about myself. I don't know exactly who I am or where I came from. I am, if you hadn't noticed, a bit of a doll." <!-- L0368:alfie -->

Eusacles grunted, shaking his head. "A doll with dreams and night terrors." <!-- L0371:eusacles -->

Alfie glared up at him, her little steel hook hand rapping sharply against the rain barrel. "I don't love it when you say that, mate. Action figure, if you please. We settled on action figure." <!-- L0372:alfie -->

"Action figure. Sure," Eusacles conceded with a dry chuckle. "Action figure with night terrors about men rowing a boat in the dark." <!-- L0381-L0383:eusacles -->

"Yeah, that about sums it up," Alfie sighed, her wooden chin sinking into her collar. "And a mad captain shouting orders to put a coastal town to the torch." <!-- L0384-L0386:alfie -->
