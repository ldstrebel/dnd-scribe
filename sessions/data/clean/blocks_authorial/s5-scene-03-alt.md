As Dravin touched the parchment, the brass pomegranate signet pinned to his tweed lapel flared with a deep crimson heat. The warmth radiated through the wool into his chest, carrying a faint scent of crushed pomegranate seeds and autumnal frost. His eyes scanned the flowing celestial script. <!-- L0241 -->

*"My attendant informed me of your arrival and safety at the Margin,"* the letter read in ancient, formal cadence. *"I am descending into the Underworld for the turn of the season. Know that the veil between realms grows thin as the cold deepens. If you require my counsel or threshold aid, call upon the earth. I am watching over you, my son."* <!-- L0242-L0244:persephone -->

Dravin’s expression tightened into a grim, stoic quiet. Without reading the words aloud, he folded the heavy parchment and slid it into his inner breast pocket beside his tarnished silver bell. <!-- L0249-L0256:dravin -->

"You look rather pale, Professor," Pierre observed, tilting his woolen beret back with a smirk. "I warned you. It was jury duty, wasn't it?" <!-- L0258-L0260:pierre -->

Before Dravin could deliver an acid retort, a heavy thud shook the timber decking. Eusacles strode into the clearing, yawning broadly beneath his denim jacket as an enchanted watch-chain glinted at his belt loop. "Morning, fellas," Eusacles rumbled. "Did you manage to steal anything decent from that museum, or did y'all get lost as hell in the fog like I did?" <!-- L0265-L0291:eusacles -->

Pierre gestured grandly toward the pine bench where Dravin’s bronze hoplite shield leaned. "Observe, my friend! The Professor has returned clad as a Spartan warlord, and Alfie has acquired the crown jewel of American culture." <!-- L0292:pierre -->

Perched atop a wooden rain barrel, Alfie puffed out her sailcloth chest and tugged down the brim of a navy blue baseball cap. The embroidered acronym arched across the crown in bright gold thread, swallow-tailing far past her miniature ears. <!-- L0293-L0298 -->

"The hat says NCMONAWMR across the front," Eusacles muttered, squinting at the alphabet soup. "What does all that stand for?" <!-- L0323-L0325:eusacles -->

"North Carolina Museum of Natural and World History," Dravin replied smoothly, taking a calm sip of tea. "The curator had an admirable collection of classical arms. We engaged four timeline redactors attempting to destroy the Greek stele. Pierre threw a spiced baguette at their explosives, and Alfie touched the relic directly." <!-- L0326-L0338:dravin -->

Alfie's posture slumped slightly on the barrel, her seashell eyes gazing into the damp mist. "I touched it, mate. Terrifying and enlightening all at once. Oarsmen rowing in the pitch dark through freezing brine, water crashing over the gunwales, and men shouting in the black. I've had the whole rotten nightmare on repeat behind my eyes ever since." <!-- L0348-L0353:alfie -->

"Two distinct timelines branched from that tablet," Pierre explained. "One was *beacon* and one was *beckon*. A lighthouse that beckoned raiders safely toward the shore, and another that acted as a beacon of warning that shattered their keel on the shoals. A single letter altered in the inscription, yet whole villages burned or survived because of it." <!-- L0361-L0366:pierre -->

"A doll with dreams and night terrors," Eusacles grunted with a dry chuckle. <!-- L0371-L0383:eusacles -->

"Action figure, if you please, mate," Alfie sighed, her wooden chin sinking into her collar. "Action figure with night terrors about a mad captain putting a coastal town to the torch." <!-- L0384-L0386:alfie -->
