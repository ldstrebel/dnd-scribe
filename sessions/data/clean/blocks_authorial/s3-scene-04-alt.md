They passed through the glass double doors into the cool atrium of the museum. Front and center in the main gallery, surrounded by velvet stanchions and informational placards, was the celebrated ancient Greek limestone tablet. <!-- L0721 -->

Behind the polished reception desk sat a woman in a crisp museum blazer adjusting a ring light. "I am—my name is Nincy, as you can see," she announced, tapping her engraved gold badge. <!-- L0725 -->

"Yes, my name is Nincy with an *I*," she emphasized with a knowing Southern smile. <!-- L0727 -->

"N-I-N-C-Y," Dravin pronounced carefully, adjusting his wire rims. <!-- L0730 -->

"Exactly, exactly! I'm glad you caught that," Nincy beamed. "Some people with my accent, they don't hear the difference." <!-- L0732-L0733 -->

Dravin offered an elegant, patrician bow. "It is an honor to meet you, Nincy." <!-- L0735 -->

Nincy let out an amused laugh, twirling the end of her lanyard around a finger. "Well! That's the first time anyone's said that about this museum when they first come in. I've never been treated like an exhibit myself." <!-- L0740 -->

"Oh, well, you are quite the exhibit, Nincy," Dravin purred with smooth, professorial charm. <!-- L0741 -->

"Now, don't stay too long, sugar," she replied with a flirtatious smirk. "I'm almost done with my shift." <!-- L0742 -->

Pierre leaned against the mahogany counter with a thick Parisian purr, gesturing toward Dravin. "This here is a professor. You don't know all the things he could teach you." <!-- L0745-L0747 -->

Nincy's eyebrows arched in mild surprise. "You don't say? And I assume Greek mythology, is that correct?" <!-- L0750 -->

Dravin lifted Alfie into both hands, presenting the tiny driftwood figure across the counter. "Look at that," Nincy blinked. "I've never seen that action figure before." <!-- L0781 -->

"First edition limited run," Dravin announced with academic gravity. "A tweed-clad shipwrecked Marvel action figure. Extremely rare." <!-- L0782 -->

Dravin tugged the twine hooked to Alfie's coat. From the carved mouth came a tinny mechanical squeak: "Stay a bit rough, isn't it?" <!-- L0787 -->

Nincy clapped her hands in delight. "Wow! Now that sounds quite authentic!" <!-- L0790 -->

While she laughed, Pierre slid along the counter toward her lanyard clip, but her gaze flicked down. "Excuse me, sir! I said my name was Nincy. You can see it right here. You don't need my badge to see my name." <!-- L0834-L0836 -->

Pierre adjusted his spectacles with an innocent French grin. "It was such a sweet name, madame. I could not believe it was real." <!-- L0837 -->

Nincy's hand reached under the counter for the radio. Panicking, Dravin grabbed Alfie from Pierre's shoulder, reared back, and hurled the doll across the museum lobby. <!-- L0850 -->
