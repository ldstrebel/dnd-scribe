## CHAPTER 32: THE CRACKLING VEIL & THE INK-BLOT PROBE

The piercing shriek of the university fire alarm tore through the lecture hall, setting the overhead halogens rattling as panicked students scrambled over desks toward the lobby. A blinding burst of ozone had erupted the moment Edward Dravin turned the brass key in Dr. Thorne's briefcase, dropping the party straight into planar violence. In the second row of raked seating, the quiet woman in the tailored coat rose, shedding her mortal glamour like melting wax. Dark hair swirled around her head in a suspended tempest crackling with blue-white voltage, while cloven-hoofed satyrs smashed through the double entrance doors. <!-- L0217-L0218 -->

Pierre ducked behind the mahogany lectern, round wire spectacles flashing beneath his woolen beret as he gripped his bronze museum javelin. In the aisle, the crackling woman locked her slitted, predatory gaze directly onto him. "I know you," she said, her voice echoing in unnatural double harmonics. "I think I will just take the information from your brain." <!-- L0240-L0259:woman -->

"No," Pierre countered smoothly in his crisp Parisian drawl, stepping clear of the podium. "I do not like that at all, actually. Three satyrs at the entrance, and now another seam behind us." <!-- L0260:pierre -->

With a wet, sucking plop, a fist-sized globular mass of viscous black ink dropped through a tear in the rear plaster, undulating across the floorboards. Pierre leaned forward, prodding the quivering substance with the blunt ferrule of his spear. The living ink immediately surged upward, clinging to the bronze head like hot asphalt. <!-- L0261-L0273 -->

"If you wish to ride my spear, very well," Pierre muttered, pivoting on his heel like a lacrosse player. "Let us see how you like the aisle!" With a sharp snap of his wrists, he whipped the javelin forward. <!-- L0274:pierre -->

The blot tore free with a wet slap, flying wide across the hall and striking the shoulder of a fleeing attendant before rolling across an empty desk. Pierre wrinkled his nose in disgust, shaking the haft of his weapon. "I do not know what this is, but you have clearly overcooked it! This is like a wheel of burnt molasses!" <!-- L0286-L0296:pierre -->

Along the aisles, the fleeing crowd slowed to a dazed crawl beneath the woman's oppressive psychic suppression field, while in the lobby, demonstrators tossed their discarded disguises onto the tiles as horned beast-men blocked the street exits. <!-- L0297-L0314 -->

Up on the stage apron, Alfie popped up from within Dr. Thorne's open briefcase, her painted seashell button eyes narrowing at the electrical storm. Beside her, Dr. Thorne sat paralyzed with shock, while Professor Dravin raised his ancient hoplite shield. Alfie gave her oversized museum ballcap a resolute tug, her tiny carved fingers resting near her sewing-needle rapier before tapping the pouches of her leather belt. <!-- L0315-L0318 -->

"Right then," the driftwood figurine rasped, releasing the bronze buckle. "I thought I'd go with the chaos belt." With a sharp twist of the latch, she prepared to unleash three random planar relics into the hall. <!-- L0318-L0340:alfie -->
