At the pine tree line, Pierre lingered twenty feet back in the gloom, hugging his woolen cloak tight against the damp chill, with the little driftwood doll nestled against his collarbone. He watched the orange embers spit into the fog, unconvinced. <!-- L0412 -->

Eusacles and Professor Edward Dravin pressed forward toward the firelight, the spartan's broad shoulders rolling with vigilant tension. <!-- L0417 -->

Pierre leaned his chin down toward the doll's carved ear, his voice dropping to a theatrical French hiss. "I am thinking that we let them talk to the scary guy by the fire... the one who thinks we are dead." <!-- L0418-L0420 -->

Dusting ash from his work-worn knuckles, he leaned forward on his knees. "We love fresh ink around here. We're happy to have you.
