## CHAPTER 29: PARCHMENT, CREPES, AND THE GOD OF TRANSIT

Daylight came reluctantly to the Margin—a pale dilution of the eternal pine fog pooling across the rough cabin floorboards, where an iron skillet sputtered with melting butter and brown sugar. <!-- L0081-L0083 -->

Perched upon the pine dining bench, Alfie swung her miniature driftwood boots, seashell button eyes gleaming in the hearthlight beneath her oversized museum ballcap. "They have nighttime and fog time in this realm," the wooden rogue rasped in her sharp Cockney cadence, inspecting two warm sunstones tucked beside her needle rapier. "Never seen an acre so allergic to an honest sun." <!-- L0084-L0086:alfie -->

At the iron range, Pierre pushed his wire spectacles up with flour-dusted knuckles, carefully adjusting his tilted woolen beret as he flipped another gossamer crepe with theatrical precision. Dark spiced rum sizzled against the metal, drawing an appreciative nod from Mike. "In Paris, Monsieur Mike, breakfast is the final bulwark of civilized humanity," Pierre declared, gesturing toward the fog-drenched pines. "Out back, I sought to construct a stone blind. Three whole weeks without a single carved archway or lintel! Only soggy loam. If one does not practice stonecraft, the fingers soften into bourgeois uselessness." <!-- L0088-L0103:pierre -->

At the far end of the long trestle table, Professor Edward Dravin sat surrounded by yellowed ledger folios, resting one hand upon the heavy bronze rim of the Spartan hoplite shield recovered from Raleigh. He dipped his ink pen, checking the balance of the classical relic against notes of threshold magic. <!-- L0108-L0115 -->

"The provenance of classical relics requires precision, Pierre," Dravin remarked mildly, tracing the hammered bronze lip. "If one alters the phonetic harmonic from *spare* to *spore*, the necrotic life-tether reverses. Any foe pressing a bloodied ally would find drifting fungal rot choking their throat before the blow could fall." <!-- L0116-L0120:dravin -->

Pierre merely shook his head with mild amusement at the grim discourse, sliding a finished rum crepe onto a tin plate before the scholar before turning back to his skillet.
