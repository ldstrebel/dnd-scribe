## CHAPTER 29: PARCHMENT, CREPES, AND THE GOD OF TRANSIT

Dawn never truly broke over the Margin; it merely leaked through the mist in pale, watery veins that turned the towering hemlocks to silhouettes of wet iron. Inside the cabin, the stillness was broken only by the rhythmic scrape of a spatula and the hiss of dark rum caramelizing over an iron hearth. <!-- L0081-L0083 -->

Perched upon the rough-hewn pine table, Alfie gave her oversized ballcap an irritable yank, the seashell buttons of her eyes gleaming in the amber embers. She swung her miniature driftwood boots over the bench edge, watching the fog press like wet wool against the windowpanes. "They have nighttime and fog time in this realm," the wooden duelist rasped in her sharp Cockney cadence, inspecting two warm sunstones tucked beside her needle rapier. "Never seen an acre so allergic to an honest midday sun." <!-- L0084-L0086:alfie -->

At the iron range, Pierre adjusted his tilted woolen beret with flour-dusted fingers, the round lenses of his wire spectacles fogged with sweet, alcoholic steam. "In Paris, Monsieur Mike, breakfast is the final bulwark of civilized humanity," Pierre declared, flipping another gossamer-thin pastry with theatrical precision. "Out back, I sought to construct a proper stone blind. Three whole weeks without a single carved archway or lintel! Only raw pine and soggy loam. If one does not practice masonry, the fingers rot into bourgeois uselessness." <!-- L0088-L0103:pierre -->

At the far end of the trestle table, Professor Edward Dravin sat surrounded by yellowed ledger folios and classical fragments. Flanking his inkstand was the heavy bronze rim of the Spartan hoplite shield recovered from the museum heist. His ink-stained fingers tapped his leather journal, where margin annotations detailed the lethal inversion of necrotic words. <!-- L0108-L0115 -->

"The provenance of threshold relics demands precision, Pierre," Dravin remarked mildly, tracing the hammered bronze lip. "A single phonetic shift—altering *spare* to *spore*—reverses the vital tether. Any foe pressing a bloodied ally would find drifting necrotic rot choking their throat before the blow could fall." <!-- L0116-L0120:dravin -->

Pierre merely shook his head with mild amusement at the grim discourse, sliding a finished rum crepe onto a tin plate and setting it before the scholar before turning back to his skillet.
