## CHAPTER 27: BAGUETTES AND BASILISKS

Professor Dravin stepped back from the slumbering Gordon sprawled across the linoleum, gesturing for the others to fall back. Across the gallery, the third Gordon leveled his pneumatic pistol down the corridor, firing an Editorial Gaze at Nincy. "Alfie, can you edit his spell into an editorial maze?" Pierre shouted, ducking behind the archaeology cart. <!-- L0552-L0554 -->

"Can I edit an enemy's spell?" Alfie's coral eyes flashed with inspiration. "You are so right, mate! I wonder if an editorial glaze would turn him into a sticky donut!" <!-- L0555-L0576 -->

Spotting a reinforced doorway marked with an illuminated *ROOF ACCESS* sign, Alfie scurried onto the metal stair treads, coiling the bright orange extension cord over his shoulder like a seasoned firefighter. "I'm heading for the roof, Pierre! You never know when you'll need to rappel in a pinch!" <!-- L0589-L0613 -->

From the reception lobby, Nincy's triumphant voice echoed through the marble rotunda over the sirens: "This is going viral! I am posting the snake video right now before I petrify!" At the security console, the surviving guard slammed the emergency lockout, sending heavy corrugated steel gates crashing down over every ground-floor exit. <!-- L0614-L0616 -->

Pierre watched Alfie ascend, but turned back toward the display case where the struck civilian attendant was stiffening into solid stone. Kneeling beside her, Pierre hoisted the petrifying woman into his arms. "Do not worry," he murmured gently. "We will find what you are meant to learn from this pilgrimage." Carrying her to the vitrine, Pierre propped her rigid form against the lifting glass so her stone hands would fall squarely across the ancient limestone tablet, before turning on his heel and sprinting for the roof. <!-- L0620-L0660 -->
