## CHAPTER 33: CHAOS RELICS & THE TWILIGHT SPOTLIGHT

Alfie unfastened the bronze clasps of her chaos belt, pulling three strange relics from the braided pouches: a bag of multicolored plastic interlocking toy bricks, an unlabeled black VHS cassette, and a slender black iron rod buzzing with blue static. <!-- L0381 -->

Her painted seashell eyes flared with mischief. "Can I strike this rod on the desk and throw up a barrier?" Alfie rasped. Without waiting, she hammered the iron rod against the mahogany blotter. A brilliant translucent barrier of crackling blue static force shot upward from the stage edge, walling off the podium from the chaotic hall. <!-- L0382-L0394:alfie -->

Pierre vaulted the railing, his woolen beret askew as he joined her behind the barrier. Alfie promptly pressed the miniature cassette against his ear. From within the magnetic spools, the brassy blast of an ancient American pop anthem echoed through the wood. Pierre recoiled in aristocratic horror. "Mon dieu! This is the exact radio music my mother strictly forbade! She called it pure vulgar propaganda!" <!-- L0399-L0403:pierre -->

"Have you ever even heard real music, Alfie?" Pierre asked in dismay. <!-- L0408:pierre -->

Alfie puffed out her sailcloth vest. "Only this tune right here, mate! First song I've ever heard in my life. It's poetry in motion! Pure genius!" <!-- L0409:alfie -->

"You raised a fortress ward just to listen to American pop in peace," Pierre sighed, shaking his head. <!-- L0411:pierre -->

At the center lectern, Professor Edward Dravin raised a silver pendulum toward the high rafters, surveying the battlefield where the crackling woman levitated in the center aisle. "Stand firm," Dravin commanded in measured cadence. "*Moonbeam.*" A pillar of silvery moonlight crashed down like a theatrical spotlight, engulfing the floating woman and vaporizing the ink blots in a hiss of celestial heat. <!-- L0417-L0426:dravin -->

The woman shrieked, shielding her eyes as the searing lunar brilliance broke her suppression field, allowing the trapped students to flee. Dravin channeled Persephone's threshold mantle, bathing the stage party in starlit violet warmth that steadied their racing pulses. <!-- L0429-L0442 -->

Pierre blinked as the celestial sheen settled over his sleeves. "My coat is glowing," he murmured. "I suddenly feel remarkably confident, though I cannot say why." <!-- L0448-L0451:pierre -->

"A sturdy threshold ward," Dravin noted, nodding toward the trembling Dr. Thorne. But before the scholar could step back, cloven hooves hammered across the orchestra tiles.
