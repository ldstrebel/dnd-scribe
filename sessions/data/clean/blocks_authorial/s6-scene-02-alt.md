## CHAPTER 33: CHAOS RELICS & THE TWILIGHT SPOTLIGHT

Alfie unfastened the bronze clasps of her chaos belt, pulling three strange relics from the braided pouches: a tiny bag of multicolored plastic toy bricks, an unlabeled black VHS cassette, and a slender black iron rod buzzing with blue static. <!-- L0381 -->

Her painted seashell button eyes flared with mischievous resolve. "I don't know what this does, but I guess I'll just hit it!" Alfie rasped. She hammered the iron rod straight against the polished mahogany blotter. A sharp crackle of blue static flared across the wood, and a brilliant translucent barrier of crackling force shot upward from the stage edge, walling off the podium from the chaotic hall. Alfie nodded with deep, smug contentment. <!-- L0382-L0394:alfie -->

Pierre vaulted the railing, his woolen beret askew as he joined her behind the barrier. Alfie promptly pressed the miniature cassette against his ear. From within the magnetic spools, the brassy blast of an ancient American pop anthem echoed through the wood. Pierre recoiled in aristocratic horror. "Mon dieu! This is the exact radio music my mother strictly forbade in our household! She called it pure vulgar propaganda!" <!-- L0399-L0403:pierre -->

"Have you ever even heard real music before, Alfie?" Pierre asked in profound dismay. <!-- L0408:pierre -->

Alfie puffed out her sailcloth vest. "Only this tune right here, mate! First song I've ever heard in my whole life. It's poetry in motion! Pure genius!" <!-- L0409:alfie -->

"You raised a fortress ward just to listen to American pop in peace," Pierre sighed, shaking his head. <!-- L0411:pierre -->

At the center lectern, Professor Edward Dravin adjusted his wire-rimmed spectacles with trembling fingers. He had never studied sorcery; he was a historian of classical antiquity who treated myth as text, not artillery. He sought a stabilizing reference point amid the chaos and lifted his tarnished silver pocketwatch toward the high auditorium rafters. The ancient timepiece ticked with a heavy, resonant heartbeat. A blinding cylinder of pale moonlight crashed down from the vaulted ceiling like a theatrical spotlight, squarely engulfing the transformed woman in row two. The searing beam instantly vaporized the residual ink blots on the floor, while Dravin stared upward in stunned academic disbelief at what his gesture had wrought. <!-- L0417-L0431:dravin -->

The woman shrieked, shielding her slitted eyes against the lunar brilliance as the searing light scorched her crackling coils. A soft violet twilight expanded outward across the stage floorboards from Dravin's ancient signet ring, bathing the podium in cool, steadying warmth. Pierre noticed that while the searing pillar flayed the hostile entities in the aisle, the perimeter of the light neatly bordered the stage apron, leaving him untouched and coating his woolen sleeves in a soothing celestial sheen. <!-- L0436-L0451:pierre -->

"A sturdy threshold ward," Dravin murmured, adjusting his spectacles as he checked on the trembling Dr. Thorne. But before the professor could retreat behind the desk, cloven hooves hammered across the orchestra pit. The satyr vanguard vaulted the stage steps, roared, and tackled Dravin to the oak floor, claws digging into tweed as the beast dragged him toward the open planar rift. <!-- L0462-L0478:dravin -->
