## PROLOGUE: NEW LANDS

For eighty years, the maps of Harmony had stood still. <!-- L0004 -->

It was not for lack of trying. For ten centuries, Harmony had expanded by momentum: every generation had charted a new resonant node, brought an independent province into the fold, and swelled the collective power of the continent through mutual connection. Discovery had not merely been an ambition—it was the steady pulse that kept civilization moving. Never before in recorded history had the empire found *nothing* for such an unnatural span. To an empire built on forward motion, eighty years of continuous, stubborn effort without a single new horizon was a drought right on the edge of too long. <!-- L0032 --> <!-- L0040 --> <!-- L0042 --> <!-- L0044 --> <!-- L0048 --> <!-- L0050 -->

The open ocean had always been deadly. Beyond the safety of Harmony's resonant shelf lay a perpetual, choking fog that hung heavy over the waves—an untamed expanse teeming with predatory aether beasts and wild magnetic turbulence that scrambled brass compasses and blinded navigators. For eight decades, every exploratory flotilla sent into that shroud had returned battered, scarred, and empty-handed. The few desolate shoals they managed to chart offered no fertile soil and no harmonic resonance to nourish the grid. The imperial charts remained locked behind glass, their borders fixed, their margins waiting for an answer. <!-- L0052 --> <!-- L0056 --> <!-- L0058 --> <!-- L0062 --> <!-- L0073 --> <!-- L0078 --> <!-- L0084 --> <!-- L0090 --> <!-- L0093 -->
