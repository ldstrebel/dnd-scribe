From the black water, oily ink-blot minions rose like liquid shadows, their gelatinous claws reaching over the gunwales. <!-- L0290 -->

<!-- L0310:pierre -->

The boatman gave Pierre a flat, unamused look from beneath his dark cowl. Naomi's distant cousin did not get paid enough for Wonka songs, but the oily ink splashed against the side of Pierre's leather boots, marking the threshold. The first slice of the Hunter clock filled with black ink. <!-- L0340 -->

<!-- L0390:dravin -->

## Chapter 53: The Storm Drain & The Sharpie Inscription

Directly across the asphalt rose the brick facade of the Metropolitan Police precinct. <!-- L0490 -->

Dravin unrolled the gray-web blueprints across the dumpster rim, his scholar's eyes narrowing at the technical anomalies: upper floors shifted three feet westward, and stairwells that terminated in solid load-bearing brick. <!-- L0530 -->

<!-- L0580:alfie -->
“The drainage clear-out pipe under the basement is only three inches wide,” Alfie reported, wiping soot from her wooden joints. “I checked. I can fit my arms through, but not the crowbar.”

<!-- L0620:dravin -->
“Which is why we aren't crawling through the sewer, Alfie,” Dravin said, uncapping a heavy black Sharpie with an audible click. “Hold still. On the bottom of your wooden foot, I am writing: Property of Professor Edward Dravin, Stanford University, and my telephone extension. If you are confiscated, you are cataloged property, not discarded rubbish.”
