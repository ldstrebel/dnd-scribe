## CHAPTER 23: THE MAINTENANCE SHED THRESHOLD

At the edge of The Margin's sanctuary grounds, the heavy wooden door of Theodore's maintenance shed stood ajar, humming with latent planar resonance. <!-- L0327 -->

"The tablet in North Carolina," Naomi had instructed during the morning briefing, pointing to the hand-drawn timeline ledger. "A fragmented Greek stele, excavated three months ago. The museum curators have no idea what they hold, but its severed inscription is destabilizing the surrounding timeline. You go through the shed, take the Lost Roads, and recover it." <!-- L0330 -->

"The North Carolina Museum of Natural History," Alfie chirped from Pierre's shoulder, tapping his needle rapier against his driftwood hip. "A world-famous place for dead fossils, apparently." <!-- L0333 -->

Pierre adjusted his woolen beret and gripped his canvas rucksack, stepping across the threshold first. "I will take the lead." <!-- L0343 -->

The moment Pierre crossed the doorframe, reality warped. The humble wooden shed stretched outward into an impossible expanse. Dust motes hung suspended like stars. Weathered wooden shelves expanded into towering aisles filled with ancient stone chisels, bronze mining picks, and massive iron ship anchors forged with archaic Hellenic hallmarks. Pierre's fingers brushed a limestone mallet, a sudden surge of instinctive memory flaring in his veins. <!-- L0358 -->

"What are the chances my mother intended for me to find my Gorgon lineage in a North Carolina museum?" Pierre murmured softly, navigating the shifting aisles with unyielding confidence. "It is strange... but meeting the Fates and stepping into the unknown feels like a pilgrimage in its own way." <!-- L0383 -->

The tool racks dissolved into an endless brick-and-steel warehouse, its high catwalks lost in shadow. <!-- L0389 -->

Beyond the warehouse, the metal beams gave way to bombed-out stone arches, opening into the silent ruins of an ancient classical colonnade. Prof. Edward Dravin stepped into the ruined hall, his heart leaping with scholarly wonder. Walking through physical layers of severed history was everything a Stanford historian had ever dreamed of. <!-- L0395 -->

Ahead, a solitary figure walked gracefully among the broken pillars—a youthful woman draped in woven gossamer robes that shimmered between winter pale and spring gold. <!-- L0410 -->

Dravin pulled a leather-bound notebook and fountain pen from his tweed coat with academic urgency. "Excuse me... greetings! We are travelers from The Margin." <!-- L0418 -->

The maiden turned, her eyes widening with serene shock. "The Margin? I have heard of this haven on the fringes of reality. The last time I encountered a traveler from those borders, it was a man named Theodore... Teddy, seeking fragments across time." <!-- L0424 -->

"Teddy!" Dravin beamed, scribbling furiously into his notebook. "You know him!" <!-- L0450 -->

"I do," the maiden answered softly. "And my mistress takes a very keen interest in what you seek." <!-- L0460 -->

"And the display pedestal is protected by active magnetic contact sensors," Dravin observed, pointing to the copper wiring running under the velvet base. "We cannot simply lift it without triggering building security." <!-- L0700 -->
