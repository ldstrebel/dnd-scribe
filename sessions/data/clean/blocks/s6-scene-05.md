## CHAPTER 36: ELECTRIFIED SHALLOWS & THE SHATTERED JAVELIN

Professor Dravin stood beside the podium, reached into his tweed pocket, and withdrew a small, silver-rimmed compact mirror. With solemn academic precision, he held the looking-glass up before his cheek, sighting the paralyzed Medusa over his right shoulder. <!-- L0768 --> <!-- L0770 -->

"I have no interest in dealing mortal harm," Dravin announced evenly. "The objective is empirical restraint." <!-- L0774 -->

He squeezed the pneumatic trigger. A sharp pneumatic hiss echoed across the stage. The heavy petrifying cartridge streaked through the air, embedding itself directly into the woman's shoulder. The projectile dissolved on impact into gray neural paste, seeping through her pores and sending rapid pulses of calcifying resonance through her nervous system. <!-- L0786 --> <!-- L0788 -->

Dravin swept his left hand in a decisive arc, shifting the vertical column of *Moonbeam* across the orchestra pit to envelop her thrashing coils. Silvery lunar fire engulfed the Medusa once more, searing her flesh with blistering radiant punishment. <!-- L0792 --> <!-- L0797 -->

Behind them, the satyr vanguard scrambled back to its cloven hooves with a guttural roar. The horned beast shook off Dravin's throw, lowered its heavy ram skull, and charged straight into Eusacles. The brutal impact crashed into Eusacles's ribs with punishing concussive force. <!-- L0803 -->

Eusacles grunted, absorbing the blow with a cynical scowl beneath his aviator sunglasses. He cocked his arm and swung *Good Vibes* in a savage counter-arc, but the agile fiend twisted beneath the spiked head, hissing as the heavy iron whistled past its ear. <!-- L0807 --> <!-- L0811 -->

A sudden, violent crack echoed from the center aisle. With a shriek of strained tendons, the transformed woman shattered Pierre's mental paralysis. She vaulted over the raked benches and landed heavily upon the stage, an oppressive, suffocating aura washing outward from her brow that locked the party's secondary impulses and severed quick tactical reactions. <!-- L0827 --> <!-- L0831 -->

The woman hissed like a nest of vipers, lashing her electric hair tendrils toward Dravin's chest. But the blinding lunar glare still burned her eyes, and the crackling coils lashed harmlessly into the oak floorboards. <!-- L0833 -->

All around them, the emergency sprinkler system continued its unrelenting deluge. Cold water cascaded from ceiling nozzles, rapidly pooling across the floorboards and running into deep shallow puddles around the stage steps. Stray arcs of blue electricity from the woman's tendrils snaked through the pooling water, setting the surface hissing with lethal voltage. Near the green room portal, the dimensional tear widened, and two more bloated black ink blots plopped into the shallows. <!-- L0834 --> <!-- L0836 -->

Pierre tightened his grip on his bronze javelin, spotting a satyr circling the stage perimeter toward Alfie's desk.

"Excuse me!" Pierre called out, striding through the shallow water with haughty indignation. "I was not finished addressing you! Why are you walking away from me? This is abominably rude! You have left me with no alternative but to pierce you! Taste the wrath of an authentic antiquities piece!" <!-- L0863 -->

Pierre thrust the bronze head forward with all the leverage of his Parisian fencing training. The museum weapon struck the satyr's shoulder with fierce, devastating momentum. <!-- L0874 --> <!-- L0878 --> <!-- L0884 -->

*CRACK.*

The ancient, brittle bronze shaft splintered on impact, shearing clean off beneath the ferrule. The blunt museum metal bent uselessly against the beast's coarse hide, leaving Pierre holding nothing but a broken wooden rod. <!-- L0888 -->

Pierre blinked down at the jagged stump in his hand, then glared at the enraged creature as its whips flailed in the spray.

The severed tendrils flailed about in blind fury, hissing with spurts of violent static electricity. <!-- L0892 --> <!-- L0894 -->

"You know, this is precisely what occurs when one fails to apply conditioner every evening," Pierre observed with clinical disdain, waving the broken stick at her writhing head. "They become entirely unruly! You should seriously consider adding some protein to your grooming regimen!" <!-- L0895 -->

The woman unleashed a bloodcurdling shriek of primal fury. <!-- L0896 -->

Crouched beside the podium, Dr. Aris Thorne pressed both hands to her face, her eyes wide with uncomprehending horror as the impossible nightmare unfolded before her. <!-- L0900 -->
