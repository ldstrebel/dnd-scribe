## CHAPTER 45: RETURN TO THE MARGIN & THE MENDED SPATULA

The morning sun filtered through the canopy of ancient oaks as the party guided Susanna down the final gravel switchback toward the clearing of the Margin. The sanctuary cabins rested peacefully against the forested ridge, woodsmoke curling lazily from the central stone chimney.

Susanna walked close beside Pierre, her hands tucked into the oversized sleeves of Dravin's spare tweed jacket. "There were a few odd-looking people on the road," she murmured, gazing around the misty lawn. "People who looked like they had shifting ink tattoos all over their skin. They didn't seem friendly. They gave me a creepy look, checked me over, and then kept on jogging through the woods when they realized I wasn't who they were looking for." <!-- L0586 -->

"I have to say, I am a little disappointed that the inky villains are able to use these roads," Pierre said, brushing road dust from his coat. "Stay away from Naomi, by the way. She has a great deal of questions." <!-- L0590 -->

"Sounds good," Susanna said with a weary smile. "This is the first time I've seen civilization in weeks. Thank you." <!-- L0591 -->

As they reached the gravel turnaround, the cabin screen door banged open. Mike strolled out onto the wide cedar porch in his faded flannel, wiping his flour-dusted hands on an apron. "Oh, sweet! You guys are back right on time. I could really go for some pancakes." <!-- L0595 -->

Pierre rushed up the wooden steps, pulling the jagged, fractured halves of his bronze javelin head from his rucksack. "Mike, look! I need help fixing my spatula. Our flipping technique is ruined!" <!-- L0599 -->

Mike dropped his spatula to his side, his face falling into genuine despair. "Oh, no, bro! What are we going to do?" <!-- L0602 -->

"I know, it is devastating!" Pierre cried, holding up the snapped bronze blades. "We had just mastered the technique. We will have to start all over!" <!-- L0603 -->

"Going to have to do it the way I used to," Mike sighed mournfully. "We can't do it the new way." <!-- L0604 -->

Eusacles rolled his eyes, pushing past them into the porch light. "Nah, I got this. Come here." He snatched the two severed pieces of bronze from Pierre's hand, pressing the jagged fracture lines together with his thick calloused palms. Eusacles closed his eyes, muttering a quick prayer under his breath. A faint golden shimmer rippled across the metal, and with a soft chime of fusing bronze, the fracture vanished. "God, I hope this works." <!-- L0612 -->

Mike let out a low whistle, eyes wide. "Oh, fuck!" <!-- L0613 -->

"Thank you very much, Eusacles!" Pierre exclaimed, examining the seamless weld with delight. <!-- L0618 -->

"Ah yeah," Mike grinned, clapping Pierre on the shoulder. "We can go back to doing our two-man flip now." <!-- L0620 -->

"I really want those pancakes," Eusacles muttered, tossing Dravin's satchel of freshly picked berries onto the porch table. <!-- L0623 -->

The screen door creaked again, and Teddy emerged into the morning light, leaning heavily on his carved cedar cane. His silver-streaked hair fell over his collar, his sharp eyes taking in their soot-stained clothing and Dravin's silent exhaustion. "So. You all saw a second artifact. Were you able to capture it, or experience it? What happened?" <!-- L0626 -->

"Here's the thing, right?" Alfie piped up, perching on the porch railing and smoothing his maroon soccer scarf. "We went to university. University! Place so nice they named it twice. We went to a lecture about the artifact, touched it, and it was intense—just like the first time. Then a rift ripped open in the hall, and out came a lady with crazy hair and some inky guys. Chaos ensued." <!-- L0633 -->

"We did capture one this time, and we did have questions," Eusacles added, leaning against the timber post. <!-- L0635 -->

Alfie reached into his pocket and handed over the coiled, rubbery length of dark matter severed in the quad. "We sliced off a hair." <!-- L0638 -->

Teddy took the cold specimen with a furrowed brow, turning it under the sunlight. "Hair? I would not have called it that. I would call this some sort of synapse, or a brain nerve. Gross." <!-- L0644 -->

From the open kitchen window above the porch, Pierre stuck his head out, clutching a mixing bowl. "Teddy, I am curious... in American television, when a police officer terminates someone in the line of duty, they have mandatory psychological counseling. We don't happen to have anything like that here, do we?" <!-- L0646 -->

Teddy stared up at Pierre in quiet surprise. "I don't believe anyone has ever killed one of the creatures. It has simply been a choice to touch and run, for lack of a better term." <!-- L0647 -->

Alfie crossed his wooden arms defensively. "Listen, mate. She called me a doll. You can only deal with so much, you know." <!-- L0651 -->

"That does sound bad," Teddy said gently, nodding toward the figurine. "I have been told that you prefer action figure, and that is how I refer to you." <!-- L0654 -->

"That's right," Alfie replied with proud satisfaction. "That's a term of respect, that one." <!-- L0657 -->

Eusacles crossed his arms, looking down at Teddy. "We asked some questions, and the creature said her boss is rewriting history to save people and create a more perfect world. And now that we're collecting artifacts, the boss is going to target us personally instead of the artifacts. And there was another monster stalking us across the quad—a woman's shape with no face, but dozens of faces shifting under the skin, and six fingers on each hand." <!-- L0663 -->

Teddy's hand tightened around the head of his cane until his knuckles turned chalk-white. His gaze drifted off into the misty forest, haunted and distant. "Unfortunately, I have seen it. And I know how frightening that can be." <!-- L0667 -->

"Early on at the Margin—it was 'The Margins' with two N's on the sign at the time, before one fell off—it happened after my second touch of a fragment," Teddy whispered, his voice trembling slightly. "It set off a spiral whenever I tried to pursue other leads. There was this creature that would be there, hunting me anytime I left this place." <!-- L0671 -->

Teddy looked directly at Eusacles and Alfie, his face pale. "You are the only others I have ever met who have touched two fragments." <!-- L0676 -->

"I have never left the Margin since," Teddy continued quietly. "I left a few times to attempt to chase down a third fragment, but I would always witness this creature. I found a way to hide, sneaking back through the Lost Roads or the Hermes Express." <!-- L0680 -->

"After a few attempts, I decided it was too dangerous," he added. "So I have stayed here to cultivate the talent of those who arrive. We haven't had anyone else reach two fragments before disappearing. I am sorry I didn't warn you earlier. I simply was not sure anyone else would survive two." <!-- L0681 -->

A sudden shout from across the grass broke the somber mood. Across the yard, Naomi had already cornered Susanna against the pump house, brandishing a thick legal pad and firing rapid-fire questions as the poor museum attendant blinked in bewildered exhaustion.

Pierre hurried out the cabin door carrying a towering platter of golden pancakes, fresh berry sauce steaming in the morning chill. "I have questions for you, Teddy, but I also do not want to leave Susanna to the Naomi questionnaire! I will be back!" <!-- L0700 -->
