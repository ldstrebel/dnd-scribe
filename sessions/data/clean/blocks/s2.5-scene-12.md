## CHAPTER 27: THE AMBER CORE & NIGHT EXPERIMENTS

The professor placed a warm, heavy claw upon Iggy's shoulder, gently guiding him toward the office exit. "The night deepens, my young scholar, and the hour grows late. You should return to the bonfire before your companions begin searching for you." <!-- L1226 --> <!-- L1230 --> <!-- L1240 -->

He paused beside a locked oak cabinet, producing a small brass key. From within a velvet-lined drawer, he drew forth a raw, unpolished shard of deep golden amber roughly the size of a walnut. It hummed with a faint, internal vibration that tickled Iggy's calloused palm as the professor pressed it into his hand. <!-- L1250 --> <!-- L1260 --> <!-- L1270 -->

"An unrefined resonator crystal," the professor said softly. "The heart of every battery on this campus. Study it. The others in this institution are content to twist wrenches and log inventory; they do not possess the hunger to understand the marrow of the craft. If you ever find yourself wandering after hours, my door remains unlocked." <!-- L1280 --> <!-- L1290 --> <!-- L1291 --> <!-- L1292 -->

"It isn't a battery at all," Iggy whispered, turning the translucent crystal over in his dirt-caked fingers. "It's a surge capacitor. It doesn't hoard the power; it balances the rhythm." <!-- L1293 --> <!-- L1294 --> <!-- L1295 --> <!-- L1301 -->

"Precisely," the professor smiled, tipping the brim of his silk hat. "Go now. Mind the flagstones." <!-- L1302 --> <!-- L1304 -->

Iggy slipped out into the cool night courtyard. The bonfire at Block 99 had burned down to a mound of glowing crimson coals, surrounded by sleepy wisps of smoke. Sitting down on a low basalt retaining wall beneath the shadow of a construction scaffold, Iggy opened his notebook to a fresh page. His mind was racing. A lifetime of council dogma—that safety lay in quiet, stony isolation—had collided head-on with the reality of a great metropolis struggling to keep its hearths lit. <!-- L1305 --> <!-- L1308 --> <!-- L1309 --> <!-- L1314 -->

Beneath the faint amber glow of the crystal, his pencil flew across the parchment. He jotted down five distinct hypotheses—five micro-experiments he could run over the coming days to test whether harmonic resonance truly strengthened when people forged genuine connection. <!-- L1315 --> <!-- L1318 --> <!-- L1322 --> <!-- L1323 --> <!-- L1325 --> <!-- L1326 -->

A burst of familiar laughter drifted across the courtyard. Looking up from his sketches, Iggy saw Ignatius, Lomi, Aggie, and Britt strolling in a loose, comfortable formation across the torchlit concourse. They were heading west toward the upper terraces where the music from the student lounges floated on the midnight breeze. <!-- L1327 --> <!-- L1329 -->

Iggy blew the graphite dust from his notebook, snapped the leather strap shut, and carefully nestled the warm amber crystal into his deepest coat pocket. Sliding off the wall, he trotted across the courtyard, his boots tapping a steady, eager rhythm against the stones as he moved to rejoin his squad.
