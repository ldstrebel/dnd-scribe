## CHAPTER 26: THE CLAN EXCHANGE

Iggy shifted his weight from foot to foot, his small clay hands clenching into fists deep inside his pockets. "If knowing where you come from and building connection is so important... why would people be forced to forget?" <!-- L1127 --> <!-- L1132 --> <!-- L1133 -->

The professor grabbed his leather-bound atlas, dragged it across the mahogany desk, and lunged forward onto his cane. "What do you mean?" <!-- L1140 -->

"Back where I'm from," Iggy muttered, hesitating as if speaking forbidden words, "there's this thing. The Exchange. Every few years, between the deep clans... people are traded. You get sent to a new clan, and you have to let go of the old one. You're not supposed to remember where you started." <!-- L1142 --> <!-- L1144 --> <!-- L1146 --> <!-- L1151 --> <!-- L1154 --> <!-- L1157 --> <!-- L1160 -->

The professor gasped, his leathery jaw dropping. "The Exchange? A forced severed connection? In all my years of cultural archaeology, I have never documented such a practice within the territories! To deliberately sever the cord of ancestral knowledge... it causes me physical pain merely to contemplate it!" He tapped his cane against the floorboards with sharp agitation. "How can you defend against the dark if you amputate your own history?" <!-- L1170 --> <!-- L1179 --> <!-- L1180 --> <!-- L1182 --> <!-- L1204 -->

"Defend against what?" Iggy asked quietly. <!-- L1205 -->

The tortle leaped to the window, pushed the heavy iron shutter open, and grabbed the brass telescope. "The Aether beasts," he replied somberly. "When the Global Amplitude began to decay thirty years ago, the wards along the continental frontier weakened. The incursions along the border began anew. No clan—no matter how deep their caverns or how high their towers—could ever survive an assault alone. It was the iron of the Sinter, the chime-spiders of the Gilded, and the life-soil of the eastern valleys joined together that held the perimeter. To imagine that a people survives by isolating and erasing themselves... it breaks my heart, child. If you ever find your way back to your clan, you must tell them that isolation is not safety. It is merely slow starvation." <!-- L1210 --> <!-- L1218 --> <!-- L1219 --> <!-- L1225 -->
