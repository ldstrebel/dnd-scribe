## CHAPTER 33: CHAOS RELICS & THE TWILIGHT SPOTLIGHT

Alfie unfastened the bronze clasps of the chaos belt, pulling three strange objects from the braided pouches in rapid succession. In her miniature driftwood hands, she held a tiny plastic bag filled with sharp multicolored interlocking toy bricks, a miniature unlabeled black VHS tape, and a slender black iron rod that hummed with vibrant, crackling static electricity. <!-- L0381 -->

Her seashell eyes lit up. "Can I strike this rod on the desk," Alfie rasped, hopping onto the edge of the mahogany blotter, "and throw up some sort of magical barrier between the podium and the rest of the hall?" <!-- L0382 --> <!-- L0384 -->

Without waiting for permission, she swung the miniature iron rod overhead and brought it down hard against the polished timber desk. A sharp crackle of blue static flared across the wood. With a blinding sizzle, a shimmering translucent barrier of crackling blue static force shot upward from the edge of the stage, arcing overhead to wall off the rostrum from the chaos of the auditorium. <!-- L0390 --> <!-- L0392 --> <!-- L0394 -->

Pierre vaulted the side railing, landing lightly on the stage floorboards just inside the glowing perimeter. He glanced at the miniature items scattered across the desktop, adjusting his beret. "Alfie, that was very impressive," he murmured, his gaze flicking toward the dark dimensional tear by the green room. "I wonder what will emerge next." <!-- L0399 -->

Alfie held up the miniature VHS cassette, pressing it against Pierre's left ear with a wide, toothy carved grin. "Listen to this first!" she demanded. <!-- L0401 -->

From within the magnetic spools of the plastic tape, the muffled, tinny brass of an ancient pop anthem filtered through the wood. Pierre's expression curdled into instant horror.

"Mon dieu," Pierre gasped, pulling his head back. "This is the exact American radio music my mother strictly forbade in our household! She told me it was pure vulgar propaganda!" <!-- L0403 -->

"Have you ever even heard real music before, Alfie?" Pierre asked, adjusting his wire spectacles in bewilderment. <!-- L0408 -->

"Only this song right here," Alfie replied proudly, puffing out her sailcloth vest. "First tune I've ever heard in my whole life, mate. It's poetry in motion! Pure genius!" <!-- L0409 -->

"You put up a magical fortress ward just so you could listen to American pop in peace," Pierre sighed, shaking his head. <!-- L0411 -->

At the center podium, Professor Edward Dravin adjusted his spectacles and took in the battlefield. Panicked students were pouring out the doors, while the crackling woman in row two levitated three feet above the aisle, her hissing tendrils searching for a gap in the static ward. Dr. Thorne huddled behind the desk, clutching her briefcase in terror. <!-- L0417 -->

Dravin drew a tarnished silver pendulum from his tweed vest, extending his arm toward the vaulted plaster ceiling. "Stand firm," the professor commanded in his measured Stanford cadence. "*Moonbeam.*" <!-- L0422 -->

A pillar of concentrated, silvery-white moonlight descended from the darkness of the high auditorium ceiling like a theatrical spotlight. The brilliant cylinder crashed down squarely upon the second row, engulfing the floating woman in blinding celestial radiance. The remaining ink blot caught in the column vaporized instantly, dissolving into a puff of soot. <!-- L0426 -->

The transformed woman hissed, shielding her slitted eyes against the searing brilliance as the radiant beam scorched her coils. Around her, the dazed haze clouding the nearby students broke; feeling the sudden heat of the spotlight, the trapped audience members gasped, shook off their stupor, and scrambled toward the rear exits with renewed terror. <!-- L0429 --> <!-- L0431 -->

Dravin lowered his hand, his silver signet ring flaring with soft violet twilight. "Persephone grants threshold sanctuary," he intoned, channeling his divine mantle. <!-- L0436 -->

A dome of soothing twilight expanded outward from his feet, washing across the stage floorboards in a calm, starlit glow. The cool radiance enveloped Dravin, Pierre, Alfie, and the trembling Dr. Thorne, knitting flesh and soothing frayed nerves with an unyielding mantle of starlit resilience. <!-- L0442 -->

Pierre looked down at his own forearms, where a soft celestial sheen played over his woolen sleeves. "My coat is glowing," Pierre murmured, blinking. "I suddenly feel remarkably confident, though I cannot say why." <!-- L0448 --> <!-- L0451 -->

"A sturdy threshold ward," Dravin noted, nodding curtly toward Dr. Thorne as the starlit glow wrapped around her shoulders. "A resolute sanctuary for everyone on this platform." <!-- L0462 --> <!-- L0464 -->

The soothing twilight had barely settled when the heavy clatter of hooves echoed across the lower orchestra pit. The satyr vanguard at the base of the stairs broke into a violent charge, ignoring the fleeing students. The horned brute vaulted the side stairs, lowered its ram-horned head, and hurtled straight onto the stage through the side wing. <!-- L0466 --> <!-- L0468 -->

With a feral roar, the beast slammed into Professor Dravin, knocking the scholar backward onto the polished oak floorboards. Claws dug into tweed as the satyr pinned Dravin's arms, dragging him bodily toward the open dimensional rift. <!-- L0469 --> <!-- L0471 --> <!-- L0478 -->
