## CHAPTER 5: SUNDER AND SLUDGE

Before they could celebrate, the larger horned beast bounded past Eusacles with terrifying speed. Lowering its ridged horns, it charged directly at Eusacles's ribs. <!-- L1235 -->

The impact hit with the force of a battering ram. The horns crushed into Eusacles's side, lifting him off his feet and slamming him into the base of a pillar. Caustic black ink splattered across his chest, burning through his shirt like acid. <!-- L1240 -->

"Ugh!" Eusacles coughed, clutching his bruised ribs as black residue sizzled against his skin. <!-- L1245 -->

The wounded ink blob struggled free of the frost, sloshing forward to finish him, but its aim was wild, leaving only a puddle of acidic black sludge near Eusacles's boots. <!-- L1250 -->

Near the back of the chamber, Pierre took one look at the gore, the horns, and the glowing weapons, and threw his hands up in immediate surrender. <!-- L1260 -->

"Hello! Hello!" Pierre called out in his rapid, lilting French accent, taking slow, deliberate steps backward toward the safety of the library stacks. "I think there must be a misunderstanding or something here! I have nothing against anyone here. I am, you know, more a lover than a fighter! Before I left, my mother told me, 'Pierre, do not be hasty, do not make harsh judgments.' So I am just going to step over here, if no one minds!" <!-- L1280 -->

The horned fiend paused, turning its ridged head toward the nervous boy. Its voice hissed like boiling tar. <!-- L1300 -->

"Then come with us..." it rasped, gesturing toward the swirling black rift. "We do not need to harm you. Come with us through the rift." <!-- L1320 -->

Pierre stopped, blinking behind his thick lenses. "Will that... be taking me back to Las Vegas? Because I think I am now in ancient times and I am not exactly sure." <!-- L1330 -->

"It will take you where you need to go..." the beast purred. <!-- L1340 -->

"Yes, okay, that sounds very pleasant," Pierre said politely, nodding with profound earnestness. He took another step backward, hooking his thumbs under the straps of his rucksack. "Fates—whom I definitely do not know because I know nothing about anything—I'm just going to go this way instead." <!-- L1345 -->

Without waiting for a reply, Pierre pivoted and ducked cleanly behind a colossal bookshelf, disengaging entirely from the combat. <!-- L1349 -->
