## CHAPTER 5: SUNDER AND SLUDGE

Before they could catch their breath, the larger horned beast bounded past the shattered flagstones with terrifying speed. The creature lowered its ridged horns and charged directly at Eusacles's ribs. <!-- L1235 -->

The impact hit with the force of an iron battering ram. Horns slammed into Eusacles's torso, lifting him off his boots and hurling him violently into the fluted base of a marble pillar. Caustic black bile splattered across his chest, hissing as it ate through his cotton shirt. <!-- L1240 -->

"Ugh!" Eusacles coughed, his knees buckling as he clutched his bruised ribs against the stone. Black residue sizzled against his skin, smelling of acrid pitch and scorched earth. <!-- L1245 -->

The horned fiend planted its cloven hooves and lowered its head for a finishing gore, but the follow-up strike scraped wild against the fluted marble, gouging deep trenches into the stone near Eusacles's boots. <!-- L1250 -->

Professor Edward Dravin flinched behind the nearest colonnade, clutching his antique ledger against his chest. His spectacles slid down the bridge of his nose, his breathing shallow and rapid as he watched the towering monstrosity corner the fallen traveler.

Near the back of the chamber, Pierre took one look at the gore, the horns, and the steaming black bile, and threw both hands into the air in immediate surrender. <!-- L1260 -->

He took slow, deliberate steps backward toward the safety of the towering library stacks. "Hello! Hello!" Pierre called out in his rapid, lilting French accent. "I think there must be a misunderstanding or something here! I have nothing against anyone here. I am, you know, more a lover than a fighter! Before I left, my mother told me, 'Pierre, do not be hasty, do not make harsh judgments.' So I am just going to step over here, if no one minds!" <!-- L1280 -->

The horned fiend paused, turning its ridged, ink-dripping skull toward the trembling boy. Its voice hissed like boiling tar over coals. <!-- L1300 -->

"Then come with us..." it rasped, its clawed hand gesturing toward the swirling black rift. "We do not need to harm you. Come with us through the rift." <!-- L1320 -->

Pierre stopped, blinking behind his thick lenses as he considered the pitch-black portal. "Will that... be taking me back to Las Vegas? Because I think I am now in ancient times and I am not exactly sure." <!-- L1330 -->

"It will take you where you need to go..." the beast purred, venom dripping from its lips. <!-- L1340 -->

"Yes, okay, that sounds very pleasant," Pierre replied politely, nodding with profound earnestness. He hooked his thumbs beneath the worn straps of his rucksack and took another measured step into the shadows. "Fates—whom I definitely do not know because I know nothing about anything—I'm just going to go this way instead." <!-- L1345 -->

Without waiting for a reply, Pierre pivoted and ducked cleanly behind a colossal mahogany bookshelf, disengaging entirely from the combat. <!-- L1349 -->
