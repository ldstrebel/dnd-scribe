## CHAPTER 6: THE DRIFTWOOD DUELIST AWAKENS

In the center of the marble floor, the limp, wooden bundle suddenly drew a sharp, violent breath. <!-- L1350 -->

Gasp. <!-- L1355 -->

The driftwood doll snapped bolt upright. Its shell-and-coral eyes clicked open, catching the ambient light of the shattered colonnade. With uncanny agility, the little figure drew its sewing-needle rapier from its canvas hip, spun the gleaming steel in a tight arc, and let out a miniature, bloodcurdling battle cry. <!-- L1360 -->

It charged straight across the flagstones, its carved joints clicking like castanets as it sprinted full tilt toward the looming horned beast, aiming its needle squarely for the creature's thick ankle. <!-- L1370 -->

The charge was valiant, but the horned fiend shifted its weight at the last fraction of a second. The steel needle struck the ridged hoof with a shower of bright sparks, skittering off harmlessly. The doll tumbled forward into a roll across the flagstones, popping back to its feet with seaweed hair whipping across its canvas brow. <!-- L1380 -->

Behind the nearest column, Professor Dravin panicked. "Stay back from it!" he shouted, fumbling frantically through his tweed pockets until his trembling fingers pulled out an antique brass bell charm. "Toll the dead!" <!-- L1400 -->

He shook the charm vigorously toward the beast, hoping against hope that the classical talisman might summon some protective omen. But whatever celestial resonance was meant to chime remained completely muffled—a thick clump of pocket lint was wedged firmly inside the bell's clapper, producing only a dull, pathetic thud. <!-- L1420 -->

Eusacles rolled his eyes from where he leaned against the pillar, his breath hissing through clenched teeth. "Oh, god no..." <!-- L1430 -->

Growling through the burning ache in his ribs, Eusacles raised his chained morningstar once more. He wound the heavy iron ball in a desperate arc and swung hard at the demon's flank. But his bruised torso flared with agony mid-strike, pulling the blow wide as the beast sidestepped the whistling spikes. <!-- L1440 -->

"Damn it! We are out here on the struggle bus!" Eusacles cursed, stumbling back against the fluted marble. <!-- L1454 -->
