## CHAPTER 26: THE SIX O'CLOCK BELL

The museum's public address system crackled to life with a polite chime: *Attention visitors, the museum will be closing in fifteen minutes. Please make your way toward the main exits.* <!-- L0730 -->

Around them, docents began gently shepherding family groups and school tours toward the central elevators. The gallery lights dimmed to a conservation baseline, casting long, dramatic shadows across the statues. <!-- L0751 -->

"Closing time," Eusacles muttered, drifting toward the shadows behind a colossal statue of Athena. "Time for the night shift." <!-- L0770 -->

A stern-looking night watchman with a heavy keyring and an electronic badge lanyarded around his neck stepped into the Aegean gallery, checking the display locks. His nametag read *THOMAS*. <!-- L0790 -->

"Excuse me, folks," Thomas announced, clapping his hands together. "Galleries are closing. Main exit is down the stairs." <!-- L0810 -->

"Of course, officer," Dravin replied smoothly, taking a slow step toward the exit while lingering near the velvet rope. "Just admiring the epigraphy one last time." <!-- L0836 -->

Alfie shifted in Pierre's pocket, whispering directly into his collar. "We need that guard's keycard, Pierre. Without that badge, we won't get through the security doors into the preservation lab." <!-- L0841 -->
