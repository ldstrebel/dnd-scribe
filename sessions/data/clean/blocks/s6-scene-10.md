## CHAPTER 41: THE JANITOR'S CART & THE ARCHITECTURAL MODELS

Dr. Aris Thorne stumbled against the mahogany edge of the podium, blinking through the receding haze as the creature's psychic field collapsed. Her knuckles turned white against the lectern rail. She stared down in unvarnished terror at the shattered rows of auditorium seats, the puddles of fire-suppressant foam, and the bound, gray-skinned woman lying motionless upon the carpet. <!-- L1441 -->

Pierre flicked a stray fleck of plaster from his lapel, his comb poised like an artist's brush. "Not a clue. She is having an exceptionally catastrophic hair day." <!-- L1442 -->

"You're... you're a talking doll!" Dr. Thorne gasped, shrinking back against the lecture desk. <!-- L1443:thorne -->

Alfie planted both small stitched boots squarely onto the brass trim of the stage apron, folding his arms across his miniature trench coat. His seashell button eyes narrowed to sharp slits. "I do not love when people call me a doll. Let's just start there." <!-- L1446 -->

Beside him, Eusacles adjusted his bronze hoplite shield with a solemn nod. "Action figure." <!-- L1450 -->

"Action figure. Exactly," Alfie affirmed, tapping the pommel of his needle rapier against his thigh. <!-- L1451 -->

Pierre looked down at the unconscious planar infiltrator, considering their options. "Perhaps we do not bring her straight back to the Margin. We tuck her away near the edge of the lost roads first." <!-- L1454 -->

He tapped his chin with the handle of his comb. "Before we expose our headquarters to someone who can slash open planar portals with her fingernails." <!-- L1456 -->

Edward Dravin gave a curt nod of concession, wiping the condensation from his spectacles as he tucked his heavy lexicon beneath his arm. He would defer to his companions' counsel. <!-- L1460 -->

"I will alternate between *Spare the Dying* and *Spore the Dying* to keep her unconscious and alive," Dravin added, ensuring the creature's volatile vital signs remained locked in deep slumber. <!-- L1464 -->

Alfie grinned, his rough linen hood bobbing. "I like the janitor cart idea." <!-- L1472 -->

In truth, Dr. Thorne was merely an ordinary academic who had stumbled into dangerous waters. She had unearthed an edited fragment of suppressed medical history—the forgotten legacy of a rogue physician—and the uncanny traction of that forbidden research had drawn predators she possessed no vocabulary to comprehend. <!-- L1475 -->

Alfie drew his curved hook, eyeing the trembling professor. "Can Alfie just full-force clock her in the side of the head with my hook and leave a note?" <!-- L1482 -->

Pierre stepped forward, holding up a placating, leather-gloved hand. "We take her to the entrance of the lost roads, tie her up, and interrogate her. If it goes wrong, we slip into the roads." <!-- L1485 -->

Pierre gestured toward Alfie's weapon. "The first thing she must see upon waking is your needle rapier pointed at her throat. That sets the tone immediately." <!-- L1486 -->

Pierre turned his gaze toward Dr. Thorne, offering a patronizing, razor-thin smile. "No one will believe you anyway, Professor. To an academic, living in a world you cannot explain is the ultimate punishment." <!-- L1490 -->

Thorne's eyes darted frantically between the companions. "What? What about the lost roads?" <!-- L1493:thorne -->

Her breath came in ragged gasps. "What if I go to the lost roads and find my way?" <!-- L1495:thorne -->

"They wanted my work!" Thorne cried, hugging her leather satchel to her chest. "I uncovered something real, and I have to get out of here!" <!-- L1499:thorne -->

Pierre inclined his head with lofty mock-sympathy. "You are very important to your own story, Madame." <!-- L1500 -->

"The big pox is returning, and without your solo research, it will be the end of days," Pierre assured her smoothly, nodding with solemn theatrical gravity. <!-- L1502 -->

Pierre leaned in closer. "I listened to your entire lecture. Nothing of actual merit was spoken outside of the vision." <!-- L1515 -->

He gave an imperious wave of his hand. "You must take all of your money, invest everything into this solitary project, and sever all ties with your colleagues. Other people are merely distractions from greatness." <!-- L1517 -->

Thorne snapped the brass clasps of her briefcase shut with a sharp click. She cast one final, terrified glance over her shoulder, bolted across the stage, vanished into the shadows of the green room, and shoved open the emergency rear exit into the rainy night. <!-- L1518 -->

Pierre adjusted his beret with immense satisfaction. "I believe we have successfully ruined her life." <!-- L1520 -->

"Never call Alfie a doll," Pierre added, nodding firmly. <!-- L1522 -->

"That is the very last time anyone makes that mistake," Alfie muttered, sheathing his hook. "Let us find that janitor's cart." <!-- L1523 -->

"Dump her right into the trash bin," Alfie directed, kicking aside a damp flyer. <!-- L1526 -->

Pierre inspected his tool belt, mourning his broken spear. "Pierre will search for replacement tools to replace my shattered bronze javelin." <!-- L1529 -->

Pierre produced the heavy iron ring of keys liberated earlier from the campus custodian, rattling them against the brass locks along the dim departmental corridor. <!-- L1530 -->

"I am not particularly skillful at institutional academia," Pierre confessed with an airy shrug, testing the brass keys in the deadbolt. "I left early, you see. I was homeschooled." <!-- L1531 -->

With an uninspired roll of four, Pierre managed only to jimmy the heavy deadbolt of the undergraduate architectural drafting studio. Instead of an armory of heavy chisels and stonemasonry mallets, the room offered merely reams of vellum, plastic triangles, steel rulers, and delicate brass drafting compasses. <!-- L1532 -->

Undeterred by the lack of heavy weaponry, Pierre hoisted Alfie up onto a wide wooden drafting table strewn with intricate balsa-wood scale models. "Alfie, climb onto these little architecture sets! Tell me, did they build the miniature proportions correctly?" <!-- L1537 -->

Alfie perched atop a miniature rooftop, surveying the tiny balsa metropolis with awe. "This is incredible." <!-- L1538 -->

"Amazing," Alfie breathed, admiring the dollhouse-sized balconies. <!-- L1540 -->

Alfie hopped down from the tabletop and wheeled the squeaking gray janitor cart toward the threshold, giving the signal. "All right, back to the lost road then with our hostage." <!-- L1542 -->
