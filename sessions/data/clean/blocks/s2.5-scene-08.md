## CHAPTER 23: THE TORTOISE IN THE TOP HAT

"Hello," a slow, deliberate voice rumbled from the office doorway. "I have been watching you for quite a little while. What have you learned?" <!-- L0761 --> <!-- L0765 --> <!-- L0770 -->

Iggy started, charcoal slipping from his stubby fingers. He spun around, pulling his arms in close against his coat. <!-- L0775 -->

Standing in the frame of the doorway was an elderly, distinguished tortle. He leaned with dignified grace upon a polished hickory cane with a carved brass grip. Upon his broad, wrinkled head sat a jaunty, slightly tilted silk top hat, and his shell was draped in an immaculate high-collared aristocratic velvet frock coat with gold filigree piping along the lapels. But beneath the aristocratic finery, his ancient, reptilian hands were coated in dense layers of white, blue, and red chalk dust—the exact colors spanning the blackboard. His dark, leathery eyes held no anger or suspicion; they shone with open, scholarly curiosity. <!-- L0780 --> <!-- L0788 -->

"You... got an interesting thing going on over here," Iggy stammered, his gravelly voice grating in the quiet office. "I guess I am a student. About the Panda-5... I don't know where I am, or who you are, or what this is. But wow, I'm learning a lot." <!-- L0789 --> <!-- L0799 --> <!-- L0800 --> <!-- L0803 --> <!-- L0813 -->

The tortoise tilted his head, blinking slowly. "Where are you from, young man? I do not recognize your accent. Please, forgive me—I did not mean to offend. I only meant that if you are unfamiliar with the Panda-5 line, you must hail from somewhere quite rural. Do you not have municipal power where you live?" <!-- L0814 --> <!-- L0815 --> <!-- L0820 --> <!-- L0821 -->

"Let's just say I'm not from around here," Iggy said, kicking gently at a floorboard. "I don't know these parts. But I'm learning fast." <!-- L0825 --> <!-- L0830 -->

The professor stepped inside, using the crook of his cane to nudge the door shut behind him. "Tell me one thing you have deduced. I saw the way you were taking notes. Your lines... they are precise. You do not look at the board like a confused novice; you look like someone who is searching for a loose bolt." He gestured toward the red question mark. "Tell me what you think of the Ash-Blood search." <!-- L0835 --> <!-- L0838 --> <!-- L0841 --> <!-- L0842 -->

"I don't know much about Ash-Bloods," Iggy admitted, scratching beneath his cap. "I only met one today. But I feel like you've got a big give-and-take situation over here. A lot of hullabaloo." <!-- L0843 --> <!-- L0854 -->

The tortoise chuckled—a deep, wheezing sound like dry bellows. "Forgive me. I do not mean to interrogate. It is merely that this is my first year of teaching, and I am still prone to lecturing. Let us establish the foundation. Every major node integrated into Harmony over the past thousand years has brought with it a tremendous increase in global amplitude. But with the Ash-Bloods... almost nothing happened. If their tiny flicker had not arrived exactly when it did, the grid would have slipped beneath the threshold. It would have been the opposite of the Night of Sparks. It would have been very, very bad." <!-- L0855 --> <!-- L0859 --> <!-- L0861 --> <!-- L0884 -->
