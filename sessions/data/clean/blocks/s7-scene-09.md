## CHAPTER 50: DRILLS AT DAWN & THE EXPEDITION TO D.C.

Naomi smoothed the police incident report across the trestle table, tapping her pen against the precinct badge printed at the top. "Both the anomalous zip-tie handcuffs and the flight manifest are currently locked away in the Metropolitan Police Department evidence locker in Washington, D.C." <!-- L1194 -->

Eusacles rubbed his chin, staring at the schematic. "The capital of the United States. In an active police precinct." <!-- L1195 -->

"I probably should have led with that," Naomi admitted with an apologetic wince. "But we can find a way in." <!-- L1196 -->

Alfie perched on the edge of the table, tilting his carved head. "Do you think they have some sort of gift shop? Because I'm really only interested in places that have merch available." <!-- L1204 -->

"If you get into the evidence locker, there's guaranteed to be plenty of odds and ends," Naomi said, suppressing a smile. "Hats, coats, badges..." <!-- L1207 -->

"It is Washington, D.C.," Eusacles chuckled, leaning back on the bench. "Between the National Mall, the museums, and the Capitol, it's probably the highest density of gift shops on the continent." <!-- L1210 -->

Alfie gave a brisk nod, patting his rapier hilt. "All right, I'm in." <!-- L1211 -->

Pierre looked between them, adjusting his wire-rimmed spectacles with a deep sigh. "I am having a crisis. I do not really want to go to a police station, but I feel it is irresponsible to leave a one-year-old action figure unattended. And I really like watching Alfie work." <!-- L1213 -->

"Well, look, Pierre," Eusacles said, lowering his voice as he leaned across the table. "I don't want to get your hopes up, but I talked with my dad last night." <!-- L1214 -->

Pierre blinked. "Who is your dad, by the way?" <!-- L1218 -->

"He's the god of death. Thanatos," Eusacles replied casually, as if mentioning a cousin from Reno. "Anyway, that's not important. Dad visited me last night, and I asked him if he knew who your mom was." <!-- L1219 -->

Pierre froze, his tumbler of rum hovering halfway to his mouth. "I am still processing. Your father is the god of death, and he visited you? But you are still alive, yes? You are not dead?" <!-- L1222 -->

"I'm definitely alive," Eusacles said. "He visited in a dream. More like a daydream where you're not completely asleep. Anyway, I asked him about your mom, and he didn't give me a straight answer. He just burst out laughing. He asked, 'Did Hermes not tell y'all? What an epic prank!'" <!-- L1239 -->

"He said he didn't want to ruin the surprise," Eusacles continued. "Which means if we do nothing and stay here, you'll never find out who your mother is." <!-- L1240 -->

Pierre's jaw dropped, then snapped shut in exasperation. "I don't know why you are even asking about my mother! I know who my mother is! She is very intimidating—she is like an Italian mafia boss! When I say 'Do you know who my mother is?', it is an intimidation tactic, not an honest inquiry!" <!-- L1246 -->

Eusacles stared at him, incredulous. "Bro, you have got to work on your intimidation! I thought you were asking honest questions the entire time!" <!-- L1256 -->

Pierre drew himself up to his full, aristocratic height, his dark eyes flashing with wounded Parisian pride. "I am exceedingly intimidating!" Pierre declared, snatching his bronze javelin from against the bench. "My presence commands absolute terror! If you require proof, I will demonstrate with some spear practice before we depart!" <!-- L1259 -->

Without warning, Pierre lunged forward, thrusting the bronze javelin straight at Eusacles' chest. <!-- L1261 -->

Before the blade could touch denim, Eusacles' left arm snapped up on pure, divine reflex. The engraved pocket watch at his wrist caught the bronze spearhead with a sharp, resonant *clink*. The bronze point glanced harmlessly off the crystal face, leaving not a scratch or a dent upon the glass. <!-- L1269 -->

Pierre stumbled back, blinking at his weapon in absolute awe. "See, that is precisely what I require! You did not even flinch, while I would have been skewered! You must instruct me in these defenses!" <!-- L1274 -->

Alfie hopped down onto the gravel path outside, drawing his sewing-needle rapier with a sharp, authoritative flourish. "Down on the grass, mate!" Alfie commanded, pointing the needle toward the lawn. "If you want to be properly intimidating, it begins with physical discipline! Push-ups! Let's get down to business!" <!-- L1278 -->

Within minutes, the sanctuary courtyard was transformed. Down on the dewy lawn, Alfie perched like a diminutive green-cloaked drill sergeant atop Pierre's shoulder blades as Pierre grunted through shaky push-ups in the grass. Then, switching positions, Pierre rested his weight upon Alfie's shoulders, watching in open-mouthed disbelief as the two-foot driftwood duelist effortlessly pumped out one-handed push-ups against the gravel without breaking a sweat. <!-- L1280 -->

With spirits renewed, gear repaired, and their tactical roles hardened, the party gathered their rucksacks on the porch. The lead was clear, the anomaly was identified, and the expedition to the nation's capital was set to begin. <!-- L1288 -->
