## CHAPTER 28: ECHOES ACROSS THE LOST ROADS

"I believe that is a North Carolina Velociraptor," Dravin replied solemnly, peering at the bizarre museum display as security klaxons wailed deafeningly behind them. <!-- L0991 --> <!-- L0994 -->

"It is not very often that we get a perfectly quiet time in the museum with no one here," Pierre observed, strolling across the polished floor. He stopped before a freestanding glass case featuring an ancient Spartan hoplite panoply. "Professor, is it frowned upon if I take this really cool Spartan armor?" <!-- L0995 --> <!-- L0997 -->

"That's a good question," Dravin mused, stroking his chin. "Anybody need some heavy armor? You're in chain mail currently, but guess where you got it from? This museum!" <!-- L0998 --> <!-- L1002 --> <!-- L1004 -->

Pierre hurried over to the auxiliary claw controls, deftly maneuvering the steel pincers across the gallery to hoist the heavy glass vitrine, scooping up a bronzed breastplate and greaves. <!-- L1005 --> <!-- L1008 -->

Alfie adjusted his souvenir cap over his wooden ears, turning around only to discover that while Pierre and the Professor were eyeing museum-grade ancient armaments, he had spent the entire heist standing in the museum gift shop. <!-- L1009 --> <!-- L1011 -->

Dravin proudly claimed the ancient hoplite shield for himself, while Pierre scooped up an authentic bronze war javelin. <!-- L1012 --> <!-- L1016 -->

"Went to the North Carolina Natural History and World History Museum, and all I got was this stupid hat," Alfie grumbled, glaring down at his green-and-gold souvenir. <!-- L1018 -->

"I am sorry they did not make the Spartan armor in your size, Alfie," Pierre teased. <!-- L1022 -->

"Yeah, my hat was clearly made for a stuffed animal," Alfie retorted. <!-- L1023 -->

"Time to go," Dravin urged, heading toward the rear of the hall. They ducked through the service door into the archaeology storage room. In the distance outside, sirens began to wail across downtown Raleigh—police cruisers responding to the alarm. <!-- L1028 --> <!-- L1030 --> <!-- L1032 --> <!-- L1034 -->

"Police sirens," Dravin clarified. "Though being in the Greek exhibit, you might think they're mythical sirens calling you back." <!-- L1035 -->

"They are beckoning us," Pierre laughed, referencing the pirate vision. <!-- L1038 -->

In the corner of the storage room, the rusted wooden service door stood ajar. Dravin pulled it open, revealing the swirling gray mist of the Lost Roads. "I have pretty good survival instincts," Dravin said, taking the lead across the threshold. <!-- L1040 --> <!-- L1045 --> <!-- L1046 -->

The party stepped through, the cold museum air giving way to the timeless, non-Euclidean fog. Although their path meandered without geographic logic, turning sharply and doubling back across forgotten paving stones, they enjoyed a smooth journey, tracking their own returning footprints with remarkable ease. <!-- L1077 --> <!-- L1084 -->

Time lost all measurable meaning in the gray ether. Whether twenty minutes passed or three full hours, the mist eventually receded, replaced by familiar pine trees and the lantern-lit porch of the Marigold Inn. They had returned to The Margin. <!-- L1086 -->

By the crackling hearth in the common room sat Michael, nursing a mug of tea. He looked up, nodding in approval. "Well, what's up? Looks like you guys had a good trip." <!-- L1088 -->

"Mike, we missed you, mate!" Alfie cheered, climbing onto a bench. <!-- L1089 -->

"You weren't gone too long for scouting out a fragment," Michael remarked. <!-- L1090 -->

"Yes, we found a particle of stone," Pierre reported proudly. "But more importantly, look at Alfie's new hat—it is very cute! Secondly, look at the Professor's new hat and shield—not as cute." <!-- L1091 -->

"We gave them bread," Pierre defended their museum salvage. "I think we deserved credit!" <!-- L1093 -->

"The only person filming was Nincy, and she kind of turned to stone," Alfie added. <!-- L1097 -->

Michael straightened up, his brow furrowing. "Did you all run into Redactors?" <!-- L1098 -->

"Is that what they call themselves? Yes," Pierre confirmed. <!-- L1099 -->

"Wow," Michael murmured grimly. "I would not have thought Redactors would be on the scene that quick." <!-- L1100 -->
