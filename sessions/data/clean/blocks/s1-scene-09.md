## CHAPTER 9: THE LEGEND STRIKE

Eusacles's vision flashed white. His legs buckled, his morningstar slipping from his limp fingers as he collapsed unconscious onto the marble tiles. <!-- L1677 -->

With a raspy hiss, the wounded demon reached down with clawed hands, wrapping its talons around Eusacles's collar, and began dragging the limp gambler toward the swirling oblivion of the rift. <!-- L1685 -->

Across the library, Pierre dashed behind the last bookshelf. His fingers caught the leather spine of a thick codex written in ancient Greek. His eyes widened behind his glasses as he translated the title: The Guide to Gorgons. <!-- L1700 -->

"This is just like the one I used to read growing up," Pierre whispered, clutching it to his chest as he felt an overwhelming, familiar kinship to the text. <!-- L1715 -->

In the center of the hall, the demon was dragging Eusacles within three paces of the planar void. Dravin was out of range. Pierre was clutching his book among the stacks. <!-- L1730 -->

The unknown named doll looked at Eusacles, then at the beast's remaining intact leg. An unmistakable spark of mythical destiny—the pure, heroic momentum of Legend—ignited within the doll’s sailcloth core. <!-- L1750 -->

With blinding speed, the doll leaped through the air. The sewing-needle rapier gleamed with silver fire as the doll drove the point straight through the beast’s right ankle tendon, pinning it to the marble floor with pinpoint force. <!-- L1770 -->

The demon let out a final, bubbling shriek. Its legs gave out entirely. It dropped Eusacles onto the cold stone and collapsed, its body dissolving into smoking puddles of black ash that were instantly sucked backward into the vacuum of the closing rift. <!-- L1785 -->

"We're done!" the Three Fates cried out together. "The weave is ready!" <!-- L1790 -->

From their towering loom, the three crones swept a colossal, shimmering tapestry into the air. The mythical cloth billowed outward like an enormous net, spiraling through the colonnade and swallowing the entire hall in dazzling light. The library vanished. Reality spun into darkness. <!-- L1793 -->
