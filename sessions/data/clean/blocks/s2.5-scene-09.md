## CHAPTER 24: NODES OF HARMONY & THE MISSING SURGE

The professor grabbed a chalk stick, swung around, and slammed his cane against the floor. "Do you know of the true power of Harmony?" he asked, lunging toward the slate. <!-- L0885 -->

"No," Iggy said honestly. <!-- L0886 -->

"For ten centuries, Harmony has grown like a great living tree," the professor explained, tracing the upward steps of the graph. "Every time an independent province or geographic node is brought into our fold, the collective reservoir swells. The Global Amplitude is not an invention of the city; it is the measurable pulse of sentient connection. Every person connected through a resonator node can draw upon that power for heat, for light, for industry. But only if they possess the proper tuning." He pointed his cane at the small copper antenna protruding from the battery casing. <!-- L0888 --> <!-- L0900 --> <!-- L0907 -->

"Historically," he continued, "each integration brought a staggering leap. Two centuries ago, a single mountain province added two hundred units to the amplitude. The integration before that added three hundred. But our greatest fear has always been excess. Have you heard of the Night of Sparks?" <!-- L0910 --> <!-- L0915 --> <!-- L0920 -->

Iggy ducked his head, climbing onto a stool to watch the diagrams. <!-- L0921 -->

"It occurred before we mastered the amber resonance lattices," the tortle murmured somberly. "An unexpected surge of unrefined power flooded the continental conduits. Cities caught fire from the inside out. Arc towers exploded across three territories, and the sky burned violet for two solid months. It was an inferno of pure, unregulated energy. That is why these Panda-5 units are over-engineered to such absurd tolerances. My task—the entire mandate of my chair—was to construct the new Apex generation of batteries to absorb the colossal surge anticipated from the Ash-Blood territories." <!-- L0925 --> <!-- L0930 --> <!-- L0935 --> <!-- L0938 -->

The professor's shoulders slumped beneath his velvet coat. He pointed a chalky claw at the stagnant end of the graph. <!-- L0939 -->

"The Ash-Bloods joined. Their volcano forges were linked to the grid. We braced for a tidal wave. And what arrived?" He tapped the slate with a hollow *clack*. "A whisper. A trickle. Just enough to keep the line from collapsing beneath the fatal threshold, but less power than this campus was consuming fifty years ago. My Apex project is useless. If there is no surge, there is nothing to buffer. I will likely be stripped of my workshop by winter." <!-- L0945 --> <!-- L0950 --> <!-- L0955 --> <!-- L0970 --> <!-- L1000 -->
