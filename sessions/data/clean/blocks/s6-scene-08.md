## CHAPTER 39: THE SCHOLAR'S TOME & THE FALLEN AESTHETE

Professor Dravin extended his open palm, guiding the hovering spectral tome with sharp intellectual focus. The heavy leatherbound volume whistled through the air once more, coming down hard across the wounded satyr's shoulders with concussive, punishing force. <!-- L1181 -->

The beast stumbled, hissed in blind agony, and dropped to all fours. Glimpsing Alfie beneath the kneehole of the teacher's desk, the satyr lowered its ram-horned brow and charged headlong into the shadows under the wood. <!-- L1203 -->

*THUD.*

The curved ram horns smashed violently into the solid oak desk framing, wedging tight against the drawers. Alfie leaned calmly against the back panel, perfectly untouched in the narrow space between the horns. <!-- L1204 -->

The second satyr saw the doll out of reach and pivoted toward Pierre, who was still kneeling dazed upon the wet floorboards. A jolt of pure, breathless panic shot through Pierre's chest—he had faced grotesque monstrosities before, and he knew how swiftly violence broke a body—but even scrambling through the puddle, he forced his trembling jaw into a defiant, theatrical grin. <!-- L1213 -->

"Pierre is a lover, not a fighter," Pierre protested, scrambling backward through the shallow water. <!-- L1216 -->

He raised his hand, attempting to project a defensive charm to turn the attack aside. "Stay your hand!" Pierre commanded. <!-- L1220 --> <!-- L1224 -->

The beast ignored the command. The horned creature vaulted over the orchestra railing and brought its cloven hooves down squarely into Pierre's chest with crushing, bone-rattling momentum. <!-- L1241 --> <!-- L1243 -->

A brutal, concussive impact exploded through Pierre's ribs. With his threshold barrier already shattered, the violent trauma crushed the breath from his lungs. His wire spectacles flew into the puddle, and Pierre collapsed onto the flooded tiles, slipping down to the barest thread of vitality before his vision blackened into unconsciousness. <!-- L1249 --> <!-- L1250 --> <!-- L1252 -->

Across the stage, the Medusa thrashed frantically against Alfie's magic hair band. Her teeth gnashed, and her scalp heaved as she attempted to tear the spectral elastic from her head. But the magical scrunchie held firm, choking her tendrils in a vice-like topknot. <!-- L1261 -->

"Wait for the shampoo to set, madame!" Pierre groaned weakly from the puddle, fighting through the fog in his skull. "It is not yet ready to be removed!" <!-- L1263 -->

Pierre struggled back onto his elbows, fumbled at his belt, and produced a slender steel scalpel. "If you refuse to behave, we will simply dispense with the hair entirely," Pierre muttered through gritted teeth. <!-- L1267 --> <!-- L1269 -->

He grabbed the glowing elastic band with his left hand, drew the steel blade across the coiled hair, and began sawing frantically at the dark tendrils. Sparks of blue neural electricity crackled against the metal edge, but the blade held true, severing coarse clumps of whipping coils. <!-- L1277 --> <!-- L1279 --> <!-- L1280 -->

"You will look vastly superior with a crew cut, I assure you!" Pierre declared. <!-- L1283 -->

Beneath the desk, Alfie drew back her sewing-needle rapier. Seeing the satyr's horned snout still stuck in the kneehole, she aimed for the creature's exposed right eye. <!-- L1285 -->

"Have some needlework, mate!" Alfie chirped. <!-- L1289 -->

*Snikt.*

The fine steel needle plunged clean into the satyr's eyeball. A fountain of black inky ichor sprayed from the socket. The blinded beast wailed in agony, rearing back on its hind hooves with blood and ink streaming down its furry snout, its depth perception utterly ruined. <!-- L1294 --> <!-- L1295 --> <!-- L1298 -->

Dravin stood over the platform, his tweed coat soaked with sprinkler spray as he surveyed the battered, reeling creatures. "Every hostile on this stage is bloodied," the professor observed coldly, holding the glowing spectral volume in check. <!-- L1310 -->
