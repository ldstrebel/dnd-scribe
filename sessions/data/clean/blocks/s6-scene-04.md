## CHAPTER 35: THUNDERWAVE & THE PETRIFYING CARTRIDGE

Across the glowing barrier, the transformed Medusa remained rigid, held fast in Pierre's invisible psychic vice. <!-- L0623 -->

"You stay right there," Pierre called out haughtily, adjusting the black beret on his brow. "You listen to me, and you remain civilized." <!-- L0627 -->

Alfie perched on the edge of the mahogany desktop, her seashell eyes narrowing at the frozen creature. "Right then," the driftwood figurine declared, drawing back her tiny wooden arms. "*Thunderwave!*" <!-- L0635 -->

A concussive shockwave of booming sonic thunder erupted from Alfie's needle rapier. The atmospheric blast tore across the orchestra rows, rattling the wooden desks and sending stray lecture notes fluttering into the deluge. The paralyzed woman shuddered as the sonic wave struck her chest, black sparks shearing from her frozen hair as the concussive force tore through her defenses with a brutal shock of thunderous damage. <!-- L0677 --> <!-- L0686 -->

Without lingering to admire the blast, Alfie hopped backward off the desk, diving under a heavy oak student chair to take tactical cover. <!-- L0687 -->

Up on the stage, the thunder blast gave Professor Edward Dravin the exact distraction he needed. Straining against the satyr's cloven-hoofed weight, Dravin braced his heels against the oak floorboards, channeled Persephone's threshold resilience, and surged upward with decisive athletic leverage. His tweed shoulders threw off the beast's grip, breaking the grapple and sending the horned infiltrator sprawling backward across the platform. <!-- L0688 --> <!-- L0693 --> <!-- L0694 -->

Dravin took two brisk strides toward the lectern, adjusting his wire-rimmed spectacles as he found an unobstructed firing lane down into the aisle. Before him stood the paralyzed Medusa, her body still locked in Pierre's spell. <!-- L0701 --> <!-- L0703 --> <!-- L0707 -->

"A scholar does not discard an empirical tool," Dravin remarked mildly, slipping his right hand deep into his inner tweed pocket. He withdrew the heavy, blued-steel pneumatic pistol stolen from the Raleigh museum's security armory. <!-- L0712 --> <!-- L0714 -->

The weapon hummed softly, loaded with specialized petrifying cartridges designed to neutralize anomalous specimens. Dravin checked the chamber with calm academic detachment. <!-- L0718 -->

"She cannot move," Dravin murmured, calmly centering the front post on the woman's forehead. "A stationary target is more than sufficient to calibrate an empirical trajectory." <!-- L0727 --> <!-- L0729 --> <!-- L0731 -->

"Close the distance, Professor!" Pierre urged from the rostrum rail. "Point-blank! Put the muzzle right to her brow so the shot cannot fail!" <!-- L0738 --> <!-- L0743 -->

"It is a ranged propellant cartridge, Pierre," Dravin pointed out, stepping forward to close the gap. <!-- L0745 --> <!-- L0747 -->

He raised the museum sidearm, lining up the iron sights directly on the paralyzed Medusa. <!-- L0749 -->
