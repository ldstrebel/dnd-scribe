## CHAPTER 18: THE STOLEN WEAVE & FRESH INK

With the lantern restored, Naomi unrolled a large, hand-drawn map across the center table. Unlike standard road atlases, this map was drawn in deep black ink, covered in shifting geographic lines, crossed-out towns, and handwritten margin notes. <!-- L1017 -->

"Everyone in the Margin is focused on staying safe and comfortable," Naomi said, her tone bristling with determined impatience. "Teddy thinks we can just hide out here forever. But reality is unraveling outside." <!-- L1025 -->

"What do you mean?" Dravin asked, leaning over the parchment. <!-- L1033 -->

"Whenever the Fates make an edit to history," Naomi explained, tapping a point marked in North Carolina, "they leave behind physical remnants—fragments of altered stone, papyrus, and memory. We call them *fresh ink*. If a fragment remains uncollected in the mortal realm, it warps the local timeline, causing dimensional anomalies and attracting correctors." <!-- L1041 -->

"Like the scrolls I took from the Library?" Pierre asked, his eyes widening. <!-- L1049 -->

"Exactly," Naomi nodded. "The Fates want those back. But more importantly, there is a Greek tablet fragment currently sitting in a museum exhibit in Raleigh, North Carolina. It was dug up three months ago, but history says it shouldn't exist. It's radiating planar resonance. If we don't retrieve it, the correctors will tear through the mortal veil to destroy it." <!-- L1065 -->

Eusacles grinned, a reckless spark returning to his eyes. "A museum heist? To steal a magic rock from ancient Greece? Now that sounds a lot more fun than hiding under a bed during a drill." <!-- L1079 -->

"We move at dawn," Naomi declared. "And we take the Lost Roads." <!-- L1137 -->
