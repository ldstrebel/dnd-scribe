## CHAPTER 15: THE SANCTUARY OF 1846

Teddy grabbed an iron fire poker from the dirt and thrust it deep into the coals, pushing the burning logs aside to send a violent shower of orange sparks into the canopy. <!-- L0640 -->

"People ask how I got here," Teddy said, his calloused hands gripping the forged iron handle. "I arrived in eighteen forty-six. I was walking across these hills with a surveyor's compass when the world suddenly shuddered. An edit happened upstream—a treaty rewritten, a mountain pass erased—and my entire hometown vanished from existence." <!-- L0660 -->

Alfie leaped from Pierre's shoulder down onto a flat river stone, brandishing his needle with a miniature flick. "Eighteen forty-six? Blimey, mate. You don't look a day over fifty-five." <!-- L0680 -->

"Time doesn't tick down in the Margin, little sailor," Teddy replied with a soft chuckle, pushing his tobacco pipe back between his teeth. "You don't age. Your boots don't wear out unless you cross the fence. We are the notes scribbled in the white margins of a manuscript. As long as we stay here, the eraser cannot reach us." <!-- L0700 -->

"The eraser..." Dravin murmured, stepping toward the hearth. "The ink beasts that attacked us at the colonnade—those were not wild beasts. They were agents of retroactive deletion." <!-- L0720 -->

"Correctors," Naomi said coldly. She lunged forward, stabbing her whittling knife into the pine log. "The Fates use cosmic ink to rewrite time. But when they sever a timeline, the loose ends bleed. The ink takes physical form to scrub away the survivors. If they breach the perimeter, they drag you screaming into the void." <!-- L0740 -->

Eusacles pushed himself off the split-rail fence with a defiant grin, wrenching his morningstar chain tight around his knuckles. "We're all heading to the underworld eventually. Might as well give them hell before they get the chance." <!-- L0745 -->
