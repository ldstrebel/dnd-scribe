## CHAPTER 27: THE NINETEEN SHEEP PRANK

Dravin stepped toward the reception counter, whispering an incantation under his breath: "I am going to cast Sleep on Nancy." <!-- L0885 -->

As the arcane sleep magic formed in Dravin's mind, Alfie’s Fate-woven Wordcraft flared in resonance. In a sharp mental edit, Alfie shifted a single letter: *Sleep* became *Sheep*. <!-- L0901 -->

With a sudden thunder of clattering hooves, an office door behind the reception desk burst wide open. Eighteen woolly, bleating sheep came stampeding out into the museum lobby, scattering tourists, security guards, and brochures across the marble floor. <!-- L0908 -->

Pierre took his cue as the chaos erupted. Diving low, he grabbed the belly of a charging ram—a daring Odysseus maneuver—and let the sheep carry him swiftly past the reaching hands of the bewildered guards and straight into the research gallery. <!-- L0909 --> <!-- L0916 -->

Dravin grinned with wicked academic delight. Quickly invoking an illusion cantrip, he painted bold black numerals across the flanks of the charging flock—numbering them **1 through 19**, deliberately omitting number 14 for the classic senior prank. <!-- L0918 --> <!-- L0921 -->
