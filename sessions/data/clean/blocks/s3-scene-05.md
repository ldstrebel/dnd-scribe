## CHAPTER 31: AZER'S INTERROGATION & THE PLAN FOR LUCKY

Heavy footsteps thundered down the cobblestone thoroughfare. Aggie rounded the corner at a frantic sprint, clay-dusted tunic askew, followed closely by Loami and Ignatius. When they saw Britt straddling a pinned man with her flint dagger pressed against his throat, Aggie gasped in relief, rushing forward and planting a heavy boot firmly upon the thief's chest. <!-- L0166 --> <!-- L0167 -->

"Britt! Are you hurt?" Aggie demanded, dark eyes wide with sisterly terror.

"He took my pendant," Britt growled through clenched teeth. With a violent jerk of her wrist, she snatched the sacred petrified acorn necklace from Azer's trembling fingers, clasping the dark wood to her chest until her knuckles turned white. Then, leaning down until her breath ruffled his hair, she caught the silver chain looped to his waistcoat, ripping his engraved pocket watch free with a sharp snap of thread. "You took something of mine. I take something of yours." <!-- L0168 -->

Azer winced, lifting two fingers to gently push the flat of Britt's blade away from his skin. "Easy, sister... easy. It was just business. You may paint me as the villain here, but look at yourselves! Drinking boiler grease at two in the morning the week before the entrance examinations! You're squandering a gift thousands would kill for! I failed my candidacy run seasons ago. Those two lads from Septal—Nickel and Tus—they just wanted a chance. I convinced them that if we took your registration receipt, they could slip into the Loom hall under your name!" <!-- L0170 --> <!-- L0171 --> <!-- L0173 --> <!-- L0174 -->

Loami stepped forward into the lantern light, thumbs hooked behind the heavy canvas suspenders of his work trousers. His jaw was set like an iron vise. "And what do you think thieves deserve around here, friend?" <!-- L0175 -->

"I'm already ruined," Azer spat bitterly, trying to sneer through the dirt. "What are you going to do? Send me to the frontier? I'm already living in the gutters. What's one more beating?"

"About eighteen points of blunt force," Loami murmured. <!-- L0176 -->

Without another word, Loami stepped in, drove his right boot into the flagstones, and planted a brutal, calloused mechanic's fist directly into Azer's floating ribs. A sharp, sickening *crack* echoed off the alley walls like snapping pine. <!-- L0177 -->

Azer's breath left him in a ragged, whistling shriek. He curled into a tight ball against the damp masonry, clutching his side, coughing flecks of blood onto the stones. He didn't speak again.

Britt slowly sheathed her dagger, stepping back to let Aggie wrap a sturdy arm around her shoulders. "What about the registration receipt?" Aggie asked, glancing into the dark passage where the two bruisers had fled. <!-- L0178 -->

"The paper is worthless," Britt muttered, rubbing the sore knot at the base of her skull. "The Loom reads the blood and the resonance. It knows who we are. They stole a scrap of dead parchment." <!-- L0179 -->

Ignatius stepped up beside Loami, looking down at the groaning thief, then across at the silver pocket watch in Britt's hand. A slow, brilliant smile spread across the flame-haired youth's face.

"Val told us back at the lounge that Lucky won't trade that study guide for gold," Ignatius said, eyes alight. "He trades for stories and unique collateral." <!-- L0190 --> <!-- L0191 -->

Loami wiped his knuckles on his work rag, chuckling softly. "A silver watch taken off an alley mugger, paired with the heroic tale of how we broke a scoundrel's ribs to protect our own? Lucky will eat it up."

Beside them, Iggy slipped off the mounting block, patting the bulging pocket of his trench coat where his freshly sketched map of the city lay tucked beside his warm amber crystal. "I have the map," the earth-kin muttered, adjusting his copper goggles. "Tomorrow after classes, we mark the quadrants. We find Lucky. We get the study guide." <!-- L0192 -->

Ignatius held out a hand, sparks dancing across his knuckles. "Tomorrow after classes. We find Lucky, we master the fundamentals, and we take the Spire." <!-- L0193 -->
