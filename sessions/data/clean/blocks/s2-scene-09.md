## CHAPTER 21: THE PETRIFYING GAZE

The convenience store grew unnervingly quiet, the hum of the fluorescent tube lights buzzing loudly overhead. <!-- L1401 -->

"Excuse me?" Pierre said, his voice tightening with sudden French formality. "I assure you, Monsieur, he is not a toy, and he is certainly not stolen." <!-- L1417 -->

The clerk leaned aggressively across the Formica counter, his long, spindly fingers reaching out toward Alfie’s canvas brow. "Around here, unusual things belong to the station. Hand it over, kid." <!-- L1425 -->

Inside Pierre's pocket, Alfie drew his needle rapier with a sharp hiss of steel. "Touch this wood, pal, and I'll carve my initials in your forehead!" <!-- L1433 -->

Pierre's eyes widened. A surge of ancient, protective fury spiked through his bloodline. As his heart pounded, a strange, hypnotic resonance washed over his vision—a dangerous golden glint flashing behind his spectacles. <!-- L1481 -->

Pierre locked his gaze squarely into the clerk's eyes and stepped forward. "You will step back, Monsieur. Immediately." <!-- L1496 -->

The moment their eyes met, the clerk froze instantly mid-reach. His sneer hardened into a rigid, petrified grimace. His skin took on a chalky, greyish pallor, his muscles seizing up as if turned to solid granite. The man stood completely immobilized—alive, terrified, but unable to move a single finger. <!-- L1497 -->

Pierre blinked, the golden flare in his eyes fading as quickly as it had ignited. He stared at his trembling hands in quiet horror. The sensation was terrifyingly familiar—a petrifying echo of an incident from his childhood in France that he had spent years trying to forget. <!-- L1506 -->

"Pierre..." Alfie whispered, tapping his arm. "We ought to grab the receipt and walk, mate." <!-- L1513 -->
