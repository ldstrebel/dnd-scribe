## CHAPTER 32: THE RALEIGH GETAWAY

The party wheeled the blanket-wrapped tablet out the side delivery doors of the museum into the cool North Carolina evening. <!-- L1537 -->

They loaded the artifact into the truck bed beneath a canvas tarp, and Eusacles fired up the Ford's engine with a triumphant roar. <!-- L1538 -->

"Pierre, you were magnificent in that lab coat," Dravin praised, smiling from the passenger seat as they pulled onto the Raleigh beltline. <!-- L1551 -->

"I must admit, Monsieur," Pierre laughed, taking off his glasses and wiping them with his handkerchief, "it is much easier to talk to museum guards than to dodge horned ink demons." <!-- L1556 -->

Alfie perched proudly on the rearview mirror, swinging his wooden legs. "And you've got to admit, the sheep spell was bloody brilliant!" <!-- L1561 -->

Behind them, the lights of Raleigh faded into the distance as the Ford turned off the main interstate onto an unmapped gravel exit. The headlights cut through the gathering mountain mist as the gateway to the Lost Roads parted before them once more. <!-- L1586 -->

They had recovered their first Fragment of altered time, stabilized an unraveling timeline seam, and proved that a French scholar, a Greek professor, a Vegas son of Thanatos, and a driftwood doll could outwit history itself. <!-- L1591 -->

Ahead lay the sanctuary of The Margin, where the next chapter of the Stolen Weave awaited them around the fire. <!-- L1636 -->
