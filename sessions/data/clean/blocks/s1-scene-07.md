## CHAPTER 7: IN THE SHADOWS OF THE STACKS

Seeing the doll dancing across the tiles, the larger horned beast pivoted. It ignored the battered mortal and lunged directly for the wooden creation, sensing the divine, meddling essence of the Fates woven into its sailcloth seams. Its claws raked across the doll’s wooden shoulder, tearing splinters and sending it tumbling across the floor. <!-- L1455 -->

Yet the little driftwood figure held together, its salt-stained wood flexing against the blow. <!-- L1460 -->

Sensing that the tide had turned against it, the wounded ink blob turned and began slithering rapidly back toward the safety of the dark rift. <!-- L1470 -->

"Oh no you don't!" Eusacles bellowed. <!-- L1480 -->

As the blob darted past, Eusacles pivoted on his heel and brought his morningstar down with pinpoint precision. The heavy spiked head pulverized the creature against the marble flagstones, detonating it into a harmless spray of drying, inert black soot. <!-- L1490 -->

"Love that," Eusacles breathed, wiping a streak of sweat from his forehead. <!-- L1500 -->

Meanwhile, behind the towering shelves of the library, Pierre was entirely absorbed in his own mission. He walked swiftly along the endless rows of cedar shelves, keeping his footsteps silent against the stone. Around him lay thousands of ancient papyri, clay tablets, and scrolls in various states of decay. <!-- L1510 -->

Pierre muttered under his breath, his voice barely a whisper: "She sent me here to find out who my parents are... If anyone would have connections between me and another, it would be the Fates." <!-- L1520 -->

With the three crones focused entirely on weaving over their loom and the monsters occupied in the center of the hall, Pierre closed his eyes. He reached out with his left hand, letting his fingertips glide along the edges of the ancient parchment. <!-- L1530 -->

A faint hum of magic—a gentle resonance of ancient knowledge—tingled against his skin. His eyes snapped open. He pulled three scrolls from the shelf, hastily stuffing them into his canvas rucksack, and darted silently to the next stack. <!-- L1559 -->
