## CHAPTER 7: IN THE SHADOWS OF THE STACKS

The larger horned beast pivoted away from the bruised mortal, locking its yellow eyes onto the animated wooden doll. The creature sensed the divine, meddling essence of the Fates woven into the doll's sailcloth seams. With a guttural hiss, it lunged forward, raking its iron claws across the doll's shoulder. Splinters flew as the force of the blow sent the tiny figure tumbling across the flagstones. <!-- L1455 -->

Yet the little driftwood warrior held together, its salt-stained timber flexing against the heavy impact. <!-- L1460 -->

Across the floor, the puddle of spilled demonic bile hissed against the marble, congealing into a second ink minion that scrambled frantically toward the safety of the dark rift. <!-- L1470 -->

Eusacles saw the creature dart for the portal and surged off the column. "Oh no you don't!" he bellowed. <!-- L1480 -->

He pivoted on his heel, planted his boots, and brought his heavy chained morningstar down with pinpoint precision. The spiked head obliterated the minion against the marble flagstones, detonating it into a harmless spray of drying, inert black soot. <!-- L1490 -->

"Love that," Eusacles breathed, wiping a streak of sweat from his forehead. <!-- L1500 -->

Meanwhile, behind the towering shelves of the library, Pierre was entirely absorbed in his own covert mission. He moved swiftly along the endless rows of cedar shelves, keeping his footsteps silent against the cold stone. Around him lay thousands of ancient papyri, clay tablets, and scrolls in various states of decay. <!-- L1510 -->

Pierre muttered under his breath, his voice barely a whisper in the echoing quiet: "She sent me here to find out who my parents are... If anyone would have connections between me and another, it would be the Fates." <!-- L1520 -->

With the three crones focused entirely on weaving over their glowing loom and the monsters locked in battle in the center of the hall, Pierre closed his eyes. He reached out with his left hand, letting his fingertips glide along the weathered edges of the ancient parchment. <!-- L1530 -->

A faint hum of magic—a gentle resonance of ancient knowledge—tingled against his skin. His eyes snapped open. He pulled three scrolls from the shelf, hastily stuffing them into his canvas rucksack, and darted silently toward the next stack. <!-- L1559 -->
