## CHAPTER 14: THE PATERNAL TETHER & THE CONSPIRACY BOARD

As dinner wound down, a crisp, sharp-eyed woman with severe posture approached Pierre's table with a leather clipboard. <!-- L0757 -->

"Hello, my name is Anna Smith," she introduced herself with clipped efficiency. "I am conducting your intake for import into the Margin. You mentioned several curious things earlier... specifically regarding snakes. I assume gorgon heritage, perhaps?" <!-- L0758 -->

Pierre blinked, adjusting his spectacles nervously. "Well, Anna, you have correctly identified my heritage, and that does mean something to me. But honestly, I was concerned when I heard there were half-bloods here. I would honestly just like to know where the gorgons are." <!-- L0759 --> <!-- L0760 -->

Anna scribbled a quick notation. "We rarely get half-monsters here on purpose. Why were you sent away from your settlement?" <!-- L0761 -->

"That is a very personal question for a first day, do you not think?" Pierre replied with a cautious shrug. "What I can tell you is that my mother told me to come to America to find perspective. As far as I can tell, crashing into Greek libraries is a pretty normal day in America." <!-- L0762 -->

Anna stiffened slightly. "I apologize for my terseness," she admitted with a rigid nod. "I have been told it is from my background, and I apologize for the aggressiveness." <!-- L0768 -->

"No, no, I find you quite lovely," Pierre reassured her with a gentle smile. "You are direct and to the point. There is nothing wrong with you—it is merely the situation you have been put in." <!-- L0769 --> <!-- L0771 -->

Anna’s expression softened slightly. "I prefer to work in the shadows on patrol, but I served as an army nurse in the United States Army. You sound as though you are on a pilgrimage. What is your goal here?" <!-- L0772 -->

"I know who my mother is," Pierre said softly. "I am doing this as a favor to her. I figured I would stay two or three weeks, fly back to Paris, tell her there was nothing for me to learn, and work on my own skills." <!-- L0774 -->

He eyed her uniform with a playful, skeptical squint. "One quick question... is your name really Anna Smith?" <!-- L0778 -->

"Of course," she replied without batting an eye. "US Army nurse. Go America. Burgers and whiskey." <!-- L0781 -->

Pierre chuckled softly. "Okay, Anna. Your secret is safe with me. And I will take an extra piece of bread—the bread here is quite good." <!-- L0784 -->

Across the room, Mike sat down across from Professor Dravin with a notebook. "All right, Edward. Looks like you got stuck with me. Curious—why was a distinguished professor like you riding a rickety bus outside Vegas?" <!-- L0787 --> <!-- L0789 -->

"There is actually a tremendous amount of Greek mythology in Las Vegas," Dravin smiled warmly. <!-- L0790 -->

Mike stared blankly. "That's news to me! What, like the Statue of Liberty is Greek mythology or something?" <!-- L0791 -->

"The origins of gambling lie directly within Greek mythology," Dravin explained, adjusting his tweed lapels. "Hermes and Pan casting lots; the Fates determining mortal fortune through chance." <!-- L0794 -->

"Well, learn something new every day," Mike grinned, shaking his head. "Egg on my face!" <!-- L0795 -->

Outside, Teddy guided them toward their guest cabin, admiring Dravin’s scholarly passion. "Spent most of my youth on the timber trails," Teddy remarked. "Out here, you learn that every myth was just history before someone decided to edit the manuscript." <!-- L0809 -->

Inside the cabin, an older resident named Rosa was laying out handmade wool blankets on cedar cots. When she saw Alfie balanced neatly on Pierre’s shoulder, her eyes softened. <!-- L0833 -->

"And who might you be, little traveler?" Rosa asked gently. "Do you prefer being on the ground?" Alfie shook his head. "Dolls don't usually drive ships, mate. I don't recommend it." <!-- L0849 -->

Alfie hopped down onto the rustic pine table, adjusting his needle rapier. "Name's Alfie, ma'am. Driftwood Duelist, at your service. Last thing I remember before waking up in that marble palace was darkness... salt water... the roar of cannon fire, and blood on the deck. Next thing I know, I'm carved out of driftwood with needle and thread."

Before Rosa could reply, Naomi barged straight into the guest cabin, breathless and clutching rolls of research parchment. "Sorry I'm late!" Naomi called out urgently. "I was knee-deep in research, but it's super important!" <!-- L0873 -->
