# Chapter 75: The Wager on Pudge & The Spore Sickness

The afternoon air felt strangely heavy against Britt’s throat as she exited Sterling Hall. Even through the coarse weave of her favorite green traveler’s tunic, her skin prickled with an uncharacteristic chill. A subtle, disorienting flutter pulsed behind her breastbone, leaving her faintly breathless and unsteady upon the flagstones. She blinked against the courtyard glare, silently chalking the fatigue up to the dry mountain altitude and late nights poring over academy maps.

Before she could sit and rest, Aunt Angela Galaspora swept across the path in an eruption of dangling leather herb pouches and trailing green leaves.

"Hey girls! Just the Mizizi I was looking for. Come with me—we have a day ahead!" <!-- L0119 -->

Britt managed a faint smile, pressing a moss-stained palm against her side as she forced her stride to match the elder’s lively pace. Together with Aggie, they threaded through the university’s iron gates. Ahead, the grand marble arches of The Colonnade stood stark and brilliant against the azure sky, looking down onto the vast expanse of the canyon.

A sudden, sharp wave of vertigo crashed over Britt. The loud chatter of the crowd and the drone of charging battery rigs blurred into a muted hum. For a breathless moment, she realized she hadn't heard a single word exchanged between Aggie and Angela since passing the gates. She stopped, rubbing her temples and inhaling deeply. Drawing upon the rooted resilience of the ancient soil back home, she centered herself with quiet willpower. The dizzy spell receded like a retreating tide, leaving her mind sharp and alert once more. Looking up, she caught Aggie’s lightning-quick eyes watching her with intense concern, alongside Angela’s furrowed brow. Britt offered a reassuring nod and stepped toward the mooring gangway.

Ascending to the grand promenade deck of the *Zephyr*, Britt paused to admire the sheer splendor of the vessel. Polished mahogany boards gleamed beneath rich gas lamps, and the circular floor of heavy plate crystal offered a sheer, vertical view straight into the heart of the basalt canyon below.

At the entrance to the main salon, Valentine Sterling Sr. stood conferring with Cade Ashveil, holding an open notebook dense with handwritten rig profiles. Angela bustled up to them with unrestrained exuberance.

"Ah Mr. Cade, thank you thank you for arranging this adventure and pass my thanks along to Lady Ignis! Mr. Sterling, it is of course fantastic to see you again. I am so excited to see this ship fly and to learn about this Resy Race." <!-- L0146 -->

The elder explorer smiled warmly, dipping his head in courteous greeting.

"Hello Elder Angela, welcome to the Zephyr, please note it is a Reso race not resy, but I am very excited for this season to begin—do you know any of the racers?" <!-- L0151 -->

Angela waved an airy, dismissive hand toward the balustrade, her attention immediately captured by the towering ornamental flora nearby.

"Well I think our fellow Mycelium Circle member Rill had something to do with one of the teams, but honestly I forget which—wow look at these ferns...!" <!-- L0155 -->

As Sterling chuckled and gestured them inward, Cade Ashveil cleared his throat, offering an earnest observation to fill the gap.

"Well these ferns are great, almost as cool as the rocks out in the race." <!-- L0159 -->

Britt politely bowed out of the circle, letting Aunt Angela chat as she wandered toward the promenade rail. Glancing across the bustling upper deck, a remarkably familiar silhouette caught her eye: the broad, rugged shoulders of Lomi, complete with his oil-stained canvas mechanic collar and woolen flat cap. To her utter astonishment, the shipwright was actually aboard the VIP airship itself, hauling heavy wooden crates of distilled grog and arguing heatedly with an exasperated banquet steward about selling his potent, furnace-grade 'Ambrosia of Luck' directly to the aristocrats.

"Lomi!" Britt shouted, pushing forward through the gathering throng to reach him.

Just as she closed the distance, a massive cheer erupted from a cluster of drunken racing fans celebrating an engine fire on the tarmac below. A boisterous patron swung around with a celebratory roar, thrusting a squirming, crying baby root-kin straight into Britt’s arms. Before she could protest, fellow spectators clapped her on the back, toasted her with frothing mugs, and pulled her into their jubilant dance. By the time Britt gently handed the crying child back to its sheepish guardian and pried herself free from the crowd, Lomi had vanished entirely into the labyrinth of service hatches.

With the race start approaching, Britt descended the brass staircase toward the ship's glass Observatory. Inside, Valerius Sterling was presiding over a live commentary terminal on the aether-radio while managing a bustling wagering slate for high-society spectators.

"Place your stakes, ladies and gentlemen!" Valerius called out, chalking up the latest track odds. "Shatter Stamper at two-to-one! Siren’s Wake at three-to-one! And young Pudge, our heavy dark horse on Team Four, sitting at four-to-one!"

Britt smiled, remembering the solid, unassuming grit of Pudge’s rig from the hangar floors. Stepping forward, she slid ten copper coins across the polished counter.

"Ten copper on Pudge to win," she declared, pushing the coins forward.

Valerius raised an eyebrow with an approving grin, swiftly inking the formal wager receipt and sliding it across to her. Tucking the slip into her pocket, she turned and made her way down the quiet stateroom corridor.

There, leaning against the glass wall overlooking the canyon launch pads, stood Valentine Sterling Jr. He looked visibly relieved as Britt approached.

"I'm glad you're here. I wanted to pick you guys up from the dorms myself, but my father wouldn't let me leave the ship. He sent Ludo instead... who I see is currently enjoying his off-duty status at the bar." <!-- L0187 -->

Britt paused beside him, murmuring an inquiry about tomorrow's entrance exam. Val looked around the corridor, then leaned in close, his voice dropping low.

"Look, you need to be ready. I plan to lock myself in my room to study tonight. My father won't say much, but there is something after the written test though even my father won't tell me what. He's fair if nothing else." <!-- L0192 -->

Britt thanked him, promising complete discretion. As she hurried back toward the promenade deck to rejoin Aunt Angela and Aggie, the great brass starting horn bellowed across the canyon chasm, shaking the glass underfoot as the engines flared to life.
