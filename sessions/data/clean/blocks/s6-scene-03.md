## CHAPTER 34: THE ISLAND-TIME SMITE & FELINE SIGHT

"I do not think this beast cares for your curriculum, Professor," Pierre shouted, lunging forward with his spear as the satyr wrestled Dravin across the oak boards. <!-- L0481 -->

From the shadowed stage wing, heavy footsteps thudded against the hardwood. Eusacles strode onto the platform, his denim jacket flapping open over his broad chest and dark aviator sunglasses gleaming beneath the harsh halogen fixtures. He cracked his knuckles, casually scanning the thrashing satyr on the floorboards. <!-- L0484 -->

"Where's the professor?" Eusacles rumbled, stepping past the lectern with practiced Vegas calm. "And where's the thing holding him?" <!-- L0487 --> <!-- L0490 -->

Pierre pointed his javelin tip toward the cloven-hoofed infiltrator pinning Dravin. "Right there! Beat this beast!" <!-- L0495 -->

Alfie bobbed her driftwood head from the podium desk. "It's just generally good vibes, you know? Just keep the good vibes going." <!-- L0506 -->

"We wouldn't want any bad vibes up here," Eusacles muttered, reaching for the heavy chain hooked to his denim belt loop. "No bad vibes allowed on this stage. Hey, is this thing considered a fiend?" <!-- L0516 --> <!-- L0520 --> <!-- L0540 -->

With a sharp flick of his wrist, the brass pocket watch at the end of the chain snapped open. Clockwork gears chimed, expanding with blinding golden friction into a massive spiked iron morningstar. Across the heavy steel head, engraved letters read *Good Vibes*. <!-- L0547 -->

Across the reverse face, engraved letters proclaimed *Island Time*. Without waiting for an answer, Eusacles raised the flanged head high overhead and brought it down with concussive demigod fury directly onto the satyr's horned brow. Golden radiant flame burst from the iron spikes—a brutal Divine Smite that cracked through planar flesh, leaving the beast reeling and drenched in black ichor. <!-- L0556 -->

Across the hall, the transformed woman hissed in fury. Seeing her vanguard crushed, she surged toward the edge of the stage, whipping crackling brain tendrils toward Alfie. But Alfie was already bobbing to the cassette tape on the desktop, and the electric tendrils snapped through empty air. <!-- L0559 --> <!-- L0562 -->

High above, the persistent scream of the fire alarm triggered the auditorium's overhead suppression system. Nozzles hissed, and a drenching deluge of cold water cascaded from the vaulted ceiling. As the torrent pooled around the woman's boots, stray arcs of planar lightning from her coils sizzled through the rising puddle, charging the floor with blue static. Near the rostrum, another dimensional tear peeled open, and a second black ink blot dropped squelching to the floor. <!-- L0567 --> <!-- L0568 -->

Pierre marched to the front rim of Alfie's static ward, glaring down into the auditorium. He locked eyes with the transformed woman, his voice rising in dramatic Parisian indignation. <!-- L0579 -->

"You are producing an excessive quantity of electricity!" Pierre announced, pointing a slender finger down at her coiled form. "It is entirely unnecessary, and you are frankly doing far too much! *Hold Person!*" <!-- L0582 -->

The moment the somatic enchantment was loosed, an intense, searing tremor shuddered through Pierre's temples. A deep, guttural purr vibrated behind his skull. Behind his wire-rimmed spectacles, Pierre's round dark pupils flattened, sharpening into vertical, luminous feline slits. The mist and smoke filling the lecture hall seemed to dissolve before his vision; the entire auditorium remapped into brilliant chromatic heat signatures, revealing the pulsing hearts of every hidden satyr and the glowing anatomy of the creature below. <!-- L0583 -->

The psychic vice slammed down over the woman. Her limbs locked, her whipping hair froze in mid-coil, and her glowing eyes widened in helpless paralysis. <!-- L0584 -->

Pierre blinked rapidly, his hand rising to steady his beret. "Alfie," Pierre called out, his voice wavering with aristocratic panic. "Is there something wrong with my eyes? I feel extraordinarily bizarre right now." <!-- L0595 -->

Alfie held the tiny cassette tape toward his ear, dancing on her driftwood heels. "Mate, you're a proper rock star!" the doll cheered. "You're doing brilliant!" <!-- L0596 -->

"This music tortures my soul in a most delightful manner," Pierre groaned, holding the polished bronze blade of his museum spear up like a hand mirror. <!-- L0597 -->

The flat metal surface caught his reflection. Pierre gasped, recoiling as he stared into his own slitted feline gaze. <!-- L0600 -->

"Mon dieu! Alfie, I should not have looked at you!" Pierre cried, whipping his head away and shielding his face with a woolen sleeve. "I could have turned you to stone! This is terribly irresponsible of me!" <!-- L0603 --> <!-- L0604 -->

He peeked through his fingers at her painted driftwood torso. "Thank heavens you have no mortal blood inside your body, or you would be a carved garden ornament by now!" <!-- L0607 -->

Pierre let out a dramatic sigh of relief, lowering his guard. "Alfie, yes. You and your incomprehensible musical tastes are remarkably accommodating." <!-- L0609 -->

"See?" Alfie chuckled, giving her needle rapier a jaunty spin. "Maybe I'm actually such a good friend for you to have!" <!-- L0610 -->

"You can't turn me to stone, so it's a pretty good deal!" Alfie added, waving her hook. <!-- L0612 -->

Pierre reached out, offering a leather-gloved fist, and Alfie bumped her tiny wooden knuckles against his fingers. <!-- L0615 -->

At the back of the auditorium, the final stragglers of the elderly audience tumbled through the open exit doors into the rainy evening, leaving only the dazed Dr. Thorne huddled beside Dravin, staring in wide-eyed shock as the full supernatural truth tore through the veil of mortal reality. <!-- L0617 --> <!-- L0620 -->
