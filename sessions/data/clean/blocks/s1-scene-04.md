## CHAPTER 4: INCURSION AT THE COLONNADE

Adrenaline surged through Eusacles’s veins. <!-- L1120 -->

As the ink blob slithered forward, an instinctive, ancient reflex sparked deep within his chest. A faint, golden radiance flared around his forearms. Before he could consciously register the phenomenon, the heavy metal band of his wristwatch snapped open. With a sharp metallic chime, the watch unwound, spiraling up his wrist and forearm, expanding into thick, interlocking iron links. By the time it reached his clenched fist, the weight coalesced into a spiked, chain-bound morningstar glowing with latent fury. <!-- L1125 -->

Eusacles stared at his armed hand for a split second, a grim grin cutting across his face.

"Well, that's dope," Eusacles muttered. <!-- L1130 -->

He stepped forward and brought the morningstar down in a vicious overhead arc. But the ink blob was unnervingly amorphous; sensing the blow, it squelched sideways across the polished marble. The heavy spiked head smashed into the flagstones, scattering stone chips and ringing through the colonnade. <!-- L1135 -->

"Damn it!" Eusacles grunted, wrenching the weapon free. <!-- L1140 -->

Behind him, Prof Edward Dravin adjusted his wire-rimmed glasses and thrust his open palm forward. He didn't know how he knew the words, but the cadence rose naturally to his lips like an old lecture. <!-- L1145 -->

"Chill Touch!" Dravin commanded. <!-- L1150 -->

Beneath the ink blob, the marble floor fractured with a pale, frost-bitten glow. A spectral, skeletal hand formed from white mist erupted from the stone seams, its bony fingers clamping shut around the ink creature. The creature let out a high-pitched, wet shriek as necrotic frost burned through its viscous form, dragging it halfway down into the floor. <!-- L1200 -->

"Amazing," Dravin whispered, admiring the necrotic frost radiating from his fingertips. <!-- L1234 -->
