## CHAPTER 4: NEEDLE AND CHIME

Before the entrapped ink blob could tear itself free from the skeletal grip in the marble, a faint skittering of carved wood echoed across the rotunda. <!-- L1120 -->

Alfie was on his feet. The tiny driftwood sailor moved with astonishing, jerky speed, his jointed limbs clicking rhythmically against the stone. With a sharp flick of his wrist, he leveled the sharpened sewing needle before him and lunged, his mismatched shell buttons catching the golden sunbeams streaming between the colonnades. <!-- L1125 -->

"Have at thee, beastie!" Alfie chirped, thrusting the needle rapier straight toward the creature's gelatinous core. <!-- L1130 -->

The strike glanced harmlessly across the creature's slick, amorphous skin, failing to bite. The wounded blob thrashed in revulsion, oozing violently sideways to slip out of the calcified skeletal grasp. <!-- L1135 -->

A few paces back, Professor Edward Dravin was still shaking. His breath misted in the unnatural pocket of cemetery cold radiating from his tweed jacket. Desperate to keep the horror from slithering any closer to his trousers, he threw out a trembling hand, snapping his fingers in a frantic, unthinking gesture of dismissal—the same sharp snap he used to quiet dozing undergraduates in his classical antiquities seminars. <!-- L1140 -->

Instead of a sharp click, a heavy, hollow resonance tolled through the rotunda—the mournful chime of a funereal iron bell echoing from an open grave. The stone floor vibrated with the mournful frequency. Yet the dark magic fizzled, dissolving harmlessly into the cold air without biting into the fiend. Dravin let out a ragged gasp, clapping his hand against his tweed lapel as if terrified his own limbs might betray him again. <!-- L1145 --> <!-- L1150 -->

Recovering his footing against the column, Eusacles pivoted with raw athleticism. The heavy iron morningstar whistled through the air, trailing glowing sparks. As the ink blob attempted to dart toward Dravin, Eusacles brought the chained weight down in a crushing, wide-sweeping arc. <!-- L1200 -->

*Crack.*

The spiked iron head struck dead-center. The living tar burst into a shower of harmless, dissipating vapor, leaving only a fading black scorch mark upon the marble flagstones before evaporating entirely into the mountain air. <!-- L1234 -->
