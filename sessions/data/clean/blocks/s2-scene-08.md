## CHAPTER 20: THE NEON MIRAGE IN THE MIST

The Ford truck rumbled through the dense mist of the Lost Roads for hours. Outside the cab windows, reality flickered between rolling Appalachian ridges, vast prairies, and stretches of dark, timeless forest that matched no known American highway. <!-- L1273 -->

Pierre sat in the middle of the bench seat with Alfie perched on the dashboard, peering through the sweeping windshield wipers. "This truck is remarkable. It does not seem to care which decade we are driving through." <!-- L1281 -->

Ahead in the twilight, a glowing island of garish artificial light cut through the gloom. Towering above the fog was a weathered neon sign reading: *24 HR GAS & MART*. Beneath the fluorescent canopy stood four rusted fuel pumps, surrounded by an empty asphalt lot. <!-- L1297 -->

"We need fuel if we're going to make North Carolina," Eusacles noted, pulling the truck alongside the pump. <!-- L1305 -->

"Stay sharp," Dravin warned, peering through his spectacles at the darkened store windows. "We are between jurisdictions of reality out here." <!-- L1313 -->

Pierre hopped down from the cab, brushing dust from his corduroy jacket. "I will go inside to pay. Monsieur Alfie, will you accompany me?" <!-- L1321 -->

"Right behind you, mate," Alfie replied, leaping down into Pierre's coat pocket so only his little driftwood head and rapier pommel poked out. <!-- L1337 -->

They pushed through the glass door of the convenience store. A cheap electronic chime rang out: *Ding-dong*. Behind the counter, a gaunt, hollow-eyed clerk in a faded blue uniform stared at them with unnatural, unblinking intensity. <!-- L1369 -->

"What pump?" the clerk rasped, his voice sounding like dry paper rubbing together. <!-- L1377 -->

"Pump two, please," Pierre said politely, offering a crisp twenty-dollar bill. "And perhaps a map of the interstate?" <!-- L1385 -->

The clerk's gaze slowly dropped from Pierre's face down to the wooden doll nestled in his pocket. A strange, sinister sneer spread across the clerk's pale lips. "That's not a normal toy you got there, boy... Where'd you steal it from?" <!-- L1393 -->
