## CHAPTER 15: THE THRESHOLD OF THE LOST ROADS

"Let's start with the Greek tablet," Eusacles agreed, stretching his arms over his head. <!-- L1273 -->

"Is the French toast ready?" Eusacles called out toward the stove. <!-- L1278 --> <!-- L1280 -->

Pierre carried a platter over with great ceremony, though Mike shook his head with a chuckle. "It's a crepe. I don't know if you missed that, but it is definitely a crepe." <!-- L1282 --> <!-- L1284 -->

Dravin set down his fork, looking at the map. "Regarding this tablet... do we have to figure out a way to get there, or are we driving?" <!-- L1289 --> <!-- L1292 -->

"For North Carolina, you can take the truck," Mike suggested, tossing the keys. <!-- L1295 -->

Pierre eagerly dashed outside and clambered into the vehicle, only to realize he had accidentally gotten in on the driver's side. <!-- L1299 --> <!-- L1301 -->

Mike chuckled from the porch. "Pierre getting in on the driver's side... you know how to drive, right?" <!-- L1305 -->

"I was not expecting the wheel to be here," Pierre said with a flustered cough. "In France, it is normally on the other side." <!-- L1310 -->

Dravin walked up to the open window. "Pierre, I assume you know how to drive stick?" <!-- L1316 -->

"It is like a spatula," Pierre insisted with absolute confidence. "You put it in the right spot, and you flip." <!-- L1318 -->

Pierre turned the ignition key. The engine coughed, sputtered, and died. <!-- L1321 -->

"This truck is broken!" Pierre declared in disgust. He threw open the door, got out, and kicked the front tire. "It does not work! I am turning the key and it doesn't!" <!-- L1323 -->

"Hey, careful now," Mike laughed. "It's an old boy here, you got to take care of it." <!-- L1326 -->

"It is impossible to drive this thing!" Pierre threw his hands up in despair. "No one can do it!" <!-- L1327 -->

Eusacles stepped up with a confident nod. "Yeah, I got this. Dude, put your hand down. Put your puppet hand down." <!-- L1331 --> <!-- L1333 -->

"I'm a good driver!" Alfie insisted proudly. The little wooden sailor jumped down onto the floorboards, planting his driftwood boots onto the gas pedal while Eusacles worked the clutch and wheel. <!-- L1336 --> <!-- L1337 -->

"Has someone here actually driven before?" Eusacles grunted as they rattled down the road. <!-- L1353 -->

They rumbled through the mist until a weathered neon sign flickered through the fog: *24 HR GAS & MART*. <!-- L1369 -->

Pierre hopped out, dusting off his jacket. "I am very curious to see if they will ID you, Alfie." <!-- L1374 -->

"Pierre and Alfie are going in," Dravin noted, watching from the passenger seat. <!-- L1377 -->

The convenience store door chimed with a brassy ding-ling as Pierre pushed it open. <!-- L1381 -->

Behind the counter, an elderly woman in a faded apron turned to them. "Hey honey, how you doing?" <!-- L1383 -->

Pierre bowed with polite French cadence. "Bonjour, madame..."

The clerk squinted through her bifocals, frowning suspiciously. "Ah, we got a Spaniard over here!" <!-- L1387 -->

Her gaze dropped from Pierre's spectacles down to the wooden doll nestled in his pocket. "I gotta say, I've never seen a grown man walk in with a doll before." <!-- L1394 -->
