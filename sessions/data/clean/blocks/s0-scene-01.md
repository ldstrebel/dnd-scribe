## PROLOGUE: NEW LANDS

For eighty years, the maps of Harmony had stood still. <!-- L0004 -->

In the high imperial archives, the brass-bound charts sat behind polished glass—their borders fixed, their margins locked, their equations balanced into quiet, comfortable certainty. To the ministries in the capital, the world was finished. It had been measured, cataloged, and filed away into history. But to those who remembered the old days of sail and steam, that peace felt less like a triumph and more like a slow, stifling stagnation. Without new horizons, civilization was losing its vital pulse. <!-- L0032 --> <!-- L0040 --> <!-- L0042 --> <!-- L0044 --> <!-- L0048 --> <!-- L0050 -->

Beyond the eastern edge of the empire lay the Great Barrier: an endless, churning wall of violent squalls, razor fogs, and shifting magnetic anomalies that imperial dogma declared the impassable boundary of the known world. For two generations, no expedition had returned from its teeth. The frontier had closed. Harmony had stopped looking out. <!-- L0052 --> <!-- L0056 --> <!-- L0058 --> <!-- L0062 --> <!-- L0073 --> <!-- L0078 --> <!-- L0084 --> <!-- L0090 --> <!-- L0093 -->
