## PROLOGUE: NEW LANDS

For eighty years, the maps of Harmony had stood still. <!-- L0004 -->

It was not for lack of trying. In this world, exploration was more than curiosity—it was the source of life itself. Harmony did not thrive on conquest, but on *resonance*: the living, vibrating harmony generated whenever distinct peoples met, learned from one another, and forged lasting connections. Every generation of exploranauts that charted a new horizon brought a new voice into that collective melody, physically enriching the ambient resonance that sustained their communities and kept their world vibrant. Connection was vitality; momentum was life. Never before in recorded history had the continent found *nothing* for such an unnatural span. To a civilization whose very heartbeat depended on forward motion, eighty years without a single new horizon was a quiet, suffocating drought. <!-- L0032 --> <!-- L0040 --> <!-- L0042 --> <!-- L0044 --> <!-- L0048 --> <!-- L0050 -->

The open ocean had always been deadly. Beyond the safety of Harmony's resonant shelf lay a perpetual, choking fog that hung heavy over the waves—an untamed expanse teeming with predatory aether beasts and wild magnetic turbulence that scrambled brass compasses and blinded navigators. For eight decades, every exploratory flotilla sent into that shroud had returned battered, scarred, and empty-handed. The few desolate shoals they managed to chart offered no fertile soil, no living cultures to learn from, and no new resonance to share. The academy charts remained locked behind glass, their borders fixed, their margins waiting for an answer. <!-- L0052 --> <!-- L0056 --> <!-- L0058 --> <!-- L0062 --> <!-- L0073 --> <!-- L0078 --> <!-- L0084 --> <!-- L0090 --> <!-- L0093 -->
