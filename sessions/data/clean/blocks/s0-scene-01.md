## PROLOGUE: NEW LANDS

For eighty years, the brass-bound charts in the imperial archives of Harmony had rested yellow and brittle, their margins fixed, their equations balance-locked into comfortable stasis. <!-- L0004 -->

To the northern magistrates, civilization had achieved eternal equilibrium. But across the uncharted ocean, on the wild southern continent of Octoumba, ancient natural cycles were beginning to freeze. In the dense southern forests of petrified giants, the living rot that nourished the soil had stalled. When things stopped dying, the land began to die. <!-- L0032 --> <!-- L0040 --> <!-- L0042 --> <!-- L0044 --> <!-- L0048 --> <!-- L0050 -->
