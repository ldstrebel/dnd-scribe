## PROLOGUE: NEW LANDS

For eighty years, the maps of Harmony had stood still. <!-- L0004 -->

To an empire whose entire thousand-year history had been propelled by momentum, eighty years was an agonizing eternity. For ten centuries, Harmony had grown like a living tree: every generation had charted a new resonant node, brought an independent province into the fold, and swelled the collective power of the continent through mutual connection. Discovery had not merely been an ambition—it was the steady, vital pulse that kept civilization alive. Never before in recorded history had the empire found *nothing* for such an unnatural span. <!-- L0032 --> <!-- L0040 --> <!-- L0042 --> <!-- L0044 --> <!-- L0048 --> <!-- L0050 -->

Then the world seemed to run dry. For eight long decades, every expedition sent toward the horizon had returned empty-handed. The sea had grown impossibly treacherous—churning with violent squalls, razor fogs, and lethal magnetic shear—while the few desolate shoals and wind-scoured rocks they did manage to reach offered nothing: no life, no fertile soil, and no harmonic resonance to nourish the fading grid. In the high imperial archives, the charts were placed behind polished glass, their borders fixed, their margins balance-locked into sterile certainty. The ministries declared the world finished. The frontier had closed. Harmony had stopped looking out. <!-- L0052 --> <!-- L0056 --> <!-- L0058 --> <!-- L0062 --> <!-- L0073 --> <!-- L0078 --> <!-- L0084 --> <!-- L0090 --> <!-- L0093 -->
