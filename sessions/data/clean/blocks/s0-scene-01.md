## PROLOGUE: NEW LANDS

For eighty years, the imperial archives of Harmony had rested in undisturbed, balance-locked twilight. <!-- L0004 -->

The brass-bound charts had sat yellow and brittle upon their mahogany stands, their margins fixed, their equations balance-locked into comfortable stasis. To the high ministers in the capital, the known world had been thoroughly cataloged, divided into administrative grids, and filed away into history. The grand age of exploration was declared finished; the heroic spirit of the frontier had been quietly replaced by paperwork, quotas, and predictable municipal routines. But to those with saltwater and grease in their veins, that peace felt less like triumph and more like a slow, comfortable decline. <!-- L0032 --> <!-- L0040 --> <!-- L0042 --> <!-- L0044 --> <!-- L0048 --> <!-- L0050 -->

Beyond the eastern horizon lay the Great Barrier: an endless, churning wall of violent fog, razor squalls, and magnetic anomalies that imperial dogma insisted was the impassable edge of the civilized world. For two generations, no vessel had returned from its teeth. The frontier had closed. Harmony had stopped looking out. <!-- L0052 --> <!-- L0056 --> <!-- L0058 --> <!-- L0062 --> <!-- L0073 --> <!-- L0078 --> <!-- L0084 --> <!-- L0090 --> <!-- L0093 -->
