## CHAPTER 40: THE TOME AND THE SEVERED RIFT

A rancid, gray haze billowed along the carpeted floorboards of the auditorium. Seeing the fallen and desperate to halt the slaughter, Dravin reached out with instinctive, unyielding intent to arrest the dying. He knew nothing of incantations, yet in this world, raw intent catalyzed the threshold: from the three-seed signet on his coat lapel, luminous typographic glyphs flared into the air, spelling out the ancient linguistic formula: *S P A R E*. Yet unguided power was perilous—an indiscriminate wave of rot radiated outward, hungry to pull both ally and intruder down into the muck. <!-- L1340 -->

Alfie, whose eyes could perceive the glowing linguistic threads and letters of magic suspended in the air, shoved both stubby porcelain hands upward, his tiny woven fabric twitching with frantic intent. "Wait! Can Alfie twist the fate—can I make it *Spore the Dying* and use a second-level weave?" <!-- L1343 -->

His thread flared blinding violet between his stitched fingers as he snatched the vowel. "Just one letter altered, to make it target only them!" <!-- L1345 -->

"Not us!" Alfie shouted over the rush of spraying water. <!-- L1348 -->

"Them!" <!-- L1350 -->

Twisting the fate thread with surgical speed, Alfie warped the glowing rune from an *A* into an *O*, bending the syntax of reality. The necrotic wave shuddered mid-air. Under the violent tug of Alfie's linguistic twist, the rot refracted, snapping clean away from the companions and coiling exclusively toward the invaders. The gray vapor sank into the flesh of the horn-crowned intruders. Where the mist touched, oily black mushrooms sprouted instantly along their gray knuckles and forearms, weeping dark spores as their skin blistered under the unnatural fungal bloom. <!-- L1355 -->

Horns lowered in panic, the three satyrs wheeled in unison toward their matriarch. The transformed woman slashed her talons downward through the empty air, carving a jagged rift across reality itself. The tear hummed with blinding violet radiance, revealing the churning ether beyond. <!-- L1356 -->

Dravin snapped his fingers, his eyes flashing behind his wire spectacles as he seized the tactical opening. "I am going to hit her with my book!" <!-- L1357 -->

"And remember, Pierre is holding her by her hair still," Pierre called out through gritted teeth, his knuckles white around the woman's thrashing tendrils. <!-- L1358 -->

The woman pulled violently against Pierre's grip, shrieking as the three surviving satyrs hurled themselves headlong into the rift to escape the spreading fungi. <!-- L1359 -->

With a sharp command, Dravin directed the massive leather-bound book downward, channeling scholarly fury into the strike. <!-- L1360 -->

The arcane weight hung in the air. Drawing upon the deepest reserves of his unyielding will, Dravin poured raw scholarly conviction into the descending volume, refusing to let the creature break free into the breach. <!-- L1371 -->

A secondary resonant shockwave gathered around the heavy leather binding, locking the full kinetic fury into place. <!-- L1388 -->

Dravin poured the full measure of his scholarly discipline into the falling lexicon, releasing the kinetic weight. <!-- L1389 -->

The heavy, leather-bound lexicon dropped from the rafters like an iron anvil. <!-- L1392 -->

The three satyrs tumbled through the glowing breach, black mushrooms sprouting across their backs as the threshold snapped shut behind them. Above the struggling woman, Dravin's massive scholarly tome plummeted downward with deafening precision, striking the crown of her skull. <!-- L1394 -->

Her knees buckled. The electric luminescence coursing through her cranial tendrils sputtered and died, fading to a lifeless, chalky gray. She collapsed limp across the auditorium carpet, completely unconscious. With her collapse, the planar rift shuddered and collapsed inward, sealing the portal behind the fleeing satyrs. <!-- L1396 -->

Dravin turned toward the crumpled form of Eusacles, whose breathing had grown shallow and ragged beside the stage steps. Dravin extended a firm palm. "Fine, I will cast *Spare the Dying*." A soft pulse of stabilizing warmth washed through the bronze-skinned warrior, steadying his heartbeat. <!-- L1401 -->

Pierre knelt beside the unconscious woman, turning a coarse clump of tendrils in his fingers to understand what he had been trying to cut. Brandishing his comb with theatrical scrutiny, though his chest heaved and cold sweat tracked down his jaw—the sheer horror of the monstrous nightmare held at bay only by the razor-thin armor of his flamboyant vanity. "Oh my god, at first it was just your hair. Your hair was absolute grace, but now you have black fungal heads popping out everywhere. I understand this was an intensely stressful evening, but we need to get you into a proper bath immediately—preferably one with an iron cage around it so I can finish your styling." Even as he complained, his hands worked with swift, practical competence, binding her wrists tightly behind her back with heavy utility cord. <!-- L1408 -->

The combat ceased. The chamber went deathly still save for the persistent patter of water droplets dripping from ceiling fixtures. <!-- L1409 -->

Out on the distant city streets, the high-pitched wails of emergency sirens began echoing between university towers. Thirty seconds had elapsed from the first shattered window to the final blow. <!-- L1411 -->

Alfie's celestial halo faded into faint steam, and the spectral broadsword dissipated into cold air. <!-- L1412 -->

The fire suppression sprinklers sputtered to a halt, leaving the battered auditorium drenched in mist and smelling of ozone and crushed mushrooms. <!-- L1414 -->

With the woman knocked unconscious, the psychic fog in their minds evaporated. A sudden rush of warmth flooded Pierre and Eusacles as their stolen memories surged back into clarity. <!-- L1418 -->

Pierre blinked, a hand flying to his chest. "Wait... the chateau! The memory of the seaside chateau is back!" <!-- L1423 -->

Eusacles groaned, sitting up slowly while rubbing his aching ribs. "I too almost lost the sacred memory of my first true love." <!-- L1424 -->

He sighed, a reverent glint in his bronze eyes. "Nineteen fifty-six. A nineteen fifty-six Indian motorcycle." <!-- L1426 -->

Pierre paused, arching an eyebrow. "That was your first love? Well. Very pretty. Very American." <!-- L1428 -->

Pierre stood up, wiping damp silt from his sleeves. "Pierre is going to locate a janitor's cart." <!-- L1433 -->

"We will throw her inside the rolling trash can and shut the lid," Pierre declared, gesturing toward the unconscious captive. <!-- L1435 -->

"Then we can inspect the architecture studio, collect our bearings, and hit the road before those sirens arrive." <!-- L1437 -->
