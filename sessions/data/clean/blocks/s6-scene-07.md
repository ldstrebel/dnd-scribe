## CHAPTER 38: THE SPECTRAL HAIR TIE & THE LOST CRUSH

Pierre lunged across the flooded floorboards, driving the bronze head of his museum spear into the beast's flank. "I do not like the brain eating," Pierre announced with a shudder of profound disgust. "That is exceedingly repulsive. Here is a gift for you!" <!-- L1042 -->

He yanked hard to wrench the weapon free, but the brittle ancient bronze gave way with a sickening metallic snap. The shaft pulled back empty in his fist; the spearhead remained lodged in the beast's hide.

"What a piece of junk!" Pierre muttered in disbelief, tossing the splintered shaft aside. Fumbling at his belt pouches, his fingers caught the stopper of an amber glass flask. "Let us see what this will do." <!-- L1071 -->

Pierre unstopped the amber vial as he descended from the podium, pouring the concentrated etching acid straight down into the writhing tangle of brain tendrils. The corrosive chemical sizzled against the dark flesh, searing several coils into smoking ash. But the torrential deluge from the emergency sprinklers washed over the woman's head simultaneously, rapidly diluting the acid in the cascading water before it could eat into the bone. <!-- L1082 -->

"I genuinely believed a proper conditioning rinse would be of service to you," Pierre grumbled, wiping sulfuric residue from his sleeve. "Instead, it has merely produced unruly smoke!" <!-- L1083 -->

The woman snarled, chanting guttural planar syllables as her remaining tendrils whipped around Eusacles's throat, desperate to complete the extraction. <!-- L1084 -->

Beside the desk, Alfie's carved fingers snapped with sudden inspiration. "Right!" the driftwood duelist shouted over the rushing spray. "*Mage Hand!* But not a hand, mate. A *Mage Band!* A proper hair scrunchie to tie these filthy tendrils away into a tidy bun!" <!-- L1087 --> <!-- L1089 --> <!-- L1091 -->

A shimmering loop of spectral lavender energy materialized above the stage, stretching wide like an elastic hair tie. <!-- L1094 -->

"Corral the coils, Pierre!" Alfie called out. <!-- L1101 -->

Pierre leaped straight into the thrashing storm of tendrils, batting them inward with his hands. "Your hair is an absolute disaster!" Pierre barked, physically corralling the flailing whips toward the glowing band. "Alfie has provided you with a magnificent accessory! Show some gratitude!" <!-- L1104 --> <!-- L1106 -->

With clockwork, pinpoint dexterity, Alfie snapped the spectral band shut. But as Pierre grabbed the final tendril, an agonizing jolt of raw psychic feedback ripped backward into his nervous system, blasting through his skull with searing neural shock. <!-- L1109 -->

Pierre stumbled backward, clutching his temples as a dizzying temporal vacuum tore through his mind. A precious, golden memory—his first adolescent crush, Janette, strolling through the sun-dappled grounds of the chateau outside Paris—began to unravel. The tactile warmth of her hand, the fragrance of lavender, the sound of her laughter in the courtyard—all dissolved like salt in water. In their place remained only cold, sterile phosphor: the distant, detached recollection of a stranger's username on an internet forum, chatting through social media across an empty screen. <!-- L1111 --> <!-- L1112 -->

"Janette!" Pierre wailed in tragicomic despair, dropping to one knee as he gripped his black beret. "Our afternoon at the chateau! Where has it gone?! It has become a message board!" <!-- L1113 -->

Yet Alfie's spectral band held true. The luminous elastic snapped tight around the woman's scalp, bundling her thrashing brain tendrils into an immaculately restrained topknot bun. Magically bound and suffocated, the coiled tentacles fell limp, completely severed from Eusacles's head. <!-- L1118 -->

"Thanks, buddy," Eusacles gasped, rubbing his bruised throat as he staggered back onto the steps. <!-- L1119 -->

"Disgusting," Alfie muttered in satisfaction. <!-- L1120 -->

"Appreciate that," Alfie chirped back toward Eusacles. <!-- L1122 -->

"No problem," Eusacles rumbled. <!-- L1123 -->

With the tendrils contained, Alfie scurried beneath the heavy teacher's desk, slipping through the circular cable-management hole to drop into the dry recess below. <!-- L1125 --> <!-- L1129 --> <!-- L1133 -->

On the opposite flank, Professor Dravin stepped forward, his eyes alight with divine resolve. Raising his tarnished silver bell, he invoked threshold judgment. "*Spiritual Weapon!*" <!-- L1137 -->

A glowing spectral manifestation of force materialized in mid-air beside the satyrs. Rather than a classical sword or heavy mace, the divine force condensed into a massive, leather-bound scholarly volume—an encyclopedia bound in heavy hide, its dense vellum pages fluttering with crackling violet threshold authority. <!-- L1159 -->

"A scholarly instrument," Dravin announced with stern academic gravity, directing the hovering volume to strike with focused threshold force. <!-- L1161 --> <!-- L1163 -->

The heavy tome whistled through the mist, slamming with concussive, righteous impact across the nearest satyr's brow. <!-- L1167 -->

Dravin flicked his fingers, pivoting on the wet tiles as he prepared to sweep the hovering tome across the advancing flank. <!-- L1171 --> <!-- L1173 -->
