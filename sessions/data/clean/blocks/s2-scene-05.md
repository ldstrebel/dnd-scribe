## CHAPTER 12: COURTYARD CLUSTERS & HIGH-HOUSE ROW

Around the warmth of the roaring blaze, the circle settled into uneasy familiarity. Serra Vox flipped open her compact notebook, pen poised like a surgeon's scalpel as she studied Britt and Aggie. <!-- L0465 -->

"Why did you say living in the capital was unfortunate?" Britt asked, leaning her heavy shoulders against a weathered pine timber. "Isn't the Seat of Harmony where everyone wants to go?" <!-- L0467 --> <!-- L0469 -->

Serra glanced down at her boots, a shadow passing over her usually manic features. "There is... immense political pressure there," she said quietly, her voice losing its academic bounce. "The weight of expectations, the lineages, the old families constantly maneuvering for battery contracts and prestige. It is exhausting. I far prefer the frontier settlements where people care about practical matters rather than genealogies." <!-- L0470 --> <!-- L0472 -->

"Why were you there then?" Britt pressed.

Serra gave a tight, evasive little smile, snapping her notebook shut with a sharp *clack*. "Everyone finds a reason to pass through the Seat eventually. But look—the fire is marvelous, isn't it?" She sprang to her feet, visibly eager to escape the personal interrogation, and hurried toward the hearth where Cassius was still hovering near the logs. "Cassius! Did you arrange this yourself?" <!-- L0482 -->

"He did," Ignatius called out from across the circle, flashing a wide, mischievous grin. "Did it pretty much all on his own. Natural pyrotechnic genius." <!-- L0483 -->

"Couldn't have managed without him," Lomi echoed, winking. "We just provided moral support." <!-- L0486 --> <!-- L0488 -->

Cassius blushed furiously, bowing with stiff formality before retreating toward the far side of the timber to avoid Serra's gaze. Ignatius ambled over to where the young scholar sat trembling, leaning down with an exaggeratedly confidential whisper. <!-- L0520 --> <!-- L0523 -->

"Hey," Ignatius murmured, elbowing the boy lightly. "I overheard her talking to the girls. She said she's obsessed with anthropology. That means she's really into ants. You should go tell her about ants." <!-- L0524 -->

Cassius looked up, horrified. "That... that is fundamentally not what anthropology means." <!-- L0525 -->

"Hey, don't overthink it, kid," Ignatius chuckled. "Just go strike up a conversation. Work with what you've got." <!-- L0526 --> <!-- L0530 -->

Lomi leaned in from the other side, patting the scholar's shoulder. "He's right, Cassius. There's definitely a spark between you two." <!-- L0537 --> <!-- L0539 -->

Cassius gasped, slapping at his woolen coat in panic. "A spark?! Did you push me too close to the coals? Am I burning?" <!-- L0540 --> <!-- L0542 -->

"You're fine, kid," Lomi laughed, catching him by the collar before he tumbled into the ash. "Just an expression. Relax." <!-- L0544 -->

With Cassius sufficiently flustered and Serra furiously sketching log angles, the newcomers took stock of the wider quad. Beyond the warm perimeter of Block 99's pit, the campus was divided into distinct social currents. To the south, where the industrial docks met the canal, a loud gathering of roughnecks and grease-monkeys had surrounded a crateside vendor. Above the din, a familiar, fast-talking voice cut through the damp air like a rusted saw blade—Lucky, standing atop an overturned munitions case, pulling gleaming trinkets from a greasy leather satchel. <!-- L0545 --> <!-- L0547 --> <!-- L0551 -->

"Look at that commotion over by the bluffs," Ignatius said suddenly, his attention shifting past the peddler toward the dark cliffs on the western rim. <!-- L0554 --> <!-- L0556 -->

Just beyond the reach of the firelight, a sharp altercation had broken out beneath a cluster of iron lampposts. A tall, impeccably tailored gentleman was waving a silver-headed cane in the face of a hulking, clockwork automaton whose brass chassis was scratched and soot-stained. Beside them, a wiry figure resembling woven vines was gesturing wildly, while a young woman with stark platinum-blonde hair stood between them, her voice cutting through the mist like cold glass.

"Hey, Lomi," Ignatius muttered, his embers brightening along his shoulders. "You hearing that?" <!-- L0562 --> <!-- L0564 -->

"Hard not to," Lomi replied, eyes narrowing as he adjusted his flat cap. "Looks like a proper scrap brewing." <!-- L0565 -->

Behind them, Britt and Aggie exchanged a glance. Aggie tilted her red cap toward the lampposts, curious and alert. <!-- L0566 -->

"Let's see what's got them stirred up," Britt said, stepping away from the fire. "Where there's shouting, there's usually something people want to hide." <!-- L0567 --> <!-- L0569 --> <!-- L0570 -->
