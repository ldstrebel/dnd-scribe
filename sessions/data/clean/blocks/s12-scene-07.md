## CHAPTER 105: NO ONE SAW A FLARE

Squad 907 was trailing the Spirit Tortoise along the basin beach when a sudden flash of vibrant electric-blue hair swept across Britt's vision.

Before Britt could raise her hands, she was enveloped in a fierce, crushing embrace that smelled faintly of sweet river reeds and canyon silt.

"Britt!" Rill cried out, squeezing her sister with breathless intensity. "I've been asking everywhere! No one saw a flare—not a single person! Did you not use it?!" <!-- L0561 -->

Britt blinked in sudden bewilderment, her stomach dropping into her boots as she looked over at Ignatius jogging up with Loami and Aggie. "Did we... did we have a flare?" Britt asked softly, a sheepish flush rising on her cheeks. "Oh shoot." <!-- L0563 --> <!-- L0574 -->

Ignatius threw his hands up in a placating gesture, embers flickering at his cuffs. "Well, look," Ignatius offered defensively. "The idea was that we would fire the flare if we ever got overwhelmed. But once we were in the thick of it, we were kind of... *whelmed*, not overwhelmed. We were just surviving! So we might have completely forgotten the flare existed." <!-- L0575 -->

Rill pulled back, staring at the two of them with wide, incredulous eyes. "You're an idiot," Rill muttered, shaking her head in disbelief before a grin broke through her exhaustion. "You're complete idiots—and you're brilliant, because you actually survived! No one should have made it through that storm! The odds were terrible!" <!-- L0578 -->

Britt gestured toward Ignatius with a proud grin. "Well, you have to know that Natty over here crafted a perfect fire lasso out of thin air, and it really, really helped." <!-- L0581 -->

Rill raised an eyebrow, crossing her arms. "I've never really understood how those things are useful in hunting, to be completely honest, but that sounds pretty cool."

"And I don't know who said you can't herd wild felines," Ignatius added with a proud swagger, "because she definitely herded some cats." <!-- L0585 -->

Britt smiled, then grabbed Rill's sleeve and pointed toward the lumbering form plodding steadily across the sand ahead of them. "Rill... do you see the tortoise in front of us? Can you see that too?" <!-- L0588 -->

Rill turned, following Britt's finger toward the massive creature. Her eyes went wide. "Whoa... I mean, yeah. What is that?" <!-- L0589 -->

"What does it look like, girl?" Britt laughed softly.

"No, but where are you all going?" Rill asked, frowning as she watched the squad march in a perfectly straight line behind the beast. "Why are you following it so strictly?"

"Isn't that your god turtle thing?" Ignatius asked, glancing between Britt and Rill.

Rill stared at the glowing lichen veins pulsing along the dark carapace. The color drained from her face as ancient clan lore surged back into her memory.

"Mwaza-Kasa..." Rill whispered, using the sacred dialect of the elders. "I... I recognize that as something I was taught to avoid at all possible costs. Why is it here? Should that even be outside the grove?!"

"Exactly," Britt said firmly. "That's why we're following it." <!-- L0615 -->

Rill's expression hardened with sudden, frantic urgency. She grabbed Britt by the wrist, pulling her toward the northern pier. "Okay, it is even more important that I get you to Professor Ink right now! Please, come with me!" <!-- L0616 -->

"Where are we going?" Britt asked, stumbling slightly as Rill dragged her across the sand.

"Look over there," Rill said, pointing toward a colossal, four-legged mechanical walker perched near the harbor docks, its steel plating bearing a painted quill sigil. "Professor Ink is up in her walker! This is crazy—this is part of her entire theory about the resonance decay!"

Ignatius jogged up beside them. "Maybe we split up," Ignatius proposed. "That way some of us go to the professor, and the rest of us keep following the god turtle so we don't lose its trail." <!-- L0619 -->

Loami nodded in agreement. "Hey, that's also great news—if the god turtle does anything crazy, we still have that flare!"

"That's why we saved it," Britt said with a smirk.

"I'll go with whoever follows the god turtle," Ignatius offered. "If something happens, I can fly back and find the walker." <!-- L0635 -->

"Wait," Loami called out to Rill. "Is Professor Kante up there in the walker?"

"No," Rill answered over her shoulder, "but we have the communication stones!"

Ignatius clapped his hands, trying to coordinate the split. "All right, Aggie, Britt—which one of you wants to follow the god turtle?"

Rill looked at Britt. "Britt, you should come to Professor Ink. She might know something about that fungal rash spreading on your skin."

Britt grimaced, glancing back at the tortoise's steady stride. "Yeah... it's a hard sell to leave this turtle, honestly. But you're right. Remind me about my imminent rash, and I'll go to the professor." <!-- L0657 -->

Ignatius nodded, then pointed between Britt and Aggie, squinting in confusion. "All right... so Britt follows the turtle, and Aggie goes to the walker?"

Britt frowned. "Wait, am I following the god turtle or is Aggie?" <!-- L0675 -->

"Sorry, Aggie," Ignatius muttered, rubbing the back of his neck. "Sorry. You two look identical to me right now." <!-- L0677 -->

Across the circle, Pip gasped in utter scandal. "Have you never seen a mushroom turtle before?!" Pip squeaked. "That's so rude!" <!-- L0683 --> <!-- L0690 -->

Before Ignatius could apologize, Pip leapt into the air and smacked him squarely across the nose with both tiny hands.

"Natty is not dealing with loss well," Loami sighed, shaking his head.

"No, I do not deal with loss well at all," Ignatius admitted sheepishly, holding his sore nose. "I apologize. Clearly different colors." <!-- L0696 -->

With the plan finalized, Squad 907 split their forces: Ignatius, Iggy, and Aggie remained on the trail of Mwaza-Kasa, while Loami and Britt sprinted across the docks behind Rill toward the towering walker.
