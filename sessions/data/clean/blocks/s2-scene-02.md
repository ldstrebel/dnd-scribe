## CHAPTER 14: AURAS OF THE DISPLACED

Eusacles narrowed his eyes, feeling an unnatural prickle at the base of his skull. As the demigod son of Thanatos, death and the divine had always carried a subtle weight in his blood, but here in the Margin, that instinct buzzed like a live wire. <!-- L0515 -->

Closing his eyes for a quiet moment, Eusacles tapped into his divine senses, letting his awareness wash over the circle of firelight. <!-- L0530 -->

The metaphysical aura around the party was startlingly dense. Beneath Professor Dravin’s scholarly tweed and mild manners hummed a steady, chilling resonance of the underworld—a necrotic tether of preservation and threshold magic. Around young Pierre, however, the radiance was far more complex: a swirling duality of monstrous fiendish blood wrapped in an odd, serpentine celestial hum. <!-- L0550 -->

Eusacles opened his eyes, staring between Dravin and Pierre in open fascination. "Well. None of us are zombies, if anyone was worried. But we are definitely not normal tourists." <!-- L0570 -->

"What did you see?" Pierre asked, adjusting his round glasses with a slight tilt of his beret. <!-- L0590 -->

"You've got some heavy divine static on you, Frenchie," Eusacles smirked, crossing his arms over his chest. "Like celestial meets something ancient and scaly. And the professor over here feels like he's been auditing classes in the underworld." <!-- L0610 -->

Dravin offered a gentle, self-deprecating smile. "My great-grandmother always warned me that Greek bloodlines carry unexpected legacies. I suppose teaching mythology for twenty years was merely my subconscious catching up to my pedigree." <!-- L0625 -->
