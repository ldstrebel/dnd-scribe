## CHAPTER 37: THE MOTORCYCLE MEMORY & THE ACID FLASK

Beside the desk, a bloated ink blot lunged from the puddle, its amorphous tentacles reaching for Dr. Thorne's briefcase. Alfie sidestepped with Cockney swagger, humming the bouncy brass melody of *All Star* under her breath as she drove her sewing-needle rapier downward. The steel needle cleaved the black globule in two, dissolving it into harmless dark puddle-foam. <!-- L0903 -->

Across the stage, Professor Dravin extended his palm, guiding the column of lunar radiance to form a defensive wall along the rear flank. "A perimeter shield," Dravin called out, his voice cutting through the spray. "*Moonbeam* anchored." <!-- L0954 -->

The lunar pillar blazed with silver fire, delivering searing radiant punishment across the closing infiltrators. The satyr vanguard staggered back, scorched and flailing in the beam. <!-- L0958 --> <!-- L0963 --> <!-- L0966 -->

Enraged by the burning light, the remaining beasts charged the platform in violent retaliation. One lunged for Alfie, but the driftwood duelist danced lithely across a chair back to the rhythm of her cassette tape. Two mind-numb ink blots blundered blindly into the moonbeam and were annihilated on contact, bursting into harmless soot that melted into the flooded tiles. <!-- L0972 --> <!-- L0980 -->

Then the transformed Medusa turned her slitted, glowing gaze upon the stage. Ignoring Pierre's insults, her hissing coils whipped forward, striking Eusacles across the chest with concussive psychic force. <!-- L0982 -->

The heavy impact tore through Eusacles's defenses. His aviator sunglasses flew from his face, and his knees buckled. *Good Vibes* crashed onto the wet floorboards as Eusacles collapsed unconscious, his eyes rolling back in his skull. <!-- L0987 -->

A sickening, reality-warping vacuum flared around his brow. Tendrils of pale neural light were wrenched from his temples, coiling into the Medusa's outstretched palms. <!-- L0991 --> <!-- L0993 -->

Through the psychic rupture, an entire year of Eusacles's mortal existence condensed and collapsed—months of memories, nights on the highway, drifting through Nevada towns—all compressed with brutal temporal force into a single, isolated relic: the chrome gear shift of his first 1956 Indian motorcycle. <!-- L0994 --> <!-- L0999 -->

At the edge of the stage, the dimensional rift began to shudder and flicker, its planar stability collapsing under the weight of the extracted memory. <!-- L1004 -->

The satyrs looked to their leader in hesitation, but the woman bared her fangs in desperate resolve. "We will just have to do it here!" she commanded, her brain tendrils coiling tighter around Eusacles's fallen form to finish extracting the memory before the rift sealed. <!-- L1007:woman --> <!-- L1016 -->

Pierre watched in cold, suffocating dread as the hissing tendrils concentrated over Eusacles's head like hungry leeches. He had crossed paths with grotesque creatures before, and he knew how narrow the boundary between life and horror truly was—every survival instinct screamed that his own mind was next. His chest constricted with near-paralyzing panic. But Pierre drew a sharp breath, forced his trembling shoulders back, and let his cultivated mask of effortless vanity lock into place. <!-- L1024 -->

"Eating memories is thoroughly repulsive," Pierre declared, his aristocratic drawl masking the adrenaline hammering in his throat. "And far worse than anything my javelin could suffer." <!-- L1025 -->

He glanced down at the flooded floor, observing how the strange blue energy rippled across the water like an alien frequency rather than mundane lightning. <!-- L1026 -->

Pierre stepped quietly around the flailing tendrils and reached the heavy oak lectern. He scrambled atop the wooden podium, his boots finding purchase on the slanted shelf. With theatrical momentum, Pierre leaped from the height of the rostrum directly toward the woman's head, brandishing an amber vial of corrosive museum etching acid. <!-- L1033 --> <!-- L1036 --> <!-- L1040 -->
