## CHAPTER 11: THE ROAD TO THE MARIGOLD

From down near Eusacles's boots, a tiny wooden hand brandished a needle rapier with an impatient swish. <!-- L1957 -->

"I'm four feet below you, mate!" a sharp, raspy Cockney voice piped up from the grass. "Somebody's got to pick me up if you're trying to talk to me!" <!-- L1992 -->

Eusacles frowned, leaning down to inspect the tiny speaker. The moment his face drew near, the cold, sharp point of the sewing needle gently pressed against the skin of his cheek. <!-- L1998 -->

"Yeah, me," the doll muttered, its shell buttons clicking as it stood tall. "I know things. I do. I know the dark." <!-- L2007 -->

Before Eusacles could recoil, the doll suddenly went limp, toppling sideways into the clover. <!-- L2015 -->

Dravin immediately leaned down, tapping the wooden chest with gentle medical sternum rubs. "Come on back now, buddy..." <!-- L2030 -->

With a sharp click, the doll sprang back to life, immediately swinging its tiny needle wildly at the tall stalks of crabgrass like a miniature lawnmower. <!-- L2040 -->

"You're half-bloods, right?" Eusacles asked, staring between Dravin and Pierre. "Like, you're half-bloods too?" <!-- L2053 -->

"On my mother's side," Dravin replied thoughtfully, "her great-grandmother was Greek. That is why I study Greek mythology." <!-- L2069 -->

"Have you ever had your class come to life before?" Pierre asked. <!-- L2071 -->

"I like to think every time I teach, the class comes to life," Dravin smiled warmly. <!-- L2072 -->

A distant sound cut through the crickets—a faint, echoing chuckle carried on the night breeze down the fence line. Pierre and Eusacles both turned their heads toward the sound. <!-- L2100 -->

"Did you hear that?" Eusacles whispered. <!-- L2102 -->

Pierre knelt and gently offered his open hand to the swinging driftwood doll. "Would you like a ride?" <!-- L2110 -->

The doll stopped swiping at the grass and looked up at Pierre with its coral eyes. "A ride would be grand. Thanks, mate." <!-- L2118 -->
