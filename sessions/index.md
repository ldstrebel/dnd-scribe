# Session Index: The Margin

## Arc 1: The Weave & The Lost Roads

### [[s1-clean-story|Session 1: The Bus from Vegas & The Library of the Fates]]
**Date:** Aug 20, 2026  
**Game Master:** Luke Foreman (`Luke F`)  
**Party:** Pierre (Luke Strebel), Prof. Edward Dravin (William Webb), Eusacles (John Hagey), Alfie (Sophie Foreman Noone)

Displaced from a late-night Greyhound bus crossing the Mojave Desert, four strangers awaken in the colossal marble Library of the Three Fates. When planar ink-beasts tear through the fabric of reality, the party fends them off with emergent divine powers and is cast into the timeless sanctuary of the Margin.

**Key Events:**
- Dimensional rift swallows the Vegas Greyhound transit.
- Awakening before Clotho, Lachesis, and Atropos at their cosmic loom.
- Combat against planar ink-beasts and horned demons in the colonnade.
- Pierre discovers his cryptic heritage among ancient scrolls.
- Alfie the driftwood doll awakens with Cockney ferocity and a fishing-hook strike.
- Re-emergence on a desolate desert highway and arrival at the weathered gates of The Margin.

### [[s2-clean-story|Session 2: The Margin, The Fragments & The Lost Roads]]
**Date:** Aug 28, 2026  
**Game Master:** Luke Foreman (`Luke F`)  
**Party:** Pierre (Luke Strebel), Prof. Edward Dravin (William Webb), Eusacles (John Hagey), Alfie (Sophie Foreman Noone)

The party is welcomed into the Appalachian refuge of The Margin by caretaker Theodore. After surviving a tense nocturnal "corrector drill," Naomi enlists the party to recover physical Fragments of altered timelines ("fresh ink"). Piloting a weathered 1978 farm truck through the interdimensional mist of the Lost Roads, the group makes an eerie pit stop where Pierre's dormant Gorgon heritage surfaces in a petrifying confrontation.

**Key Events:**
- Gathering at the campfire; Theodore explains the 1846 founding and ageless nature of The Margin.
- Eusacles uses Divine Sense to analyze the party's celestial, necrotic, and fiendish auras.
- Rosa interviews Alfie regarding his fragmented memories of dark stormy seas and cannons.
- Naomi leads a blackout "corrector drill" as shadowy ink anomalies prowl the perimeter.
- Naomi explains the cosmic crisis of altered time Fragments and targets a Greek tablet in North Carolina.
- The team takes the 1978 Ford truck through the mist of the Lost Roads.
- Pierre's protective gaze triggers a petrifying paralysis on a sinister gas station clerk.
- The party speeds into the night toward Raleigh, North Carolina.

### [[s3-clean-story|Session 3: The Museum Heist in North Carolina]]
**Date:** Sep 1, 2026  
**Game Master:** Luke Foreman (`Luke F`)  
**Party:** Pierre (Luke Strebel), Prof. Edward Dravin (William Webb), Eusacles (John Hagey), Alfie (Sophie Foreman Noone)

Arriving in Raleigh, the party executes a nighttime infiltration of the North Carolina Museum of Natural History to recover a fractured Greek limestone stele. Using Alfie's Wordcraft to manifest spectral sheep as a distraction, Pierre poses as a late-night archival intern in a stolen lab coat. Breaching the tempered glass display in the Mediterranean wing, Pierre matches the recovered stone Fragment into the ancient tablet, fusing the broken inscription, stabilizing the timeline resonance, and escaping into the Carolina night.

**Key Events:**
- Arrival in Raleigh; casing the Museum of Natural History and the Mediterranean Antiquities exhibit.
- Dravin and Eusacles identify security cameras, roving guard patrols, and the 6:00 PM closing timeline.
- Alfie uses Wordcraft (*Sleep* $\rightarrow$ *Sheep*) to unleash spectral livestock, distracting guard Thomas while Pierre swipes his blue security badge.
- Infiltrating the preservation workshop; Pierre disguises himself in an oversized archival lab coat and bluffs receptionist Nincy as a first-day Sorbonne intern.
- Disengaging magnetic locks on the centerpiece display case.
- Pierre inserts the recovered limestone Fragment into the jagged void of the stele.
- The stone fuses with radiant golden energy, completing the ancient Gorgon hero inscription and quenching the temporal anomaly.
- The party wraps the stabilized artifact and escapes onto the Lost Roads back to The Margin.

### [[s4-clean-story|Session 4: The Medusa Protocol & The Gorgon Ambush]]
**Date:** Sep 10, 2026  
**Game Master:** Luke Foreman (`Luke F`)  
**Party:** Pierre (Luke Strebel), Prof. Edward Dravin (William Webb), Eusacles (John Hagey), Alfie (Sophie Foreman Noone)

As the party attempts to decode the ancient Mediterranean stele inside the North Carolina Museum of History, four identical security operatives named "Gordon" drop their human disguises, revealing living viper crowns and calcifying petrification weapons. Pierre, Dravin, Alfie, and Eusacles mount an unorthodox defense utilizing extension cords, baguette-traps, Wordcraft sleep-to-sheep rewrites, and ceramic urn drops from the mezzanine. After reversing the attendant's petrification and escaping the cordoned museum onto the Lost Roads, the party returns to The Marigold hearth in The Margin to regroup and plan their next expedition.

**Key Events:**
- The Medusa Protocol detonates: four identical snake-crowned operatives named Gordon ambush the party at the stele vitrine.
- Gordon operatives shoot ink sludge pellets, petrifying a museum attendant into solid stone.
- Pierre and Alfie counter with an improvised extension-cord whip, baguette decoys, and Athenian sleep spells.
- Alfie drops terracotta pots from the mezzanine while Dravin bludgeons petrifiers with a heavy flashlight.
- Pierre taps the limestone resonance to unpetrify the museum attendant.
- The party retreats through the broom closet threshold back into the Appalachian mist of the Lost Roads.
- Debrief at The Marigold diner over rum pancakes and Naomi's true-crime podcast on Flight 422.
- The party weighs their next target between a rogue physician's facility and an airport hijacking.

### [[s5-clean-story|Session 5: The Forgotten Trail & The Mad Doctor's Lecture]]
**Date:** Sep 18, 2026  
**Game Master:** Luke Foreman (`Luke F`)  
**Party:** Pierre (Luke Strebel), Prof. Edward Dravin (William Webb), Eusacles (John Hagey), Alfie (Sophie Foreman Noone)

The party enjoys a brief respite at the Margin hearth over spiced rum crepes before venturing into the dense Appalachian fog along the uncharted Forgotten Trail. After encountering a spectral child whose imaginary toy causes phantom shockwaves through the ancient timber canopy, the companions infiltrate a prestigious northeastern academic consortium where Dr. Aris Thorne is lecturing on her miraculous medical breakthrough. Coordinating a chaotic academic distraction at the standing receiver regarding 1940s refrigeration timelines, the party tricks Dr. Thorne into handing over the key to her padlocked briefcase. When Alfie touches the buckram-bound primary trial binder, reality shatters into a horrific temporal vision of a clandestine 1948 subterranean ward where an ink-stained hand rewrites "STABLE" to "STALE". Snapping back into the lecture hall amidst blaring alarms and stampeding undergraduates, the companions find themselves cornered by towering, ram-horned beasts crashing through the rear doors.

**Key Events:**
- Morning downtime at the Margin hearth: Pierre perfects spiced rum crepes with Mike, Dravin refines necrotic threshold variations (*spare* $\rightarrow$ *spore*), and Hermes delivers an urgent sovereign missive.
- Treking the Forgotten Trail through the Lost Roads; an encounter with a translucent ghost boy offering an invisible toy triggers phantom giant shockwaves through the forest canopy.
- Arrival at the university consortium; confronting picketers waving "Think About It" placards and conspiracy brochures.
- Infiltrating the lecture hall: Pierre pesters graduate attendants for sculpture atelier directions, while Eusacles holds court in the rear rows.
- The distraction: Eusacles takes the standing receiver to aggressively challenge Dr. Thorne's historical refrigeration timeline, while Pierre and Dravin play the roles of eccentric heckler and conciliatory department chair.
- Shamed by the academic challenge, Dr. Thorne hands Dravin the brass key to retrieve her raw 1948 trial notes from her padlocked leather briefcase.
- Behind the podium kneehole, Alfie and Dravin unlock the briefcase; Alfie's driftwood touch triggers an active Fragment vision.
- The Vision: A 1940s basement laboratory filled with dazed, comatose test subjects strapped to iron cots; an ink-stained hand rewrites the trial ledger from *STABLE* to *STALE*, followed by the physician violently smashing his equipment in remorse.
- Reality snaps back as fire klaxons scream and panicked students stampede the exits.
- Three towering, ram-horned cloaked beasts smash through the lobby doors—the ambush is sprung.
