# TH-09: The Hunter Clock & Majora
**Codex Category:** Active Mystery
**Type:** 🌟 Main Story / Threat
**Status:** 🚨 Active Threat
**Origin:** Origin: Session 8

## 🔍 Core Mystery Question
The Stalker in the Ink: What is Majora, and how much time does the party have before the ticking Hunter Clock runs out?

## 🏛️ Established In-World Evidence
Teddy and Naomi revealed three ticking clocks governing the heist: finding the handcuffs before evidence moves, avoiding the precinct lockdown, and the Hunter Clock. The Hunter Clock tracks Majora—a faceless terror that stalks anyone collecting severed pieces of the Loom. During transit across Lost Creek on the boatman's skiff, ink-blot creatures attacked; touching the black ink ticked the first notch on the Hunter Clock.

## 💡 Party Hypotheses & Inquiries
The party deduces that Majora is an ancient executioner awakened whenever stolen threads of reality are moved, and that the handcuffs must be extracted before it arrives.
