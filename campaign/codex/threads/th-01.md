# TH-01: Alfie's Origin
**Codex Category:** Active Mystery
**Type:** 🛡️ PC Quest (Alfie)
**Status:** 🔍 Active
**Origin:** Origin: Session 1

## 🔍 Core Mystery Question
The Driftwood Construct & The Burning Coast: Who carved the driftwood construct, and whose memories of a burning coastline are trapped in her cedar grain?

## 🏛️ Established In-World Evidence
Crafted from weathered coastal driftwood and drowned sailors' cloaks in the Library of the Three Fates. Carries 458 years of memory embedded in her timber. Touching ancient sea-carved stone triggers terrifying sensory visions—freezing saltwater, silent oarsmen in the dark, and a coastal village burning to the waterline. Wields the Fates' editing magic (Wordcraft).

## 💡 Party Hypotheses & Inquiries
The party suspects her wood was salvaged from an ancient flagship whose history was cut from the Weave by Redactors.
