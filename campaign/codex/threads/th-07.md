# TH-07: Physical Anchors
**Codex Category:** Active Mystery
**Type:** 🗺️ Side Inquiry
**Status:** 🔍 Active
**Origin:** Origin: Session 2

## 🔍 Core Mystery Question
Why Erased Stories Cling to Objects: Why can't the Redactors simply snip a story out of thin air, and why must they hunt down physical relics to burn them?

## 🏛️ Established In-World Evidence
The Three Fates revealed that an erased story or person cannot completely fade as long as a physical piece of that event still exists in the world. The Raleigh Greek stele anchors the memory of the sea battle; Dr. Thorne's altered binder anchors the 1948 trial. Redactor agents hunt down and incinerate these physical objects to ensure the memories dissolve forever.

## 💡 Party Hypotheses & Inquiries
The party debates whether recovering enough anchor relics can force the severed events back onto the Great Loom of reality.
