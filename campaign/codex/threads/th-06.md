**Codex Category:** Active Mystery
**Type:** 🌟 Main Story Arc · Current Target
**Status:** 🔍 Next Objective
**Origin:** Origin: Session 4, 7 & 8

## 🔍 Core Mystery Question
The Erased Flight Handcuffs: How can the party infiltrate a modern D.C. police precinct to recover handcuffs from an erased 1980s airline flight?

## 🏛️ Established In-World Evidence
Naomi tracked the next thread to a Metropolitan Police precinct evidence room in Washington, D.C. Involves modern security: metal detectors, badge checkpoints, cameras, and booking desks. The relic is a pair of handcuffs from a 1980s airliner flight scrubbed from air traffic history. Dravin spotted a spliced architectural seam where Redactors patched an old evidence cage into the modern precinct. Dravin disguised Alfie as a red clay brick with Meld into Stone (sharpied with 'PIG') so police carried her straight past security into the evidence processing room.

## 💡 Party Hypotheses & Inquiries
The party plans to grab the handcuffs and retrieve Alfie before station lockdown alarms trip or the Hunter Clock runs out.
