# TH-02: The Raleigh Aegean Stele
**Codex Category:** Active Mystery
**Type:** 🌟 Main Story Arc
**Status:** 🔍 Active
**Origin:** Origin: Session 3

## 🔍 Core Mystery Question
The Stone That Shocks: What ancient naval battle is trapped inside the Greek museum stone, and why does touching it spark static and nightmares?

## 🏛️ Established In-World Evidence
Snatched from the high-security vault of a museum in Raleigh, North Carolina; carved with archaic Aegean Greek lettering. Physical touch causes electrostatic shock and blinding hallucinations of blood, rowing benches, and bronze warships. Causes chronic insomnia, forcing the party to burn lanterns all night. Naomi verified it as an anchor stone keeping the battle from being completely erased.

## 💡 Party Hypotheses & Inquiries
The party believes the stone anchors an erased Mediterranean naval defeat that someone wanted struck from human memory.
