# TH-03: Dr. Thorne's Notebook
**Codex Category:** Active Mystery
**Type:** 🌟 Main Story Arc
**Status:** 🔍 Active
**Origin:** Origin: Session 5

## 🔍 Core Mystery Question
The 1948 Big Pox Alteration: Why did someone alter Dr. Thorne's clinical notes from STABLE to STALE, and who sent the satyrs to burn the binder?

## 🏛️ Established In-World Evidence
Recovered Dr. Aris Thorne's 1948 trial notes from behind the university auditorium stage. Microscopic inspection shows physical ink tampering: 'STABLE' was overwritten to 'STALE' to make a successful cure look like a fatal poisoning. Satyr strike teams ambushed the party to seize and burn the notebook. Secured at the Margin safe house under Dravin's care.

## 💡 Party Hypotheses & Inquiries
Dravin suspects an enemy ('The Master') altered the trial record to hide a deliberate massacre as medical incompetence.
