# TH-08: Thanatos by the Campfire
**Codex Category:** Active Mystery
**Type:** 🛡️ PC Quest (Eusacles & Pierre)
**Status:** 🔍 Active
**Origin:** Origin: Session 7

## 🔍 Core Mystery Question
The Five-Dollar Watch & Pierre's Mother: What protection did Thanatos carve into Eusacles' watch, and why did he laugh at questions about Pierre's mother?

## 🏛️ Established In-World Evidence
Thanatos appeared in Eusacles' campfire dream in the Lost Roads, physically carving holy ward runes into the steel backplate of his cheap five-dollar quartz watch. When Eusacles asked about Pierre's mother, Thanatos laughed uproariously and vanished without answering. The runes remain etched in the waking world, humming with protective magic.

## 💡 Party Hypotheses & Inquiries
Eusacles suspects Pierre's mother is a powerful Greek mythological entity or deity hiding her child in the mortal realm.
