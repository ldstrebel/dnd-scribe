# TH-04: Teddy's Warning
**Codex Category:** Active Mystery
**Type:** 🌟 Main Story / Threat
**Status:** 🚨 Immediate
**Origin:** Origin: Session 7

## 🔍 Core Mystery Question
The Curse of Carrying Two Relics: Why does holding more than one severed thread draw Redactor hunters straight to the party?

## 🏛️ Established In-World Evidence
Caretaker Teddy warned the party at the cabin: carrying two recovered relics at once marks you. One piece can slip by unnoticed, but two create a pull that hunters can sense from leagues away. By holding both the Raleigh stele and Thorne's notebook, the party is actively broadcasting their location to Redactor tracking units.

## 💡 Party Hypotheses & Inquiries
The party suspects the resonance acts like an acoustic echo between severed threads seeking to reunite, alerting both Redactors and the faceless entity Majora.
