# TH-05: The Six-Fingered Man
**Codex Category:** Active Mystery
**Type:** 🌟 Main Story / Threat
**Status:** 🚨 Immediate
**Origin:** Origin: Session 5

## 🔍 Core Mystery Question
The Redactor Stalker: Who is the shapeshifting hunter shadowing the party across the mortal world?

## 🏛️ Established In-World Evidence
Stalked the party across university grounds; his visible mark is having exactly six fingers on each hand. Able to alter facial features and skin tone to blend into crowds. Pierre broke his pursuit with an illusory glowing rabbit decoy. Still on the loose in the mortal world.

## 💡 Party Hypotheses & Inquiries
The party suspects he is an elite Redactor field agent tasked specifically with recovering stolen relics before they reach safe custody.
