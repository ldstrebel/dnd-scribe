## 🎯 Pacing & Narrative Highlights
The campaign opening achieves immediate high-stakes traction by subverting the traditional tavern trope. Casting four modern strangers from a mundane Las Vegas Greyhound into cosmic freefall establishes deep vulnerability. Dravin's patrician intellectual calm contrasts beautifully with Eusacles' casino brawler cynicism, while Pierre's instinctive theft of ancient scrolls plants an immediate, compelling personal flaw. The emergence of Alfie as a freshly woven driftwood construct creates an instant table favorite.

## 🌱 Character Growth Cards (The Stormlight Standard)

Demonstrated quick-witted survival instincts and academic curiosity, pocketing classical manuscripts under extreme peril. Reluctant combatant.

### [Alfie](../characters/alfie.md)
Instant breakout performance: proud, martial, rejecting construct diminishment, executing lethal low-profile fencing strikes.

### [Eusacles](../characters/eusacles.md)
The practical combat anchor; immediately shields companions with improvised chain strikes without hesitation.

### [Dravin](../characters/dravin.md)
Grounds the cosmic terror with scholarly composure, deploying threshold frost magic to enforce tactical spacing.

## 🎲 GM & Worldcraft Evaluation
Masterful pacing of the reality breach; introduced the Fates not as omnipotent overseers, but as frantic weavers overwhelmed by cosmic sabotage.

## 🔮 Narrative Bets & Forward Hypotheses
- Bet #1: Pierre's stolen scrolls contain forbidden knowledge regarding his own ancestry that will cause friction with the party.
- Bet #2: Alfie's missing memories stem from an ancient Mediterranean naval battle connected to the Greek stele.
