## 🎯 Pacing & Narrative Highlights
Session 7 is a triumph of tonal balance. It pivots effortlessly between cold, visceral interrogation in a damp university arcade, eerie cosmic horror against a multi-faced stalker, warm domestic respite on a mountain porch, high mythic communion with the God of Death, and completely unhinged culinary slapstick. Alfie's nostril execution and subsequent 458-year thread confession cement him as the dramatic heart of the campaign, while Pierre and Eusacles' dynamic balances genuine vulnerability with laugh-out-loud comedy.

## 🌱 Character Growth Cards (The Stormlight Standard)
### [Pierre](../characters/pierre.md)
* **Actionable Growth & Trajectory:** Pierre is undergoing a fascinating transition from a self-absorbed, neurotic French academic who hides behind his rucksack into an active moral guardian. His immediate instinct to shield Susanna, keep her safe from Naomi's interrogation gauntlet, and ensure she has warm food reveals genuine heart. However, his defense mechanism is still comical evasion—insisting his mother is an 'Italian mob boss' when challenged, and fainting into the bushes at genuine violence. Next step: lean into the cognitive dissonance between Pierre's delicate domestic aspirations (croissants, clean berets, wine) and his terrifying primordial monstrous heritage. As the party prepares to infiltrate a mortal police precinct in Washington, D.C., how will Pierre handle the truth when he finally discovers why the God of Death found his mother so hilarious?
* **Table Chirping & Banter:** *"Let's review the tape: Pierre literally vomited in the campus boxwood hedges because Alfie gave a Redactor a fatal nosebleed, but two hours later he shanks his demigod teammate in the kidney with a bronze spear because Eusacles cast Divine Flavor on his flapjacks? Absolutely unhinged culinary priorities. And Pierre, my brother in Christ, nobody on the Eastern Seaboard believes your mother is an Italian mafia don. You are an archaeology intern with a beret and a pocket full of blueberry jam. Work on your intimidation face before we hit D.C., because the Metropolitan Police aren't going to be cowed by an accidental glowing rabbit."*

### [Alfie](../characters/alfie.md)
* **Actionable Growth & Trajectory:** Alfie delivered an absolute masterclass this session. The contrast between his newborn, wide-eyed wonder at mortal life (eating pancakes and jam for the first time, asking if police stations have gift shops and merch) and his chilling, centuries-old nautical violence is the emotional core of the campaign. When Alfie calmly states 'I'm filled with memories of really bad men, so it's fine' before executing the captive through the nostril, Sophie establishes Alfie as an ancient soul carrying heavy trauma in a tiny wooden frame. Next step: explore the boundary between Alfie's borrowed thread memories (458 years of drowned sailors' garments) and his own emergent identity that has only walked the earth for four days. Does Alfie want to be an action figure, a duelist, or something entirely his own?
* **Table Chirping & Banter:** *"A two-foot driftwood action figure who downs a shot glass of dark rum the size of a ceremonial baptismal font, staggers onto the porch slurring 'I murdered someone earlier!' to the local commune, and then explains the entire campaign cosmology using Harry Potter Horcrux lore is peak tabletop energy. Just make sure someone holds his hand in Washington, D.C., or the entire party's emergency funds are going directly into novelty FBI windbreakers and souvenir snowglobes."*

### [Eusacles](../characters/eusacles.md)
* **Actionable Growth & Trajectory:** Eusacles continues to be the pragmatic, unflinching anchor of the group. His nocturnal communion with his estranged father Thanatos is a major milestone: moving from a bitter, abandoned kid ('You've missed a lot of birthdays') to accepting his demigod heritage and earning his Tier 2 Birthright. The pocket-watch warding armor gives him a mechanical and narrative identity as Death's favored son in denim. Next step: Eusacles is terrified of the 'hero' label because he knows Greek tragedies always end in slaughter. As the stakes escalate toward federal jurisdiction in D.C., challenge Eusacles to step into genuine moral leadership rather than just reactive survival. He has the ear of Death himself—what kind of legacy does he actually want to build?
* **Table Chirping & Banter:** *"Eusacles finally gets a sit-down with his deadbeat father, the literal Greek God of Peaceful Death, and instead of asking about cosmic redemption, why planar tears are opening, or why dad never showed up to Little League, he basically asks for better dental coverage and a defensive gear buff. Classic John. And the ink isn't even dry on Thanatos' divine blessing before Eusacles gets impaled by his own baker over breakfast! At least the watch parry works. Next time, just let Pierre have his unsalted butter."*

## 🎲 GM & Worldcraft Evaluation
Thanatos was portrayed with perfection—not an ominous grim reaper, but a tired, amused celestial bureaucrat with genuine love for his son. Grounding the six-fingered stalker into Teddy's backstory transformed a random encounter into deep campaign lore.
