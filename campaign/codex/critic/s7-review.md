## 🎯 Pacing & Narrative Highlights
Session 7 is arguably the strongest narrative session of the campaign to date. It balances razor-sharp dramatic interrogation with eerie campus stealth, warm domestic comfort, divine father-son mythic resonance, and propulsive mission preparation. Alfie's cold, unflinching severing of the tendril ('We don't say the D-word around here') is a triumph of character definition. The rum pancake breakfast grounds the ensemble beautifully, and Thanatos' dream communion provides divine validation with sublime dark humor.
