## 🎯 Pacing & Narrative Highlights
Session 5 permanently elevates the campaign stakes from local survival to a war over the historical record itself. Hermes' arrival is brisk, transactional, and mercenary, setting a fantastic divine tone. Infiltrating the campus amidst student protests provides vivid modern texture, and the revelation of 'STABLE' altered to 'STALE' in Thorne's handwritten binder delivers a spine-chilling proof of active reality manipulation.
