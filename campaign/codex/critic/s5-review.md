## 🎯 Pacing & Narrative Highlights

Session 5 permanently elevates the campaign stakes from local survival to a war over the historical record itself. Hermes' arrival is brisk, transactional, and mercenary, setting a fantastic divine tone. Infiltrating the campus amidst student protests provides vivid modern texture, and the revelation of 'STABLE' altered to 'STALE' in Thorne's handwritten binder delivers a spine-chilling proof of active reality manipulation.

## 🌱 Character Growth Cards (The Stormlight Standard)

### Pierre

Excellent comedic complaints regarding American collegiate brutalism; served as keen lookout during backstage search.

### Alfie

Demonstrated stealth acumen, darting through ventilation shafts to unlock the green room for Dravin.

### Eusacles

Skeptical anchor; pressed the party on whether Dr. Thorne was a savior or an irresponsible medical charlatan.

### Dravin

Academic MVP: his epigraphic and historical training allowed him to instantly spot the altered ink in the 1948 trial ledger.

## 🎲 GM & Worldcraft Evaluation

Superb atmosphere; captured the chaotic energy of a 1970s academic hall while seamlessly weaving in planar satyr ambushes.
