## 🎯 Pacing & Narrative Highlights
A masterclass in tactical combat coordination. The fight in the amphitheater feels desperate, chaotic, and dangerous. The mechanical and narrative horror of cognitive redaction—tendrils physically siphoning Eusacles' memories—creates genuine terror. Dravin knocking out the transformed operative with a massive leather-bound classical lexicon is an all-time campaign highlight.
