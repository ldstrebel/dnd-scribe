## 🎯 Pacing & Narrative Highlights

A masterclass in tactical combat coordination. The fight in the amphitheater feels desperate, chaotic, and dangerous. The mechanical and narrative horror of cognitive redaction—tendrils physically siphoning Eusacles' memories—creates genuine terror. Dravin knocking out the transformed operative with a massive leather-bound classical lexicon is an all-time campaign highlight.

## 🌱 Character Growth Cards (The Stormlight Standard)

### Pierre

Tactical linchpin: deployed acidic barriers and landed a clutch Hold Person on the primary transformed operative.

### Alfie

Wild arcane combatant: triggered her Chaos Belt and anchored herself with a spectral mage band to hold the stage.

### Eusacles

Took the brunt of the assault; suffered a disorienting memory drain while shielding the scholarly party members.

### Dravin

Iconic moment of scholarly violence: channeled Twilight Sanctuary and flattened the paralyzed operative with an ancient lexicon.

Kept the combat dynamic and environmental, utilizing lecture podiums, tiered amphitheater seating, and janitor carts.
