## 🎯 Pacing & Narrative Highlights
Session 2 provides necessary narrative breathing room following the cosmic assault. Teddy's introduction as an 1846 surveyor living in a reality crease grounds the rules of The Margin with rustic elegance. The convenience store supply run brilliantly demonstrates that ordinary mortal reality is now deeply perilous, culminating in the sudden, shocking manifestation of Pierre's dormant Gorgon gaze.
