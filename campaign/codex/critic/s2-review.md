## 🎯 Pacing & Narrative Highlights

Session 2 provides necessary narrative breathing room following the cosmic assault. Teddy's introduction as an 1846 surveyor living in a reality crease grounds the rules of The Margin with rustic elegance. The convenience store supply run brilliantly demonstrates that ordinary mortal reality is now deeply perilous, culminating in the sudden, shocking manifestation of Pierre's dormant Gorgon gaze.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🧶 Plot Lines & Thread Audit

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Opened:</strong>  (The Nature of Timeline Fragments) — Naomi's Anomaly Board establishes the overarching campaign roadmap.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Pacing Note:</strong> The contrast between quiet domestic pancakes and sudden petrification preserves tension without dragging.

## 🌱 Character Growth Cards (The Stormlight Standard)

### Pierre

Fleshed out with rich neurotic details; his accidental petrification of the clerk induces genuine guilt and existential terror.

### Alfie

Deepened bonds with the settlement; found a gentle confidante in Rosa while sharpening her needle rapier.

### Eusacles

Showed domestic resourcefulness by rebuilding a damaged cabin and hanging a hammock; the pragmatic skeptic of the group.

### Dravin

Engaged Naomi's anomaly tracking models with academic rigor, helping synthesize the historical significance of the Raleigh stele.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🎲 GM & Worldcraft Evaluation

Seamless introduction of the Lost Roads as an atmospheric transit mechanic, turning travel into a tense navigation challenge.
