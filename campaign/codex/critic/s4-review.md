## 🎯 Pacing & Narrative Highlights
A tour de force of tabletop action staging. The mezzanine ambush is propulsive, creative, and physically grounded. Improvising a tripwire with an extension cord, dropping heavy clay urns, and using baguettes as distractions showcase party ingenuity under lethal pressure. The sudden shift into psychological horror when Alfie touches the tablet lands with immense emotional weight.
