## Editorial Verdict
Level 5 progression unlocks ancestral birthrights and clock-driven infiltration mechanics. Alfie's Sharpie-inscribed foot, Dravin's Meld into Stone brick trick, and Pierre's Make-A-Wish reception bluff deliver an unforgettable heist into modern mortal custody.

Endured having Prof. Dravin's contact info Sharpied onto her wooden foot, rode inside a thrown evidence brick into precinct custody, and slipped through the 3-inch drain to reach the evidence vault.
