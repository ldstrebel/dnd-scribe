## 🏛️ Directives & Methodology

The Redactors are hostile planar enforcers operating under a veil of mundane normalcy to eliminate temporal anomalies,

suppress unauthorized historical epigraphy, and enforce retroactive timeline redactions across mortal academies and planar crossings.

Answering to an unseen 'Master,' their operations seek to overwrite historical branch points to manufacture a curated timeline.

Operatives operate across several distinct echelons:

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Gorgon Units:</strong> Serpent-crowned infiltration operatives capable of direct assault and relic destruction.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Satyr Strike Teams:</strong> Heavily muscled frontline assault units employed for rapid kinetic ambushes in confined structures.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Tendril-Grafted Operatives:</strong> Captured mortals biologically altered with parasitic shadowy appendages to siphon memories.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Six-Fingered Stalkers:</strong> Elite multi-faced tracking specialists capable of hunting anomaly vessels across campus quads.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## ⚔️ Known Campaign Engagements

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Session 4 (Raleigh Museum):</strong> Gorgon strike units assault the Aegean antiquity wing to destroy the Greek inscription stele.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Session 5 & Session 6 (University University):</strong> Satyr assault teams and transformed operatives ambush the backstage corridors and storm the auditorium to seize Dr. Thorne's altered trial binder.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Session 7 (Campus Quad & Lost Roads):</strong> A multi-faced, six-fingered stalker shadows the party across campus before being repelled by Pierre's patronus.
