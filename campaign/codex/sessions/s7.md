**Codex Category:** Session Chronicle


# 📜 Session 7: The Interrogation, The Redactor & The Dream of Thanatos

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 📋 Session Overview

Tucked away in a university arcade, the party interrogates their captive Redactor operative, discovering that the mysterious 'Master' is curating a utopian timeline and that the party members themselves have become living anomaly vessels targeted for elimination. When the captive mocks Alfie, the driftwood duelist executes her with a needle rapier through the skull, triggering a blinding violet signal flare that draws an ink-cloaked, six-fingered stalker. Fleeing into the misty Lost Roads behind Pierre's luminous rabbit patronus, the company encounters and rescues Susanna, the museum worker lost since Raleigh. At The Margin, morale rebounds over rum-infused griddle pancakes as Alfie reveals four centuries of thread memory, Eusacles receives a nocturnal visitation and divine armor blessing from his father Thanatos, and Naomi outlines their next high-stakes target: an evidence locker in the heart of Washington, D.C.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🎬 Session Narrative Chronicle

### Act I: The Cart Interrogation & Alfie's Execution (Chapter 42)

Tucked beneath an arcade where modern university brick meets the misty fringe of the Lost Roads, Pierre, Alfie, and Eusacles interrogate their bound captive inside a rubberized janitor cart. The woman reveals the chilling doctrine of her 'Master': he is rewriting history to create an egalitarian, flaw-free utopia by erasing historical errors like Dr. Thorne's medical experiments. Crucially, she reveals that the Master no longer seeks physical relics because their temporal energy is now absorbed inside the party—making them living anomaly vessels marked for death. When she insults Alfie as disposable driftwood junk, Alfie calmly states that he is filled with memories of bad men and drives his sewing-needle rapier through her skull. As Pierre retches into the campus hedges, the dying woman's brain unleashes a brilliant violet flare into the night sky, acting as an arcane beacon alerting planar trackers.

### Act II: The Six-Fingered Stalker & The Rabbit Patronus (Chapter 43)

The violet beacon draws an immediate predator across the quad: a footless, ink-cloaked entity drifting over the grass with six twitching fingers and dozens of drowned, shifting human faces churning beneath translucent skin. Sensing the horrifying predator tracking their scent, Eusacles and Alfie lead a frantic retreat toward the tree line. Lingering at the threshold seam, Pierre is spotted by the multi-faced watcher. In blind panic, Pierre snaps his fingers and channels his dormant heritage, accidentally manifesting a dazzling, luminous white hare that bounds across the lawn in playful loops. Momentarily transfixing the dread stalker, the distraction allows Pierre to dive through the spatial fold into the safety of the Lost Roads.

### Act III: The Lost Roads, Susanna's Rescue & Return to Sanctuary (Chapters 44–45)

Traversing the perpetual twilight of the Lost Roads, the company is startled when a disheveled woman crashes through the ferns: Susanna, the innocent museum attendant from the North Carolina Museum of Natural History, who had been lost wandering the temporal mists for weeks after escaping petrification. Pierre greets her with theatrical Parisian charm ('You know that would make me your angel'), while Alfie shows off his souvenir museum cap. Harvesting ripe wild blackberries and golden plums along the mossy verge, the party guides Susanna safely out of the fog onto the sunlit grounds of The Margin. On the porch, Eusacles uses divine Mending to repair Pierre's snapped bronze javelin, restoring the party's two-man pancake-flipping technique, while Teddy listens to their report in grim silence.

### Act IV: Rum Pancakes, The Two-Fragment Curse & Four Centuries of Memory (Chapter 46)

Examining the severed rubbery tendril Alfie took as a trophy, Teddy blanches and confesses a long-buried truth: the six-fingered stalker is the Redactor, and it hunted Teddy decades ago because touching two fragments permanently curses a traveler to be tracked outside the sanctuary. Despite the looming threat, domestic comfort restores morale. Mike prepares a mountain of golden flapjacks drenched in rum-infused berry jam, and Alfie downs a massive shot glass of dark liquor. Tipsy and swaggering across the porch like an old sea captain, Alfie announces he has murdered someone, then reveals a profound ontological truth: while he walked the earth for only four days, he carries 458 years of collective memory from the drowned sailor cloaks that form his body. Recognizing his trauma, Rosa gently leads Alfie to her studio for counseling.

### Act V: The Dream of Thanatos, The Pancake Duel & The Washington Directive (Chapters 47–50)

Past midnight, Thanatos visits Eusacles in a silver campfire dream. The demigod confronts his absentee father, asking for armor rather than cosmic answers. Inscribing glowing sigils onto Eusacles' pocket watch, Thanatos grants his Tier 2 Birthright: grave-ward armor that hardens his skin whenever mortally pressed. When Eusacles inquires about Pierre's mother, Thanatos bursts into booming laughter, calling it an epic prank and asking if Hermes noticed. At dawn breakfast, tensions erupt comically when Eusacles casts Divine Flavor on Pierre's plain pancakes; outraged by the culinary desecration, Pierre shanks Eusacles in the ribs with his mended bronze spear, heals and stitches the wound with rum, and demands proper butter. Naomi then drops their next mission: an anomalous 1980s airliner hijacking whose evidence is locked in a Washington, D.C. police precinct. Following rigorous push-up drills in the courtyard led by Alfie, the party gears up, ready to infiltrate the mortal capital.

## 👥 Dramatis Personae (Ensemble Cast)

### Active Party Members

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Pierre</strong> — Horrified by Alfie's cold violence, bluffs the six-fingered stalker with an accidental rabbit patronus, flirts with Susanna in the mist, shanks Eusacles over pancake ingredients, and is trained in push-ups by Alfie.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Alfie</strong> — Executes the captive through the nostril without flinching, reveals 458 years of accumulated thread memory, gets hammered on Mike's rum pancakes, diagnoses the plot using Harry Potter, and drills Pierre on the lawn.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Eusacles</strong> — Pragmatic frontline anchor; mends Pierre's bronze javelin with prayer, receives Tier 2 grave armor on his pocket watch from father Thanatos, survives Pierre's culinary stabbing, and parries spear thrusts.

### Absent Party Members

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Prof. Edward Dravin</strong> — Absent from active scenes; conducting independent archival research in university history stacks on Dr. Thorne's altered 1948 trial ledger.

### Recurring Margin Allies

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Teddy</strong> — Margin Caretaker; reveals his own dark history with the Six-Fingered Redactor Stalker and the curse of touching two fragments.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Naomi</strong> — Strategic coordinator; debriefs the party on the podcast investigation of the D.C. airliner anomaly and preps precinct schematics.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Mike</strong> — Lost Roads gatekeeper; cooks rum-infused flapjacks, mixes rum jam, and assists in the two-man pancake spatula flip.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Rosa</strong> — Cabin matron; provides gentle emotional and trauma de-escalation for Alfie following his brutal execution of the captive.

### Notable Guests & Celestial Interventions

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Susanna</strong> — Innocent civilian museum attendant from Raleigh; stumbled through the Lost Roads for weeks before being rescued and brought to sanctuary.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Thanatos</strong> — Greek God of Peaceful Death; visits Eusacles in a nocturnal dream, engraves protective watch armor, and laughs uproariously at Pierre's lineage.

### Adversaries & Threats

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">The Six-Fingered Redactor Stalker; a multi-faced, ink-cloaked planar hunter drawn by the purple death flare to track living anomaly vessels.</strong>

## 🏛️ In-World Lore & Cosmological Discoveries

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">The Two-Fragment Curse & The Stalker:</strong> Teddy reveals that touching two timeline fragments permanently brands a traveler, drawing the six-fingered, multi-faced Redactor Stalker to hunt them whenever they step outside The Margin.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">The Master's Utopian Doctrine:</strong> The overarching philosophy of the timeline architect: systematically rewriting historical flaws and medical atrocities to forge an egalitarian, flaw-free world, regardless of paradoxes.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Living Anomaly Vessels:</strong> The critical revelation that physical contact with fragments infuses mortals with temporal resonance; the party members themselves are now the primary targets of Redactor erasure.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">The D.C. Airliner Fragment:</strong> A 1980s commercial airliner flight bound for Washington, D.C. where an armed hijacker was spontaneously neutralized and zip-tied without any passengers seeing who did it.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Thanatos' Bureaucracy & Tier 2 Birthright:</strong> The Greek God of Peaceful Death operates apart from Olympus and Hades as an independent cosmic arbiter, granting Eusacles grave-ward armor on his pocket watch.
