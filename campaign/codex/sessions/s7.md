## 📋 Session Overview
Interrogating the captive operative reveals a chilling truth: the party members themselves have absorbed anomalous energy, transforming them into living timeline vessels targeted for erasure. Alfie severs the parasitic tendril, triggering a purple flare that liberates the woman, Susanna, who is safely escorted along the Lost Roads. At The Margin, Eusacles receives a nocturnal dream visit from Thanatos, who engraves divine protection onto his pocket watch, while Naomi prepares the company for an expedition into the Washington, D.C. police evidence locker.

## 🎬 Session Narrative Chronicle
### Act I: The Cart Interrogation & Tendril Severing (Chapter 42)
Huddled around the janitor cart, the party interrogates the captive operative. She reveals that an entity known as the Master is systematically editing history to create a curated timeline, and that the party members themselves have absorbed timeline anomalies, making them living vessels targeted for erasure. When the woman insults [Alfie](../characters/alfie.md) as disposable 'junk', Alfie severs the parasitic cephalopod tendril with her needle rapier. The severed organism dissolves in an agonizing purple flare, alerting the Master.

### Act II: The Campus Stalker & The Rabbit Patronus (Chapters 43–44)
A six-fingered, multi-faced [Redactor](../factions/redactors.md) stalker shadows the party across the quad. [Pierre](../characters/pierre.md) conjures a radiant white rabbit patronus to draw the hunter away while guiding the liberated operative, [Susanna](../characters/susanna.md), through the Lost Roads to sanctuary.

### Act III: Rum Pancakes & Four Centuries of Memory (Chapters 45–46)
At The Margin, morale is restored by domestic warmth. Over rum-infused griddle pancakes prepared by [Mike](../characters/mike.md), Alfie reveals that while she has walked the earth for four days, she carries 458 years of accumulated thread memory from drowned sailor cloaks.

### Act IV: The Nocturnal Dream of Thanatos (Chapters 47–48)
[Thanatos](../characters/thanatos.md) visits [Eusacles](../characters/eusacles.md) in a campfire dream, engraving his pocket watch with divine protective armor (Tier 2 Birthright). When Eusacles asks about Pierre's mother, Thanatos bursts into booming laughter, calling it 'an epic prank' before vanishing into the mist.

### Act V: The Washington D.C. Directive & Dawn Preparations (Chapters 49–50)
Following Eusacles' nocturnal communion with Thanatos and the morning ritual of rum-griddle pancakes, [Naomi](../characters/naomi.md) pins a series of reconnaissance photographs to the strategic Anomaly Board. She identifies their next mandatory target: an anomalous artifact recovered from an unrecorded 1980s airline hijacking, held in the high-security evidence locker of the Washington, D.C. Metropolitan Police Department. As dawn breaks over the mountain ridge, the party straps on salvaged hoplite plates and readies their gear, bracing to infiltrate the heart of the mortal capital.
