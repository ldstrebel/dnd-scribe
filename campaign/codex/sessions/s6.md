# 📜 Session 6: The Auditorium Incursion & The Memory Seam

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 📋 Session Overview

Violent planar combat erupts across the university amphitheater as satyr strike teams and a tendril-grafted Gorgon operative assault the party.

Dravin establishes a defensive sanctuary of moonlight while Eusacles smites intruders before suffering a cognitive memory siphon.

Coordinating with Alfie's chaotic enchantments and Pierre's corrosive spells, the party paralyzes the primary operative and subdues her with a classical lexicon strike. Stashing the unconscious captive in a janitor cart, the company escapes through campus architecture tunnels.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🎬 Session Narrative Chronicle

### Act I: Amphitheater Under Siege & Memory Siphon (Chapters 32–34)

Horned satyr strike units and a tendril-grafted Gorgon operative assault the stage. Professor Dravin casts Twilight Sanctuary and Moonbeam to establish a luminous perimeter. Eusacles crushes an attacker with his morningstar before an enemy tendril strike drains a portion of his living memories.

### Act II: Acrobatic Chaos & Acidic Wards (Chapters 35–37)

Alfie activates her enchanted Chaos Belt and deploys a spectral mage band, while Pierre weaves acidic defensive barriers to deny ground to the flanking satyrs.

### Act III: The Lexicon Strike & Subterranean Extraction (Chapters 38–41)

Pierre lands a clutch <em class="text-slate-300 italic">Hold Person</em> spell to immobilize the primary transformed operative mid-stride. Seizing the opening, Professor Dravin brings a massive, leather-bound classical lexicon down upon her skull, knocking her unconscious. Bloodied and nursing cognitive memory wounds, the party hauls the captive into a heavy rolling janitor cart and dives into the subterranean campus steam tunnels, fleeing into the Lost Roads just as sirens wail across the university above.
