## 📋 Session Overview
Violent planar combat erupts across the university amphitheater as satyr strike teams and a tendril-grafted Gorgon operative assault the party. Dravin establishes a defensive sanctuary of moonlight while Eusacles smites intruders before suffering a cognitive memory siphon. Coordinating with Alfie's chaotic enchantments and Pierre's corrosive spells, the party paralyzes the primary operative and subdues her with a classical lexicon strike. Stashing the unconscious captive in a janitor cart, the company escapes through campus architecture tunnels.

---

## 🎬 Session Narrative Chronicle
### Act I: Amphitheater Under Siege & Memory Siphon (Chapters 32–34)
Horned satyr strike units and a tendril-grafted Gorgon operative assault the stage. [Professor Dravin](../characters/dravin.md) casts Twilight Sanctuary and Moonbeam to establish a luminous perimeter. [Eusacles](../characters/eusacles.md) crushes an attacker with his morningstar before an enemy tendril strike drains a portion of his living memories.

### Act II: Acrobatic Chaos & Acidic Wards (Chapters 35–37)
[Alfie](../characters/alfie.md) activates her enchanted Chaos Belt and deploys a spectral mage band, while [Pierre](../characters/pierre.md) weaves acidic defensive barriers to deny ground to the flanking satyrs.

### Act III: The Lexicon Strike & Subterranean Extraction (Chapters 38–41)
Pierre lands a clutch *Hold Person* spell to immobilize the primary transformed operative mid-stride. Seizing the opening, Professor Dravin brings a massive, leather-bound classical lexicon down upon her skull, knocking her unconscious. Bloodied and nursing cognitive memory wounds, the party hauls the captive into a heavy rolling janitor cart and dives into the subterranean campus steam tunnels, fleeing into the Lost Roads just as sirens wail across the university above.
