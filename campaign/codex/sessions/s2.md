## 📋 Session Overview
Within the timeless mountain refuge of The Margin, Teddy reveals the sanctuary's 1846 surveyor origins as a crease in reality where calendar decay cannot reach. While Naomi organizes defensive blackout drills and briefs the company on four scattered timeline Fragments, Pierre inadvertently triggers his ancient petrifying Gorgon gaze during an encounter at a rural station. Reunited at the compound, the party boards a vintage farm truck to navigate the uncharted fog of the Lost Roads toward North Carolina.

## 🎬 Session Narrative Chronicle
### Act I: Intake at the Mountain Sanctuary (Chapter 13)
Welcomed onto the cedar porch of [The Margin](../factions/margin-wardens.md) by founding caretaker [Teddy](../characters/teddy.md) and [Rosa](../characters/rosa.md), the survivors learn that the haven exists within a temporal crease where calendar decay cannot reach. Teddy reveals he has resided there since 1846 following the upstream redaction of his home town.

### Act II: Naomi's Anomaly Briefing & Blackout Drills (Chapter 14)
Eleven-year-old tactical coordinator [Naomi](../characters/naomi.md) introduces the party to the strategic Anomaly Board, identifying four scattered historical Fragments that anchor severed timelines across North America. To prepare the newcomers, she institutes rigorous blackout corrector drills.

### Act III: The Golden Glare & Departure into the Mist (Chapter 15)
During a supply run to a nearby rural gas station, [Pierre](../characters/pierre.md) is confronted by an aggressive attendant. Under acute survival stress, his dormant Gorgon bloodline flares: a flash of golden light erupts from his eyes, petrifying the clerk into stone-like paralysis. Reeling from the horrifying revelation of his monstrous heritage, Pierre retreats to the compound. With planar predators tracking the disturbance, [Mike](../characters/mike.md) cranks the ignition of the vintage 1978 farm truck. As the iron lever grinds to part reality, the vehicle plunges into the disorienting, unmapped dimensional fog of the Lost Roads bound for North Carolina.
