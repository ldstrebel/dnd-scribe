## 📋 Session Overview
A celestial dispatch delivered by Hermes bears a sealed winter warning from Persephone, alerting the Margin that planar veils are thinning. Traveling across the Lost Roads to University University in Pennsylvania, the company infiltrates a chaotic lecture hall amidst student protests over medical historian Dr. Aris Thorne. Infiltrating the backstage green room, Dravin recovers Thorne's altered 1948 trial binder—where 'STABLE' was retroactively overwritten to 'STALE' by an unseen hand—just as satyr strike teams smash through the doors.

---

## 🎬 Session Narrative Chronicle
### Act I: The Hermes Express & Persephone's Dispatch (Chapter 29)
Golden-sandaled courier [Hermes](../characters/hermes.md) arrives at The Margin, charging steep transit fees to deliver a sealed parchment from Persephone. The letter warns that as the goddess descends for the winter solstice, planar boundaries will thin, exposing the haven to predatory incursions.

### Act II: Infiltrating University University (Chapter 30)
The company travels via the Lost Roads to Pennsylvania, infiltrating a turbulent university campus engulfed in student protests against guest lecturer [Dr. Aris Thorne](../characters/thorne.md).

### Act III: The 1948 Trial Notes & Satyr Ambush (Chapter 31)
Infiltrating the backstage green room, [Professor Dravin](../characters/dravin.md) and Alfie locate Dr. Thorne's locked research binder. Examining the handwritten 1948 clinical trial logs, Dravin uncovers chilling physical proof of active historical manipulation: the diagnostic word 'STABLE' was retroactively overwritten to 'STALE' by an unseen hand. Before the party can formulate an extraction plan, heavy hooves thunder down the corridor and the green room doors splinter inward as horned satyr Redactor strike units assault the room with drawn weapons.
