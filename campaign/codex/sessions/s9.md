# Session 9: The DC Precinct Heist & The Flight 840 Vision
**Codex Category:** Session Chronicle

## Quick Session Summary

Crouched in an alleyway off Massachusetts Avenue under gathering rain clouds, the coterie mounts an audacious urban infiltration of the Metropolitan Police Department Third Precinct to recover confiscated anomalous epigraphy. With the masonry gargoyle triggered by Pierre shattering the front portico to create mass panic among the patrol squads, Professor Edward Dravin bluffs the subterranean evidence intake desk with bureaucratic manifests about missing "pig" bricks while deploying a clairvoyance sensor to monitor the security bulkheads.

Crawling through the galvanized ventilation shafts, Alfie drops into the high-security basement vault only to discover shelf 74 empty: Captain Brenda Bob requisitioned the relic two hours earlier to her private third-floor executive office. When automated lockdown shutters begin descending, Eusacles smashes his morningstar into the door frame with thunderous force, drawing the tactical squad away as Dravin lifts Captain Bob's magnetic keycard.

Alfie and Pierre infiltrate the Captain's office, uncovering a vast corkboard webbed in crimson yarn tracking decades of aviation disappearances, centered on a pair of silver flight-crew handcuffs labeled Flight 840. Facing approaching officers, Alfie unleashes devastating bardic mockery to drive an intake clerk into superstitious panic, while Dravin pacifies the corridor with *Calm Emotions*.

Touching the handcuffs, Dravin suffers a violent psychometric seizure, witnessing a 1974 Boeing cockpit over the Atlantic, a trench-coated operative with a timer, and the transponder code shearing from DOA to DCA as the Redactors severed the doomed flight into the temporal fold. Exfiltrating through the storm culverts onto the boatman's punt, the party returns to The Margin bunkhouse, where Pierre crafts Alfie a whoopee-cushion mattress and the companions debate Pierre's multiple maternal guardians late into the night.

## 🎬 Scene-by-Scene Detailed Review
- **Massachusetts Avenue & The Threefold Pressure:** Crouched beneath a rain-slicked alley archway, Pierre reviews Naomi's three operational constraints: tight time before the graveyard shift rotation, the precinct internal alarm threshold that will seal blast shutters, and the closing Redactor hunters tracking their planar wake. Pierre signals the weathered New Deal masonry gargoyle, which fractures and crashes onto the precinct portico, crushing two patrol cruisers in a storm of limestone dust and drawing lobby guards into the street.
- **The Clipboard Deception & The Air Duct Infiltration:** Dravin strides into the eastern intake carrying a black leather clipboard and fabricated municipal transfer slips, bamboozling a panicked intake clerk with bureaucratic liability threats regarding missing "pig" bricks while floating an invisible clairvoyance sensor above the vault bulkhead. Simultaneously, Pierre grapples a second-story fire escape and slips into the galvanized HVAC ducts.
- **The Empty Vault & The Cold Case Requisition:** Alfie slices out of a cardboard carton inside the high-security basement evidence cage, scampering across steel crossbeams to shelf 74. She finds the locker empty except for a yellowed MPD Cold Case transfer slip signed by Captain Brenda Bob: the anomalous cuffs were moved upstairs to the third-floor executive suite two hours prior.
- **The Lockdown Alarm & The Paladin's Hammer:** Automated emergency klaxons sound as hydraulic blast shutters begin dropping. Eusacles steps into the corridor and brings his heavy iron morningstar down against the reinforced vault frame with the fury of a siege engine, shouting about collapsing hydraulic lines. The diversion draws Captain Bob and her tactical response team down the stairs, allowing Dravin to pickpocket the Captain's magnetic keycard as she rushes past.
- **The Executive Suite & The Crimson Yarn Web:** Alfie climbs vertical conduit chutes into Captain Bob's third-floor corner office, finding the walls covered in a massive corkboard webbed with red yarn connecting 1974 airline disappearances, charcoal sketches of Redactors, and classified transit memos. At the center of the web hangs an evidence pouch containing dull silver handcuffs marked Flight 840. Pierre swipes the stolen keycard and joins her inside.
- **Vicious Mockery & Calm Emotions:** Hearing an approaching sergeant and technician investigating the door badge swipe, Alfie perches atop a brass desk lamp and unleashes supernatural mockery, convincing the technician that the cold case room is haunted by cursed spirits. As the terrified clerk throws the outer deadbolt, Dravin casts a silvery wave of *Calm Emotions* along the corridor, soothing the panicked officers into docile tranquility.
- **Psychometric Fracture & The Flight 840 Divergence:** Dravin grasps the silver handcuffs and is instantly hurled into a violent psychometric vision: the turbulent cabin of a 1974 Boeing airliner over the Atlantic, a trench-coated saboteur monitoring an explosive device, frozen clock hands, and the transponder code glitching from DOA to DCA as the Redactors sheared the flight into the crease. Dravin collapses onto the carpet but secures the anchor.
- **Aqueduct Exfiltration & Four Mothers:** Plunging down the back spillway into the subterranean stormwater conduits, the coterie boards the boatman's flat-bottomed punt, slipping along Lost Creek back into the Appalachian sanctuary. At The Margin bunkhouse, Pierre inflates a novelty whoopee cushion to serve as Alfie's camping mattress, and the party reflects late into the night on Alfie's sailor advice and Pierre's mysterious maternal demigods.
