# Session 1: The Bus from Vegas & The Library of the Fates
**Codex Category:** Session Chronicle


## Quick Session Summary

Displaced from a late-night Las Vegas Greyhound bus into dimensional freefall, four disparate strangers awaken inside the marble colonnades of the Library of the Fates.

As amorphous ink-beasts tear through the stacks to consume severed timeline threads, the company rallies with necrotic frost, chained morningstars, and fencing steel alongside Alfie, a newly woven driftwood duelist.

Escaping through a cosmic tapestry rift, the survivors tumble into the Appalachian foothills, crossing the fog-shrouded threshold of The Margin.

## 🎬 Session Narrative Chronicle

### The Shattered Highway & The Celestial Colonnade (Chapters 1–2)

A routine midnight Greyhound journey through the Nevada basin shatters when the vehicle plunges through a planar tear. Pierre, Professor Dravin, and Eusacles awaken not on asphalt, but upon vast flagstones surrounded by colossal marble columns stretching beyond mortal sight: the extra-dimensional Library of The Three Fates.

### Assault of the Correctors (Chapters 3–5)

The cosmic haven is under siege. Predatory ink-beasts known as Correctors pour through reality tears, seeking to devour severed timeline threads. Professor Dravin anchors an improvised defense with necrotic frost and silver chime vibrations, while Eusacles unleashes brutal brawling maneuvers with his watch-chain morningstar. In the chaos of the crumbling stacks, Pierre recovers and conceals ancient classical manuscripts.

### The Weaving of Alfie (Chapters 6–9)

Desperate for frontline combatants, the Three Fates weave Alfie from weathered driftwood, sea-threads, and sailcloth. Drawing her needle rapier, the two-foot construct immediately joins the fray, crippling massive horrors with surgical tendon strikes.

### The Tapestry Rift & Appalachian Sanctuary (Chapters 10–12)

As the cosmic Library buckles under the weight of reality fissures, the Three Fates cast a shimmering tapestry net across planar boundaries. The four disoriented survivors are torn from the collapsing marble halls and cast through cold, dimensional freefall, tumbling into the Appalachian night. Guiding their steps along an abandoned logging track through dense pine fog, they emerge before a weathered wooden porch glowing with warm kerosene lamplight: The Margin. Displaced from their lives, pursued by ink-beasts, and clutching stolen scrolls, the survivors cross the threshold into the unknown.

## 🏛️ In-World Lore & Cosmological Discoveries

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">The Loom of Time:</strong> The primordial metaphysical tapestry from which all mortal history is woven. Breaches or severed threads cause localized reality tears, spawning predatory ink-beasts.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">The Three Fates:</strong> Clotho, Lachesis, and Atropos—ancient cosmic weavers residing in an extra-dimensional marble library, currently overwhelmed by widespread temporal redactions.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">The Margin:</strong> A timeless sanctuary hidden in an Appalachian crease of reality where calendar decay is halted and displaced timeline survivors find refuge.
