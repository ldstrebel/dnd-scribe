## 📋 Session Overview
Ambushed inside the Aegean antiquity wing by serpent-crowned Gorgon Redactors, the company mounts a desperate defense utilizing improvised baguette explosives, a high-altitude clay urn drop, and salvaged ancient armor. Touching the fractured stele, Alfie is overwhelmed by a traumatic vision of ancient oarsmen and a burning coastal port. Grounding the relic's electrical surge, Pierre secures the tablet, and the party escapes through the Lost Roads back to The Margin.

---

## 🎬 Session Narrative Chronicle
### Act I: The Mezzanine Ambush & Urn Drop (Chapter 26)
Serpent-crowned [Redactor](../factions/redactors.md) Gorgons assault the Aegean wing to shatter the relic. The party mounts a dynamic defense: [Alfie](../characters/alfie.md) topples a heavy ceramic urn from the upper mezzanine while [Pierre](../characters/pierre.md) deploys an improvised baguette distraction and an extension-cord tripwire.

### Act II: The Burning Coast Nightmare (Chapter 27)
When Alfie physically touches the ancient stele, an agonizing sensory flashback overwhelms her mind: freezing brine, oarsmen rowing in pitch darkness, and a coastal village put to the torch by an ancient captain.

### Act III: Grounding the Surge & Flight to the Mountain Haven (Chapter 28)
With the severed stele discharging dangerous surges of raw temporal electricity, Pierre grounds the charge through an already-petrified attendant, salvages a set of ancient Spartan hoplite armor, and bundles the shivering Alfie into his coat. As museum reinforcements close in, the party dives into the spatial fold of the Lost Roads, fleeing back into the quiet shelter of The Margin. Though the relic is secured, Alfie remains haunted by traumatic phantom memories of burning naval ports, realizing that touching timeline fragments means absorbing the suffering of slaughtered histories.
