## 📋 Session Overview
Threading through the dimensional mist of the Lost Roads, the company arrives in downtown Raleigh to infiltrate the North Carolina Museum of Natural History. Donning academic disguises, Pierre bluffs security as a visiting Sorbonne intern while Alfie deploys Wordcraft to trigger a spectral sheep distraction in the lobby. Deep within the Aegean antiquity gallery, the pair inspect the fractured Greek stele anchoring a severed timeline, setting off museum alarms as hostile forces converge.

---

## 🎬 Session Narrative Chronicle
### Act I: Navigating the Dimensional Fog (Chapter 23)
[Mike](../characters/mike.md) navigates the vintage 1978 farm truck through the treacherous, disorienting mist of the Lost Roads, bypassing mortal geography to deposit the party in downtown Raleigh, North Carolina.

### Act II: Academic Disguise & The Sheep Stampede (Chapter 24)
Infiltrating the North Carolina Museum of Natural History, [Pierre](../characters/pierre.md) bluffs security by posing as a visiting Sorbonne intern in an oversized lab coat. To draw guards away from the security desk, [Alfie](../characters/alfie.md) executes a Wordcraft alteration (*Sleep* $\rightarrow$ *Sheep*), releasing a chaotic flock of spectral livestock into the lobby.

### Act III: The Aegean Vitrine & The Tripped Alarms (Chapter 25)
Deep within the darkened Aegean antiquity wing, [Pierre](../characters/pierre.md) and [Alfie](../characters/alfie.md) reach the glass vitrine safeguarding the fractured Greek marble stele. As Pierre inspects the severed classical inscription anchoring a broken timeline branch, the delicate security seals trip. Klaxons howl through the cavernous museum halls, emergency iron shutters begin descending across the exits, and armed shadowy silhouettes move rapidly through the corridors to intercept the intruders before they can touch the stone.
