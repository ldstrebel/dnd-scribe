# Session 8: Infiltration, The Lost Roads & The DC Precinct Vault
**Codex Category:** Session Chronicle
**Role:** Act V · The Capital Incursion

## Quick Session Summary
Advancing to 5th level at The Margin, the party receives critical birthrights—including Theodore's ancestral boon to Eusacles and Prof. Dravin's 3-seed pomegranate signet pin—before embarking toward Washington, D.C. Guided along Lost Creek's subterranean aqueducts by a reticent boatman, they repel swarming ink-blot stalkers as tracking clocks begin ticking toward the manifestation of Majora, a terrifying faceless entity hunting historical relic collectors. Reaching a storm drain opposite the D.C. metropolitan police precinct, Dravin detects misaligned architectural anomalies on municipal blueprints, Sharpie-inscribes Alfie's foot with his faculty contact info, and hurls a brick through a patrol cruiser window before casting *Meld into Stone* to smuggle the wooden automaton directly into evidence custody. With a living stone statue sent by Pierre's mother shattering the front portico to create mass panic, Prof. Dravin is swept into the suspect processing room where Alfie rests inside a tagged evidence brick, while Pierre races for the rear entrance as the cliffhanger incursion begins.

## Swain MRU Dramatic Scene Breakdown
- **Act I · The Hearth at Dawn & The Clocks of Majora:** Resting at The Margin, the party advances to Level 5. John realizes his Paladin had been fighting unarmored; Theodore bestows an ancestral birthright boon, while Dravin attunes to a 3-seed pomegranate pin granting a 30-ft temporary hit point aura. GM Luke Foreman introduces Blades-in-the-Dark tracking clocks: Prize, Threat, and Hunter, warning of *Majora*—a terrifying faceless entity that relentlessly stalks timeline collectors.
- **Act II · Lost Creek & The Wonka Crossing:** A cloaked boatman steers the party along Lost Creek through morphing subterranean vistas. Ink-blot minions ambush the vessel; Pierre repels the swarm with javelin thrusts while reciting Willy Wonka lyrics, though stray ink contact fills the first segment of the Hunter Clock.
- **Act III · The Storm Drain & The Sharpie Inscription:** Emerging from a manhole directly opposite the D.C. police precinct, the travelers survey the target. Dravin analyzes downloaded gray-web blueprints, uncovering architectural anomalies and shifted upper floors. To ensure Alfie is not treated as stolen contraband upon discovery, Dravin uses a Sharpie to write *"Property of Prof. Edward Dravin"* and a contact number across the wooden automaton's foot.
- **Act IV · The Brick Diversion & Meld into Stone:** Staging an intake crime, Dravin hurls a brick marked with a pig through a police cruiser window, then casts *Meld into Stone* to slip Alfie inside the brick itself. Officers collect the brick as evidence, unwittingly escorting Alfie directly through security checkpoints and into the evidence locker.
- **Act V · The Living Statue Diversion & Suspect Processing:** Across the street in the park, a living stone statue sent by Pierre's mother arrives to assist. Pierre directs the statue toward the precinct, where it scales the front portico and batters loose bricks off the masonry awning until decorative limestone crashes into the main entrance, triggering building-wide klaxons. As officers draw weapons and sprint into the lobby, the receptionist panics and ushers Prof. Dravin into the secure suspect processing room—where the red brick carrying Alfie in stone form rests on an examination table alongside three other tagged "PIG" bricks under active police scrutiny. Pierre breaks into a sprint toward the rear fire exit as the session concludes on a knife-edge cliffhanger.
