## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory
Teddy's quiet, stoic companion who maintains the settlement's vintage 1978 farm truck and operates the hidden oak-trunk lever that opens the dimensional gateway into the Lost Roads.

---

## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence
Spares words whenever possible; speaks in quiet, gruff murmurs accompanied by a slight nod.

---

## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment
- **Gate Mechanics:** Expert knowledge of the Lost Roads gateway mechanisms.
- **Camp Cooking:** Legendary rum-infused griddle pancakes that revive exhausted fighters.

---

## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory
Transports the party to and from the Lost Roads, cooking celebratory breakfasts upon their return in [Session 7](../sessions/s7.md).

---

## 👥 Ensemble Dynamics & Key Relationships
- **[Teddy](teddy.md):** Decades of unspoken camaraderie.
- **[Alfie](alfie.md):** Serves her extra helpings of rum pancakes while listening to her thread tales.
