# Mike
**Codex Category:** Non-Player Character (NPC)


The grease-stained pilot and gatekeeper who operates the mechanical lever junctions of the Lost Roads. Mike navigates the party across unstable dimensional transit corridors in modified transport trucks, avoiding Redactor hunting parties along the asphalt seams.

## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory

Teddy's quiet, stoic companion who maintains the settlement's vintage 1978 farm truck and operates the hidden oak-trunk lever that opens the dimensional gateway into the Lost Roads.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence

Spares words whenever possible; speaks in quiet, gruff murmurs accompanied by a slight nod.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Gate Mechanics:</strong> Expert knowledge of the Lost Roads gateway mechanisms.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Camp Cooking:</strong> Legendary rum-infused griddle pancakes that revive exhausted fighters.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory
