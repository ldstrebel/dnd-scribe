# Alfie
**Codex Category:** Player Character (PC)


An animated driftwood construct carved with enigmatic coastal runes, wielding a rapier with blinding speed. Alfie suffers from sensory trauma flashbacks involving freezing brine, dark oarsmen, and a coastal town ablaze whenever she touches ancient nautical stone tablets. In Session 7, she severed the tendrils of a captive Gorgon and dueled with purple flare magic across the Lost Roads.

## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory

Alfie is a living construct standing roughly two feet tall, carved from salt-crusted driftwood with sailcloth joints, polished seashell buttons for eyes, and a steel hook for a left hand. Woven into reality by The Three Fates in the Library of Destiny, she carries four centuries of accumulated thread memory from drowned sailor cloaks, despite having walked the world for mere days.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence

Raspy, confident Cockney sailor cadence packed with maritime idioms and razor-sharp wit. Fiercely proud and sensitive regarding her dignity—demanding to be recognized as an 'action figure' or 'fencing master,' never a 'doll' (which she vehemently dubs 'the D-word').

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Wordcraft (Reality Editing):</strong> Supernatural ability to alter spells and physical concepts by substituting a single letter (e.g. <em class="text-slate-300 italic">Sleep</em> into <em class="text-slate-300 italic">Sheep</em> to summon spectral distractions).

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Needle Rapier:</strong> A razor-sharp steel fencing needle wielded with deadly speed and surgical accuracy.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Tendon Sever:</strong> Low-profile acrobatic melee strikes aimed at crippling large adversaries at the heel.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Chaos Belt:</strong> An enchanted Fate-bound buckle capable of releasing wild, unexpected planar surges.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Signature Gear:</strong> Needle rapier, steel hook hand, sailcloth vest, seashell button eyes, and severed tendril scarf.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory

Awakening without personal memories in the Library of the Fates in Session 1, Alfie immediately proved her valor fending off ink-beasts. Touching the Raleigh stele in Session 4 unlocked harrowing flashbacks of burning coastal ports. In Session 7, she revealed 458 years of collective thread consciousness over rum pancakes, executed the captive Redactor operative in the arcade, and coached Pierre in dawn push-up drills.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 👥 Ensemble Dynamics & Key Relationships

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Pierre:</strong> Her trusted steed and confidant. She perches on his shoulder during travel and infiltration.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Eusacles:</strong> Sparring banter partner; Eusacles watches out for her safety despite her constant protests of independence.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Rosa:</strong> A gentle maternal bond; Rosa was the first Margin resident to listen to Alfie's fragmented memories.
