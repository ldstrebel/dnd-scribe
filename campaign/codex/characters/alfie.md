## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory
Alfie is a living construct standing roughly two feet tall, carved from salt-crusted driftwood with sailcloth joints, polished seashell buttons for eyes, and a steel hook for a left hand. Woven into reality by [The Three Fates](fates.md) in the Library of Destiny, she carries four centuries of accumulated thread memory from drowned sailor cloaks, despite having walked the world for mere days.

---

## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence
Raspy, confident Cockney sailor cadence packed with maritime idioms and razor-sharp wit. Fiercely proud and sensitive regarding her dignity—demanding to be recognized as an 'action figure' or 'fencing master,' never a 'doll' (which she vehemently dubs 'the D-word').

---

## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment
- **Wordcraft (Reality Editing):** Supernatural ability to alter spells and physical concepts by substituting a single letter (e.g. *Sleep* into *Sheep* to summon spectral distractions).
- **Needle Rapier:** A razor-sharp steel fencing needle wielded with deadly speed and surgical accuracy.
- **Tendon Sever:** Low-profile acrobatic melee strikes aimed at crippling large adversaries at the heel.
- **Chaos Belt:** An enchanted Fate-bound buckle capable of releasing wild, unexpected planar surges.
- **Signature Gear:** Needle rapier, steel hook hand, sailcloth vest, seashell button eyes, and severed tendril scarf.

---

## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory
Awakening without personal memories in the Library of the Fates in [Session 1](../sessions/s1.md), Alfie immediately proved her valor fending off ink-beasts. Touching the Raleigh stele in [Session 4](../sessions/s4.md) unlocked harrowing flashbacks of burning coastal ports. In [Session 7](../sessions/s7.md), she revealed 458 years of collective thread consciousness over rum pancakes and severed the parasitic tendril that freed [Susanna](susanna.md).

---

## 👥 Ensemble Dynamics & Key Relationships
- **[Pierre](pierre.md):** Her trusted steed and confidant. She perches on his shoulder during travel and infiltration.
- **[Eusacles](eusacles.md):** Sparring banter partner; Eusacles watches out for her safety despite her constant protests of independence.
- **[Rosa](rosa.md):** A gentle maternal bond; Rosa was the first Margin resident to listen to Alfie's fragmented memories.
