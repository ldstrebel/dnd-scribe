## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory
Theodore is the founding caretaker of [The Margin](../factions/margin-wardens.md). An Appalachian surveyor and woodsman from 1846, Teddy was displaced when an upstream timeline redaction wiped his entire home county from mortal maps. Living in the timeless crease for nearly two centuries without aging, he tends the cabins, smokes his pipe, and guides newly arrived refugees.

---

## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence
Slow, warm mountain drawl filled with rural wisdom, calm patience, and steady reassurance.
