# Teddy
**Codex Category:** Non-Player Character (NPC)


The founding surveyor and groundskeeper of The Margin compound. Theodore surveyed the Appalachian boundaries in 1846 before slipping into the temporal fold. He maintains the woodstoves, mended tools, and boundary wards that keep the Redactors from locating the sanctuary.

## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory

Theodore is the founding caretaker of The Margin. An Appalachian surveyor and woodsman from 1846, Teddy was displaced when an upstream timeline redaction wiped his entire home county from mortal maps. Living in the timeless crease for nearly two centuries without aging, he tends the cabins, smokes his pipe, and guides newly arrived refugees.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence

Slow, warm mountain drawl filled with rural wisdom, calm patience, and steady reassurance.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
