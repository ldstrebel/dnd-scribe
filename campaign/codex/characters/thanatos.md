# Thanatos
**Codex Category:** Celestial Entity


The Greek divine personification of peaceful death. Thanatos appears in divine dreams to Eusacles, having chosen him to bear his enchanted pocket watch. He provides cryptic guidance on the encroaching timeline redactions and warned Eusacles of deep secrets regarding Pierre's maternal heritage.

## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory

The ancient Greek personification of peaceful, non-violent death. Draped in midnight feathers and possessing a dark, booming sense of humor, Thanatos operates outside the clerical bureaucracy of the Fates, intervening through nocturnal dreams and threshold communion.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence

Deep, resonant, booming laughter with rich warmth and cosmic irony.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Divine Armor Blessing:</strong> Can enchant artifacts to bestow divine armor protection during physical peril.

## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory

Manifested in Eusacles' campfire dream in Session 7, engraving his watch and laughing uproariously at the mystery of Pierre's mother.
