## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory

The golden-sandaled messenger god of Olympus. Operating across cosmic creases and planar thresholds, Hermes functions as an extortionate interdimensional courier who demands exorbitant fees to transport travelers or deliver urgent missives.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence

Brisk, smooth, transactional, and relentlessly witty.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Planar Transit:</strong> The Hermes Express, bypassing normal geographic space in seconds.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory

Delivered Persephone's sealed solstice warning in Session 5 and extorted Teddy for passage in past decades.
