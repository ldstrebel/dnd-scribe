## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory
An intense, extraordinarily brilliant eleven-year-old girl residing at [The Margin](../factions/margin-wardens.md). Her sharp maturity and tactical discipline far exceed her physical age. Refusing to merely hide in safety, Naomi actively tracks 'fresh ink'—physical stone and parchment Fragments resulting from timeline alterations.

---

## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence
Rapid-fire, highly articulate cadence with zero tolerance for excuses, foolishness, or hesitation.

---

## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment
- **Anomaly Tracking:** Operates the strategic Anomaly Board, analyzing timeline fractures across the continent.
- **Combat Drills:** Conducts rigorous blackout drills and dawn push-up regimens to prepare refugees for incursions.

## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory
Directed the company to Raleigh in [Session 2](../sessions/s2.md), and in [Session 7](../sessions/s7.md) uncovered the airline hijacking lead pointing to Washington, D.C.
