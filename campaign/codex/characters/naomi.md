# Naomi
**Codex Category:** Non-Player Character (NPC)


The tactical coordinator of the Margin Wardens. Naomi maps anomalous timeline seams, organizes party infiltrations, and runs dawn drills for the travelers. At the close of Session 7, Naomi debriefs the group and assigns them to infiltrate the Washington D.C. police evidence locker to recover confiscated anomalous epigraphy.

## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory

An intense, extraordinarily brilliant eleven-year-old girl residing at The Margin. Her sharp maturity and tactical discipline far exceed her physical age. Refusing to merely hide in safety, Naomi actively tracks 'fresh ink'—physical stone and parchment Fragments resulting from timeline alterations.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence

Rapid-fire, highly articulate cadence with zero tolerance for excuses, foolishness, or hesitation.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Anomaly Tracking:</strong> Operates the strategic Anomaly Board, analyzing timeline fractures across the continent.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Combat Drills:</strong> Conducts rigorous blackout drills and dawn push-up regimens to prepare refugees for incursions.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory

Directed the company to Raleigh in Session 2, and in Session 7 uncovered the airline hijacking lead pointing to Washington, D.C.
