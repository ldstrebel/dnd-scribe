## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory

The primordial Greek personifications of destiny who dwell in a colossal marble Library beyond normal time. As they weave the cosmic tapestry on the Loom of Time, they actively combat timeline incursions by crafting living champions.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence

Multivocal, resonant, urgent, and stressed by cosmic timeline breaches.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Loom Manipulation:</strong> Weaving, measuring, and severing the threads of mortal history.

## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory

Spun Alfie from driftwood and dead sailors' threads in Session 1, extracting the party from ink-beasts via a shimmering tapestry net.
