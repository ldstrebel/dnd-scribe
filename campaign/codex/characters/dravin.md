# Prof. Edward Dravin
**Codex Category:** Player Character (PC)


Professor of Classical Greek history at Stanford University. Dravin bears an engraved signet ring tied to Persephone, allowing him to decipher ancient script and sense planar veil thinning. He provided crucial translations for the Raleigh museum Gorgon stele and led the expedition into the University University historical archives.

## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory

Professor Edward Dravin is a senior academic of classical Greek history at Stanford University. Clad in rumpled tweed coats and wire spectacles, he approaches cosmic timeline ruptures through disciplined historiography and epigraphic rigor. His maternal Greek ancestry connects him to Persephone, granting him quiet authority over threshold and preservation magic.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence

Patrician, measured scholarly diction with academic cadence. Even in life-or-death combat, he articulates observations as though delivering a guest seminar, maintaining remarkable analytical calm.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Necrotic Threshold Magic:</strong> Manifests spectral skeletal hands, frosty mist, and <em class="text-slate-300 italic">Chill Touch</em> to freeze adversaries.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Toll the Dead & Silver Chime:</strong> Strikes a tarnished pocket bell to inflict psychic dread upon planar beasts.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Twilight Sanctuary & Moonbeam:</strong> Radiant lunar wards that shield allies and bathe battlefield corridors in twilight.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Lexicon Strike:</strong> Crushing physical melee strikes delivered with colossal, leather-bound classical lexicons.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Signature Gear:</strong> Rumpled tweed coat with deep pockets, tarnished silver chime, pomegranate pin, and hoplite shield.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory

Dravin provided immediate tactical calm during the bus rift in Session 1. In Raleigh, he secured an ancient hoplite shield. At University University in Session 5 and Session 6, his scholarship proved vital in identifying Dr. Thorne's altered trial notes and neutralizing the transformed operative with a lexicon blow.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 👥 Ensemble Dynamics & Key Relationships

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Pierre:</strong> Fellow intellectual sparring partner; they debate epigraphy, historical truth, and translation.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Naomi:</strong> High tactical respect; Dravin admires Naomi's strategic acumen and anomaly tracking models.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Persephone:</strong> Devotional bond; he wears her pomegranate signet and heeds her seasonal solstice warnings.
