## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory
Professor Edward Dravin is a senior academic of classical Greek history at Stanford University. Clad in rumpled tweed coats and wire spectacles, he approaches cosmic timeline ruptures through disciplined historiography and epigraphic rigor. His maternal Greek ancestry connects him to Persephone, granting him quiet authority over threshold and preservation magic.

---

## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence
Patrician, measured scholarly diction with academic cadence. Even in life-or-death combat, he articulates observations as though delivering a guest seminar, maintaining remarkable analytical calm.

---

## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment
- **Necrotic Threshold Magic:** Manifests spectral skeletal hands, frosty mist, and *Chill Touch* to freeze adversaries.
- **Toll the Dead & Silver Chime:** Strikes a tarnished pocket bell to inflict psychic dread upon planar beasts.
- **Twilight Sanctuary & Moonbeam:** Radiant lunar wards that shield allies and bathe battlefield corridors in twilight.
- **Lexicon Strike:** Crushing physical melee strikes delivered with colossal, leather-bound classical lexicons.
- **Signature Gear:** Rumpled tweed coat with deep pockets, tarnished silver chime, pomegranate pin, and hoplite shield.

---

## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory
Dravin provided immediate tactical calm during the bus rift in [Session 1](../sessions/s1.md). In Raleigh, he secured an ancient hoplite shield. At University University in [Session 5](../sessions/s5.md) and [Session 6](../sessions/s6.md), his scholarship proved vital in identifying Dr. Thorne's altered trial notes and neutralizing the transformed operative with a lexicon blow.

## 👥 Ensemble Dynamics & Key Relationships
- **[Pierre](pierre.md):** Fellow intellectual sparring partner; they debate epigraphy, historical truth, and translation.
- **[Naomi](naomi.md):** High tactical respect; Dravin admires Naomi's strategic acumen and anomaly tracking models.
- **Persephone:** Devotional bond; he wears her pomegranate signet and heeds her seasonal solstice warnings.
