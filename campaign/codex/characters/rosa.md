## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory
A warm, maternal resident of [The Margin](../factions/margin-wardens.md) who weaves wool quilts, prepares cedar sleeping cabins, and gently interviews newcomers about their earliest pre-Margin memories to preserve their identity against temporal drift.

---

## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence
Gentle, soothing cadence that disarms trauma and invites honest reflection.

---

## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment
- **Memory Preservation:** Skilled at cataloging oral histories of erased communities.
- **Sanctuary Hospitality:** Provides medical care, bedding, and comfort.

---

## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory
Helped Alfie process her initial awakening in [Session 2](../sessions/s2.md) and provided refuge for Susanna upon her rescue in [Session 7](../sessions/s7.md).
