# Pierre
**Codex Category:** Player Character (PC)


A cynical Parisian stonemason pulled from a Vegas Greyhound bus into planar freefall. Despite his insistence on grounded physical reality, Pierre's Gorgon lineage manifested with serpentine shadows and petrifying power during the Raleigh museum ambush. In Session 7, he summoned a glowing rabbit patronus to escape a six-fingered Redactor operative on the university quad.

## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory

Pierre presents himself as a cultured, slightly neurotic Parisian architecture student with wire-rimmed spectacles, a dark woolen beret, and an instinctive disdain for brutalist architecture and American convenience-store cuisine. Beneath his fussy exterior lies an ancient, terrifying heritage: direct maternal bloodline descent from Echidna, the primordial Mother of Monsters. Sent by his mother to America to discover his origins, Pierre was swept into cosmic freefall aboard a Nevada bus.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence

Flamboyant, theatrical French cadence with articulate vocabulary and dramatic flourishes. Frequently sighs with exasperation, adjusts his wire spectacles when flustered, and peppers his speech with defensive French polite formulas ('Pardon, monsieur', 'Naturellement'). Under severe pressure, his voice drops into an intense, quiet focus.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Petrifying Glare (Gorgon Bloodline):</strong> A golden flare behind his spectacles that can lock living targets into frozen, rigid paralysis under moments of acute stress.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Classical Stonecraft & Epigraphy:</strong> Deep tactile resonance with ancient masonry, architectural seams, and Hellenic inscriptions, allowing him to decipher timeline stele.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Disguise & Infiltration:</strong> Masterful social deception, disarming guards with the persona of a bumbling academic intern.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Luminous Patronus:</strong> Ability to conjure a radiant white rabbit patronus to guide refugees through planar mist.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Signature Gear:</strong> Heavy canvas rucksack containing scrolls stolen from the Fates, spare spectacles, woolen beret, and bronze javelin.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory

From trembling panic in the Library of the Fates to standing as the party's defensive tactician, Pierre has gradually embraced his dangerous lineage. In Raleigh, he bluffed museum security and grounded a massive electrical surge through a petrified guard. At University University, he coordinated the takedown of a transformed operative, and subsequently manifested a radiant patronus in Session 7 to distract a six-fingered stalker while safely guiding Susanna to The Margin.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 👥 Ensemble Dynamics & Key Relationships

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Alfie:</strong> A fiercely protective, comedic bond. While Pierre complains about Alfie riding on his shoulder and making blunt Cockney remarks, he treats the tiny duelist with unwavering loyalty.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Eusacles:</strong> Constant banter between Pierre's Parisian refinement and Eusacles' gritty Las Vegas brawling.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Prof. Edward Dravin:</strong> Mutual academic respect; Dravin grounds Pierre's architectural passion with historical context.
