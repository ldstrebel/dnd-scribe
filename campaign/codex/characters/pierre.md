## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory
Pierre presents himself as a cultured, slightly neurotic Parisian architecture student with wire-rimmed spectacles, a dark woolen beret, and an instinctive disdain for brutalist architecture and American convenience-store cuisine. Beneath his fussy exterior lies an ancient, terrifying heritage: direct maternal bloodline descent from Echidna, the primordial Mother of Monsters. Sent by his mother to America to discover his origins, Pierre was swept into cosmic freefall aboard a Nevada bus.

---

## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence
Flamboyant, theatrical French cadence with articulate vocabulary and dramatic flourishes. Frequently sighs with exasperation, adjusts his wire spectacles when flustered, and peppers his speech with defensive French polite formulas ('Pardon, monsieur', 'Naturellement'). Under severe pressure, his voice drops into an intense, quiet focus.

---

## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment
- **Petrifying Glare (Gorgon Bloodline):** A golden flare behind his spectacles that can lock living targets into frozen, rigid paralysis under moments of acute stress.
- **Classical Stonecraft & Epigraphy:** Deep tactile resonance with ancient masonry, architectural seams, and Hellenic inscriptions, allowing him to decipher timeline stele.
- **Disguise & Infiltration:** Masterful social deception, disarming guards with the persona of a bumbling academic intern.
- **Luminous Patronus:** Ability to conjure a radiant white rabbit patronus to guide refugees through planar mist.
- **Signature Gear:** Heavy canvas rucksack containing scrolls stolen from the Fates, spare spectacles, woolen beret, and bronze javelin.

---

## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory
From trembling panic in the Library of the Fates to standing as the party's defensive tactician, Pierre has gradually embraced his dangerous lineage. In Raleigh, he bluffed museum security and grounded a massive electrical surge through a petrified guard. At University University, he coordinated the takedown of a transformed operative, and subsequently manifested a radiant patronus in [Session 7](../sessions/s7.md) to guide [Susanna](susanna.md) to safety.

## 👥 Ensemble Dynamics & Key Relationships
- **[Alfie](alfie.md):** A fiercely protective, comedic bond. While Pierre complains about Alfie riding on his shoulder and making blunt Cockney remarks, he treats the tiny duelist with unwavering loyalty.
- **[Eusacles](eusacles.md):** Constant banter between Pierre's Parisian refinement and Eusacles' gritty Las Vegas brawling.
- **[Prof. Edward Dravin](dravin.md):** Mutual academic respect; Dravin grounds Pierre's architectural passion with historical context.
