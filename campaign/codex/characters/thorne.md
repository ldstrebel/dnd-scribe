# Dr. Aris Thorne
**Codex Category:** Non-Player Character (NPC)


An eccentric medical historian whose lecture on the 1948 Big Pox clinical trials at University University was interrupted by a Redactor satyr strike team. His recovered notes binder is at the center of Campaign Thread TH-03.

## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory

An eccentric, outspoken medical historian who discovered that archival records of the 1948 Big Pox clinical trial were being retroactively rewritten. His public lecture at University University drew fierce protests and violent Redactor ambush.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence

Heated, rapid, passionate academic elocution punctuated by nervous urgency.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Archival Epigraphy:</strong> Unmatched forensic knowledge of 20th-century institutional trial records.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory

Delivered the pivotal lecture in Session 5 that exposed the alteration of 'STABLE' to 'STALE' before fleeing into campus tunnels.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 👥 Ensemble Dynamics & Key Relationships

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Prof. Edward Dravin:</strong> Fellow academic whose discovery of Thorne's binder confirmed the reality of timeline manipulation.
