## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory
A cynical, battle-hardened drifter from Las Vegas who was catching a midnight Greyhound to play roulette when reality tore open. Eusacles is the mortal demigod son of [Thanatos](thanatos.md), the Greek personification of peaceful death. Despite his gruff exterior and constant longing for a casino lounge, he is the party's unflinching physical protector.

---

## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence
Dry, gravelly American drawl marked by cynical humor, casino slang, and no-nonsense practicality. He speaks plainly, cuts through academic theorizing, and demands to see concrete physical evidence.

---

## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment
- **Divine Sensory Scout:** Ability to perceive metaphysical auras, distinguishing celestial, necrotic, and planar signatures.
- **Watch-Chain Morningstar:** A five-dollar wristwatch that magically extends into a heavy, spiked iron morningstar.
- **Thanatos' Tier 2 Birthright:** An engraved pocket watch enchanted by his divine father that bolsters his defensive armor whenever his physical vitality falls below a quarter.
- **Frontline Brawling:** Spartan kicks, shoulder charges, and brutal close-quarters melee combat.
- **Signature Gear:** Heavy denim jacket, aviator sunglasses, combat boots, and engraved Thanatos watch.

---

## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory
Displaced from the Nevada desert into celestial chaos, Eusacles has embraced his role as frontline guardian. He rebuilt a damaged cabin at [The Margin](../factions/margin-wardens.md), stood firm against Gorgons in Raleigh, and suffered psychic memory drain in the University auditorium. In [Session 7](../sessions/s7.md), his father Thanatos visited him in a campfire dream, validating his lineage and armoring his pocket watch.

## 👥 Ensemble Dynamics & Key Relationships
- **[Pierre](pierre.md):** Affectionate adversarial banter; Eusacles teases Pierre's Parisian elegance while relying on his magic.
- **[Alfie](alfie.md):** Protective big-brother dynamic; he checks the rum content of her breakfast and guards her flanks.
- **[Thanatos](thanatos.md):** An estranged, wry father-son dynamic marked by cosmic deadpan humor and unspoken respect.
