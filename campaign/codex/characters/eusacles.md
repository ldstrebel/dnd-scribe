# Eusacles
**Codex Category:** Player Character (PC)


A hard-hitting Las Vegas brawler whose pocket watch is an enchanted divine focus from Thanatos. Eusacles serves as the frontline shield for the displaced group, wielding brawler fists and protective spells. In Session 7, Thanatos visited him in a campfire dream, empowering his watch with a defensive ward (+2 AC when bloodied) and hinting at a cryptic secret regarding Pierre's lineage.

## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory

A cynical, battle-hardened drifter from Las Vegas who was catching a midnight Greyhound to play roulette when reality tore open. Eusacles is the mortal demigod son of Thanatos, the Greek personification of peaceful death. Despite his gruff exterior and constant longing for a casino lounge, he is the party's unflinching physical protector.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 🎙️ Personality, Voice & Physical Cadence

Dry, gravelly American drawl marked by cynical humor, casino slang, and no-nonsense practicality. He speaks plainly, cuts through academic theorizing, and demands to see concrete physical evidence.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## ⚡ Core Abilities, Artifacts & Equipment

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Divine Sensory Scout:</strong> Ability to perceive metaphysical auras, distinguishing celestial, necrotic, and planar signatures.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Watch-Chain Morningstar:</strong> A five-dollar wristwatch that magically extends into a heavy, spiked iron morningstar.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Thanatos' Tier 2 Birthright:</strong> An engraved pocket watch enchanted by his divine father that bolsters his defensive armor whenever his physical vitality falls below a quarter.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Frontline Brawling:</strong> Spartan kicks, shoulder charges, and brutal close-quarters melee combat.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Signature Gear:</strong> Heavy denim jacket, aviator sunglasses, combat boots, and engraved Thanatos watch.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 📜 Key Story Milestones & Campaign Trajectory

Displaced from the Nevada desert into celestial chaos, Eusacles has embraced his role as frontline guardian. He rebuilt a damaged cabin at The Margin, stood firm against Gorgons in Raleigh, and suffered psychic memory drain in the University auditorium. In Session 7, his father Thanatos visited him in a campfire dream, validating his lineage and armoring his pocket watch.

<hr class="my-4 border-slate-800/80">
## 👥 Ensemble Dynamics & Key Relationships

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Pierre:</strong> Affectionate adversarial banter; Eusacles teases Pierre's Parisian elegance while relying on his magic.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Alfie:</strong> Protective big-brother dynamic; he checks the rum content of her breakfast and guards her flanks.

- <strong class="text-slate-100 font-semibold">Thanatos:</strong> An estranged, wry father-son dynamic marked by cosmic deadpan humor and unspoken respect.
