# University University
**Codex Category:** Key Locale
**Type:** 📍 Academic & Clinical Site
**Status:** 🔍 Infiltrated & Investigated
**Origin:** Session 4

## 📍 Overview & History
An old-line academic campus in North Carolina with extensive medical and epidemiological archives. The campus lecture halls served as the site for Dr. Aris Thorne's controversial presentations on the 1948 Big Pox clinical trials.

## 🏛️ In-World Landmarks & Key Incidents
- **Medical Auditorium:** Site of Dr. Thorne's clinical trials presentation, interrupted by a Redactor satyr strike team.
- **Central Quad & Colonnades:** Open stone walkways where Pierre manifested his rabbit patronus to escape a six-fingered operative.
