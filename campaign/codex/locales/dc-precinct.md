# MPD Third Precinct (Washington, D.C.)
**Codex Category:** Key Locale
**Type:** 📍 Infiltration Objective
**Status:** 🔍 Heist Completed
**Origin:** Session 8 / Session 9

## 📍 Overview & Architecture
The Metropolitan Police Department Third Precinct station located off Massachusetts Avenue in Washington, D.C. A fortress of municipal law enforcement featuring heavy masonry porticos, subterranean evidence vaults, and hydraulic security lockdown shutters.

## 🏛️ In-World Landmarks & Secure Zones
- **Massachusetts Avenue Portico:** Front entrance portico shattered during Session 9 by a falling masonry gargoyle to stage a tactical diversion.
- **Subterranean Vault (Shelf 74):** High-security basement cage accessed via ventilation shafts by Alfie, where the Flight 840 evidence pouch was initially scheduled for storage.
- **Captain Brenda Bob's Executive Office:** Third-floor corner office housing Captain Bob's cold case conspiracy board webbed in crimson yarn, where Dravin recovered the Flight 840 handcuffs.
- **Stormwater Culvert Outflow:** Subterranean drainage pipe beneath Massachusetts Avenue used by the coterie for exfiltration onto the boatman's punt.
