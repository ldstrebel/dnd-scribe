# The Margin Sanctuary
**Codex Category:** Key Locale
**Type:** 📍 Planar Haven & Sanctuary
**Status:** 🛡️ Active Sanctuary
**Origin:** Session 2

## 📍 Overview & Geography
The Margin is an Appalachian mountain sanctuary founded in 1846 by frontier surveyor Theodore (Teddy). Situated within a natural fold or "crease" in the temporal weave, it remains insulated from calendar decay and Redactor erasure.

## 🏛️ In-World Infrastructure & Key Landmarks
- **The Central Cabin & Hearth:** The domestic anchor maintained by Teddy, featuring wood-burning iron stoves, ancestral survey maps, and common bunks.
- **The Bunkhouse:** Shared traveler quarters where the coterie sleeps, crafts gear, and holds tactical debriefs.
- **The Perimeter Boundary:** Stone survey markers driven into Appalachian bedrock that disrupt external scrying and disguise planar wakes from Redactor hunters.
- **The Boatman's Spillway & Lost Creek:** The subterranean culvert dock leading out into the aqueducts of the Lost Roads.
