# The Lost Roads
**Codex Category:** Key Locale
**Type:** 📍 Dimensional Transit Network
**Status:** 🗺️ Active Transit Grid
**Origin:** Session 2

## 📍 Overview & Geography
The Lost Roads are an inter-dimensional transit matrix of asphalt ribbons, crumbling turnpikes, and subterranean waterways winding through planar mist. They connect disparate historical eras and physical geographies, bypassing standard mortal geography.

## 🏛️ In-World Characteristics & Hazards
- **Lever Junctions & Seam Gates:** Mechanical transit switches maintained and operated by Mike the Mechanic using modified transport trucks.
- **Lost Creek Aqueducts:** Subterranean drainage canals navigated by flat-bottomed punts and silent boatmen to enter modern municipal storm systems undetected.
- **Redactor Hunter Patrols:** Hostile tracker teams and ink-beasts that patrol the road shoulders, hunting traveler wakes and dimensional flares.
