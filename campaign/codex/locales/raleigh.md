# Raleigh Museum of Natural History
**Codex Category:** Key Locale
**Type:** 📍 Historical Investigation Site
**Status:** 🔍 Infiltrated & Cleared
**Origin:** Session 3

## 📍 Overview & Architecture
Located in downtown Raleigh, North Carolina, this municipal museum housed classical antiquities and subterranean archival storage vaults. In Session 3, the party mounted an infiltration under academic cover to recover confiscated Greek epigraphy.

## 🏛️ Key Landmarks & Relics
- **Main Lobby & Portico:** The public atrium where Pierre bluffed guards as a visiting French researcher while Alfie staged a Wordcraft distraction.
- **Subterranean Vault 4:** Reinforced basement archive where the ancient Aegean Stele was held prior to coterie extraction.
