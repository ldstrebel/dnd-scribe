# Campaign Arc & Macro-Lore Ledger

This document is the **canonical cross-session narrative codex** used by the **Campaign Arc & Macro-Lore Steward (`arc-steward`)** to track long-term plot trajectories, faction movements, cosmological laws, and character transformation arcs across the multi-book publishing engine.

---

## 🌌 1. The Core Campaign Cosmology & Rules of Reality

### The Loom & The Fracture
* **The Loom of Fate:** History and reality are woven from temporal threads. An ancient, deliberate meddling has severed key threads, creating localized fractures where multiple conflicting histories attempt to occupy the same spacetime.
* **The Fragments (Physical Anchors):**
  - **Fundamental Law:** *A Fragment is NEVER purely abstract or merely a "moment in time."*
  - Every timeline rupture must anchor to a **tangible, physical relic** left behind from temporal tampering.
  - Touching or interacting with a Fragment triggers sensory resonance (visions of alternate timelines, auditory echoes, spatial destabilization).
  - Resolving or altering a Fragment re-weaves the local timeline (e.g., Alfie's Wordcraft shifting *Beacon* to *Beckon*).
* **The Reductors (Timeline Erasers):**
  - Hostile temporal enforcers (often taking forms like Satyrs, shadowy agents, or armed operators).
  - Their goal is to eradicate unapproved timeline branches and destroy evidence of historical tampering, cementing false or catastrophic realities.
* **The Thresholds & The Lost Roads:**
  - **The Margin:** A temporal sanctuary existing outside the normal calendar. Time dilates; the fog preserves and conceals. Governed by threshold wardens (Naomi, Teddy).
  - **The Lost Roads:** Interstitial pathways threading through the fog. They bridge massive geographical and temporal distances without physical vehicles, but require strict navigation or risk scattering travelers across disjointed eras.
* **The Celestial Registry:**
  - Classical deities and demigods exist in modern registers. Hermes acts as a divine courier across the veil; Persephone rules the underworld thresholds, periodically descending and weakening planar boundaries.

## 🎭 3. Character Macro-Arc Trajectories

### 1. Alfie (Player: Sophie Foreman Noone)
* **Archetype:** Cockney Driftwood Duelist / Reality-Bending Inscriptionist.
* **Core Trauma & Mystery:** Has no memory of being carved or animated. Deep psychological terror over being seen as an inanimate "doll" rather than an "action figure" with personhood.
* **The Naval Nightmare:** Touching the Raleigh stele triggered a recurring vision of oarsmen rowing in freezing brine through pitch blackness while a captain commands a coastal town to be put to the torch. She keeps lanterns lit all night to avoid sleeping or thinking.
* **Ability Awakening:** **Wordcraft.** Can physically and conceptually alter reality by manipulating letters and words (e.g. *Spare* $\rightarrow$ *Spore*, *Beacon* $\rightarrow$ *Beckon*).
* **Arc Trajectory:** From an insecure, comical mascot to the central metaphysical key to re-weaving the broken timeline.

### 2. Pierre (Player: Luke S)
* **Archetype:** Parisian Aesthete / Secret Gorgon Bloodline.
* **Core Mask vs. Truth:** Pompous, hyper-critical French art snob who claims classical proportions and obsesses over sculpture, berets, and masonry. In reality, he conceals a petrifying Gorgon heritage that he fears and despises.
* **Tactical Dynamics:** Uses bronze javelins, wire spectacles, and food diversions (spiced baguettes thrown at explosives).
* **Arc Trajectory:** From superficial, narcissistic comic relief hiding terrifying petrification powers to an unshakeable frontline brother who embraces his monstrous heritage to protect his found family.

### 3. Professor Edward Dravin (Player: William Webb)
* **Archetype:** Patrician Stanford Classicist / Necrotic Threshold Scholar.
* **Core Burden:** Possesses deep academic pedigree and aristocratic calm, but carries an occult pact sealed by a brass pomegranate signet linked to Persephone.
* **Tactical Dynamics:** Wields a Greek hoplite shield and brass pocketwatch; channels necrotic frost, *Thaumaturgy* theater bells, and threshold warding.
* **Arc Trajectory:** An arrogant academic who believes history can be mastered from textbooks, forced to confront the visceral horror and human cost of ancient gods and temporal tampering.

### 4. Eusacles (Player: John Hagey)
* **Archetype:** Cynical Vegas Gambler / Disgraced Demigod Brawler.
* **Core Insight:** Behind dark aviator sunglasses and a battered denim jacket lies divine blood and unclouded truth-sight that pierces planar illusions, temporal glints, and bureaucratic lies.
* **Tactical Dynamics:** Wields an iron morningstar and an enchanted celestial watch-chain; acts as the party's pragmatic, grounded anchor.
* **Arc Trajectory:** A burnt-out cynic on the run from divine debts who rediscovers heroic purpose as the unwavering shield of the vulnerable.

## 🔍 5. Active Campaign Mysteries & Open Narrative Threads

1. **The Origin of Alfie:** Who carved her driftwood frame, set her seashell eyes, and bound her soul? What was the ship rowing into the dark?
2. **The 1948 Trial Controversy:** Did the physician's compound cure Big Pox, or was it a lethal experimental atrocity erased by the Fates to preserve modern history?
3. **Persephone's Warning:** Why is Persephone descending early into the underworld? What will happen to the Margin's protective veil when winter arrives?
4. **Hermes's Agenda:** Is Hermes genuinely a neutral celestial courier, or is Olympus monitoring the party's intervention in the Fates' work?
