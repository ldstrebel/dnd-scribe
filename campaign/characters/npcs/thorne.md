# Dr. Aris Thorne
**Type:** Non-Player Character (NPC)

**UI Color:** `#0284c7`

## 🎭 Lore & Background
Dr. Aris Thorne is an eccentric, highly vocal medical historian who discovered that archival records of the controversial 1948 Big Pox clinical trial were undergoing anomalous retroactive revisions. Traveling between academic institutions to deliver warnings regarding historical manipulation, his lectures drew intense controversy and protest.

In Session 5, Thorne delivered a high-stakes guest lecture in the University University amphitheater. While the party infiltrated the green room to inspect his research binder—discovering that the critical classification "STABLE" had been overwritten to "STALE" by an unseen hand—Thorne fled into the campus tunnels when satyr Redactors attacked the hall, leaving his notes behind as pivotal evidence of the timeline war.
