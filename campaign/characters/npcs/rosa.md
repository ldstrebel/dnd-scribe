# Rosa
**Type:** Non-Player Character (NPC)  
**Role:** Cabin Matron · Memory Chronicler  
**UI Color:** `#f43f5e`  

---

## 🎭 Lore & Background
A warm, maternal resident of The Margin who maintains the cedar sleeping cabins, weaves wool quilts, and gently interviews newcomers about their earliest pre-Margin memories. She was the first to listen to Alfie's fragmented memories of naval battles and dark waters.
