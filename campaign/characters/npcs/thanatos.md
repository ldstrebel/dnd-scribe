## 🎭 Lore & Background
Thanatos is the ancient Greek personification of peaceful, non-violent death. Unlike the frantic, bureaucratic Fates who weave and snip reality from their marble library, Thanatos operates as an aloof, quiet nocturnal presence wrapped in a mantle of midnight feathers.

In Session 6 and 7, Thanatos manifests to his mortal demigod son Eusacles in a nocturnal dream beside the Margin campfire. Possessing a wry, dark sense of humor, Thanatos enchants Eusacles' wristwatch—his Tier 2 Birthright—to bestow divine armor whenever Eusacles falls below a quarter of his physical strength. When Eusacles inquires about the true identity of Pierre's mother, Thanatos bursts into booming, celestial laughter, declaring it "an epic prank" before vanishing into the night mist.
