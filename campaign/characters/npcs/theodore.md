# Theodore ("Teddy")

* **Role:** Caretaker and Founder of The Margin
* **Appearance:** Elderly yet vigorous gentleman in casual countryside attire, often seen resting in a rocking chair on the front porch of the inn.
* **Background:** Founded The Margin in 1846. Has not aged a single day since arriving. Meticulously documents every soul who enters the sanctuary and maintains historical records of time edits.
