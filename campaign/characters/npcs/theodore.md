## 🎭 Lore & Background
Theodore is the founding caretaker of The Margin. A surveyor and woodsman from 1846, Teddy was displaced when an upstream timeline edit erased his entire hometown from existence. 

Having lived in the timeless mountain sanctuary for nearly two centuries without aging a day, Teddy serves as a calm, pipe-smoking mentor to newcomers, explaining the nature of the Margin as a "crease in the map" where the eraser cannot reach.
