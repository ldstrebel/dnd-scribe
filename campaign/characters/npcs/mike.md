# Mike

* **Role:** Margin Host & Camp Chef
* **Appearance:** Easygoing man in his 40s with a surfer-boy smile and sun-weathered warmth.
* **Background:** Arrived in 2009 after surviving a cruise ship disaster in the Seven Seas that flashed to white, waking up in front of an ATM in West Virginia. Welcomes new arrivals at the campfire and cooks legendary Saturday pancakes.
