# Susanna
**Type:** Non-Player Character (NPC)  
**Role:** Rescued Operative · Former Captive of the Redactors  
**UI Color:** `#a855f7`  

---

## 🎭 Lore & Background
Susanna is a mortal woman who was captured, biologically altered, and weaponized by the Redactors. Grafted with shadowy cephalopod-like tendrils and subjected to memory-erasure procedures, she was deployed to retrieve altered historical records.

In Session 6, she participated in the incursion at University University, engaging the party inside the campus auditorium before being paralyzed by Pierre and knocked unconscious by Professor Dravin. Stashed in a janitor cart and transported across the campus quad, she was interrogated by the party in Session 7. Alfie severed the parasitic tendril clinging to her neck, triggering an agonizing dissolution of the dark parasite into a blinding purple beacon. Freed from the Redactor corruption, Susanna was guided through the Lost Roads by Pierre's luminous rabbit patronus to find sanctuary at The Margin.
