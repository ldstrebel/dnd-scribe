## 🎭 Lore & Background
Susanna is a mortal museum attendant from the North Carolina Museum of Natural History in Raleigh. During the events of Sessions 3 and 4, she was caught in the crossfire of the Aegean antiquity gallery heist and shielded from petrification by Pierre, who guided her toward Theodore's maintenance shed. 

Separated during the chaotic escape, Susanna stumbled into the spatial fold of the Lost Roads and spent weeks wandering the timeless, disorienting mist. In Session 7, she emerged from the ferns along the mossy flagstones just as Pierre, Alfie, and Eusacles were retreating from University University. Recognizing her immediately, Pierre welcomed her with theatrical Parisian flair, and the party harvested wild berries with her before escorting her out of the fog into the safety of The Margin. Exhausted but resilient, Susanna is currently recovering in the sanctuary cabins under Rosa's gentle care, safe from the Redactor stalkers who roam the dimensional mist.
