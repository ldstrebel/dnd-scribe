## 🎭 Lore & Background
Hermes is the swift, golden-sandaled messenger god of Olympus, operating across mortal thresholds, cosmic creases, and planar boundaries. Rather than a benevolent benefactor, Hermes functions as an extortionate planar courier who charges exorbitant fares ("an arm and a leg") to transport mortals or deliver sensitive correspondence across the cosmos.

Operating the legendary "Hermes Express," Hermes carries urgent missives between planar factions. In Session 5, he delivers a sealed letter from Persephone to the Margin, warning that her seasonal descent into the Underworld for the winter solstice will thin planar veils and heighten danger from timeline predators.
