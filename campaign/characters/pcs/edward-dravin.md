---

## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory
Professor Dravin is a mild-mannered, deeply intellectual professor of Greek history at Stanford University. Dressed in rumpled tweed, sweater vests, and wire spectacles, he views the party's bizarre temporal displacement through the disciplined lens of classical historiography.

His maternal great-grandmother possessed Greek heritage, which awoke in him an instinctive mastery over threshold and preservation magic. When ink-beasts assaulted the party in the Library of the Fates, Dravin spontaneously manifested necrotic frost and spectral life-tethers.

---

## ⚡ Core Abilities & Equipment
* **Chill Touch & Spectral Frost:** Manifests misty skeletal hands from floor seams to freeze and drag planar beasts down.
* **Toll the Dead & Tarnished Chime:** Strikes a pocket silver bell to unleash necrotic psychic dread against enemies.
* **Spare the Dying (Life-Tether):** Stabilizes fallen allies with a surge of necrotic threshold vitality.
* **Cause Fear:** Invokes an aura of classical dread to shatter the resolve of colossal rift demons.
* **Key Equipment:** Tweed coat with deep library pockets, tarnished silver bell, lecture notebooks, and spectroscopic magnifying lenses.
