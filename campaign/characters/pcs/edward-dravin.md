# Professor Edward Dravin

* **Player:** William Webb
* **Archetype:** Greek History Professor / Scholar-Necromancer
* **Appearance:** Distinguished gentleman with salt-and-pepper hair, keen inquisitive eyes behind wire-rimmed glasses, rumpled brown tweed jacket with elbow patches, button-down shirt, and leather satchel.
* **Personality:** Scholarly, excitable, endlessly fascinated by historical artifacts and ancient mythology. Treats real-world encounters with myths like an exhilarating interactive lecture.
* **Background:** Professor at the Institute of Research & Greek Mythology at Stanford University in California. Claims Greek ancestry through his mother's maternal grandmother.
* **Spells & Magic:** *Chill Touch* (spectral skeletal hands), *Toll the Dead* (sound of a muffled bronze chime), *Spare the Dying* (silver stabilization pulse), *Eyes of Night* (radiant amber darkvision).
