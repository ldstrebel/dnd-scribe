# Alfie ("The Driftwood Duelist" / "Little Man")

* **Player:** Sophie Foreman Noone
* **Archetype:** Miniature Sailcloth Duelist / Fate-Crafted Construct
* **Appearance:** A living doll standing less than a foot tall. Carved from salt-bleached ocean driftwood and held together with threadbare sailcloth canvas. Two mismatched buttons carved from sea shell and pink coral serve as chest fasteners. Matted strands of green kelp and hemp twine form his hair. In his right hand he brandishes a needle ground down into a razor-sharp rapier; his left hand is a wicked curved steel fishing hook.
* **Voice & Cadence:** Sharp, raspy Cockney sailor accent. Often goes temporarily limp like an inert toy when exhausted or hiding in plain sight ("playing Toy Story").
* **Origins:** Woven and animated on the fly by the Three Fates to defend their library during the planar incursion. Retains faint, fragmented memories of a shipwreck, dark water, and drowning sailors.
* **Special Mechanic — Wordcraft:** Possesses the divine ability from the Fates to edit spoken and cast spells by altering a single letter (e.g., transforming *Sleep* into *Sheep*, or *Vicious Mockery* into *Vicious Rockery*).
