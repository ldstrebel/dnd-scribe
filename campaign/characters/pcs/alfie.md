## 🎭 Character Identity & Backstory
Alfie is a sentient construct less than a foot tall, carved from weathered driftwood with sailcloth joints, polished seashell buttons for eyes, and a sharp fishing hook for a hand. She speaks in a raspy, confident Cockney sailor cadence and has fragmented memories of dark stormy seas, naval cannons, and blood on the deck before awakening in the Library of the Fates.

Perched atop Pierre’s shoulder or darting through air ducts, Alfie possesses the reality-warping Fate power **Wordcraft**—the ability to subtly alter the spelling of spells and physical concepts to produce unexpected, chaotic effects.
