# The Redactors
**Type:** Faction  
**Role:** Timeline Enforcers & Erasure Operatives  

---

## 🏛️ Overview & Directives
The Redactors are hostile planar enforcers operating under a veil of normalcy to eliminate temporal anomalies, suppress unauthorized historical epigraphy, and enforce redactions across mortal academies and planar crossings.

Operatives identified in field engagements include serpent-crowned Gorgon units and satyr strike teams targeting relics such as the 1948 trial notes and the Greek Inscription Stele.
