# The Fragments

* **Definition:** Anomalous physical artifacts leftover from historical time edits performed by the Fates.
* **Properties:** Emit subtle energy and temporal resonance. Touching a fragment can trigger visions of alternate realities and reveal hidden figures manipulating history.
* **Identified Fragments:**
  1. **The Greek Inscription Tablet:** Ancient artifact in the North Carolina Museum of Natural History sparking intense debate over whether it warns of an attack (*Beacon*) or summons one (*Beckon*).
  2. **The Toy Catapult Bolt:** UK pawn shop artifact debated by antiquities experts.
  3. **The Mad Doctor's Tour:** Popular book tour connected to unexplained medical anomalies.
  4. **The DC Plane Incident:** Commercial airliner hijacking where the plane landed safely with zero explanation.
