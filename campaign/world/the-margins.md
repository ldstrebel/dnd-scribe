# The Margin

* **Nature:** A consecrated sanctuary outside of conventional time and space, hidden within rural West Virginia near Ripley.
* **The Mist:** A protective veil that prevents the outside world from perceiving the sanctuary, making it appear as a defunct, abandoned property to ordinary mortals.
* **The Edited:** Displaced souls who were removed from regular history during unexpected edits and fatal accidents. Residents do not age while residing within the Margin.
