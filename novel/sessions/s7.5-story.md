# Chapter 74: The Mycelium Dampener & The Diplomat's Deck

The heavy oak doors of Sterling Hall creaked shut behind Aggie, muffling the lingering murmurs of the afternoon lecture. Thursday sunlight spilled across the cobblestones of the courtyard, where cadets and mechanics hurried toward the harbor and the colonnade. Aggie adjusted her dark traveler’s pack, her quiet eyes sweeping the stone perimeter with the sharp instinct of an unburdened scout beneath the open sky, tracking the atmospheric charge that always preceded a major festival. Before she could plot a path toward the dormitories, an unmistakable whirlwind descended upon the path.

Laden with bulging canvas satchels, dried root bundles dangling from leather straps, and pockets overflowing with fresh aromatic herbs, Angela Galaspora bustled through the archway. A crown of living woven green leaves rested lopsided upon her graying hair. She beamed with uncontainable warmth, waving both hands vigorously as she ambushed Aggie in the open court.

"Hey girls! Just the Mizizi I was looking for. Come with me—we have a day ahead!" <!-- L0027 -->

Aggie exchanged a quick, knowing glance with Britt, whose green woven tunic rustled as she tried to match the elder’s relentless pace. Aunt Angela was an elemental force of nature; resisting her was like trying to redirect a summer thunderstorm. Nodding, Aggie fell into step beside the botanist, letting the elder herd them through the university’s iron gates and toward the sprawling thoroughfare.

The crisp white marble pillars of The Colonnade soon rose before them, framing a breathtaking panoramic vista of the canyon below. Sunlight glinted off polished brass balustrades and the massive suspension cradles of the airship berths. Far below in the half-mile basalt chasm of the Apex Ring, the fairgrounds swarmed with thousands of spectators. Team pennants whipped in the canyon gusts, while the deep, resonant hum of heavy motor blocks and the high-pitched whine of next-generation harmony batteries vibrated through the stone floorboards. Vendors threaded through the throngs hoisting backpack dispensers of spiced cider and cider-grog.

As they paused near the ramp of the VIP berth, Aunt Angela leaned in close to Aggie, lowering her voice to an urgent murmur. Her sharp eyes darted toward Britt, who lagged half a step behind with slightly bowed shoulders.

"Is Britt okay, child? She looks... slouched. Out of breath." <!-- L0045 -->

A spark of apprehension flickered through Aggie’s mind like lightning behind cloud cover. Focusing her gaze, she stepped closer to inspect her friend. Britt’s breathing was noticeably shallow, strained and uneven against the mountain air. Worse, the delicate velvet fungal blooms along her carapace appeared brittle and parchment-dry, lacking their usual vibrant moisture. Aggie’s fingers hovered near the dried patches, sensing deep discordance within the living tissue. While the exact chemical nature of the botanical toxin remained unfamiliar, Aggie knew beyond all doubt that an unnatural affliction had taken root.

Aggie turned to Aunt Angela, keeping her voice low and tight so as not to alarm the passing socialites. She described the dry desiccation on the shell, the unnatural exhaustion, and the constricted rhythm in the lungs.

Angela’s face tightened into deep, troubled furrows as she gently took Britt’s fingers into her own, turning them over in the daylight.

"I thought she looked strange. She does not look too bad, but I get a strange feeling of deja vu looking at her right now, though I can't place it. I need my books—you must find me in the forest after exams!" <!-- L0056 -->

The elder offered a steadying squeeze, urging them onward toward the gangway before lingering eyes could notice the tension. They crossed the boarding ramp and stepped onto the grand observation deck of the *Zephyr*.

The flagship vessel was a triumphant display of imperial Harmony engineering and luxury. Warm, polished mahogany planking gleamed under rich gaslight lanterns, and ornate brass railings bounded the perimeter. At the center of the promenade, a colossal circular floor of reinforced crystal looked straight down a mile into the dizzying depths of the basalt canyon floor.

Standing near the entryway to welcome dignitaries was Valentine Sterling Sr., the renowned explorer who had first mapped the Mizizi territories. He wore a crisp field blazer and held an open leather journal covered in handwritten vehicle schematics and canyon drafts. Beside him stood Cade Ashveil, a tall, composed Ash-blood with the proud posture of his volcanic ancestry.

Aunt Angela immediately surged forward into the diplomatic reception, beaming as if no shadow hung over the day.

"Ah Mr. Cade, thank you thank you for arranging this adventure and pass my thanks along to Lady Ignis! Mr. Sterling, it is of course fantastic to see you again. I am so excited to see this ship fly and to learn about this Resy Race." <!-- L0063 -->

The explorer offered an amused, polite chuckle, bowing his head slightly as he adjusted his spectacles.

"Hello Elder Angela, welcome to the Zephyr, please note it is a Reso race not resy, but I am very excited for this season to begin—do you know any of the racers?" <!-- L0068 -->

Angela’s hands fluttered as she gestured vaguely toward the chasm, her thoughts already flitting across the ship’s elaborate decor.

"Well I think our fellow Mycelium Circle member Rill had something to do with one of the teams, but honestly I forget which—wow look at these ferns...!" <!-- L0072 -->

The elder pivoted abruptly toward a massive, cascading urn of exotic botanical fronds lining the entryway. As Sterling smiled knowingly and began guiding them inside toward the hospitality lounges, Cade Ashveil cleared his throat, attempting to bridge the polite social pause.

"Well these ferns are great, almost as cool as the rocks out in the race." <!-- L0076 -->

Aggie nodded courteously, pointing out how dense cliffside foliage could easily mask an overtaking crawler on the track’s blind switchbacks, drawing an approving nod from the Ash-blood officer. Leaving the elder botanist and the dignitaries to their pleasantries, Aggie drifted along the perimeter of the passenger deck.

Liveried stewards carrying silver trays of delicate appetizers and bubbling refreshments glided between clusters of aristocrats and academy patrons. Toward the refreshment tables, Aggie spotted two familiar first-year cadets: Pip, frantically stuffing pockets full of sweet sky-bites, and Bramble, a towering, gentle youth who stood clutching an immense, leather-bound manual on structural mycelium physiology with an expression of pure dread.

Aggie approached them with a quiet smile. Seeing Bramble’s exhaustion, she pulled a compact primer on aero-static lift and glider balance from her satchel, offering an impromptu trade. Bramble’s eyes lit up with profound relief as he practically thrust the heavy Mycelium treatise into her hands, eager to escape botanical theory for basic aeronautics.

Tucking herself into a quiet corner, Aggie leafed through Bramble’s dense, margin-crowded notes. Her sharp gaze picked out crucial scribbles detailing the extraction of active dampening enzymes from local root fibers. Reading between the hasty ink lines, she reconstructed the forgotten schematic for brewing a potent Mycelium Spore-Dampener—a vital counter-agent capable of soothing acute fungal stress.

Pocketing the breakthrough, Aggie slipped past the busy dining saloon into the stateroom corridor. Standing alone by a wide panoramic viewing pane was Valentine Sterling Jr. The young heir turned as she approached, his shoulders relaxing as he recognized a trusted classmate.

"I'm glad you're here. I wanted to pick you guys up from the dorms myself, but my father wouldn't let me leave the ship. He sent Ludo instead... who I see is currently enjoying his off-duty status at the bar." <!-- L0090 -->

Aggie paused beside him, murmuring an inquiry about tomorrow’s entrance trials. Val glanced down the carpeted hall, leaning in close.

"Look, you need to be ready. I plan to lock myself in my room to study tonight. My father won't say much, but there is something after the written test though even my father won't tell me what. He's fair if nothing else." <!-- L0095 -->

Aggie offered a firm nod of gratitude, committing the warning to memory. As she made her way back to rejoin Aunt Angela, the deep blast of the first ceremonial race horn reverberated through the hull, announcing that the trials of the day had officially begun.

# Chapter 75: The Wager on Pudge & The Spore Sickness

The afternoon air felt strangely heavy against Britt’s throat as she exited Sterling Hall. Even through the coarse weave of her favorite green traveler’s tunic, her skin prickled with an uncharacteristic chill. A subtle, disorienting flutter pulsed behind her breastbone, leaving her faintly breathless and unsteady upon the flagstones. She blinked against the courtyard glare, silently chalking the fatigue up to the dry mountain altitude and late nights poring over academy maps.

Before she could sit and rest, Aunt Angela Galaspora swept across the path in an eruption of dangling leather herb pouches and trailing green leaves.

"Hey girls! Just the Mizizi I was looking for. Come with me—we have a day ahead!" <!-- L0119 -->

Britt managed a faint smile, pressing a moss-stained palm against her side as she forced her stride to match the elder’s lively pace. Together with Aggie, they threaded through the university’s iron gates. Ahead, the grand marble arches of The Colonnade stood stark and brilliant against the azure sky, looking down onto the vast expanse of the canyon.

A sudden, sharp wave of vertigo crashed over Britt. The loud chatter of the crowd and the drone of charging battery rigs blurred into a muted hum. For a breathless moment, she realized she hadn't heard a single word exchanged between Aggie and Angela since passing the gates. She stopped, rubbing her temples and inhaling deeply. Drawing upon the rooted resilience of the ancient soil back home, she centered herself with quiet willpower. The dizzy spell receded like a retreating tide, leaving her mind sharp and alert once more. Looking up, she caught Aggie’s lightning-quick eyes watching her with intense concern, alongside Angela’s furrowed brow. Britt offered a reassuring nod and stepped toward the mooring gangway.

Ascending to the grand promenade deck of the *Zephyr*, Britt paused to admire the sheer splendor of the vessel. Polished mahogany boards gleamed beneath rich gas lamps, and the circular floor of heavy plate crystal offered a sheer, vertical view straight into the heart of the basalt canyon below.

At the entrance to the main salon, Valentine Sterling Sr. stood conferring with Cade Ashveil, holding an open notebook dense with handwritten rig profiles. Angela bustled up to them with unrestrained exuberance.

"Ah Mr. Cade, thank you thank you for arranging this adventure and pass my thanks along to Lady Ignis! Mr. Sterling, it is of course fantastic to see you again. I am so excited to see this ship fly and to learn about this Resy Race." <!-- L0146 -->

The elder explorer smiled warmly, dipping his head in courteous greeting.

"Hello Elder Angela, welcome to the Zephyr, please note it is a Reso race not resy, but I am very excited for this season to begin—do you know any of the racers?" <!-- L0151 -->

Angela waved an airy, dismissive hand toward the balustrade, her attention immediately captured by the towering ornamental flora nearby.

"Well I think our fellow Mycelium Circle member Rill had something to do with one of the teams, but honestly I forget which—wow look at these ferns...!" <!-- L0155 -->

As Sterling chuckled and gestured them inward, Cade Ashveil cleared his throat, offering an earnest observation to fill the gap.

"Well these ferns are great, almost as cool as the rocks out in the race." <!-- L0159 -->

Britt politely bowed out of the circle, letting Aunt Angela chat as she wandered toward the promenade rail. Glancing across the bustling upper deck, a remarkably familiar silhouette caught her eye: the broad, rugged shoulders of Lomi, complete with his oil-stained canvas mechanic collar and woolen flat cap. To her utter astonishment, the shipwright was actually aboard the VIP airship itself, hauling heavy wooden crates of distilled grog and arguing heatedly with an exasperated banquet steward about selling his potent, furnace-grade 'Ambrosia of Luck' directly to the aristocrats.

"Lomi!" Britt shouted, pushing forward through the gathering throng to reach him.

Just as she closed the distance, a massive cheer erupted from a cluster of drunken racing fans celebrating an engine fire on the tarmac below. A boisterous patron swung around with a celebratory roar, thrusting a squirming, crying baby root-kin straight into Britt’s arms. Before she could protest, fellow spectators clapped her on the back, toasted her with frothing mugs, and pulled her into their jubilant dance. By the time Britt gently handed the crying child back to its sheepish guardian and pried herself free from the crowd, Lomi had vanished entirely into the labyrinth of service hatches.

With the race start approaching, Britt descended the brass staircase toward the ship's glass Observatory. Inside, Valerius Sterling was presiding over a live commentary terminal on the aether-vox while managing a bustling wagering slate for high-society spectators.

"Place your stakes, ladies and gentlemen!" Valerius called out, chalking up the latest track odds. "Shatter Stamper at two-to-one! Siren’s Wake at three-to-one! And young Pudge, our heavy dark horse on Team Four, sitting at four-to-one!"

Britt smiled, remembering the solid, unassuming grit of Pudge’s rig from the hangar floors. Stepping forward, she slid ten copper coins across the polished counter.

"Ten copper on Pudge to win," she declared, pushing the coins forward.

Valerius raised an eyebrow with an approving grin, swiftly inking the formal wager receipt and sliding it across to her. Tucking the slip into her pocket, she turned and made her way down the quiet stateroom corridor.

There, leaning against the glass wall overlooking the canyon launch pads, stood Valentine Sterling Jr. He looked visibly relieved as Britt approached.

"I'm glad you're here. I wanted to pick you guys up from the dorms myself, but my father wouldn't let me leave the ship. He sent Ludo instead... who I see is currently enjoying his off-duty status at the bar." <!-- L0187 -->

Britt paused beside him, murmuring an inquiry about tomorrow's entrance exam. Val looked around the corridor, then leaned in close, his voice dropping low.

"Look, you need to be ready. I plan to lock myself in my room to study tonight. My father won't say much, but there is something after the written test though even my father won't tell me what. He's fair if nothing else." <!-- L0192 -->

Britt thanked him, promising complete discretion. As she hurried back toward the promenade deck to rejoin Aunt Angela and Aggie, the great brass starting horn bellowed across the canyon chasm, shaking the glass underfoot as the engines flared to life.
